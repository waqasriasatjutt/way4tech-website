Background Remover: Cut Out. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

Your photos

All photo work (finding the person, cutting out the background, the brush edits, new backgrounds, blur, resizing and saving) runs on your phone. We do not upload, store or look at your photos. The cut-out uses the Google ML Kit selfie segmentation model, which is built into the app and runs on the device without an internet connection.

The app only reads the photos you pick (the photo to edit and, if you choose one, a background photo). It only saves a picture when you tap Save, into the Pictures / Background Remover folder on your phone. When you tap Share, the picture goes to the app you choose. Saved pictures do not carry the location or camera details of the original.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, including an optional rewarded video that unlocks HD export for a day. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general usage and diagnostic data to show ads, to limit how often you see them and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time from the menu (Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so ads stay off and HD export stays unlocked after a reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
