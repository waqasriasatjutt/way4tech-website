DealDesk. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

Summary

DealDesk works entirely on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no DealDesk server. Way4Tech never receives your catalogue, your customers, your messages or anything else you type into the app. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

Your business profile (name, logo, phone number, currency, country code, business type), your items (titles, prices, status, notes, stock and property details, photos), your customers and leads (names, phone numbers, stage, interest, budget, requirements, groups, notes and reminder times), your deals, visits and appointments, the automatic timeline of shares, chats and stage changes, your message templates, your saved lists and, if you turn it on, the Inbox described below are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app; use the app's own backup if you want a copy.

Photos

The app only uses the photos you choose in the system photo picker or take with the camera from inside the app. A resized copy of each one is kept in the app's private storage so your catalogue keeps working if you delete the original. The app does not ask for permission to read your photo library.

Contacts

The app does not read your contact list. When you tap "Pick from contacts", Android's own contact picker opens and only the one contact you choose (its name and phone number) is handed to the app.

Shared text

When you share a chat message into DealDesk, the text is shown to you and, if you save it, stored as a note on a customer. Phone numbers in the text are detected on the phone.

Microphone (voice notes)

The microphone permission is asked only when you tap the microphone for a voice note, after an explanation. While you speak, the phone's own speech recognition service turns your words into text; depending on the phone and the language, that service may work offline or may use its provider's online service under that provider's terms. DealDesk does not record or keep any audio. It keeps only the text you choose to save.

Inbox from business chat notifications (optional, off by default)

The Inbox is off until you turn it on in Settings, agree to an explanation shown in the app, and grant DealDesk notification access in Android's settings. While it is on, DealDesk reads only the notifications of new messages posted by the business chat app and the regular chat app of the same messaging service, and only from one-to-one chats (group chats are skipped). From each such notification it stores the sender's name or phone number, a short preview of the message text (at most 200 characters), the time and which of the two apps posted it. Notifications of all other apps are ignored. DealDesk never replies to, dismisses or changes a notification. This data stays in the app's private storage on your phone, is never uploaded or shared, and is included only in the full backup file you choose to make. You can delete any Inbox entry, block a sender, turn the Inbox off in Settings and remove the notification access in Android's settings at any time. If you turn on "Save new senders as leads automatically", new senders are saved as customers on your phone with the message in their timeline.

App lock

If you set a PIN, only a salted hash of it is stored on the phone. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; DealDesk never receives your biometric data.

Home-screen widget

The optional widget shows the number of follow-ups due and your next visit on your home screen. It reads this from the app's storage on the phone.

Sending messages and files

When you tap Message, Send to chat app or Share, the app hands the text, picture cards or PDF to the chat app or other app you choose, or opens a click-to-chat link with the number and text. Nothing is sent automatically: you send it yourself in that app, and what that app does is covered by its own privacy policy. Picture cards and PDFs are created in the app's temporary storage for that share.

Notifications and reminders

The app asks for notification permission to show follow-up reminders, visit reminders and low-stock alerts that you set. Reminders are scheduled on the phone.

Backup and export

CSV exports and full backups are written only to the file and place you pick with the system file picker (phone storage, memory card or a cloud drive app). Restoring and CSV import read only the file you pick.

Purchases

The app includes Google Play Billing for a possible future optional upgrade. Nothing is for sale in this version. If a purchase is offered later, payment is handled by Google Play; Way4Tech does not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
