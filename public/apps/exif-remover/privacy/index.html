Photo Metadata Remover: EXIF & GPS. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

Your photos

The app reads the photos you choose or share to it, shows the metadata inside them (for example GPS location, date and time, phone make and model, camera settings) and makes clean copies without that metadata. All of this runs on your phone. We do not upload, store or look at your photos or their metadata, and the app has no server.

To read and clean a photo, the app keeps a temporary copy in its private cache on your phone. Android removes the cache when space is needed or when you clear the app's data. Your original photos are never changed.

The app only saves a photo when you tap Save clean, into the Pictures / Clean Photos folder on your phone. When you tap Share clean, the clean copies go to the app you choose. When you tap Open in maps, the photo's coordinates are handed to the maps app you pick on your phone; that app's own privacy policy then applies.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general usage and diagnostic data to show ads, to limit how often you see them and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time from the menu (Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Ads never receive your photos or their metadata.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so ads stay off after a reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
