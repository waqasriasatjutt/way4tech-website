Field Sales & CRM. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

What the app does

Field Sales & CRM is a mobile front end for your company's own business server. It shows and edits customers, products, quotations, sales orders, opportunities and route visits that already live on that server. Way4Tech does not run a service behind the app: the app talks only to the server address you type in (or to our demo server if you tap "Try the demo"). Way4Tech never receives your business data, your login or your password.

Login details

The server address, database name and login are saved in the app's private storage so you do not have to type them again. Your password is saved only if you tick "Remember me", and then it is encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore of your phone. The session cookie your server sends after login is also kept in the app's private storage. "Sign out" ends the session on the server and deletes the saved password. If the server address starts with http:// instead of https://, the app warns you that the connection is not encrypted.

Business data

Customer and product details are read from your server and a copy (names, phone numbers, email addresses, street, city, country, GPS position, product names, codes, prices and stock) is kept in a database inside the app's private storage so you can look them up without signal. Orders, notes, activities, visits and payments you create are sent to your server when you save them; the app does not create anything offline. Quotation PDFs are downloaded from your server into the app's cache only when you tap Share PDF, and then go to the app you choose. Signing in as a different user or server clears the offline copy. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Camera

The camera is used only when you tap the scan button, to read product barcodes. Camera frames are analysed on your phone by a barcode reader built into the app (Google ML Kit with a bundled model, no download, no network use) and are dropped straight after. The app never saves or uploads pictures.

Location

The app asks for your location only when you tap "Use my location" on a customer form or "Check in" at a route stop. The position is then sent to your own server, stored on the customer or on the visit. The app does not track you in the background and does not send your location anywhere else.

Calls, messages, email and maps

The Call, Message, Email and Navigate buttons hand the phone number, email address or address to the app you choose (phone, messages, email or maps). What that app does is covered by its own privacy policy.

No ads, no tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking libraries, and it does not use the advertising ID.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
