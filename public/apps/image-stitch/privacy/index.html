Image Stitch: Join Photos. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

Your photos

All photo work (reading the photos you pick or share to the app, joining them, cutting repeated bars, finding the overlap between screenshots and creating the final JPG or PNG) runs on your phone. We do not upload, store or look at your photos, and the app has no account or server of its own.

The app only reads the photos you choose in the system photo picker or share to it. It saves an image only when you tap Save, into the Pictures / Image Stitch folder on your phone. When you tap Share, the joined image goes to the app you choose. A temporary copy of the last joined image is kept in the app's private cache so it can be shared, and Android may clear it at any time.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general usage and diagnostic data to show ads, to limit how often you see them and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time from the menu (Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Rewarded videos

To join more than 6 images you can watch a short rewarded video from Google AdMob, which unlocks the feature for that day. The app stores only the date of the unlock on your phone.

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. It also unlocks joining more than 6 images. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so ads stay off after a reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
