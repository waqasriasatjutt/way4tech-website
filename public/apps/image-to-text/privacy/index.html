Image to Text: OCR Scanner. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

Your photos and text

Text recognition runs on your phone. The app uses Google ML Kit text recognition models that are built into the app and work on the device, also without an internet connection. We do not upload, store or look at your photos or the text found in them.

Photos you pick, share to the app or take with the camera are only read to find text. A camera photo is kept in the app's temporary cache folder and deleted by the app later (Android may also clear it). While you look at a result, the app keeps a copy of each page in its cache so you can see where text was found; these copies are replaced by your next scan.

History: the text of your last 30 scans and a small thumbnail of the first page are stored in the app's private storage on your phone, so you can open them again. Nothing in history leaves your phone. You can delete one scan or clear the whole history in the app at any time, and uninstalling the app removes it.

The app only writes a file to your phone's shared storage when you tap Save .txt or Save PDF (into Documents / Image to Text, or Download / Image to Text on phones that do not allow the Documents folder). When you tap Share, the text goes to the app you choose. When you tap Search the web, the selected words are sent to the search app or browser you use, under that app's own privacy policy. Copy all puts the text on your phone's clipboard.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general usage and diagnostic data to show ads, to limit how often you see them and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time from the menu (Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so ads stay off after a reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
