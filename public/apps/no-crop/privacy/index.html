No Crop: Square Fit Photo. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

Your photos

All photo work (fitting the photo into a frame, the blurred, colour, gradient, mirror and pattern fills, the border, corners and shadow) runs on your phone. We do not upload, store or look at your photos. The app only reads the photos you pick in the system photo picker or send to it with Share.

The app only saves a picture when you tap Save, into the Pictures / No Crop folder on your phone. When you tap Share, the picture goes to the app you choose. Pictures waiting to be shared are kept in the app's private cache and replaced the next time you save or share.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional rewarded videos that unlock HD saving or larger batches for a day. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general usage and diagnostic data to show ads, to limit how often you see them and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time from the menu (Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so ads stay off after a reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
