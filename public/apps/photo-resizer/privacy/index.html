Photo Resizer: KB & Passport. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

Your photos

All photo work (resizing, compressing, face framing and background change) runs on your phone. We do not upload, store or look at your photos. Face detection and background removal use Google ML Kit through Google Play services, which also run on the device.

The app only saves a photo when you tap Save, into the Pictures / Photo Resizer folder on your phone. When you tap Share, the photo goes to the app you choose.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general usage and diagnostic data to show ads, to limit how often you see them and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time from the menu (Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so ads stay off after a reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
