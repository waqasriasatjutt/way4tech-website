QR & Barcode Scanner. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

Camera

The app asks for the camera only to read QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are analysed on your phone by a code reader built into the app (Google ML Kit with a bundled model, no download and no network use) and are dropped straight after. The app never saves, uploads or shares camera pictures or video.

Scan from image and logos

When you pick a picture to scan, or a logo to put in a QR code you create, the app reads it on your phone only. The picture is not uploaded or kept.

History

The text of the codes you scan or create is saved in a history file inside the app's private storage on your phone, so you can open a result again. It is never sent anywhere. You can delete single entries, clear the history, or switch history off in Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

When you act on a result

The app does not open links, call numbers or join Wi-Fi networks by itself. Only when you tap a button (for example Open, Call, Add to contacts, Connect or Search the web) does it hand the value to the app you choose, such as your browser, phone, contacts, calendar, maps or the system Wi-Fi dialog. What that app does is covered by its own privacy policy.

Saved and shared QR codes

A QR code you create is saved only when you tap Save, into the Pictures / QR Codes folder on your phone. When you tap Share, the image goes to the app you choose.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (never over the camera). AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general usage and diagnostic data to show ads, to limit how often you see them and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time from Settings (Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. The content of your scans is never shared with AdMob.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so ads stay off after a reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
