Signature Maker: Sign Photos. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

Your signatures never leave your phone

Everything the app does with your signature happens on your phone: drawing it, writing your name in a handwriting font, lifting a signature from a photo of paper, and placing it on a photo of a document. We do not upload, store, receive or look at your signatures, your photos or your documents. The app has no account and no server of its own.

Saved signatures (up to 10) are kept as image files in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read them, and they are excluded from Android cloud backup, so they are not copied to any online backup by this app. Uninstalling the app or deleting a signature in the app removes them.

The app only writes to your gallery when you tap Save: transparent signature images and signed photos go to the Pictures / Signatures folder on your phone. When you tap Share, the image goes to the app you choose.

When you take a photo of your signature on paper, the app asks your camera app for one picture and keeps it only in its temporary cache while you work. The app does not request the camera permission itself.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general usage and diagnostic data to show ads, to limit how often you see them and to prevent fraud. AdMob does not receive your signatures or photos. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time from the menu (Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so ads stay off after a reinstall.

Legal use of signatures

The app makes signature images. It is not a certified electronic signature service and does not verify identity. Only sign documents you are allowed to sign.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
