Staff Attendance & Leave. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

Who receives your data

The app connects only to the company server address you type in (or the Way4Tech demo server when you tap "Try the demo"). Everything the app shows or sends, such as your check-ins, leave requests, profile and payslips, is read from and written to that server, under the user rights your company gave you. Your employer runs that server and is responsible for the data on it. Way4Tech does not receive, store or see any of it. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking libraries in the app.

Sign-in details

The server address, database name and login are saved on your phone so you do not have to type them again. Your password is saved only when you tick "Remember me", and then it is encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore of your phone. The session cookie from your server is kept on the phone until you sign out. Signing out deletes the saved password and the session.

Location

Location is optional. The app asks for it only when you tap Check in, after explaining why. When you allow it, the app reads your position once at the moment you tap Check in or Check out and sends it to your company server together with that attendance record. The app never reads your location in the background and does not keep it on the phone. If you do not allow it, you can still check in and out; your company may require it.

Notifications

When you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and shows one local notification a day at the time you chose. Nothing is sent anywhere for this.

Payslips

Payslip pages open inside the app from your company server. When you download a payslip, the PDF is saved on your phone in Downloads / Staff Attendance and opened with the PDF app you choose.

App reviews

After a few uses the app may ask Google Play to show its review prompt. Google handles that prompt; the app does not learn whether or what you rated.

Security

For servers on https, all traffic is encrypted. Some company servers use plain http; the sign-in screen warns you when the address is not secure, because the password then travels unencrypted.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time from the Profile screen, turn off the reminder, and withdraw the location or notification permission in your phone settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone. For data on your company server, contact your employer.

Children

The app is a workplace tool, not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
