Stock Scanner: Warehouse. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

What the app does

Stock Scanner connects to an inventory server that you or your company already run, so you can look up products, count stock and process receipts, deliveries and internal transfers with a barcode scanner. The app has no account of its own, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking code.

Your server and your business data

Everything you see and change in the app (products, prices, stock quantities, locations, transfers, counts) is read from and written to the server address you enter on the login screen, and nowhere else. That data is handled under the privacy policy of whoever runs that server, usually your employer. Way4Tech does not receive, store or see it.

If you tap "Try the demo", the app connects to a demonstration server run by Way4Tech (apps-demo.way4tech.com) that holds sample data only. Do not enter real business information there.

What is stored on your phone

  • The server address, database name and login you used, so you do not have to type them again.
  • The session cookie the server gives you after login, so you stay signed in.
  • Your password, only if you tick "Remember me". It is encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore of your phone and is used only to sign in again when your session ends. Signing out deletes it.
  • Scanner settings (beep, vibration, keyboard scanner input, broadcast action).

All of this stays in the app's private storage on your phone. It is excluded from cloud backups and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Camera

The app asks for the camera only to read barcodes. Camera frames are analysed on your phone by a barcode reader built into the app (Google ML Kit with a bundled model, no download and no network use) and are dropped straight after. The app never saves, uploads or shares camera pictures or video. You can use the app without the camera by typing codes or using a hardware scanner.

Hardware scanners

When keyboard scanner input is on, key presses from a physical keyboard or scanner are read as barcodes while the app is open. When a built-in scanner sends a scan broadcast, the app reads the code from it while the app is open. These codes are used only to look up the item on your server.

Network security

The app talks to your server over the address you enter. With an https:// address the connection is encrypted. Some company servers only offer http://; the app allows that but shows a "not secure" warning, because the password and data are then sent unencrypted over the network.

Sharing

The app does not share any data with Way4Tech or third parties. There are no advertising IDs, no analytics and no crash reporting services in the app.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.
