Text on Photo: Poster Maker. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

Your photos and designs

All design work (adding text, fonts, colours, stickers, templates and backgrounds) runs on your phone. We do not upload, store or look at your photos, your texts or your designs. The app has no account and no cloud storage. The fonts and templates are built into the app, so nothing is downloaded while you design.

The app only reads a photo you choose with the system photo picker, or one you share to the app. It only saves an image when you tap Save, into the Pictures / Text on Photo folder on your phone. When you tap Share, the image goes to the app you choose. On Android 9 and older the app asks for storage permission only to save that image.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general usage and diagnostic data to show ads, to limit how often you see them and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time from the menu (Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. An optional rewarded video unlocks full-size export for the day.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so ads stay off after a reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
