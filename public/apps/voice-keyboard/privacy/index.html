Voice Keyboard & Translator. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

What you type with the keys

Voice Keyboard is an Android keyboard. Every key you press goes straight into the text box you are typing in. The keyboard does not store, log or send what you type with the keys, to us or to anyone else. It does not read your clipboard. Voice input is switched off in password fields.

Android shows a standard notice when you turn on any keyboard, saying it may be able to collect the text you type. Voice Keyboard does not do this.

Your voice

When you tap the microphone, your speech is handled by your phone's own speech recognition service (the one set in your phone's settings). On Android 13 and newer the keyboard uses the on-device recogniser when it has your language installed. Otherwise, and for some languages, the speech recognition service may send the audio to its provider's online service to turn it into text. That processing is covered by the privacy policy of the speech service on your phone. We never receive, store or listen to your audio, and the keyboard does not record audio itself.

The microphone permission is only used while you are speaking, after you tap the mic.

Translation

Translation (Urdu, Hindi, Arabic or Punjabi to English) runs on your phone with Google ML Kit. The first time you use a language, its language pack (about 30 MB) is downloaded from Google's servers, on Wi-Fi only unless you change that setting. After that, translation works offline and the text you speak is not sent anywhere to be translated.

What the app stores on your phone

Only settings (chosen mode, switches), the number of voice inserts used today, and whether you own Voice Keyboard Pro. The text of your last voice insert is kept in memory only, so Undo can take it back, and is gone when the keyboard closes. Nothing typed or spoken is saved to a file.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob inside the Voice Keyboard app screen only. The keyboard itself never shows ads. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general usage and diagnostic data to show ads, to limit how often you see them and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time from the menu (Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchase

"Voice Keyboard Pro" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so Pro stays on after a reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
