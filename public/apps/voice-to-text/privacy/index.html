Voice to Text: Audio to Text. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026. Publisher: Way4Tech, Lahore, Pakistan. Contact: waqasriasatjutt@gmail.com

Your audio and text

Speech recognition runs entirely on your phone. The app uses the open-source whisper.cpp speech engine, which is built into the app, together with a speech model file stored on your phone. Your audio is never uploaded: not to us and not to anyone else. We do not receive, store or look at your audio or the text made from it.

Audio you share to the app, pick from your files or record with the microphone is read on your phone and converted to text there. The decoded audio is kept only in the phone's memory while the app needs it (for example for "Translate to English") and is discarded when you start the next file or the app closes. The app does not save your audio or your recordings as files.

Microphone: the app asks for microphone access only when you tap Record, and it records only while the recording screen is open. The recording is turned into text on your phone and is not saved or sent anywhere.

History: the text of your last 50 transcriptions, with the file name, language and length, is stored in the app's private storage on your phone so you can open it again. Nothing in history leaves your phone. You can delete one transcription or clear the whole history in the app at any time, and uninstalling the app removes it.

The app only writes a file to your phone's shared storage when you tap Save .txt (into Documents / Voice to Text, or Download / Voice to Text on phones that do not allow the Documents folder). When you tap Share, the text goes to the app you choose. Copy puts the text on your phone's clipboard.

Speech model download

Before the first transcription the app downloads one speech model file (about 31 MB, 57 MB or 190 MB, depending on the model you choose) from the public whisper.cpp model repository on huggingface.co. This is a normal file download: the app sends no audio, text or personal information with it. Like any website, the server sees your IP address and the file being downloaded, under Hugging Face's own privacy policy. The file is checked and stored in the app's private storage; after that, transcription works without an internet connection. By default the download only runs on Wi-Fi.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general usage and diagnostic data to show ads, to limit how often you see them and to prevent fraud. AdMob does not receive your audio or your text. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time from the menu (Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Apart from the model download and the ads, the app does not use the internet.

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so ads stay off and the unlocked features stay on after a reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
