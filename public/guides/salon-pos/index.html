What this module does

This module adds a Salon screen on top of the normal Odoo Point of Sale till. The person at the till gets one new button, Salon, that opens a board of today's appointments: who is booked, with which stylist, for which service, and whether they have paid. From the same screen the till can book an appointment for later, take a walk-in on the spot, and load a finished appointment straight onto the order for payment. Everything else about the till, the product screen, payment and receipts, is the Odoo Point of Sale you already know, with one addition: a paid salon order's receipt names the stylist and the service, not just the price.

Requires WT Salon Management

This module has no appointment calendar, service catalogue or stylist records of its own. Those belong to WT Salon Management, a separate required module this one is a bridge on top of, not a replacement for.

What this isA bridge that brings the salon back office into the Odoo till. It adds no appointment system of its own; it puts WT Salon Management's appointments, services and stylists onto the POS order, the receipt and the commission.
What it requiresWT Salon Management. This module does nothing on a database that does not already have it installed and set up.
Who it is forA salon already running on WT Salon Management whose front desk wants to take money at a till.
Who should buy something elseA spa or a business with no back office already in place should buy WT Spa and Salon POS instead, which is self contained with its own bookings, rooms and therapists. The two are not versions of one product and should not be installed on the same database.
1

Before you start

Five minutes, once.

You need Odoo 19 Community with Point of Sale and WT Salon Management both installed. This module adds nothing on its own without the second one; install it first.

Good to know

Install with demo data on and WT Salon Management's own demo runs first, building a working example salon (Bloom & Blade Salon) with six stylists, twenty six customers and a service catalogue. This module's own demo data then books four of those same stylists and eight of those same customers through a week of real till sessions and paid orders, on top of that same salon, exactly what every screenshot in this guide shows. A production database normally installs without demo data, so your own till opens with nothing booked and you set it up using the steps below.

Here is that back office, so you see the whole system before you go looking for it on the till. These four screens belong to WT Salon Management, not to this module:

2

Setting up your first till

Stylists, services and rooms live in WT Salon Management.

A production database installs with no demo data, so before you open the till for the first time, WT Salon Management needs at least one stylist and one service already set up, and a room too if the salon books people into a specific chair. None of this setup happens inside POS Salon; it all happens in WT Salon Management, and this module only reads what is already there.

  • Stylists. Go to Employees and add each stylist the normal way: name, job title, working hours. A stylist is an ordinary Odoo employee.
  • Services. Go to Salon > Services > Services and add each service with its price and duration. Tick stylists on a service's Available Stylists tab to limit who the till offers for it, or leave it empty to offer everyone.
  • Rooms and stations, if you use them: go to Salon > Configuration > Rooms and Stations and add each one with a code and a capacity.
  • Salon settings: go to Salon > Configuration > Settings for the salon's time zone and the hours the booking slots are offered in.

WT Salon Management's own user guide walks through each of these screens in full; this guide only names them, so a receptionist opening the till for the first time knows where to send an owner who has not set them up yet.

3

Turning on Salon Mode for a till

One checkbox, per till.

Every till (a "config" in Odoo) needs Salon Mode turned on before the Salon button appears on it. Open Point of Sale > Configuration > Point of Sale, open your till, and find the Salon Mode setting.

Salon Mode is a checkbox. Turning it on shows the Salon button on this till's navbar and lets it load WT Salon Management's service catalogue; turning it off hides the button and keeps the catalogue out of this till entirely, so a plain retail counter never shows a Salon button by accident.

Once Salon Mode is on, Stylist Tips appears beside it. It is Point of Sale's own tip setting, not a copy: tick it and pick the tip product, and the payment screen gets a Tip button. On a salon order the tip is recorded for the stylist and printed on the receipt. It is off until you turn it on.

4

Opening the till

The same way you always have.

Open Point of Sale, find your till's card and press New Session or Continue Selling. Turning Salon Mode on adds nothing to opening a till; it only adds the Salon button described next, once the till's product screen is showing.

5

The Salon board

Everything booked today, in one screen.

Look for the Salon button in the top bar and press it. This opens the Salon board as a full screen on top of the normal product screen.

Each card shows the room or station the visit is booked in, and a card that is In Progress shows how long the service has been running, from the moment Start was pressed.

The Rooms tab shows every room, chair and station from WT Salon Management with its state right now: Available, Reserved (a booking still to come today) or In service. Open the booking in a room from its tile, or press Book here to book straight into it.

6

Booking an appointment for later

Services, a stylist, a customer.

From the Salon board, press Book. Pick one or more services; the total price and duration update as you go. Pick a stylist, or leave Any selected; each stylist's chip shows how many appointments they already have today, so it is easy to spread the load. Search for the customer by name or phone, or press + New to add someone on the spot with just a name, then press Book Appointment.

Pick a Room too, or leave Any: the chips list the rooms the chosen services may use, with a red dot on one in service and an orange dot on one reserved. When a service is sold in time slots, the time box gives way to the free times for that day, stylist and room, worked out by WT Salon Management itself.

7

A customer walks in without a booking

One service, straight to the order.

From the Salon board, press Walk-In. Pick one service, and a room if you want one, and press Select. This books the appointment as already arrived, source "Walk-in", and loads its service onto the current order at once, so the next step is straight to payment.

8

Loading a booking onto the order

One click, service and price carried across.

An appointment whose service is finished but has not been rung up yet shows an Order button, on its card, in the list, and in the detail panel. Press it and every service line lands on the order with its price, the customer already attached, ready to pay. If an order for that appointment is already open, this switches to it instead of starting a duplicate.

9

Taking payment, and tips

Nothing about payment itself is changed by this module.

However the appointment reached the order, it is now an ordinary line on the Point of Sale order. Press Payment the same way you would for any sale, pick the payment method, and validate.

If Stylist Tips is turned on for this till (step 3), the payment screen also carries a Tip button. Enter the amount before validating and the tip is recorded against the stylist who did the work, and it prints on the receipt in the next step.

10

The receipt

Names the appointment, the service and the stylist.

When the sale is tied to an appointment, the receipt prints the appointment reference, the time, the service, the stylist, the room and, when the till takes tips, the tip for that stylist, above the footer, so the customer leaves with more than a product name and a price. This is the actual printed and emailed receipt, not only the on-screen preview. A sale with no linked appointment prints exactly the normal Odoo receipt.

11

Refunding a salon sale

The chair goes back to Confirmed.

Refund a salon order the way you always refund a Point of Sale order, through the till itself. The moment the refund reaches the server, the appointment it was billed against is put back to Confirmed automatically, ready to be rebooked or rung up again.

The visit's payment follows the money: after a full refund it reads Unpaid, after refunding part of it Partly paid, with the refunded part still owed on its invoice. The invoice is not edited or cancelled.

Paid partly with a credit

A bill paid partly with loyalty points, a package session or a gift card is refunded whole. Refund the bill, then ring up again what the customer keeps.

12

Reopening an old order

The salon story stays visible.

Press Orders in the navbar to see every order in the current session. A salon-linked order carries a small badge next to the customer's name naming the service and the stylist, so a long list from a busy day is still readable at a glance, without opening each order to check.

13

Customer history, on the spot

No need to ask what they always have done.

Open the detail panel of any appointment linked to a customer and their recent history loads underneath: total visits, total spent, their preferred stylist if one is set, and their last ten appointments with what was paid.

14

Closing the till and reading the day's figures

A normal Odoo Point of Sale session.

Close the till the way you always have: Close Register from the till, or from the backend session list. Every order and payment taken through the Salon board is a normal Point of Sale order underneath, so it appears in the session's totals like any other sale.

The backend Orders list (Point of Sale > Orders > Orders) carries the same story: Salon Service and Stylist are optional columns, shown by default. Open a single order and its form shows the linked Salon Appointment, Stylist and Salon Service, read only, right after the customer.

15

Where the stylist's commission comes from

Computed the moment the sale is paid.

Paying a salon order does more than take the money. It raises a real invoice for the appointment, registers the payment through Odoo's own payment register wizard so it appears correctly matched on the invoice, and runs WT Salon Management's own commission rules for the stylist who did the work. The result appears in that module's Commission Earnings list, against the stylist and the appointment.

16

Who can actually use the Salon board

This module adds no access rights of its own.

The Salon board runs on whatever access WT Salon Management already grants: Stylist, Receptionist or Manager.

  • A cashier with only the standard Point of Sale group, and none of those three, cannot open the Salon board at all.
  • A Stylist can see and update appointments, including their own, but that group carries no create right in WT Salon Management, so a Stylist working the till alone cannot book a new appointment or take a walk-in.
  • A Receptionist or a Manager can do everything on the Salon board, including booking and walk-ins.
If a walk-in button does not work

Give that member of staff the Receptionist or Manager role in WT Salon Management, not the Stylist role alone.

17

With the Salon Loyalty add-on installed

Points, packages, memberships, gift cards and deposits, at the till.

Everything in this chapter appears only when the Salon Loyalty add-on (WT Enterprise Salon) is installed on the same database. Without it, POS Salon works exactly as the chapters above describe, and none of these buttons or receipt lines show.

Each card on the Salon board carries the customer's loyalty tier and points, their live packages, their member discount, a deposit already paid, and the commission the visit will earn the stylist. The Loyalty button in the board's header opens the day's figures: points issued and redeemed today, deposits held, gift card balance outstanding and packages sold today, all read from the add-on's own records.

The star button on a card, and Salon perks on the order screen, open the customer's panel: tier and points, packages with the sessions left, memberships, open gift cards, the deposit, what is left to pay after credits, the points this visit earns and the stylist's commission.

With the appointment on the order, a receptionist or manager can:

  • Redeem points. The discount line is added at the add-on's own rate; the points are burned when the order is paid.
  • Use a package session. Only a package that covers a service on the ticket offers the button, and one with no session left is refused.
  • Apply the member discount. The plan's percentage goes on the service lines, so the invoice, the commission and the points earned follow the lower price.
  • Redeem a gift card. Pick one of the customer's cards or type the code they hand over. An expired or cancelled card, or more than the card holds, is refused.

From a booking's panel on the board, Take deposit opens an order of its own with the deposit on it. When the visit is later loaded onto its final order, the deposit comes off the bill automatically, once. A final bill that leaves it off is refused, so nobody pays the deposit twice.

What the server checks

The till never decides what a credit is worth. When an order is validated, the server applies what the cashier chose through the add-on's own code, works every amount out again, and refuses the whole order if a line says anything else. Only the Receptionist and Manager roles can redeem, and a card made out to one customer cannot be spent on another customer's visit.

Under the salon block, the receipt lists what was taken off, the points earned on the sale and the new balance with the tier. The same figures are kept on the order, on its Salon Loyalty tab in the back office.

Good to know

A refund at the till gives back, through the add-on, the points the bill burned (and takes back the points it earned), the gift card amount, and on a full refund the package session, in proportion and once per refund. A deposit comes back by refunding the order it was taken on. If the add-on is installed after POS Salon, its demo customers are added on the next start or upgrade. A bill paid partly with loyalty points, a package session or a gift card is refunded whole; refund the bill, then ring up again what the customer keeps.

18

Reports from WT Salon Management

POS Salon keeps none of its own.

POS Salon has no reports of its own. Every figure a salon sale feeds into is read from WT Salon Management's own screens, from the backend, whether the sale that fed it happened at this till or was booked and paid from the backend directly.

  • Salon > Reports > Appointment Analysis: revenue by stylist and month, as a pivot table or a bar graph.
  • Salon > Reports > Room Usage: how many hours each room or station has actually been booked, by week.
  • Salon > Reports > Commission Earnings (the same list as Salon > Staff > Commission Earnings, step 15): every stylist's commission, draft, confirmed or paid.
  • Salon > Staff > Sales Targets: a stylist's monthly target against what they have actually sold, computed live from invoiced appointments.

The Reports menu itself is restricted to the Manager role; a Stylist or Receptionist working the till day to day does not see it.

19

Upgrading from POS Salon 1.x

What changes, what does not.

Upgrading from POS Salon 19.0.1.x: a note for your accountant. Before 19.0.2.0.0, settling a salon visit at the till recorded the sale twice. The till order was booked when the session closed, and the module also raised a separate customer invoice for the appointment and registered a payment on it in your first bank or cash journal. From 19.0.2.0.0 the till order is the only accounting document: the visit's invoice is the order's own invoice, paid by the till's payments. The upgrade does not change posted entries. To find the older double entries, look for customer invoices linked to a salon appointment that has a POS order, where the invoice is not that order's own invoice and its payment sits in a journal the till does not use. The upgrade counts them and shows the number under Settings > Technical > System Parameters as wt_pos_saloon.settled_twice_before_2_0. Reverse each with a credit note and cancel its payment; do not delete them.

What this module does not do

Said plainly, so nothing here is a surprise after you buy it.

  • No appointment calendar, service catalogue or stylist records of its own. All of that is WT Salon Management, a separate required module.
  • No online booking for customers. Every booking is made by staff, from the till.
  • No SMS or email appointment reminders.
  • No payroll. Commission is calculated and shown in WT Salon Management's own reports; it is not paid out by this module.
  • No stylist working hours calendar. The module will not refuse to book someone outside their normal hours.
  • No double-booking guard of its own. WT Salon Management refuses to book a stylist twice at the same time, and that applies at the till too.

Common questions

Do I need WT Salon Management as well?
Yes. This module has no service catalogue, stylist records or appointment calendar of its own; it puts WT Salon Management's appointments inside the Point of Sale till. Install both.
Does this replace the normal Odoo Point of Sale screen?
No. The Salon board is opened by one navbar button and closes back to the ordinary product screen. Payment and receipts underneath are the Odoo Point of Sale you already have, with the stylist and service added to the receipt.
Why can't one of my stylists take a walk-in from the till?
They likely hold only the Stylist role in WT Salon Management, which can see and update appointments but cannot create a new one. Give them the Receptionist or Manager role instead (step 16).
What happens to the appointment if I refund the sale?
It is put back to Confirmed automatically, the moment the refund syncs from the till, ready to be rebooked or rung up again.
Can I still sell a plain retail product with no appointment attached?
Yes. A salon appointment is optional on every order; a sale with nothing booked works exactly as core Point of Sale always has, receipt included.
What happens if there is no cash or bank journal configured for the company?
The appointment still reaches the Invoiced stage, because the sale at the till already happened. It will not read as Paid on the appointment, since no payment was actually registered; fixing the journal and paying the invoice from the backend settles that afterwards.
Do I need the Salon Loyalty add-on?
No. POS Salon works on its own. With the Salon Loyalty add-on (WT Enterprise Salon) installed as well, the till also shows each customer's points, packages, memberships and gift cards, redeems them on the order, takes booking deposits and prints points earned on the receipt.
