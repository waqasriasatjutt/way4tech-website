The Odoo Community back office works, but it looks like the free version: an apps dropdown instead of the Enterprise home screen, and a dark purple top bar. For a team that sits in Odoo all day, or a client who has seen Enterprise in a demo and expects that, the look matters more than it should. These two modules change it. They contain no Python, add no models and change no data; they are stylesheets and web client components. The website themes are in the [rest of this series](/odoo-guides/odoo-website-themes/).

## Enterprise Backend Theme Lite

Install `wt_enterprise_theme` from Apps and reload the browser. There are no settings. It needs only the `web` module, so it runs on any Odoo 19 Community database, and it declares that it cannot be installed where Odoo Enterprise's `web_enterprise` is present, which already looks like this.

What changes:

- **The home menu.** After sign in you land on a full page grid of your apps on a soft gradient, instead of the last app you used. The nine dot icon at the top left of every screen, or Alt+H, brings the grid back.
- **Keyboard use.** On a computer, arrow keys, Tab and Shift Tab move between apps, Enter opens one and Escape returns to the app you came from.
- **Your own app order.** Press an icon for half a second and drag it. The order is saved to the user's Odoo settings, so each person gets their most used apps first on any device.
- **The Enterprise look.** A white top bar, the purple `#714B67` brand colour and the teal `#017e84` action colour, the Enterprise grey scale, and the same gradient on the login page.
- **Phones.** The menu button on a phone closes the grid first, then opens the current app's menu.

## Backend Theme with Color Picker

Install `wt_custom_backend_theme` from Apps. If Enterprise Backend Theme Lite is installed, uninstall it first: the two are declared as mutually exclusive. You get the same home menu, keyboard use and drag to reorder as above, plus a theme panel.

### The theme panel

A paint brush button in the top bar opens **Customize Theme** from the right. Click outside it or on the cross to close it. Everything applies as you click, without a reload.

1. **Color Presets.** Seven: Enterprise (the purple and teal default), Dark, Midnight, Navy, Ocean, Rose and Amber. Dark and Midnight are dark schemes, the rest light. Navy, Ocean and Amber colour the top bar and sidebar too; Enterprise and Rose keep them white.
2. **Dark Mode.** A switch that flips backgrounds, text and borders to a dark set while keeping your brand and action colours, so a custom brand colour survives.
3. **Color Palette.** 22 colour pickers in six groups: Brand and Action (brand, action, and the text colour on each), Backgrounds (page, app, cards and forms, inputs, hover, home menu), Text (primary, muted, light), Borders (border, input border), Navbar (background, text) and Sidebar (background, text, muted text, active background, active text). Touch any of them and the preset becomes Custom.
4. **Layout.** Top Navbar keeps the normal Odoo layout. Side Menu moves navigation into a fixed left sidebar with the current app's menus and the top bar icons. Collapsed sidebar shrinks it to icons. On a phone the sidebar becomes a drawer opened from the menu button.

<Callout title="The choices live in the browser">The preset, custom colours, dark mode, layout and collapsed state are stored in the browser's local storage, not in Odoo. Each person chooses their own look, and it follows the browser rather than the user: another laptop or a cleared browser starts again on the Enterprise preset. There is no company wide setting to give everyone the same scheme. Only the app order on the home menu is saved in Odoo, per user.</Callout>

### One oddity in the website theme chooser

Because its category sits under Theme, the Color Picker module shows up as a card in Odoo's website theme chooser, next to the website themes. It is not a website theme. Install it from Apps and leave that card alone.

## Installing and removing safely

Both modules only change assets loaded by the browser, so installing one is low risk: install it, reload, and every user sees the new look on their next page load. To remove one, uninstall it from Apps and reload; Odoo goes back to the standard Community navbar and apps menu. The app order saved by the home menu stays in each user's settings and is simply unused. Try either on a copy of your database first if you have other modules that restyle the navbar.

## Which one to pick

Take **Enterprise Backend Theme Lite** if you simply want Community to look and open like Enterprise, the same for everyone, with nothing to configure. Take **Backend Theme with Color Picker** if people want their own colours, a dark scheme for long days, or a side menu on a wide screen, and you accept that the choice is per browser. Neither adds Enterprise features such as Studio or the Enterprise apps; they change the look and navigation only.
