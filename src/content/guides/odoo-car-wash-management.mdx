A car wash makes its money in short, repeated visits. A basic wash takes thirty minutes, a ceramic coating takes a full day, and both have to fit around the same team of washers. What goes wrong is rarely the wash itself. It is the booking taken on the phone and forgotten, the detailer double booked at eleven, the upsell added on the forecourt but never charged, the washer who did the work and is not paid for it. This series shows how a car wash or detailing shop runs those parts on Odoo 19 Community with the Car Wash Management module, and what the module does not do. The screenshots come from the live demo of a fictional detailing business, Sparkle Auto Spa, which bills in AED.

## What a car wash actually needs from its system

Under the menu boards and loyalty cards, a car wash runs on five things:

1. **A service menu with a price and a time on every line.** The time matters as much as the price, because it decides how many cars a technician can take in a day.
2. **One booking record per visit.** Customer, services, technician, start time and stage, whether the booking came from a walk-in, a phone call, the website or the counter.
3. **A board that shows the day.** Who is waiting, who is being washed, which cars are done and not yet paid.
4. **Billing off the finished job.** The invoice and the receipt should come from the booking, so an extra service added at the bay ends up on the bill.
5. **Pay for the people who do the work.** Most car washes pay part of the wage as commission per service, and that needs to come from the same records as the sales.

## What standard Odoo does and does not do

Odoo 19 Community has the pieces around a car wash: Sales for quotations and orders, Invoicing, Point of Sale for the counter, Employees for staff and Website for a public site. What it does not have is the visit itself. There is no record that holds the services, the technician and the time slot together, no stage board for the day, and nothing that works out a washer's commission per service from what was billed. You can sell a wash as a product in Point of Sale, but you lose the booking, the technician and the time.

That missing record is what the Car Wash Management module adds. It sits on top of the standard apps rather than replacing them: services are ordinary Odoo service products, bookings create ordinary sales orders, and invoices are ordinary customer invoices.

## The modules and what each covers

[Car Wash Management](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_carwash_management/) is the base app at USD 1,299. It holds the service catalogue, bookings, the website booking pages, invoicing, the A4 and 80 mm prints, commission rules and sales targets. [POS Car Wash](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_car_wash_pos/) is a USD 1,299 add-on that puts the day's bookings inside the Point of Sale screen, so the cashier can book a walk-in or load a booking onto the order and take payment. Both are for Odoo 19 Community.

## The service menu

Every wash, add-on and package is a service in **Services, Services**. A service has a name, a category, a duration in hours, a price and a flag for whether it shows on the website. Behind each one sits a normal Odoo service product, which is what lands on the sales order and the invoice, so the price you see on the service is the product's sales price.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-car-wash-management/service-catalogue.webp" alt="Car wash service list in Odoo with exterior wash, interior cleaning, detailing, premium packages and ceramic coating services, each with duration, price in AED, an on portal tick and a booking count" width={1440} height={900} caption="The demo's service menu: duration, price and how often each service has been booked." />

A service can also be limited to certain technicians, so a ceramic coating is only offered with the people trained for it. If you consume stock on a service, such as shampoo, wax or microfibre cloths, you can link it to a bill of materials. When a booking is marked done, the module creates stock moves for those materials out of the main warehouse. The move is only completed if the stock is there to reserve, so a shortage leaves the move waiting rather than taking stock below zero.

Categories group the menu on the website and in reports. The demo uses Exterior Wash, Interior Cleaning, Full Service, Detailing, Premium Packages, Ceramic Coating and Other Services, with an SUV surcharge as its own zero minute service.

## How a booking moves through the day

A booking has a customer, a lead technician, a start time and one or more service lines. Each line carries its own technician, quantity, price, discount and taxes, and the booking's duration and end time are worked out from the lines. The source field records where it came from: walk-in, phone, online portal, staff booking, or POS with the add-on.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-car-wash-management/booking-form.webp" alt="Car wash booking form in Odoo in progress, with customer, lead technician, appointment date, end time and a six hour duration, service lines for a one year ceramic coating and an SUV surcharge, and totals with tax in AED" width={1440} height={900} caption="A booking in progress: two service lines, one technician, the end time worked out from the durations." />

The stages are New, Confirmed, In Progress, Done, Invoiced and Cancelled, and the buttons on the form move a booking along them:

- **Confirm** moves it to Confirmed and creates a draft sales order from the service lines.
- **Start Service** moves it to In Progress.
- **Mark Done** moves it to Done, confirms the sales order and consumes any bill of materials.
- **Invoice** creates a customer invoice from the lines in one step and moves the booking to Invoiced. **Invoice (Custom)** opens a wizard first, so you can change prices or discounts before the invoice is made. If the sales order is already confirmed, the wizard invoices through it.

Creating the invoice is also the moment commission lines are worked out, which the [commission chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-car-wash-staff-commission/) covers.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-car-wash-management/booking-kanban.webp" alt="Car wash kanban board in Odoo with columns for New, Confirmed, In Progress and Done, each card showing the booking reference, date and time, technician badges and the amount in AED, with Invoiced and Cancelled folded" width={1440} height={900} caption="The kanban board: the day by stage, with Invoiced and Cancelled folded away." />

Alongside the kanban there is a list of all bookings, a calendar and a Today view that filters to the current day in the user's own time zone. The calendar colours bookings by technician, which makes a double booked washer easy to spot.

## Receipts and the paper trail

The **Print Receipt** button prints an 80 mm thermal receipt from the booking: company header, reference, date, customer, technician, the service lines with tax and total, and the invoice number and paid status once there is one. An A4 booking print is also in the Print menu.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-car-wash-management/thermal-receipt.webp" alt="80 mm car wash receipt from Odoo showing the booking reference, date, customer, technician, a ceramic coating service with subtotal, tax and total in AED, and the invoice number with paid status" width={1260} height={510} caption="The 80 mm receipt, printed straight from the booking." />

The customer record gets a count of bookings, the date of the last finished visit, a preferred technician and a free text note for the car and any instructions.

## How to set it up in Odoo

1. Install Car Wash Management. Sales, Invoicing, Inventory, Manufacturing, Employees, Portal and Website install with it, and nine starter services and seven categories are created.
2. Assign the access groups in Settings, Users: **Technician** sees only their own bookings, targets and commission, **Receptionist** can create and edit bookings and services, **Manager** sees everything including commission rules, targets and configuration.
3. Add your technicians as employees and check they show under **Staff, Technicians**.
4. Replace the starter services in **Services, Services** with your own menu: price, duration, category, website flag, qualified technicians and an optional bill of materials. Set the sales tax on each service product.
5. Open **Configuration, Settings** and set the first and last booking hour and the slot length for the website. The defaults are 9:00 to 19:00 in 30 minute slots.
6. Check the stage names and their kanban labels in **Configuration, Appointment Stages**. The stage form has an email template field, but nothing in the module sends it when a booking changes stage, so do not count on it.
7. Set up outgoing mail if you want customers to get the booking confirmation email from the website.
8. Run one booking end to end: confirm, start, mark done, invoice, print the receipt. Then look at the sales order, the invoice and the commission line it produced before you take real cars.

<Callout title="What the module does not do">There are no bays in the code. Bookings are scheduled against technicians, and the only clash check, on the website form, is against the technician's other bookings, not against a bay or a lift. There is no vehicle or number plate record, no wash plan that counts visits down, no queue display screen and no licence plate camera link. Plan around those before you buy.</Callout>

## Limitations

- **No bay allocation.** The store listing mentions bays, but the module has no bay record. Scheduling is by technician and time.
- **No vehicle record.** Car details live in a note on the customer.
- **No clash check in the back office.** The website form refuses a technician who is already booked at that time, but a booking typed in the back office is not checked.
- **Bookings only invoice in one go.** A part payment or deposit is handled in Invoicing, not on the booking.
- **Commission validity dates are not applied.** Rules have Valid From and Valid To fields, but the matching only looks at the technician, the services and whether the rule is active. Archive an old rule rather than relying on its end date.
