A clinic's records usually live in three places that do not talk to each other: a paper register or a spreadsheet at the front desk, the doctor's notes, and the billing software. The receptionist does not know which doctor is running late, the doctor does not see that the patient is allergic to penicillin until they ask, and the bill is typed again from the prescription. Putting the clinic on Odoo means the visit, the clinical notes and the invoice are one record. This guide covers what a clinic, a small hospital and a dental practice need, what standard Odoo does not do, and how the Way4Tech modules cover it on Odoo 19.

## What a clinic actually needs

Whatever the size, a clinical front office needs a short list of things to work:

1. **One patient record** with a medical record number, demographics, allergies and history, that the doctor sees at every visit.
2. **A queue the front desk can run.** Appointments and walk-ins in order, a token per doctor per day, and a clear view of who is waiting, who is with the doctor and who is done.
3. **The consultation on one screen.** Vitals, symptoms, diagnosis, prescription and test requests, written while the patient is in the room.
4. **Beds and wards** for anyone who stays overnight, with a board that shows which bed is free, occupied or being cleaned.
5. **Billing without retyping.** The consultation fee, tests and room charges go to the invoice from the visit or the admission.
6. **Roles.** A receptionist should not read clinical notes the way a doctor does, and a lab technician needs the request, not the bill.

A dental practice adds its own list: a chair-time calendar per dentist, priced treatments with set durations, online booking, and the commission and targets dentists are paid on.

## What standard Odoo does and does not do

Odoo's standard apps give a clinic the business side: contacts, invoices, payments, stock and accounting. What they do not have is anything clinical. There is no patient model with an MRN, no vitals, no diagnosis code list, no prescription lines, no OPD token, no admission with a bed, and no ward and bed board. You can add fields with Studio, but the workflow (a token issued on confirm, a bed set to cleaning on discharge, a follow-up raised from the visit) needs code. That gap is what the modules below fill. The [clinic management](/solutions/clinic-management/) and [hospital management](/solutions/hospital-management/) solution pages describe the wider setup.

## The modules and what each covers

The hospital suite is three modules that stack. The dental app is separate.

- [Hospital Management](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_hospital_management/) (`wt_hospital_management`, USD 10) is the clinical core: patients, physicians, departments, appointments and OPD visits, admissions, wards, rooms and beds, services, invoicing, a front desk dashboard, three PDF reports and five roles. It needs only Invoicing, Discuss and Products. This is what runs on the [Clinic demo](https://clinic.way4tech.com/).
- [Hospital Management Enterprise](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_hospital_management_enterprise/) (`wt_hospital_management_enterprise`, USD 200) adds a laboratory and radiology system with a test catalogue, parameters, normal ranges and abnormal flags, insurance companies, patient policies and claims raised from invoices, operation theatres and surgeries with consumables, a blood bank with donors and units, ambulance trips, and analytics. It needs the core module and Inventory.
- [Hospital Management Ultimate](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_hospital_management_ultimate/) (`wt_hospital_management_ultimate`, USD 500) adds pharmacy dispensing from stock with batches, doctor commission, cashier sessions, insurance pre-authorisation, insurer tariffs, care packages and batch claim export to CSV, emergency triage, ICU charts, recurring procedures, a waiting list, vaccinations, an ICD-10 catalogue and facility assets. It needs Enterprise.
- [Dental Clinic Management](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_dental_management/) (`wt_dental_management`, USD 1,299) is a dental practice app: appointments with treatment lines, a treatment catalogue with durations and materials, a website booking page, commission rules, sales targets, invoicing and thermal receipts. It needs Sales, Inventory, Manufacturing, Employees and Website.
- [POS Dental Clinic](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_dental_clinic_pos/) (`wt_dental_clinic_pos`, USD 1,299) brings dental appointments into the Odoo Point of Sale. It needs the dental app and Point of Sale.

### The front desk dashboard

**Hospital, Front Desk, Dashboard** opens on today: appointments, bed occupancy, in-house patients and posted revenue this month. Below that, the OPD flow counts today's appointments that are confirmed, waiting (checked in), in consultation and completed. Beds are counted as available, occupied and in maintenance, and patients as registered, new this month and admitted today. Refresh reloads the figures.

### Roles

Five groups ship with the core module: Receptionist / Front Desk, Nurse, Lab / Radiology Technician, Physician / Doctor and Hospital Administrator. Every patient, physician, bed, ward, department and service carries a multi-company rule, so one database can run several branches.

## Setting up Hospital Management, in order

1. Install Hospital Management. Invoicing and Discuss install with it. A chart of accounts must exist before you invoice, because the invoice lines post to an income account.
2. Under **Hospital, Configuration**, add departments, then physicians with specialty, department, consultation fee, consultation service, working days and hours, and the Odoo user they log in as.
3. Add wards (with ward type, building and floor), rooms and beds (with bed type and daily charge).
4. Add your chargeable **Services and Charges**: consultation, procedure, room and board, nursing, laboratory, radiology, pharmacy or other, each with a price and taxes.
5. Load the **Diagnoses** you use, with ICD codes, and the **Allergies** list.
6. Assign each user one of the five roles.
7. Register a test patient, book, check in, consult and invoice one visit before you go live.

## Limitations of the core module

- **No online booking or patient portal.** Appointments are created by staff. Online booking exists only in the dental app.
- **No automatic reminders.** A Send Appointment Reminders setting is present, but nothing in this version sends reminders. The Appointment Confirmation email template is there to send by hand.
- **No clash check on doctors' time.** Two appointments can be booked for the same physician at the same time; the token is simply the next number for that doctor that day.
- **Basic laboratory.** In the core, a lab or radiology request is a line with a status and a free text result. Test catalogues, parameters and normal ranges are in Enterprise.
- **No stock movement on prescriptions.** A prescription line can name a product, but nothing is dispensed from stock until Ultimate.

If you run a US medical billing company rather than a clinic, that is a different product, [US Medical Billing and RCM](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_medical_billing/), and not covered here.
