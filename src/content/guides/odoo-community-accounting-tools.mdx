The books close every month whether the software helps or not. In Odoo 19 Community the ledger is solid: customer invoices, vendor bills, payments, taxes and journals are all proper double entry. What is missing is the work around the ledger that an accountant does every month. The van is depreciated in a spreadsheet and typed in as a journal entry. The budget lives in another spreadsheet and nobody knows about the purchase order for a trade show stand until the bill lands. The balance sheet is a pivot of journal items. The bank statement is typed in line by line. And the customer who is sixty days late gets chased when somebody remembers.

This guide covers five Way4Tech add-ons that fill those gaps, one module per chapter, shown on a demo company with a year of books in it.

## What Odoo Community does and does not do

Odoo sells its full accounting app as part of Enterprise. Community ships the Invoicing app, which already gives you:

- a chart of accounts and taxes for your country, through the localisation modules
- customer invoices, vendor bills, credit notes and payments
- journals, journal entries and analytic accounts
- bank statements, typed in by hand

It does not give you a fixed asset register, budgets, the formatted financial statements and ledgers, a way to read the bank's own file, or automatic follow-up of late payers. Those are the five jobs below.

| Job | Community on its own | With the add-on |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed assets | No register; depreciation kept in a spreadsheet | Asset types, depreciation boards, automatic posting, revaluation, disposal |
| Budgets | None | Budget against actual by department or account group, plus committed spend from purchase orders |
| Financial statements | Journal item lists only | Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow, Trial Balance, ledgers, aged balances and a tax report, with comparison, PDF and Excel |
| Bank statements | Typed in line by line | OFX, QIF, CAMT.053 and CSV import with duplicate protection |
| Late payers | Re-send the invoice by hand | Reminder rules at 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, four e-mail templates and a daily run |

The modules are independent. Each depends only on the accounting module, so a business that only needs the reports can buy just that one.

## The demo company

The demo is Halden Pike Trading Ltd, an invented wholesaler of timber, paint, fixings and garden lighting in Bristol. It runs on Odoo 19 Community with the UK chart of accounts and VAT, in sterling. Trading starts in November 2025, so there is a full year of invoices, bills, payments, payroll and VAT returns. There are thirteen fixed assets bought between 2021 and 2026, four budgets, ten bank file imports and a set of overdue customers being chased. Every name, bank account and number on it is invented. You can ask us for a login from the [demos page](/demos/).

## Dynamic Financial Reports

The reports are the reason most people come looking. Nine reports run on one engine: Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss and Cash Flow (indirect method) from an editable line structure, then Trial Balance with opening balances, General Ledger with a running balance, Partner Ledger, Aged Receivable, Aged Payable and a Tax Report. Every report takes a date range, posted or all entries, a comparison against the previous period, the same period last year or a custom range, and filters by journal, account, partner and analytic account. You view it on screen, print it as a PDF, download a real Excel workbook, or jump to the journal items behind it.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-accounting-tools/fr-pl-cmp.webp" alt="Profit and Loss for 1 January to 6 November 2026 with comparison and variance columns: income 851,309.82, cost of revenue 412,660.10, gross profit 438,649.72, operating expenses by account and depreciation, net profit 59,439.44" width={1440} height={900} caption="The Profit and Loss with a same period last year comparison and a variance column." />

The statement structures map accounts by account type, so they work with any localisation's chart. Two limits worth knowing: the aged reports place a partly paid invoice at its full amount and the part payment as a negative in its own bucket, so the bucket split is off while the totals are right, and the Tax Report is a list of tax per tax code, not a VAT return.

## Fixed Asset Management

An asset type holds the accounts, the journal, the method (straight line, declining balance, or declining then straight line), the number and length of periods and whether entries post themselves. A new asset is then three fields, or none at all: put the type on a fixed asset account and every vendor bill line posted to it raises the asset.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-accounting-tools/asset-van-board.webp" alt="Asset AST/00015 Delivery Van Ford Transit Custom, gross value 34,500, salvage 4,500, depreciated 21,854.84, book value 12,645.16, straight line over 60 months, with the depreciation board lines and their journal entries" width={1440} height={900} caption="The depreciation board: each posted line is a real journal entry, and the chatter logs every posting." />

Each board line becomes a posted journal entry, by hand or by the daily scheduled action. Assets can be revalued with an entry for the difference, paused while out of service, and sold or scrapped with the gain or loss worked out. A printable depreciation schedule covers one asset or a selection.

## Budget Management

A budget line watches an analytic account, an account group, or both, with a planned amount for the period. Actual comes from posted journal items. Committed comes from confirmed purchase orders not yet billed, because once an order is confirmed the money is promised. Available is what is left.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-accounting-tools/budget-fy.webp" alt="FY2026 Operating Budget, confirmed, with Block Over Budget ticked at 5 percent tolerance and lines for revenue, cost of goods sold, marketing in red and warehouse energy in amber, showing planned, actual, committed and available" width={1440} height={900} caption="A confirmed budget: the marketing line is over once its open orders are counted." />

Tick Block Over Budget and a purchase order that would push a line past its planned amount plus tolerance is refused at confirmation, with a message that names the line and the overspend. Only the Approve Budgets group can confirm, close or change the block. Budget lines also come as a pivot, a graph and a printable report.

## Bank Statement Import

Upload the file the bank gives you: OFX (both the older SGML flavour and OFX 2), QIF, CAMT.053 in any schema version, or CSV through a column mapping you set up once per bank. The journal is found from the account number in the file, a file in the wrong currency is refused, and counterparties are matched to partners by account number first.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-accounting-tools/bi-log-overlap.webp" alt="Import record IMP/2026/0003 for an OFX file with 6 transactions in the file, 2 imported and 4 skipped as already imported, with the opening and closing balances and the list of transactions" width={1440} height={900} caption="An overlapping 30 day download: four lines were already in Odoo, two were new." />

Every line keeps the bank's reference, or a fingerprint when the format has none, so the same file loaded twice imports nothing the second time. Each import leaves a record with what was created, what was skipped and who ran it. The module creates the statement; matching its lines to invoices is still done in Odoo.

## Payment Follow-Up

Four rules ship with the module: a friendly reminder 3 days past due, a second notice at 7, an urgent one at 14 and a final notice at 30, each with its own e-mail template. A daily run sends each overdue invoice the next rule it has reached, once, and logs it on the invoice.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-accounting-tools/inv-reminded.webp" alt="Invoice INV/2026/00039 to Fellgate Hospitality Group, partly paid, with the Late Payment Reminder group showing four reminders sent and the final notice e-mail in the chatter" width={1440} height={900} caption="An invoice after four reminders, each one in the chatter." />

An invoice can be taken out of the run, a reminder can be sent now from the invoice, and settings hold a master switch, the sender address and a minimum amount.

<Callout title="Read the e-mails before the first run">
The reminder templates go to your customers as soon as the daily run finds an overdue invoice. As shipped, the rule names and subjects use long dashes and the amount box in the body is pale text on a pale background. Reword the four templates first, and on a database with years of old unpaid invoices turn the master switch off until the ledger is tidy.
</Callout>

## Prices and how they install

| Module | Technical name | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed Asset Management | `wt_account_asset` | USD 89 |
| Budget Management | `wt_budget_control` | USD 99 |
| Dynamic Financial Reports | `wt_dynamic_financial_reports` | USD 149 |
| Bank Statement Import | `wt_bank_statement_import` | USD 79 |
| Payment Follow-Up and Dunning | `wt_late_payment_reminder` | USD 10 |

All five are for Odoo 19 Community and install from the Odoo app store. None of them replaces a localisation: VAT returns, e-invoicing and filings still come from Odoo's country modules or your accountant. The full guide in PDF has a setup table for all five, a fifteen minute demo script and a menu map.
