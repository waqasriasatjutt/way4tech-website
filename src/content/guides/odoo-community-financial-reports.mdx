At month end somebody asks for the balance sheet and the profit and loss, ideally against last year, and in Odoo 19 Community the honest answer is a pivot of journal items and an afternoon in a spreadsheet. The figures are all there, because Community keeps a full double entry ledger. What it lacks are the reports that put them in shape, which Odoo sells with Enterprise. Dynamic Financial Reports (`wt_dynamic_financial_reports`) adds nine of them on one engine. The examples come from the demo company, Halden Pike Trading Ltd, on the UK chart, for 1 January to 6 November 2026.

## One window for every report

Open **Financial Reports** and pick a report from Statements, Ledgers or Receivables and Payables. People who live in Invoicing find the same window under **Invoicing, Reporting, Dynamic Financial Reports**.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-financial-reports/fr-wiz-bs.webp" alt="Balance Sheet filter window with report, from 1 January, to 6 November, posted or all entries, comparison set to no comparison, hide zero lines, filters for journals, accounts, partners and analytic accounts, and View, Print PDF, Export Excel and Journal Items buttons" width={1440} height={900} caption="The filter window, the same for all nine reports." />

1. **Dates.** From the 1st of January to today by default.
2. **Entries.** Posted Entries, or All Entries to include drafts. Cancelled entries are always left out.
3. **Comparison.** None, previous period, same period last year, or a custom range with its own dates.
4. **Hide Zero Lines.** Leaves out accounts and partners with no activity and no balance.
5. **Filters.** Journals, accounts, partners and analytic accounts. The Partner Ledger also has Unreconciled Only.
6. **Output.** **View** shows the report in Odoo, **Print PDF** prints it with the company, period and filters in the header, **Export Excel** downloads a real .xlsx workbook, and **Journal Items** opens the items behind the filters.

The drill down is that last button. The rows of the on-screen report are not clickable.

## Profit and Loss

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-financial-reports/fr-pl-cmp.webp" alt="Profit and Loss 2026 compared with the same dates in 2025, with balance, comparison and variance columns for income, cost of revenue, gross profit, expenses by account, depreciation and net profit 59,439.44" width={1440} height={900} caption="Income, cost of revenue, gross profit, expenses, depreciation and net profit, with last year alongside." />

Income (operating and other), Cost of Revenue, **Gross Profit**, Operating Expenses (expenses then depreciation) and **Net Profit**. On the demo: income GBP 851,310, gross profit GBP 438,650, net profit GBP 59,439 after GBP 31,681 of depreciation posted by the [asset module](/odoo-guides/odoo-community-fixed-asset-depreciation/). With a comparison, each line gets a comparison column and a variance column. The demo's 2025 column is thin because trading only starts in November 2025.

## Balance Sheet

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-financial-reports/fr-bs.webp" alt="Balance Sheet with assets 784,371.07 split into bank and cash, receivables, current, fixed and non-current assets, liabilities split into payables, current and non-current, equity with capital and reserves and earnings to date, and total liabilities and equity 784,371.07" width={1440} height={900} caption="Cumulative to the To date: assets equal liabilities plus equity." />

Assets, Liabilities and Equity, each broken into the usual headings. The balance sheet is cumulative: it reads everything up to the To date, whatever the From date says. **Earnings to Date** carries the profit and loss not yet closed to equity, which is what makes the two sides agree.

## Cash Flow Statement

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-financial-reports/fr-cf.webp" alt="Cash Flow Statement with net profit, movement in receivables, other current assets and payables, cash from operations, investing and financing, net movement in cash, and the movement on each bank account" width={1440} height={900} caption="Indirect method, checked against the bank accounts underneath." />

Indirect method: net profit, then the movement in receivables, other current assets and payables gives cash from operations; fixed assets give investing; equity and long term liabilities give financing. Net movement in cash is shown against the actual movement on each bank and cash account, so you can see at once if something is unmapped.

## Trial Balance and the ledgers

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-financial-reports/fr-tb.webp" alt="Trial Balance with initial balance, debit, credit and closing balance per account, from 001000 Technology through receivables, bank, VAT control accounts, loans, share capital and sales" width={1440} height={900} caption="Initial balance, movement and closing balance for every account." />

- **Trial Balance**: one row per account with the balance before the From date, debit and credit in the period, and the closing balance.
- **General Ledger**: per account, the opening balance, then every item with date, entry, partner, label, debit, credit and a running cumulative balance.
- **Partner Ledger**: receivable and payable items by partner with the same running balance; tick Unreconciled Only to see what is still open.

Each takes the window's filters, so one account or one journal is a single selection away.

## Aged balances and the tax report

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-financial-reports/fr-ar.webp" alt="Aged Receivable by customer with columns not due, 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90, 91 to 120, older and total, totalling 256,800.60" width={1440} height={900} caption="Who owes what, by days past the due date." />

**Aged Receivable** and **Aged Payable** put each partner's open items into Not Due, 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90, 91 to 120 and Older. The **Tax Report** lists each tax with its base and its tax, sales taxes first, then purchase taxes, for the dates you choose.

<Callout title="Known limits">
The aged reports place each open journal item by its own due date at its full amount. A partly paid invoice shows at its original amount and the part payment shows as a negative figure in the payment's bucket, so each customer's total is right but the bucket split is not. The Tax Report is a list per tax code, not a filed VAT return. In the on-screen report the column titles are dark text on a dark blue band and hard to read; the PDF and the Excel export are not affected.
</Callout>

## Change a structure without code

The three statements are trees of lines under **Financial Reports, Configuration**.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-financial-reports/fr-struct-bs.webp" alt="Balance Sheet report structure with code BS and the top level lines ASSETS, LIABILITIES and EQUITY as sums of child lines and TOTAL LIABILITIES AND EQUITY as the formula LIAB+EQ" width={1440} height={900} caption="Top level lines; open a line to edit its children." />

Each line takes its figure from one of five sources:

| Source | The line adds up |
|---|---|
| Sum of child lines | Its children |
| Accounts of given types | Accounts with those Odoo account types |
| Accounts whose code starts with | Code prefixes such as 40,41 |
| A hand-picked list of accounts | Exactly those accounts |
| Formula over other line codes | Codes joined by + and -, for example REV-COGS |

A line can reverse its sign so income and liabilities read as positive, hide itself when it is zero, print in bold as a total, and expand to list every account under it. The six ledger style reports have a fixed shape and nothing to edit.

## Setting it up

Install the module, run the Balance Sheet for today and check the movement on the bank accounts in the Cash Flow agrees with net movement in cash. If an account lands in the wrong place, its account type is usually the reason; fix the type or add a code prefix line. The module costs USD 149 on Odoo 19 Community. For a quick visual summary instead of formal statements, see the [accounting dashboard guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-accounting-dashboard/); the [series overview](/odoo-guides/odoo-community-accounting-tools/) covers the other four add-ons.
