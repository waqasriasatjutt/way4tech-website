Somebody moves to Odoo Community and the first month goes well. Then the project manager asks where the Gantt chart is, sales wants to see customers on a map before planning a day of visits, the team would rather type hours into a weekly grid than open a form per line, finance wants a folder nobody else can open, the director wants to sign off any quotation over a set amount before it goes out, and after a price changes by mistake nobody can say who changed it. Odoo Enterprise answers most of those with built-in views and apps. Community answers none of them. This guide covers eight Way4Tech modules for Odoo 19 Community that fill those gaps, what each one does and does not do, and how they look on one live demo.

## What standard Odoo Community leaves out

Odoo's view types in Community are list, form, kanban, calendar, pivot, graph and activity. The Gantt view, the map view and the grid view that Project, Field Service, Planning and Timesheets use in Enterprise are Enterprise only, and so is the Documents app with its folders. Community does have pieces you can build on:

- **Project** with stages, deadlines and, if you switch it on in settings, task dependencies. It does not move a task's dates when the one before it slips.
- **Timesheets** with one line per entry, in a list. There is no week-by-person grid to type into.
- **Attachments** on every record, with no folders, no permissions of their own and no versions.
- **Chatter tracking** on the fields a developer marked as tracked, which shows a change on the record but gives no log you can search across the database.
- **Contacts with coordinates** through base_geolocalize, with nothing to draw them on.

Each module below picks up one of those, and each was written as a separate product rather than a copy of the Enterprise feature.

## Planning with dependencies

A project plan is a chain: the wireframes wait for the sitemap, the build waits for design sign-off, the launch cannot move past a fixed date. Gantt View keeps the chain as links between tasks, with lag in working days, and when one task slips every task that depends on it moves in one pass. A critical path run gives each task its float, so you know which delays cost you the finish date and which do not. Resources carry a capacity, and the module lists every window where someone is booked past it.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-community-productivity-apps/gantt-board.webp" alt="Planning board at week scale with one row per person and resource, bars coloured by client project, progress shading inside each bar and red outlines on tasks on the critical path" width={1440} height={900} caption="The board at week scale: one row per person, bars coloured by project." />

The board is a configuration, so it can draw any model with two date fields: the demo has a second board on projects and a third on confirmed orders and their promised dates.

## Maps and routes

Map View puts any model on a map, positioned by a linked contact's address or by latitude and longitude fields, and coloured by any field with a legend. Addresses are geocoded once and every answer is cached, misses included, so a contact is only looked up again when its address changes. Route planning turns the pins into a visiting order with a nearest neighbour walk and a 2-opt pass, then gives each leg's distance, a running total and an arrival estimate. No map tiles or outside library are needed: pins are drawn by the module itself.

## Hours in a grid

Timesheet Grid View draws rows down the side, periods across the top and a total in every cell that you can type over. The difference is written through Odoo's normal create and write, so access rules and constraints still apply. A lock closes everything up to a date, fixed or rolling, and the server refuses a write into a closed cell. Installed next to Timesheets, a timesheet grid by employee and project is set up for you.

## Documents with permissions

The Document Management System keeps files as Odoo attachments and adds folders with three group lists: who reads, who writes, who manages. Record rules enforce them on the folder, the documents, their versions and the download link. Uploading over a file keeps the old one as a version you can restore. Upload requests send a customer or supplier a link that needs no account, and two wizards split a scanned PDF by page range and merge several into one.

## Sign-off and an audit trail

Approval Workflow holds a sales order, purchase order or invoice until the right people approve it. Rules pick documents by amount band and an Odoo domain, levels name users or a group, and a level can be skipped below a threshold so only big orders reach the director. Audit Log records creations, changes and deletions on the models you pick, with old and new values per field. Two smaller tools round the pack off: recently viewed and pinned records in the top bar, and saved import and export profiles that match on any field.

<Callout title="What none of these do">
They do not add the Enterprise apps that sit on top of those views. There is no Field Service app, no Planning app with shifts, no Sign app and no payroll here. Route distances are straight-line distances between pins, not road distances. The Gantt engine schedules the module's own plans and tasks; a board on Project tasks draws them and lets you drag them, but does not run links and float on them.
</Callout>
