A meeting room is the most argued over thing in a coworking space. Two teams want it at ten, one of them booked it on the wall calendar, and the member who has used three times their included hours thinks it is all free. This chapter of the [coworking space management series](/odoo-guides/odoo-coworking-space-management/) sets up spaces with seats and rates, then follows a booking through the checks, the credits, approval, cancellation and the front desk.

## Spaces

Configuration, Spaces holds every seat and every room. A space has a **kind**: hot desk, dedicated desk, private office, meeting room, phone booth or event space. It has a **capacity** in seats, an **hourly** and a **daily rate**, the hours from which the day rate applies (**Day Rate From**, 6 by default), a **minimum** and **maximum booking**, a **turnaround** in minutes kept between bookings so the room can be reset, the product it is invoiced under, and its **amenities**.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-coworking-meeting-room-booking/room.webp" alt="Space form for the Spinner Room at Northlight Ancoats, a meeting room on the ground floor with 8 seats, hourly rate 30 and daily rate 180, a 15 minute turnaround, amenities video conferencing, wall screen, whiteboard, catering allowed and step free access, and its bookings and utilisation over 30 days" width={1440} height={900} caption="A meeting room: seats, rates, turnaround and amenities." />

Three things change how a space is booked:

- A **hot desk bank** is one space with many seats. Bookings overlap until the seats are full.
- A **dedicated desk or private office** let to a member (Assigned To) leaves the open diary. Nobody else can book it, and it still counts in the capacity.
- **Requires Approval** sends bookings to a manager. It suits an event space, where a weekend booking means somebody has to open the building.

Rates, limits, the turnaround and the approval flag can only be changed by a space manager; the module refuses the change from hosts and coordinators even though they can edit the space otherwise. A space can be **taken out of service** with a note the desk can read out, and put **back in service** later.

## The booking and its checks

Front Desk, Bookings opens as a calendar, with list, board, pivot and graph views. A booking has the space, the member, who it is booked for and billed to, from and to, the number of people and a setup note. When it is saved and confirmed, it must pass these checks:

| Check | Refused when |
|---|---|
| Clash | a room, booth, office or event space is already booked in the window, counting the turnaround either side |
| Seats | a hot desk bank would go past its seats in the window |
| People | more people than the space seats |
| Length | shorter than the minimum or longer than the maximum booking |
| Book ahead | further ahead than the diary is open, 60 days by default |
| Space | out of service, retired, or let to another member |
| Opening hours | outside the building's hours, or at a closed weekend, unless the member is on a 24/7 plan |
| Member | not active |
| Account | the company account is over its credit limit |

When the room a member wants is taken, **Find Another Room** lists up to eight spaces of the same kind in the building with enough seats that are free for the window.

## Credits, overage and the day rate

Credits are what make a plan worth buying, and they are where the money leaks if nobody counts them. The module counts them:

1. A booking as long as the space's Day Rate From or longer is charged at the **daily rate** per day. Anything shorter is **hourly**.
2. On an hourly booking of a meeting room or phone booth, the member's **remaining credits** for the month are applied first, up to the length of the booking.
3. The rest is charged at the plan's **credit overage rate**, or the room's hourly rate if the plan has none.

On the demo, a Hot Desk plan includes 4 hours a month at 18 an hour after that; a Private Office seat includes 10 hours at 15. The booking form shows **Credits Left**, a one line reminder of where the member stands.

<Callout title="Credits and next month's bookings">
Credits are counted per calendar month by each booking's start date, but when a booking is confirmed the module draws on what is left in the current month. A booking confirmed now for next month uses this month's remainder, so check the credits on bookings made across a month end.
</Callout>

## Approval

When a space requires approval and the person confirming is not a space manager, Confirm puts the booking in **Waiting Approval** with a note in the chatter. Confirming it again does nothing, and setting the status to confirmed by any other route is refused. A space manager opens Front Desk, Waiting Approval and presses **Approve**, which draws the credits and records who approved it.

## Cancellations and no shows

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Cancelled before the free window (4 hours by default) | nothing charged, credits back |
| Cancelled inside the window | late cancellation charge (50% of the untaxed amount), credits stay spent |
| Nobody turned up | No Show, once the start has passed, charges 100% by default |
| Checked in or finished | cannot be cancelled; close it as finished |
| Already invoiced | cannot be cancelled; credit the invoice |

The charge and its reason sit on the booking and go onto the next monthly invoice as a penalty line. The filter **Charged for Not Turning Up** lists them.

## The front desk

**Check In** on a booking marks it in use and opens a visit for the member; **Finished** closes both. Members also badge in and out on their own record, and **Front Desk, On Site Now** lists every open visit grouped by building, which is the live head count and the fire roll call. A business hours member cannot badge in while the building is closed. An hourly job closes any visit left open for 12 hours and marks it as closed by the job, so the morning count is right.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-coworking-meeting-room-booking/on-site.webp" alt="On Site Now list grouped by building, showing members at Northlight Ancoats and Northlight Wellington Place with their check in time and method card or app" width={1440} height={900} caption="Who is in each building right now." />

There is no door or badge reader integration in the module: the card number is stored on the member and visits are recorded in Odoo. A job that ships switched off, **remind tomorrow's bookings**, emails a reminder for bookings in the next 24 hours.
