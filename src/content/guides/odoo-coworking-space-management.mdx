On Monday morning two teams arrive for the same meeting room, because one booked it in the shared calendar and the other told the front desk. A hot desk member has used twelve hours of room time on a plan that includes four, and nobody has billed the other eight. A company with five people in a private office gets five invoices and pays none of them on time. And a letter from a court arrives for a business that has used your address as its registered office for a year, and you cannot say who signed it up. A coworking space sells seats, room hours and an address. This guide is about running all three in Odoo with our Coworking Space Management module.

## What a coworking operator needs

- **Spaces with seats and rates.** A hot desk bank of 24 chairs is one space with 24 seats, so 24 people can book it and the 25th is refused. A meeting room is one space that nobody else gets while it is booked, with a turnaround between meetings.
- **Plans with allowances.** A monthly fee that includes meeting room hours (credits), a printing allowance, guest passes and access hours, business or 24/7.
- **Credits before money.** Room time comes off the credits first, then the rest is charged at the plan's overage rate.
- **Cancellation rules that are applied.** Cancel in good time and the credits come back; cancel late or fail to turn up and the slot is charged.
- **Company accounts.** One company paying for several people, with one invoice a month.
- **A live head count.** Who is in the building now, which is the occupancy figure and the fire roll call.
- **Registered addresses done properly.** The exact legal entity using the address, the identity document seen, and every letter logged.
- **One invoice a month.** Plan fees, room overage, printing, add-ons and day passes gathered and billed once.

## What standard Odoo does

There is no coworking app in Odoo, Community or Enterprise. People try:

- **Appointments or a shared calendar** for rooms. They book a slot but know nothing about plans, credits or seats on a desk bank.
- **Subscriptions** for memberships. That is Enterprise, and it bills a fee but not room overage, printing or day passes.
- **The Events app** for the community side, which is fine on its own.

Odoo does give you the parts underneath: contacts for members and companies, Invoicing for the bill and VAT, and the chatter as an audit trail. Our [appointment booking guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-appointment-booking/) covers the stock booking tools if that is all you need.

## The module

[Coworking Space Management](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_coworking_space) (USD 249) runs on Odoo 19 Community and installs Invoicing. It adds one app, Coworking, with seven menus: Front Desk, Membership, Community, Virtual Office, Billing, Reporting and Configuration. Three roles decide who sees what: a **Community Host** runs the front desk of the buildings they are listed on and sees nothing from others, a **Membership Coordinator** signs members up and works every building, and a **Space Manager** also sets rates and plans, approves bookings, records identity checks and runs the billing.

### Setting it up, step by step

1. **Settings.** The free cancellation window (4 hours), late cancellation charge (50%), no show charge (100%), how far ahead the diary is open (60 days), when forgotten visits are closed (12 hours), the identity check for address services (on), the billing day (1st) and the desk occupancy target (85%).
2. **Locations.** Each building with a code, opening hours, weekends, its community hosts, net lettable area, monthly property cost, billing journal and day pass price.
3. **Floors and spaces.** Hot desk banks, dedicated desks, private offices, meeting rooms, phone booths and event spaces, each with seats, hourly and daily rates, minimum and maximum booking, a turnaround, amenities and, for an event space, a manager approval step.
4. **Plans.** Fee, credits, overage rate, printing allowance, guest passes, access hours, minimum term and notice.
5. **Accounts and members.** Company accounts with a credit limit, then members with their plan, card number and emergency contact. Dedicated desks and offices are let to a member and leave the open diary.
6. **Run the desk.** Bookings, check-in, day passes, the post and events, day to day.
7. **Bill the month.** The billing run gathers the month and raises one invoice per payer.

### The figures

Each building works out desk occupancy against capacity and the target, who is on site now, and for managers the monthly recurring revenue, revenue and cost per square metre, revenue per member and margin. Reporting, Revenue Analysis splits the invoiced money into membership, room overage, printing, add-ons, day passes and penalties, which is the split most operators never measure.

## Limitations

<Callout title="What the module does not do, and what to watch">
- **No member portal or app.** The front desk books; members cannot book themselves in this module.
- **No door or badge reader integration.** Visits are recorded in Odoo; the card number is stored for your access system.
- **Printing is typed in,** not read from a print server.
- **MRR on a building and the monthly value on an account read low** in the current version, because each plan's fee is counted once however many members share it. The plan's own MRR and the invoiced Revenue Analysis are right.
- **Per unit add-ons** such as catering, and room bookings without a member, are not picked up by the billing run; invoice those by hand.
- **Credits** are drawn against the current month when a booking is confirmed, so a booking confirmed now for next month uses this month's remainder.
- **It files nothing with Companies House** or any regulator; it holds the identity checks and the mail log you need to be able to show.
- **Three of the four scheduled jobs ship switched off:** booking reminders, the monthly billing run and the identity expiry check.
</Callout>
