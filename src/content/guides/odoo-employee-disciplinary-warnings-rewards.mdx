When a dismissal is challenged, the first question is what came before it. Was there a verbal warning, and when? Was the written warning actually given to the person, and did they see it? Was the final warning issued while the earlier ones were still live, or a year and a half later? In most small companies the honest answer is a Word file on someone's laptop and a memory of a conversation. The same gap runs the other way: the employee who did three good things in March is remembered for two of them at appraisal time.

The **Employee Rewards and Disciplinary Action** module keeps both in one register on Odoo 19 Community. This chapter covers recording a reward or a warning, how the ladder counts, how warnings lapse, and why acknowledgement is the step that makes a warning hold up. The screens are from the HRMS demo, a fictional company called Meridian Group.

## The record and its six types

Every entry is one record under **WT HRMS, People, Rewards & Disciplinary**. It belongs to an employee and has a type:

| Type | Severity |
|---|---|
| Reward / Appreciation | Low |
| Verbal Warning | Low |
| Written Warning | Medium |
| Final Warning | High |
| Suspension | High |
| Termination | Critical |

Severity follows the type by itself, so nobody files a final warning as "low". Everything except a reward counts as a warning for the ladder.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-employee-disciplinary-warnings-rewards/active-warnings.webp" alt="Rewards and Disciplinary list in Odoo filtered on Active Warnings, showing written and verbal warnings and a suspension with severity, step on the ladder, active until date and state" width={1440} height={375} caption="The Active Warnings filter: confirmed warnings that have not lapsed yet." />

In the list, rewards are shown in green and high or critical records in red, so the serious cases stand out on a long page.

## Step 1: Record it

**People, Rewards & Disciplinary, New**:

1. Pick the **Employee**. The department fills itself in.
2. Choose the **Type**. Severity is set from it.
3. Set the **Date** of the event or conversation, a short **Subject**, and the person in **Issued By**, usually the manager or HR officer who raised it.
4. On the **Details** tab, write what happened in full: dates, what was said, what was agreed. It is a formatted text field, so it can carry a properly written warning.
5. On the **Evidence** tab, attach what supports it: an email, a photo, a signed letter, a meeting note.

The record is saved as a **Draft**. A draft is HR's working copy: the employee cannot see it.

## Step 2: The ladder

The block called **Ladder** on a warning answers the question a manager asks before deciding the next step: how many live warnings does this person already have?

- **Active Until** is twelve months after the warning date unless you change it. After that date the warning stops counting.
- **Prior Active Warnings** is the number of confirmed or acknowledged warnings the employee carried, still active, on the day this one was issued.
- **Step on the Ladder** is that number plus one: 1 for a first warning, 2 for a second.

The **Prior Warnings** tab lists those earlier warnings, so the history is on the record itself.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-employee-disciplinary-warnings-rewards/final-warning-prior.webp" alt="Final warning in draft in Odoo for a demo employee, severity High, active until a year later, step 3 on the ladder with 2 prior active warnings, and the Prior Warnings tab listing a written warning and a verbal warning" width={1440} height={802} caption="A final warning in draft: step 3, with the verbal and written warnings that came before it on the Prior Warnings tab." />

Rewards have no ladder and no active until date. A suspension or termination counts as a warning like the others.

<Callout title="Lapsed warnings stay on file">A warning past its Active Until date is not deleted. It stays on the employee's record and in the list, it just stops counting towards the active total and is not listed as a prior warning for anything issued later. That is how most written disciplinary policies work: a clean year wipes the slate for escalation but not the history.</Callout>

## Step 3: Confirm, then acknowledge

**Confirm** issues the record. Only an HR manager sees the button. Once confirmed:

- the employee can see it, under **People, Rewards & Disciplinary** with the **My Records** filter;
- if it is a second or later step on the ladder, a note is posted in its chatter saying which step it is and how many live warnings the person already carried.

**Acknowledge** is the employee's own step. Only the person named on the record can click it: anyone else, including HR, gets "Only ... can acknowledge this record". The record then shows **Acknowledged by Employee** with the date and who clicked.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-employee-disciplinary-warnings-rewards/suspension-acknowledged.webp" alt="Suspension record in Odoo acknowledged by the employee, severity High, with the acknowledged date and the user who recorded the acknowledgement" width={1440} height={835} caption="An acknowledged suspension: the date and the person who clicked are on the record." />

The one exception is a person with no Odoo login, such as a machine operator or a driver. For them an HR manager can record the acknowledgement on their behalf. The chatter then says "Acknowledgement recorded by ... on behalf of ..., who has no login", so it is never mistaken for the employee's own click.

<Callout title="Why acknowledgement matters">An acknowledgement clicked by HR for a person who could have clicked it themselves proves nothing. That is why the module refuses it. If staff have logins, have them acknowledge in the system; if they do not, keep the signed paper copy on the Evidence tab as well as the on-behalf record.</Callout>

An HR manager can use **Reset to Draft** on a confirmed record that was issued by mistake. An acknowledged record cannot be reset.

## Rewards use the same record

A reward goes through the same three states: draft, confirmed, acknowledged. Record the occasion, the date and who gave it, attach the certificate or the email from the client, and confirm it. Rewards do not touch the ladder, but they sit in the same list and on the same employee tab, so an appraisal or a promotion discussion sees both sides of the year.

## On the employee record

HR officers see a **Rewards & Warnings** tab on every employee form, with each record's date, type, subject, severity, ladder step, active until date and state. The employee also carries three counts: rewards, warnings and **Active Warnings**, the confirmed warnings not yet lapsed. The tab is hidden from people without HR rights, even on their own record; they use the menu instead.

## Finding what needs attention

The search filters cover the usual questions:

- **Active Warnings**: who is currently on the ladder.
- **Awaiting Acknowledgement**: confirmed records the employee has not acknowledged yet. Chase these.
- **Rewards** and **Warnings**: each side on its own.
- Group by **Employee** to see one person's history together, or by **Type**.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-employee-disciplinary-warnings-rewards/grouped-by-employee.webp" alt="Rewards and Disciplinary list in Odoo grouped by employee, each group showing that person's rewards and warnings with type, severity and state" width={1440} height={551} caption="Grouped by employee: one person's rewards and warnings together." />

Records are private per company. HR users and managers see their own company's records, and employees see only their own confirmed ones.

## What it does not do

- **No printed warning letter.** Use the warning letter template in the [HR Letter Generator](/odoo-guides/odoo-employee-onboarding-offboarding/) and attach the signed copy as evidence.
- **No pay effect.** A suspension is recorded, not deducted. Unpaid days go through leave or payroll.
- **No automatic next step.** The ladder tells you the step; deciding what to issue is still HR's call.

To set it up, there is nothing to configure beyond rights: HR managers confirm and reset, HR officers can read every record, and every employee with a login can see and acknowledge their own. Agree your policy's lapse period first; if it is not twelve months, change Active Until on each warning as you issue it.
