A new joiner's first week sets how they feel about the company for a long time. A missing laptop, an appointment letter with last year's wording or a login that arrives on Thursday all say "nobody planned for you". At the other end, a leaver who walks out with a laptop, a live email account or an unsettled loan costs real money. Both ends are lists of small tasks owned by different people: HR, IT, administration, the line manager and the employee. This chapter follows one joiner from record to confirmation, the records HR keeps along the way, and one leaver through to archive.

## 1. Create the employee

**People, Employees, New.** Fill in, tab by tab:

- Name, work email, mobile. **Work** tab: department, job position, manager. The Time Off approver fills in from the manager.
- **Personal** tab: ID number in Identification No, date of birth, gender, private address and phone, emergency contact, bank account.
- **Payroll** tab: contract start date and Wage (monthly basic), working hours.
- **HR Details**: blood group, passport and licence with expiry, emergency details; dependants, education, experience and references on their own tabs.
- **Settings** tab: Badge ID for the attendance machine, and Related User to create the login.

The wage and contract start date are what payroll, service length, leave eligibility and letters all read. Fill them on day one, not at month end. For many joiners at once, use the [import chapter](/odoo-guides/import-employees-into-odoo/). If you hire through the suite's recruitment module, an accepted offer becomes an employee record in one click and you continue from here.

## 2. The onboarding checklist

Checklist templates live under **WT HRMS, Configuration, Checklist Templates**. A Standard Onboarding and a Standard Offboarding list ship with the module; add your own tasks and who owns each: HR, IT, Administration, Direct Manager or the Employee.

1. On the employee, open the **Onboarding** tab and click **Generate from Template**. The tasks are copied to the person, each with the team responsible.
2. Tick **Done** as each task is finished. Who ticked it and when is recorded. Tasks owned by the employee appear on their own My Space.
3. When probation ends, click **Confirm Employee (End Probation)**. The system refuses, naming the tasks, while any task is still open.

## 3. The appointment letter

**People, Letters, New.** Choose the employee and the template, for example Appointment Letter, and who signs. Click **Generate Letter**: the text fills in from the record, and you can correct anything by hand. Then **Print PDF** or **Email to Employee**, and **Mark Sent**. The letter stays on record with its own number.

Ten templates ship: appointment, experience, increment, no objection certificate, offer, promotion, salary certificate, termination, transfer and warning. Before go-live, open each one in **Configuration, Letter Templates**, put in your own wording and the right signatory. Placeholders such as `{{employee.name}}`, `{{employee.job_title}}`, `{{employee.joining_date}}`, `{{wage}}`, `{{company.name}}` and `{{signatory.name}}` are filled in when a letter is made.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-employee-onboarding-offboarding/appointment-letter.webp" alt="Generated appointment letter in Odoo numbered LTR/2026/0001 for a dyeing operator, with template, date, signatory and the letter text filled in from the employee record" width={1440} height={900} caption="An appointment letter generated from the template, numbered and kept on record." />

<Callout title="{{wage}} is the basic, not gross pay">The wage placeholder prints the basic salary from the Payroll tab. The supplied appointment wording calls that figure the gross salary, which is wrong for any structure with allowances. Reword the template, or describe the figure as the basic salary.</Callout>

## 4. Documents and expiry

**People, Documents, New**: employee, document type, number, issue and expiry dates, and the scan attached. A daily job warns HR as the expiry approaches, and the dashboard counts expiring documents. Document types (ID card, passport, driving licence, degree, experience letter, medical certificate) ship with the module; add others and set how many days before expiry HR is warned.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-employee-onboarding-offboarding/employee-documents.webp" alt="Employee Documents list in Odoo with a passport and a national ID card for demo employees, document numbers, expiry dates and state Valid" width={1440} height={900} caption="Documents with expiry dates. A passport running out next month will be flagged in good time." />

The ID number typed on the employee's Personal tab is what payslips and letters print. To be warned before it expires, upload it here as a document too.

## 5. Issue equipment

Register each item once under **People, Assets** with name, category, serial number and value. Asset categories start empty: add Laptop, Mobile Phone, SIM Card, Safety Gear, Locker Key, Vehicle. To hand an item over: **People, Allocations, New**, asset and employee, save. When it comes back, open the allocation and click **Return Asset** with its condition.

## Records HR keeps along the way

**Rewards and warnings.** **People, Rewards & Disciplinary, New**: type (appreciation, verbal, written or final warning, suspension, termination), severity, date, subject and details, evidence attached. Confirm it and the employee can acknowledge it. Earlier warnings are counted, so the next step on the ladder is visible.

**Appraisals.** **Growth, Appraisals, New**: employee and period. Add KRAs with weights that add up to 100. The employee scores themselves, the manager scores, and the final score and rating follow. Goals with progress sit on the Goals tab. Create the review period first under **Configuration, Appraisal Periods**.

**Training.** Training requests come from My Space and go through approval; programmes are listed under **Growth, Training, Programs**. The supplied skills list is aimed at software (Python, SQL), so replace it with your own trades.

**Announcements.** **People, Announcements, New**: title and message, departments or everyone, Pinned to keep it at the top, Show Until to make it disappear. **Publish** with "Tell people when published" ticked notifies everyone it reaches.

## The leaver

1. The employee raises a resignation from My Space, or HR creates it under **People, Resignations**: resignation date, last working day, reason. The notice period comes from the contract.
2. It walks its chain, normally the manager and then HR.
3. On the employee, **Offboarding** tab, **Generate from Template**: recover laptop, phone and access card, disable logins, exit interview, final settlement, handover.
4. Return the assets, generate the experience letter, and run the final payslip as the [payroll chapter](/odoo-guides/run-payroll-odoo-community/) describes.
5. When every task is ticked, archive the employee. The system will not archive someone while an offboarding task is still open.

Never delete a leaver. Archive them: their payslips, letters and history are kept. For a raise or a move, change it on the employee instead, set the new wage with the date it starts so the contract history stays right, and generate an Increment or Promotion letter.

## Reporting lines

**Employees, Workforce Structure** shows each person's level, direct reports and total team. Check **Employees With No Manager** after every import and every exit: anyone listed there has nobody to approve their requests except HR. **Circular Reporting Lines** should always be empty.
