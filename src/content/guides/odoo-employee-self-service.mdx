Self service is where an HR system either saves HR time or creates more of it. If staff cannot find their leave balance, they ask HR. If a manager has to hunt through three menus to approve something, it waits a week. If a payslip is only on paper, someone prints twenty of them every month. In WT HRMS, everything an employee does happens on one page, **My Space**, and a manager's approvals take about five minutes a day. This chapter can be printed and handed to staff and managers as it stands.

## Log in and find your way

Open the address HR gave you in Chrome, Edge or Safari, on a computer or a phone. Log in with your login (usually your work email) and password. You land on My Space; if not, open the app grid and click WT HRMS.

| Part of the page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Attendance | Check in when you arrive and out when you leave. Hidden if the company uses a fingerprint machine. |
| Leave balance | Days left out of the days given for the year, per leave type. |
| Today's shift | Your shift today, if the company plans shifts. |
| New request | One button per kind of request, with your name already filled in. |
| Notices | Announcements from HR. |
| My requests | Everything you asked for: Waiting, Approved, Rejected, Draft. |
| My profile | Your own record: contact details, emergency contact, documents. |

## Ask for time off

1. On My Space click **Time off** and choose the type, for example Annual Leave.
2. Pick the first and last day. The working days are counted for you; days off and public holidays are not counted.
3. Write a short reason, attach a document if the type asks for one, such as a medical certificate.
4. Save. The request goes to your approver at once and shows under My requests as Waiting.

If the request is refused, read the message: it says why, for example you have not completed the service months for that type, or the request is longer than the policy allows in one go. The [leave chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-leave-management/) explains those rules for HR.

## Other requests: the same three steps

Every other button works the same way: click it, fill the form, click **Submit**. A request saved without Submit stays in Draft and nobody sees it.

| Button | What you fill in |
|---|---|
| Work from home | Dates, full or half day, reason. |
| Overtime | Date, hours, weekday, weekend or holiday, reason. Approved hours are paid with your salary. |
| Salary advance | Amount, reason, months to repay and the first month. There is a limit, normally half a month's salary. |
| Loan | Amount, number of instalments, start date, reason. |
| Expense claim | Date, category, amount, description, a photo of the receipt. |
| Travel | Destination, dates, purpose, advance needed, hotel and visa details. |
| Service request | Category (IT, admin, building), subject, what is wrong. |
| Comp-off | A day off in return for working a holiday or weekend. |
| Leave encashment | Leave type and days to sell back, if the company allows it. |
| Training | The programme or course and why. |
| Shift swap | Your shift, the colleague and the shift you swap with. |

### Expenses go on a sheet

1. Click **Expense claim** once per receipt. Fill it, attach the photo, save.
2. Open **Pay, Expenses, Expense Sheets**, click **New** and give it a name such as "Client visit, October".
3. Add your claims to the sheet and click **Submit**. The sheet is approved as one; once paid it shows Reimbursed.

## Follow your requests

The **My requests** panel at the bottom of My Space shows everything you asked for, with its status. Click a line to open it and read any message from the approver. You also get a notification in Odoo, under the speech bubble at the top, when a request is decided.

## Your payslips

Open your company's Odoo address followed by `/my/payslips` and save it as a bookmark on your phone. The top shows gross, deductions and take home pay so far this year. Click **View** to see a payslip line by line, or **PDF** to download it.

New joiners also see **My onboarding / offboarding checklist** at the bottom of My Space: the tasks that are theirs, such as handing in copies of their ID and degree. Tick them as they are done.

## For line managers: five minutes a day

A manager's My Space has everything an employee's has, plus a **Waiting for your decision** panel and the **My Team** tile.

1. Open My Space, or **My Space, To Approve** for the list on its own.
2. Read the line: the kind of request, who, the summary and the date. Click it to open the full request with attachments and history.
3. Click **Approve** or **Reject**. When you reject, open the request and write the reason with **Send message**, so the employee reads it.
4. The line leaves your list and the employee is told. If HR has a step after you, it moves to HR.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-employee-self-service/manager-to-approve.webp" alt="Line manager My Space in WT HRMS with Waiting for your decision listing two time off requests and one overtime request, each with Approve and Reject buttons" width={1440} height={900} caption="Waiting for your decision: one click per request, nothing to type unless you reject." />

Decide within a day. A request left alone may move to the next approver after the escalation time, and the person waiting notices. When you reject, say why and what would be acceptable ("Not that week, we ship the export order. The week after is fine."). For overtime, approve only hours you know were worked, because approved hours are paid.

**My Team** shows each person who reports to you: in, not checked in yet, or off today, with how many of their requests wait for you and who is away this week. If someone in your team cannot use the system, for example they are off sick without a phone, tell HR: HR raises the request in their name and you approve it as usual.

<Callout title="Two-step leave can stay in your list">When a leave type needs you and HR, your approval moves it to Second Approval. In this version the line can stay in your list until HR decides. You do not need to act on it again; a second click only shows a message that it cannot be approved, which is harmless.</Callout>

## Employees without a computer

Machine operators and drivers often have no email. You can mix three approaches: no login at all, with HR raising requests and payslips printed; a login on a shared or personal phone, using their mobile number or an invented address as the login; or attendance only from a device, so they punch in and never log in. Every internal user counts as a user for your Odoo subscription or hosting plan, so decide with the owner how many people really need a login before the import. The [import chapter](/odoo-guides/import-employees-into-odoo/) sets the login role per person.
