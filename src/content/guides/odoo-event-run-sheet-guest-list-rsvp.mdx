On the day nobody opens the contract. The event runs off one sheet of paper: what happens when, where, and whose job it is. Behind it sit two lists that cause most of the panic in the last fortnight: who is working, with the right certificate, and how many people are eating what. This chapter covers the day side of the [Event Management](/odoo-guides/odoo-event-management/) module for Odoo 19 Community: the run sheet, the roster, the guest list, tables, dietary totals and final numbers.

## The run sheet

The run sheet is the event's Run Sheet tab, and across all events under **On the Day, Run Sheets**. Each cue has:

- **Time** and **minutes**. Run sheets are written in minutes: thirty means half an hour.
- **Cue**: what happens, such as crew call and venue access, doors open, call to table, speeches.
- **Kind**: set up, arrivals, ceremony, food and drink service, speeches, photography, entertainment, breakdown or other.
- **Location** in the building, read by the crew off the printout.
- **Owner**: one crew member against each cue.
- **Cannot slip**: the cues the day hangs off. A cue marked this way cannot be saved without an owner.

A cue timed before the crew can get into the venue (the event's venue access time) is refused.

### On the day: actual against plan

During the event each cue is moved with **Start** and **Done**, which stamp the actual start and end times, and the cue shows how many minutes it ran late. **Skip** needs a reason, because on the debrief it is the only record of why it did not happen. When the event is marked delivered, any cue still open is skipped with "Event closed down", and the chatter records how many of the cues were worked through.

**Print Run Sheet** produces a landscape A4 sheet: reference, venue and layout, access, guest and carriage times, the covers and the day contact, the kitchen's dietary totals, every cue with its owner and a done column, then the crew call with roles, names, uniform and a column to tick in.

## The staffing roster

### Roles and ratios

Staffing roles live under **Configuration, Staffing Roles**. The module ships ten: event manager, floor coordinator, head waiter, waiting staff, bar staff, chef, kitchen porter, AV technician, security and cloakroom. Each role has:

- An **hourly rate**, copied onto a roster line and overridable per person.
- **One per N guests**, the ratio the roster is built on (one waiter per twelve is 12), or a **supervisor** flag for a role you need once on every event whatever the numbers.
- **Needs a valid certificate**, for bar staff with a personal licence, a first aider or an electrician signing off the rig.
- **Call time before start**, two hours unless you change it.

Your people go under **Configuration, Crew**: the roles each can do, an optional own rate, whether they are freelance, and their certificate expiry.

### Drawing up the roster

**Draw Up the Roster** on the event works out, for every role, how many slots the guests charged need (rounded up), and adds the missing ones. Each slot is called in at the role's call time before the guests arrive and finishes 90 minutes after carriages. For each slot it picks someone who can do the role, is not already on a shift that overlaps, and holds a certificate valid on the day if the role needs one. Your own people are offered before freelancers, because a freelance shift costs the event more. Where nobody fits, the slot stays open as **To Fill**, and **On the Day, Slots To Fill** lists every open slot across the diary.

The same rules apply when you edit by hand. A crew member cannot be put in a role they are not down for, on a shift that overlaps another of their shifts, or on a certificate role with no certificate or one that runs out before the shift.

Each shift moves through planned, confirmed, on site (with **Check In** stamping the time), worked or no show. **Shift Worked** is refused for someone who never checked in; use **No Show**, which also notes it on the event.

<Callout title="Open Up refuses an empty slot">On the morning, Open Up is refused while any roster slot has nobody in it, and while the event has no run sheet. Either fill the slot or delete it if you have decided to run short.</Callout>

Crew cost on the event is each shift's call to finish hours times its rate. It is worked out from the planned shift, so a no show or a shift that ran late does not change the cost by itself: correct the times on the line if you pay on actual hours.

## The guest list

Guests are on the event's Guests tab, and across events under **Guests, Guest List**. Each line has:

- **Name**, and the Odoo contact if the guest already is one.
- **Seats**: two for a couple invited on one line.
- **Age group**: adult, child or infant. An infant takes no seat.
- **RSVP**: not replied, attending, not attending, tentative or wait list, recorded with the **Attending**, **Not Attending** and **Wait List** buttons. A guest who declines or is wait listed comes off their table, and an Attending reply after the event has run is refused.
- **Dietary**: vegetarian, vegan, gluten free, dairy free, nut allergy, shellfish allergy, halal, kosher or other, with a note for the kitchen. Other is refused without a note.
- **Side** (host, partner or both) on a wedding, and a **VIP** flag for the top table, the speech list or someone to greet at the door.

The event shows the seats confirmed, declined and still waiting, and the dietary totals for every guest who has said yes.

## Tables and the seating plan

On the Tables tab, **Lay the room out from the venue layout** creates as many tables as the layout calls for, sized to seat the guests charged evenly, round for a banquet layout and long otherwise. Each table has a capacity, a shape (round, long, sweetheart or poseur) and a position note such as "nearest the band". A guest cannot be put on a table that is full, or on a table laid for another event.

**Print the seating and dietary plan** gives the caterer and the head waiter one document: the confirmed count, the kitchen's dietary totals, then each table with how many of its seats are taken and every guest's seats, dietary requirement and note.

## Final numbers

Every event has a **final numbers due** date, worked back from the event day by the days set in Settings (14 by default). Once the client confirms, enter the **final numbers** on the event: that is the number the kitchen cooks for and the package is charged on, never below the contracted minimum. A confirmed event past its deadline with no final numbers is flagged, and the scheduled job "Events: ask for final guest numbers" emails the client and gives the coordinator a to-do. It ships switched off, like the other three jobs.

## Limits on the day side

- **No online RSVP, guest portal or invitation email.** The Invitation Sent button records that an invitation went out and dates it; it does not send anything.
- **No floor plan drawing.** Tables are records with a capacity, shape and position note, not a diagram of the room.
- **No mobile app for crew.** Crew use the normal Odoo web screens, where the Crew group limits them to their own shifts and run sheets.
- **No timesheets or payroll link.** Shift hours and rates feed the event's crew cost only. If your staff clock in on a terminal, the [attendance and shifts guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-attendance-shifts-overtime/) covers that side in Odoo HR.
