A wedding planner's margin is decided by suppliers. The package price was fixed when the client signed; what moves after that is what the caterer, the florist and the band actually charge, and whether the extra marquee panels came back. And the one supplier you cannot book at any price is the one whose insurance lapsed, because the venue will turn them away at the loading bay. This chapter covers the supplier side of the [Event Management](/odoo-guides/odoo-event-management/) module for Odoo 19 Community: the approved list, quotes, the event budget, hire stock and the margin report.

## The approved supplier list

Suppliers live under **Suppliers, Supplier List**. Each one is linked to an Odoo contact and a supplier category, and carries:

- **On the approved list.** Only an approved supplier can have a quote accepted.
- **Rating**: first choice, good, solid, below par or would not use again. When quotes are drafted for an event, the best rated approved supplier in each category is picked first.
- **Notice period (days).** How long before the event the supplier has to be booked. A quote accepted inside it is flagged as a late booking on the quote.
- **Payment terms** in words, as agreed with them.
- **Insurance**: policy reference, expiry date and the value of cover, because some venues insist on a floor.
- **Licence**: reference and expiry for food registration, a personal alcohol licence, pyrotechnics and the like.
- **Private notes**: what you really think of them, kept off the client paperwork.

**Configuration, Supplier Categories** decides which documents each kind of supplier must hold. The module ships eleven categories (catering, bar and drinks, photography and film, floristry, music and entertainment, sound and lighting, marquee and furniture, transport, cake and patisserie, stationery and print, agency staffing), each pointed at a budget heading so an accepted quote lands in the right place.

### Paperwork status

From the expiry dates and the category rules, every supplier shows a paperwork status: in date, expiring soon (inside the warning window from Settings, 30 days by default), expired or missing. **Suppliers, Paperwork Due** lists the ones that need chasing.

The rules that follow from it:

- **Add to Approved List** is refused while required paperwork is expired or missing.
- **Accept** on a quote is refused for a supplier that is not approved, or whose required paperwork is expired or missing.
- **Suspend** takes a supplier off the approved list. Quotes already out can still be rejected, but nothing new can be accepted.
- The scheduled job "Events: supplier insurance and licence check" posts a note on every approved supplier whose paperwork is expiring, expired or missing, and suspends the expired and missing ones. It ships switched off.

## Supplier quotes, from brief to budget

On a quoted event, **Open Supplier Quotes** drafts one quote per heading in the package (venue excluded), each with the best rated approved supplier who is clear to book, and a brief that names the event, the guest number and the venue. You can also add quotes by hand on the event's Suppliers tab or under **Suppliers, Quotes**.

A quote moves through draft, requested, received, accepted or rejected:

1. **Send the Brief** marks the quote as requested and dates it. It records that the brief went out; send the brief itself in whatever way you work with that supplier.
2. **Price Received** needs their figure on the quote first.
3. **Accept** takes the quote onto the event budget. Only one quote per category can be accepted on an event; reject the first if you are changing supplier.
4. **Reject** needs a reason, which is the record the next planner reads.

Each quote shows an **order by** date (the event date less the supplier's notice period), and a quote can carry a valid until date.

## The event budget

The budget is the event's Budget tab, and across all events under **Money, Budget Lines**. It starts when the event is quoted: the package inclusions, multiplied by the guests charged, become one estimated line per heading, plus a venue hire line from the venue's hire fee.

Each line has a budgeted figure, an actual figure and a state:

- **Estimate**: the plan only.
- **Committed**: the cost is agreed. An accepted supplier quote lands on the matching heading's line as the actual figure, so the variance column reads as "the caterer came in this much over what we allowed".
- **Settled**: the real figure from the supplier invoice is on the line and it is final. **Settle** is refused while the actual is empty, because a line settled at the estimate tells you nothing, and a settled line cannot be deleted without reopening it first.

The committed figure used in every total is the actual where there is one, and the budget where there is not.

### Crew cost and margin

Below the budget lines, the event shows budgeted cost, actual cost, supplier cost, crew cost, total cost, budget variance, gross margin and margin percentage. Crew cost is the roster: hours times each person's rate, kept off the coordinator's screens. Gross margin is the untaxed event value less committed cost and crew cost.

**Reporting, Margin by Event** pivots revenue, gross margin and guests charged by month, event type and venue, which is where you find out which package or which venue is carrying the business.

<Callout title="The file does not close on an estimate">Close the File is refused while any budget line is not settled. That is deliberate: the margin on a closed event is the reported figure, and reopening a cost line on a closed event is refused for the same reason.</Callout>

## Hire stock: out, back and broken

Furniture, linen, glassware, staging, power and catering equipment you own or hire in sits under **Configuration, Hire Stock**, with the quantity you hold, whether it is cross hired and from which supplier, a hire rate per event and a replacement value.

On the event's Equipment tab you allocate kit for a window, from going out to coming back:

- Availability is checked across the **whole window** against every other allocation that is reserved or out, so twelve tables cannot be promised to two weddings on the same weekend.
- An item marked as cross hired puts a committed **cross hire** line on the event budget at its hire rate times the quantity.
- **Load Out** marks the kit as on site. Open Up on the morning does this for everything still reserved.
- **Book Back In** records the return. Any quantity damaged or not returned goes onto the event budget at its replacement value, as a committed damage and loss line.
- **Equipment Out** lists what is still away, and the scheduled job "Events: chase equipment still out" chases kit past its return date plus the days set in Settings. It ships switched off.

Close the File is refused while anything is still reserved or out.

## What this chapter does not cover

- **Purchase orders and vendor bills.** The module keeps supplier commitments on the event budget. If your accountant wants purchase orders, raise them in Odoo Purchase; nothing links them back to the budget line, so the actual figure is entered on the line by hand.
- **Supplier portal.** Suppliers do not log in to see briefs or upload certificates. Documents are recorded by your team on the supplier record.
