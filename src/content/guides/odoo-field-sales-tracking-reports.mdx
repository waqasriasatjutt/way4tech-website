A distribution manager's day is mostly questions. Has the North Market route started? Why is van two short of detergent for its drops? Can Green Valley have more credit? Which salesman is behind target, and which route has not made money in three months? With paper and phone calls, each answer takes a call to someone who is driving. With every visit, sale, drop and payment already in Odoo, most of them are one screen away.

This chapter covers what the manager uses: the phone cockpit, tracking, and the reports. The cockpit, tracking and most reports are Enterprise; the dashboard, targets, Sales by Route and Visit Analysis are in every edition. It is part of the [FMCG distribution guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-distribution-management/).

## The manager's cockpit on the phone (Enterprise)

A manager who opens /field on a phone gets a cockpit instead of the field tiles. **Today** shows people on duty, sales and collections against target, drops delivered and open, visits and the strike rate against its goal (60% on the demo), overdue customers by age and new shops waiting.

### Approvals

**Approvals** collects everything that needs a yes or no: new shops, big returns, sales over the credit limit, cash counts and van settlements with differences, expense claims and load requests. Each carries the facts needed to decide. For a sale over the limit, that is what the shop owes, its limit, this sale and the salesman's reason. For a new shop, the owner, phone, address, GPS pin, licence and ID photos, and a credit limit to set when approving. Nobody can approve their own request, and a refusal carries a note the salesman sees.

### Team and alerts

**Team today** shows who is on duty, since when, at which shop, phone battery, visits and orders. "Late start" flags anyone who began after the route's start time. **Alerts** lists routes not started, check-ins outside the fence, phones with no location, a van short of stock for its open drops, and someone stopped away from any customer.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-field-sales-tracking-reports/s03-team.webp" alt="Team today screen with 3 of 4 on duty, 8 of 13 visits, 40 drops open, Route profit and Collect shortcuts, and cards for Aisha Khan, order taker at Bazaar Wholesale Traders with a Late start flag and 74 percent battery, and Bilal Ahmed in van sales" width={800} height={1720} caption="Team today: who is where, and who started late." />

The cockpit also has Collect today, Route profit and the live map, and a manager can **Work as** an order taker or a driver from the same phone.

## Tracking and maps (Enterprise)

No Google Maps key is needed, and positions are only recorded between Start my day and End my day.

- **Live Map**: everyone on duty, refreshed every 30 seconds, with last seen time, route, visits and orders.
- **Route Replay**: pick a person and a day and replay the trail with a time slider and the distance covered.
- **Customer Map**: every shop coloured by days since its last order: green within 7, orange within 30, red over 30, grey never. A red cluster is a part of town the team has stopped selling to.
- **Location History**: every position recorded on duty, deleted after the days set in Settings.

### Geofence and field exceptions

In Settings, the **Geofence Radius** says how far from the shop's saved location a check-in may be. The policy either flags the visit for review or blocks the check-in. **No-Ping** and **Away-From-Customer** alerts raise an exception when an on-duty phone goes silent, or someone is stopped away from every customer on the route, for the minutes you set. **Tracking, Field Exceptions** lists them all: late start, check-in outside the fence, no location, route not started, van short of stock, stopped away from customers. Each can be opened on the map and resolved.

## The back office every day

Field orders are sales orders, deliveries are transfers and collections are payments, so accounting and stock stay standard Odoo. The daily lists in the Distribution app are **Field Orders** (by route, channel, van and delivery state), **Deliveries** (with signature, photo and skip reason), **Collections** (with route and who collected) and **Visits** (outcome, reason, order and duration, in list, calendar, pivot and graph). Enterprise adds Store Checks, New Shops, Approval Requests, Quarantine, Van Settlements, Cash Counts, Cheques, the Collection Planner, Promises to Pay, Expense Claims and Counter Days.

## Reports

Every report opens in pivot, list or graph, and the main ones print as PDF. Days, weeks and months follow the **Report Time Zone** in Settings, so a sale at 23:30 lands on the right day.

| Question | Report |
|---|---|
| Who is behind target this month? | Dashboard, Target Performance |
| Which salesperson or van earns the most margin? | Van and Salesperson Performance |
| Are reps visiting and selling? | KPIs: strike rate, route compliance, lines per call |
| Who owes us, and how long? | Debt Ageing, Collection Planner, Customer Statement |
| Which routes or shops lose money? | Cost to Serve, Route Profitability |
| Where did van stock go? | Van Stock Movements, Van Settlements |
| What did vans cost to run? | Fuel, Cost per km |
| Is the shelf right? | Perfect Store |
| What happened today? | Daily Sales Report |

**KPIs** shows, by user, route, territory or day: visits, productive visits, strike rate, route compliance, orders, lines per call, average drop, sales, collected, collection ratio and new outlets.

**Van and Salesperson Performance** shows sales, orders, cash and bank collected, credit sales, debts collected, returns, and stock received, sold, damaged and left, with gross margin from real cost. **Print Performance** makes the PDF.

### Cost to serve and route profit

Selling more to a shop is not always good. **Cost to Serve** costs each shop per month from three things: kilometres driven on its route (from fuel logs and odometers, or the cost per km in Settings), time spent at the shop (staff cost per hour) and the number of deliveries (cost per drop). Gross margin minus cost to serve is what the shop really earns, and shops below zero are listed. **Route Profitability** adds it up to the net contribution of each route by month.

### Targets, incentives and the daily report

- **Sales Targets** (every edition): per salesperson, route or (Enterprise) van, with sales, orders, units, shops served and collections. Each person sees progress on the phone. **Target Performance** compares target with actual by week, person, route and van.
- **Incentive Plans, Points, Badges, Contests** (Enterprise): pay a percentage of sales or collections by achievement slab, or an amount per delivered drop; points for orders, productive visits, collections and store checks; weekly or monthly contests with a prize and a live leaderboard. **Incentives** produces monthly statements to approve.
- **Daily Sales Report** (Enterprise): print it, or have it emailed to every distribution manager each evening at a set hour.

<Callout title="What tracking does not do">Positions are only recorded while someone is on duty, so the map shows nothing for staff who never press Start my day. Route optimisation uses straight line distance times a road factor unless you run your own OSRM server. The Base edition has the dashboard, targets, Sales by Route and Visit Analysis, but no live map, KPIs or margin reports.</Callout>
