A forwarder rarely loses money on the freight rate it negotiated. It loses it in the gaps around the rate: a terminal handling charge left off the quote, a chargeable weight that was right on the booking and wrong on the invoice, a haulier's bill booked a month after the file was closed, forty thousand dollars of import duty passing through the books and making the margin report unreadable. Odoo can hold every one of those facts, but only if they sit on one record that follows the shipment from the first rate to the last supplier bill. This guide explains how that works with the Freight Forwarding and Customs Clearance module on Odoo 20, chapter by chapter.

## What a forwarding business needs from its system

Whether you move air freight, full containers, groupage or trucks across a border, the office needs the same few things:

1. **One file per shipment.** Customer, shipper, consignee, notify party, incoterm, load and discharge points, the trade lane and the salesperson. Everything else hangs off that file.
2. **Chargeable weight worked out the same way every time.** Air rates on the greater of actual and volumetric weight, groupage on weight or measure, full containers on the box. If the quote and the invoice do the sum differently, the customer wins the argument.
3. **Two sides on every charge.** What it costs you and what you sell it for, on the same line, so the profit on a file is a fact and not a month end estimate.
4. **The documents the cargo moves against.** Master and house bills of lading, air waybills, CMR notes, released only against something you can account for.
5. **Containers and legs that tell the truth.** A container number that is checked when it is typed, a verified gross mass before loading, and routing that includes the inland leg so the ETA means something.
6. **Customs duty kept out of the margin.** Duty and import VAT are the importer's money. They have to be recharged at cost and never counted as revenue.
7. **Reports that find the leaks.** Files carrying cost with nothing invoiced, costs with no supplier bill behind them, files under the lane's target margin.

## What standard Odoo does and does not do

Standard Odoo gives a forwarder the accounting and the address book. Contacts hold customers, shippers and carriers. Invoicing raises customer invoices and supplier bills in more than one currency. Incoterms are already there as a list. That is a sound base, and this module uses all of it rather than replacing it.

What Odoo does not have is any of the freight records themselves. There is no job file, no chargeable weight, no bill of lading, no container with a seal and a VGM, no routing leg with a vessel and voyage, no customs entry with tariff lines and duty, and no report of profit by trade lane. People who try to run forwarding on plain Odoo usually end up with a sales order per shipment and a spreadsheet beside it for everything that matters. The spreadsheet is where the money goes missing.

## The job file, and the four modes

Everything hangs off the job file. A file is opened in one of four modes, and the mode decides the numbering, the document checklist and how the cargo is rated:

| Mode | Document | Rated on |
|---|---|---|
| Air | Air waybill | Greater of actual weight and volume at 6,000 cm³ per kilo |
| Sea FCL | Bill of lading | The container and the TEU |
| Sea LCL | Bill of lading | Weight or measure, a tonne to the cubic metre |
| Road | CMR consignment note | The truck, or volume at 3,000 cm³ per kilo |

The file number is drawn from a separate series per mode and starts with the office code, so a reference such as MBA/FCL/2026/00004 tells anyone reading a booking which branch, which mode and which file it is before they open it. That is also why the mode cannot be changed once a file is booked: the number is already on a booking with a carrier.

## Who uses it: four roles

The module ships four roles, and three of them sit beside each other rather than on top of each other:

- **Freight Sales** quotes and opens the files they sold. A record rule limits them to their own quotations and files, so a commission sheet can go in front of one salesperson without showing them the whole branch.
- **Operations Desk** runs every file on the branch: cargo, documents, bills, containers, routing, charges, invoices and supplier bills. It reads customs entries but does not lodge them.
- **Customs Broker** owns the declarations from building to clearing, and maintains the tariff codes. Clearing an entry moves the file forward without the broker needing write access to it.
- **Freight Manager** has all of the above plus configuration, deleting, and three figures nobody else sees: the standard cost on a rate card, an agent's profit share rate, and the agent share owed on a file.

Roles are given in **Settings, Users and Companies, Users**, on the person's Access Rights tab under Freight Forwarding. Add Invoicing rights for anyone who posts invoices or bills. These are internal roles; do not give one to an overseas agent or a customer.

## The demo database

Every screen in these chapters comes from one demo database, so the numbers carry across: a made up forwarder and licensed customs broker called Meridian Freight Services, with one branch that forwards and clears and one that forwards only, books in US dollars, about twenty five job files across all four modes, fifteen quotations and seven customs entries. Your company name, ports, currency and tariff rates will be your own. The system is not tied to one country; duty rates, VAT rates, regimes and levies are configuration.

## What it does not do

- **No link to a customs authority.** You file with customs in their own system, then record the entry number, lodging, query and clearance on the entry here.
- **No carrier or tracking integration.** Schedules, departures and arrivals are typed or confirmed on the routing legs. Nothing pulls vessel positions or airline status.
- **No customer or agent portal.** Outside parties receive documents and the arrival notice by email; they do not log in.
- **No warehouse or bonded store management.** Customs warehousing is a regime on the entry, not a stock location with bins.
- **No trucking fleet management.** Road files carry a truck reference on the leg. For running your own trucks, drivers and trips, see the Transport demo on the [demos page](/demos/).
