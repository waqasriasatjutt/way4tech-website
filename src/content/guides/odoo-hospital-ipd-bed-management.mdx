A ward runs on two questions asked a hundred times a day: which bed is free, and what has this patient had since they came in. When the answer lives on a whiteboard and a paper chart, the bed shows as free while it is still being cleaned and the bill at discharge misses the dressing changes from Tuesday. Hospital Management keeps each bed as a record with a status, and each stay as an admission that collects its services until discharge.

## Set up wards, rooms and beds

Under **Hospital, Configuration**:

- **Wards** have a name, code, department, building or block, floor and a ward type: general, private, ICU, CCU, NICU, isolation or maternity. A ward shows its bed count and opens its beds.
- **Rooms** sit inside a ward.
- **Beds** have a bed number and code, the ward (required) and room, a bed type (standard, electric, ICU, paediatric or bariatric) and a **daily charge**, which is the room and board rate per day of stay.

Every bed has a status: **Available**, **Reserved**, **Occupied**, **Cleaning** or **Maintenance**. Buttons on the bed set it available, cleaning or maintenance. The bed shows its current admission and patient while someone is in it.

## The bed board

**Hospital, Inpatient, Bed Board** is a kanban of every bed grouped by status, with the ward, the bed type and, for an occupied bed, the patient's name. This is the screen the admissions desk keeps open. The dashboard shows the same figures as counts and an occupancy percentage.

## Admit the patient

Create an admission under **Hospital, Inpatient, Admissions**. It holds:

- **Patient**, **attending physician** and **department** (the department follows the physician).
- **Bed**, chosen from beds that are available or reserved. The ward follows the bed.
- **Admission date** and the **reason for admission**.

**Admit** is refused without a bed and refused if the bed is already occupied. On admit, the admission moves to Admitted, the bed becomes Occupied and a note on the chatter records which bed. Only a draft admission can be admitted.

The **length of stay** is counted in whole days from the admission date to the discharge date, or to now while the patient is still in, and is never less than one.

## During the stay

The admission has five tabs:

- **Services**: one line per chargeable item with a date, a service from your list (which fills the description and price), quantity, unit price and subtotal. Nursing care, procedures, pharmacy items and anything else you bill go here.
- **Diagnosis and Notes**: diagnoses from your list with ICD codes, and admission notes.
- **Prescription**: the same structured medicine lines as an OPD visit.
- **Lab and Radiology**: requests with status and results, as on a visit.
- **Discharge**: the discharge type and the discharge summary.

The **estimated total** at the bottom is the bed's daily charge times the length of stay, plus all service lines. It moves every day the patient stays.

## Discharge

**Discharge** moves the admission to Discharged, stamps the discharge date, and sets the bed to **Cleaning**, so it does not show as free until the ward staff have turned it round and set it back to Available. Only an admitted patient can be discharged.

On the Discharge tab, choose the **discharge type**: recovered, referred, left against medical advice, or deceased, and write the summary. The **Discharge Summary** report on the admission's Print menu produces the PDF for the patient and the referring doctor.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-hospital-ipd-bed-management/discharge-summary.webp" alt="Discharge tab of a discharged Odoo admission with a three day length of stay, discharge type Recovered and the written discharge summary" width={1440} height={900} caption="A discharged admission: three days, recovered, with the summary written on the record." />

Cancelling an admitted patient's admission sets the bed back to available.

## Bill the stay

**Create Invoice** on the admission raises a customer invoice for the patient's billing contact with:

- a **room and board** line: the bed's daily charge times the length of stay, named after the bed and the number of days;
- one line per **service**, at its quantity and unit price, with the service's taxes.

The invoice is linked to the patient and the admission and counts towards the dashboard's monthly revenue once posted. You can raise it at discharge, or raise interim invoices during a long stay. Each press creates a new invoice from everything on the admission, though, so an interim invoice followed by a final one bills the same lines twice unless you adjust the second.

<Callout title="Check room and board before posting">The room and board line carries no tax and uses the bed's current daily charge for the whole stay. If a patient moved from a general bed to a private one, the whole stay is charged at the private rate, so correct the line on the draft invoice. As with OPD invoices, lines post to the first income account found in your chart of accounts in this version; agree the account with your accountant.</Callout>

## What the IPD part does not do

- **No bed transfer step.** An admission holds one bed. A move is an edit to the bed field plus two manual status changes, and the history is only in the chatter.
- **No reservation calendar.** A bed can be set to Reserved, but there is no booking of a bed for a future date.
- **No nursing chart or medication administration record.** Prescriptions are listed; doses given are not recorded. ICU observation charts are part of the Ultimate add-on.
- **No theatre booking in the core.** Operation theatres, surgeries and consumables are in the Enterprise add-on.
- **No insurance split.** The invoice goes to the patient's contact. Insurance policies and claims raised from invoices are in Enterprise, and pre-authorisation and insurer tariffs are in Ultimate.
