A small hotel's front desk keeps three lists that must agree: which rooms are booked for which nights, who is in the building tonight, and what each guest owes. When they live in a paper diary, a spreadsheet and the accounting system, they stop agreeing by the second week. A room is sold twice over a long weekend, a guest leaves without paying for breakfast, housekeeping cleans a room that is still occupied. This series explains how a hotel, resort or guest house can run all three on Odoo 19 Community with the Hotel Management PMS module, what each part does, and what it does not do. The screenshots come from the live demo, which uses the module's own sample property of twelve rooms on four floors.

## What a hotel needs from its system

Whatever the size, a property management system has to cover a handful of jobs:

1. **The property itself.** Floors or wings, room types with a rate and an occupancy, and every physical room with its status.
2. **Reservations by night.** Guest, dates, rooms and rate, with a check that a room cannot be sold twice for the same night.
3. **The front desk day.** Arrivals to check in, departures to check out, no-shows to release, and the guest's ID on a registration card.
4. **The folio.** One running bill per stay that collects room nights and extras, then becomes an invoice.
5. **Housekeeping.** Which rooms are dirty, which are being cleaned, which are ready to sell.
6. **The numbers.** Occupancy, average daily rate and revenue per available room, without building a spreadsheet each month.

## What standard Odoo does and does not do

Odoo 19 Community has Invoicing, Contacts, Discuss and, if you install them, Sales, Point of Sale and Website. None of them knows what a room or a night is. You can sell a "room night" as a product on an invoice, but there is no room inventory, no availability check, no check-in, no folio that grows during the stay and no housekeeping status.

The Hotel Management PMS module adds those records and keeps the money in standard Odoo: the folio becomes a normal customer invoice in Invoicing, and payments, taxes and receivables work as they do for any other invoice.

## The module

[Hotel Management PMS](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_hotel_management/) costs USD 2,000 and runs on Odoo 19 Community. It depends only on Invoicing and Discuss. Once installed it adds a **Hotel** app with these menus:

| Menu | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Front Desk | The live dashboard: occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue this month, today's arrivals and departures, room states, the reservation pipeline |
| Reservations | Reservations list and the booking calendar |
| Front Office | Rooms (the room board), Folios and Guests |
| Housekeeping | Cleaning, turndown, inspection, maintenance and linen tasks |
| Reporting | Reservations analysis in pivot and graph views |
| Configuration | Room types, floors, amenities and chargeable services |

Three access groups ship with it: **Receptionist / Front Desk**, **Housekeeping** and **Hotel Manager**, with multi-company rules on every hotel record.

The two chapters in this series go into the daily work:

- [Reservations, check-in and housekeeping](/odoo-guides/odoo-hotel-reservations-front-desk/): taking a booking, the availability check, check-in and check-out, no-shows, the registration card and the housekeeping board.
- [Folio billing and invoicing](/odoo-guides/odoo-hotel-folio-billing/): how room charges and daily services reach the folio, adding extras, and turning the folio into an invoice.

## Setting up the property

The property is built from four configuration lists:

- **Floors** (Configuration, Floors): a name, a code and an optional building or wing. The demo has a ground floor, two upper floors and a third floor sea view wing.
- **Room types** (Configuration, Room Types): base rate per night, maximum adults and children, bed type (single to suite), size in square metres, a description, a photo and the standard amenities.
- **Rooms** (Front Office, Rooms): room number, type, floor, amenities, a nightly rate that defaults from the type and can be changed per room, a photo and notes.
- **Amenities** (Configuration, Amenities): Wi-Fi, minibar, sea view and so on, grouped by kind, with an optional icon.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-hotel-management/room-board.webp" alt="Hotel room board in Odoo as a kanban grouped by status: available, occupied, out of order and reserved, each card showing room number, room type, floor, occupancy, bed type and a clean or dirty housekeeping badge" width={1440} height={900} caption="The room board: every room by status, with its housekeeping state as a coloured badge." />

Every room has two separate states. The **status** is available, reserved, occupied or out of order. The **housekeeping state** is clean, dirty, cleaning in progress or inspected. Check-in and check-out change them for you, and a manager can set a room out of order or back to available, and clean, dirty or inspected, from the room itself. An out of order room is refused by the availability check, so it cannot be booked.

## The front desk dashboard

**Front Desk** opens the dashboard. It is the first screen the receptionist sees and refreshes on demand.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-hotel-management/front-desk-dashboard.webp" alt="Hotel front desk dashboard in Odoo with occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and revenue month to date at the top, today's arrivals, departures, in house guests and housekeeping tasks, room counts by status and the reservation pipeline by state" width={1440} height={660} caption="Front Desk: today's movements, rooms by state and the month's rate figures in one screen." />

What each figure means, as the module works it out:

- **Occupancy** is occupied rooms divided by all rooms, right now.
- **ADR** (average daily rate) is room revenue this month divided by room nights this month, taken from the room charge lines on folios.
- **RevPAR** is the same room revenue divided by all rooms times the days elapsed in the month.
- **Revenue (MTD)** adds every folio charge this month, rooms and extras, including tax.
- **Arrivals** are confirmed or checked in reservations arriving today; **departures** are checked in guests leaving today; **in house** is every checked in reservation.

<Callout title="How the month is counted">A stay's room charge is one folio line dated on the check-in day, with the number of nights as the quantity. So the dashboard puts a whole stay in the month the guest arrived. A guest who checks in on the 30th for five nights counts five room nights in that month and none in the next. For monthly reporting that has to split nights across months, use the accounting figures instead.</Callout>

## How to set it up in Odoo

1. Install Hotel Management PMS from Apps. Invoicing and Discuss install with it. Install a chart of accounts for your country before the first invoice, because the folio needs an income account.
2. In **Settings**, under the hotel section, set the default check-in and check-out times and decide whether a cleaning task should be raised automatically at check-out.
3. Create floors, then room types with their base rate and occupancy, then the rooms.
4. Create the chargeable services in **Configuration, Services** (breakfast, laundry, minibar, transfers, parking) with price, unit and sales tax. Tick **Recurring (Daily)** on anything charged every night, such as breakfast.
5. Give each user one of the three groups: Receptionist / Front Desk, Housekeeping or Hotel Manager.
6. Take one test reservation through confirm, check-in, extras, invoice and check-out, and look at the folio, the invoice and the housekeeping task it leaves behind.

## Limitations

These are what the code does not do, so plan around them:

- **No channel manager or OTA link.** Booking.com, Expedia and similar are recorded as a source, not connected.
- **No online booking engine.** There is no public availability search or booking page.
- **No rate plans or seasons.** A room has one nightly rate, set on the type or the room, and you change it on the booking line by hand.
- **No restaurant or bar posting to the room.** Point of Sale is not linked; extras are added to the folio by the front desk.
- **No night audit, city tax or deposit handling.** Room charges carry no tax unless you add it to the folio line, and deposits are taken in Invoicing.
- **Settings that do nothing yet.** The default check-in and check-out times are stored but not used, and the auto no-show switch does not stop the daily no-show job. The reservation confirmation email template exists but is not sent automatically.
