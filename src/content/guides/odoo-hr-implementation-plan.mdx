Most HR systems that go wrong go wrong in the order of the work, not in the software. Payroll is set up before the wages are agreed, so the first run is wrong. Employees are imported before the managers exist, so nobody's leave reaches anyone. Holidays are added after the leave allocation, so balances are off by a day. This chapter gives the order that works, a timetable you can hold a company owner to, and the foundation every later chapter rests on. It ends with the test script and checklist that decide whether you switch the old system off.

## The order of the work

Each step depends on the one before it. Departments need the company. Employees need departments, jobs and managers. Leave allocations need employees and leave types. Payroll needs employees, wages, bank accounts and the accounts finance wants to post to.

1. Company and working hours: name, address, currency, working week, timezone.
2. Settings: manual check-in or device, the salary journal.
3. Departments and job positions.
4. Public holidays.
5. Leave types and leave policies ([leave chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-leave-management/)).
6. Employees with managers, logins, bank accounts and wages ([import chapter](/odoo-guides/import-employees-into-odoo/)).
7. The year's leave entitlements, then opening balances from the old system.
8. Approval chains.
9. HR lists: letter templates, checklists, document types, expense, asset and service categories, shifts, overtime policy, appraisal period.
10. Payroll: structure, rates, rules, accounts, bank format, trusted bank accounts ([payroll chapter](/odoo-guides/run-payroll-odoo-community/)).
11. Attendance device, if any ([attendance chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-attendance-shifts-overtime/)).
12. Test, train, go live.

## A three week plan

| When | Work | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1, days 1 to 2 | Kick-off. Agree the decisions below. Collect staff list, ID numbers, joining dates, salaries, bank accounts, leave balances, holidays. | Owner, HR, Finance |
| Week 1, days 3 to 5 | Company, settings, departments, jobs, holidays, leave types and policies. Fill the onboarding workbook; first import on a test copy if you have one. | HR |
| Week 2, days 1 to 2 | Import for real. Check every manager and Time Off approver. Allocate the year, import opening balances. Approval chains. | HR |
| Week 2, days 3 to 4 | Payroll with Finance: structure, allowances, accounts, journal, bank format. Trust the bank accounts. Connect the attendance device. | HR, Finance, IT |
| Week 2, day 5 | Parallel payroll: last month in the system, compared line by line. | Payroll officer, Finance |
| Week 3, days 1 to 2 | Fix differences. Letters, checklists and other lists. Run the test script with real managers. | HR, two managers |
| Week 3, days 3 to 4 | Train managers (30 minutes) and staff (15 minutes). Send logins. | HR |
| Week 3, day 5 | Go live. The old process stops. HR watches the To Approve list closely for a week. | Everyone |

A company of 20 to 100 people fits this comfortably. For several hundred, allow an extra week for data cleaning.

### Who decides what

Most delays are a decision nobody has made. Put these on the kick-off agenda and write the answers down:

- **The owner:** working days, hours and timezone; the department structure and who heads each one.
- **The owner with HR:** the reporting line for every person; leave types, days, carry forward, encashment and probation rules; who approves which request and in what order; who gets a login; whether manual check-in is allowed.
- **The owner with Finance:** the salary structure (allowances as a percentage of basic or fixed, provident fund, overtime rate) and loan and advance limits.
- **Finance:** ledger accounts, the salary journal, the salary bank and its file format, and who trusts bank accounts.
- **IT:** the attendance device model, location and internet access.

### The data to collect

Ask in the first two days: company legal name, tax number, address and HR email; departments with their heads and parents; for every person their name, work email or mobile, department, job, manager, joining date, date of birth, gender, ID number, contacts and emergency contact; monthly basic salary and any personal allowances; salary bank and account number or IBAN; badge number on the attendance machine; the leave policy and each person's remaining balance on the go-live date; open loans and advances; and the last two months of payroll for the parallel run.

## The company foundation

Log in as the HR manager and open WT HRMS. On a new database My Space shows a **Set up HRMS** panel with eight steps. Each step ticks itself off when the data exists, so it doubles as your progress list, and the button under each step opens the right screen.

### Company details

Open **Settings, Users & Companies, Companies** and fill in the legal name, address, phone, email, website and tax number. Upload the logo: it prints on payslips and letters. Check the currency, then save. Set your own timezone too, under your name at the top right, **My Preferences**, because every user sees dates and check-in times in their own timezone.

The onboarding workbook fills these fields if you use it." />

### Working hours

The working schedule tells Odoo which hours count as work. Leave is deducted in days of this schedule, lateness is measured against it and public holidays are laid over it. In **Settings, Employees**, open the schedule under Company Working Hours. Give it a plain name such as "Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 17:30", set the timezone, and keep one morning line, one break line and one afternoon line for each working day. If shift workers keep different hours, make a second schedule and set it on those employees.

### Settings that change behaviour

**WT HRMS, Configuration, Settings** has a WT HRMS block at the top. The one that matters most is **Check in and out from My Space**: "Unless a device does it" shows the Check in button until a ZKTeco device is active and then hides it, "Always" keeps it, "Never" removes it. The same page holds Presence Display, the default Company Working Hours, and (in the Invoicing settings) the Default Salary Journal that payslips post to.

### Departments, job positions, holidays

Create departments top level first, then the ones under them, in **People, Departments**. Each has a manager, the head of department; leave it empty until the people exist. Odoo shows a department by its full path, such as Management / Production, and you will need that path when importing. Job positions live in **Employees, Configuration, Job Positions**; the workbook creates any job title it meets, so you only need this screen for jobs nobody holds yet.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-hr-implementation-plan/departments.webp" alt="Odoo departments list for the demo company with department name, manager, employee count and parent department such as Management / Production" width={1440} height={900} caption="Departments with their heads and parents." />

Public holidays go in the **Time Off** app, **Configuration, Public Holidays**. A one day holiday runs from 12:00 AM to 11:59 PM. Leave Working Hours empty so it applies to everyone. Holidays that move with the moon need updating when the dates are announced.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-hr-implementation-plan/public-holidays.webp" alt="Odoo public holidays list for 2026 with each holiday starting at 12:00 AM and ending at 11:59 PM" width={1440} height={900} caption="Each holiday should start at 12:00 AM. Check this after a workbook import." />

<Callout title="Holiday times after a workbook import">In this version the workbook stores holidays without converting from your timezone. Open the list after importing and read the Start Date column. If the holidays read 5:00 AM instead of 12:00 AM (as they do for Pakistan), open each one and set it back to 12:00 AM to 11:59 PM.</Callout>

### The quick route for a small company

**Help, Set up in ten minutes** does departments, a handful of people with logins and the approval chains in four short steps. It is safe to run more than once: what already exists is kept. Step two has an "I have a spreadsheet" button that takes you to the workbook instead.

## Testing and go-live

One afternoon of testing with real users saves weeks of complaints. Pick one department with a manager and two staff, and log in as each in turn (a private browser window per person is easiest). Do not test as the administrator. The script:

1. **Employee:** open My Space. Name, job, manager and leave balances are right.
2. **Employee:** check in and out, if manual check-in is on.
3. **Employee:** ask for two days of annual leave next week. It shows as Waiting.
4. **Manager:** the leave is under Waiting for your decision. Approve it. A two-step type moves to Second Approval.
5. **HR manager:** approve it. The employee's balance drops by two.
6. **Employee:** ask for a salary advance and a work from home day. Each reaches the approver its chain names.
7. **Manager:** reject the work from home day and send the reason as a message. The employee sees it under Rejected.
8. **Employee:** ask for more annual leave than allowed in one go. It is refused with a clear message.
9. **HR manager:** generate an appointment letter and print it. Name, job, date and company are filled in.
10. **Payroll officer:** run last month as a batch, without posting. Every payslip matches the old system.
11. **Payroll officer:** generate the bank file and send it to the bank as a test. The bank accepts the format.
12. **Employee:** open My Payslips. Only their own payslip is visible.
13. **Employee:** punch on the device, if there is one. Attendance appears within a minute.

### The parallel payroll

This is the test that matters most. Put last month's run beside the old payroll, person by person: basic, each allowance, gross, contributions, tax, loan and advance deductions, net. Every difference has a cause: a percentage set differently, a missing personal allowance, an outdated tax slab, a wage entered as gross instead of basic. Fix the cause, recompute, compare again until every net pay agrees, then cancel and delete the test batch. Staff forgive a slow leave approval. They do not forgive a wrong salary.

### Training

Managers need 30 minutes: My Space, the approval list, My Team and rejecting with a reason. Staff need 15 minutes in groups, ideally on their own phones: logging in, checking in, asking for leave and finding the payslip. The payroll officer needs two hours with Finance on the monthly run and the bank file. The [self service chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-employee-self-service/) is written so it can be handed to staff and managers.

### Go-live checklist

- Company details, logo, currency and timezone correct; schedule and the year's holidays checked.
- Every employee imported with department, job, manager, joining date, ID number, wage and badge.
- Time Off approver set on every employee; nobody under Employees With No Manager except the top person.
- Leave policies allocated, opening balances imported and approved, leave types set to the right approval, a chain for every request type.
- HR users have Employees, Time Off and Attendances rights; the payroll officer has accounting rights.
- Letter templates reworded, checklists adjusted, categories filled.
- Statutory rates confirmed for the current year, overtime rule added, salary journal, accounts and bank format set, bank accounts trusted.
- Parallel payroll agrees; device tested or manual check-in decided; logins handed out and people trained.
- Old forms and spreadsheets withdrawn on the go-live date, with a notice published on My Space.
