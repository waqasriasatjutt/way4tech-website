A bus of forty men lands at midnight and the camp boss has a spreadsheet that says there are twelve free beds, except two of them are broken bunks and one is held for a gang arriving Thursday. Three of the men already in camp have visas that ran out last week and are still being sent to site. At month end the main contractor disputes the accommodation invoice because nobody can say how many nights his men actually slept. Labour camp management is mostly this: occupancy you can trust, papers before they bite, and a bill that stands up. This guide covers the UAE and Saudi rules an inspector checks, then shows how to run a camp in Odoo with our Labour Camp Management module.

## The rules a camp is held to

### UAE

The [UAE government portal](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/Sector-of-employment/employment-in-the-private-sector/labour-accommodation) lists the instruments: Ministerial Resolution No. 44 of 2022 on occupational health, safety and labour accommodation, Cabinet Resolution No. 13 of 2009 on collective labour housing, Ministerial Decree No. 212 of 2014 for group housing under 500 workers, and Administrative Decision No. 19 of 2023. The headline requirements it gives:

| Requirement | UAE rule |
|---|---|
| Who must house workers | Establishments with 50 or more workers earning AED 1,500 a month or less |
| Space per person | Not less than 3 square metres |
| People per bedroom | 8 to 10, provided each keeps the minimum space |
| Registration | MoHRE Labour Accommodation System, kept accurate and up to date |
| Air conditioning | Central AC, or individual units where there is none; relative humidity 30% to 60% |
| Toilets and washing | One toilet per 8 people (at least two per shared bathroom), one urinal per 25, one bathing place and one basin per 8, hot and cold water |
| Medical | A first aid room with a nurse, plus a quarantine room |
| Location | At least 5 km from family housing areas |

Kitchens must be run by a licensed company or the unit's own food staff, with gas cylinders kept outside and out of the sun.

### Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Municipalities and Housing set a framework for group housing in 2025. As [Gulf News reported](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-introduces-new-rules-for-shared-housing-capping-capacity-and-boosting-safety-1.500230066), it requires at least **4 square metres per resident**, **no more than 10 occupants per room**, prior approvals for buildings and safety and health plans, and a Saudi national overseeing operations in residential buildings. It splits housing into residential buildings, large residential complexes and mobile cabins. Licensing runs through the Balady platform; check the current conditions there for your city before you rely on any single figure.

Other GCC states have their own rules, and municipalities add licence conditions of their own. The licence on your wall is the authority on headcount, which is why the module enforces it.

## What standard Odoo does

Standard Odoo, Community or Enterprise, has no labour camp app. People try three things:

- **Employees plus a spreadsheet.** HR holds the men, the spreadsheet holds the beds. They drift apart in a week.
- **Rental or property modules.** They think in units and tenants, not in beds, gangs and man nights, and they have no idea of a labour card or a medical fitness date.
- **Field Service or Maintenance.** Useful for repairs, no help with occupancy.

Odoo does give you the parts that matter underneath: contacts for contractors, Invoicing for the bill, HR for the employee record, and the chatter for an audit trail. The [HR overview](/modules/hr/) and [accounting overview](/modules/accounting/) cover what ships.

## How to set it up in Odoo

[Labour Camp Management](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_labour_camp) (USD 200) runs on Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise. It needs Invoicing, which ships with Community; HR is optional.

1. **Install and set the rules.** Install Labour Camp from Apps, then open **Labour Camp, Configuration, Settings**. Set how many days before expiry a man shows as expiring (30 by default), keep **Refuse to Exceed the Licence** ticked, set the complaint answer time (48 hours by default) and choose the product accommodation charges are invoiced under.
2. **Create the camp.** **Configuration, Camps**: name, licence and **licensed capacity**, then the blocks underneath it.
3. **Add rooms and beds.** **Accommodation, Rooms**: block, room type, capacity, floor area and AC units. Press **Create Beds** to make the beds from the capacity, numbered so a bunk can be named out loud. The room shows square metres per bed, the figure an inspector asks for.
4. **Register the men.** **People, Occupants**: employer, trade, and the expiry of visa, Emirates ID, labour card and medical fitness. Passport and ID numbers sit behind their own access right. Link to the HR employee if you run HR.
5. **Allocate the intake.** Open one man, press **Allocate a Bed**, add the rest of the busload to the Men field, and name a block or room to keep a gang together. Tick Check Them In for arrivals tonight, or leave it to reserve. The wizard shows beds needed against beds free and refuses two men in one bed or a camp over its licence.
6. **Set up billing.** **Billing, Agreements**: one per contractor, billed **Per Bed Held**, **Per Man Accommodated** or **Per Man Night**, with the rate and, for per bed, the beds reserved. Press Start.
7. **Run month end.** Turn on the scheduled action "Camp: draft last month's accommodation charges" (it ships switched off). On the first of the month it drafts a charge per agreement from the allocations. Check it under **Billing, Monthly Charges**, Approve, then **Raise the Invoice**.
8. **Services.** Configure **Mess and Catering** and **Transport Routes**, then record **Meal Headcount**, **Bus Runs** and **Utilities** per block.
9. **Welfare and compliance.** Under **Welfare**: Permits and Certificates with expiry, Inspections from your own checklists, Complaints, Incidents and the Gate Register.
10. **Watch the boards.** **Dashboards, Camp Board** for the camp boss and **Compliance and Cost** for the compliance officer.

### The camp board

The board answers the morning questions: whose papers have expired, which complaints are past the time the men were promised, who is booked in without a bed, how many beds are free once blocked ones are left out, and whether any camp is over licence. Every figure opens the records behind it.

### Papers falling due

A man with an expired visa or labour card should not be on site. Each occupant carries one papers status worked out from all four documents, and a daily job lists those inside the warning window.

### Billing the contractor

Per bed held suits a main contractor reserving a block whether it is full or not. Per man night is the fairest and needs reliable check in and check out, which the allocations give you.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-labour-camp-management/monthly-charges.webp" alt="Monthly Charges list with two agreements for August, showing the contractor billed, number of men, man nights, the amount to bill and a Draft or Invoiced status, with the total at the bottom" width={1280} height={800} caption="Monthly charges worked out from men and man nights, then invoiced." />

| Basis | Billed on | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Per Bed Held | Beds reserved on the agreement, slept in or not | A main contractor holding a block |
| Per Man Accommodated | Men on the agreement in the month | Simple monthly rates |
| Per Man Night | Nights actually slept | Mixed or short stays, disputed bills |

## Limitations

Most of these come from the module's own description, and we would rather you read them before you buy:

- **It files nothing with an authority.** No labour accommodation regulator publishes an interface. MoHRE's Labour Accommodation System and Balady are still updated by you; the module holds the permits, expiries and certificates an inspector asks for.
- **There is no tenant or worker app.** Complaints and gate entries are recorded by camp staff in the back office.
- **It is not payroll.** Wages go through your payroll and, in the UAE, through WPS; see the [WPS SIF guide](/odoo-guides/uae-wps-sif-file-odoo/).
- **It does not read gate or attendance hardware.** If you want biometric punches, the [ZKTeco ADMS guide](/odoo-guides/zkteco-adms-odoo/) covers getting them into Odoo.
- **Area per bed is shown, not enforced.** The licensed headcount is enforced; the space rule is displayed for you to act on.
- **It does not raise maintenance work orders.** A complaint records the problem and its resolution; the repair to a bunk or an AC unit belongs in a maintenance system such as Facility Management CAFM, covered in the [CAFM guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-cafm-facility-maintenance/).
