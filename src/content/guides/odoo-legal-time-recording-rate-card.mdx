Time is the stock a law firm sells, and most of the loss happens before a bill is drafted. A call is not recorded, or it is recorded on Friday from memory as "attendance". A senior associate's hour goes out at last year's rate because nobody updated a spreadsheet. A partner writes off three thousand pounds of time to keep a client happy, and the realisation report never shows why. The fix is dull and specific: record as you go, round to an agreed unit, price from a rate card that keeps its history, and make every write-off say who and why. This chapter covers how the Law Firm Management module does each of those.

## Set up fee earners and the rate card first

**Legal, Configuration, Fee Earners** holds one record per person who records time. Link each to their Odoo user, so time entries default to them and activities reach them. Fill in:

- **Initials**, which appear on bill lines and in the default our-reference on a matter.
- **Grade** and **Supervised By**. The supervisor is who a large write-off is referred to. A chain that loops back to the same person is refused.
- **Practice Areas** they work in.
- **Standard Rate**, the last-resort charge-out rate.
- **Weekly Chargeable Target** in hours, which drives the utilisation figure.

The form shows **Hours This Week** (billable time from Monday, excluding written-off entries), **Utilisation** against the target, and **WIP** at that person's rates.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-legal-time-recording-rate-card/fee-earner.webp" alt="Odoo fee earner form for a senior associate with initials, grade, linked login, supervisor, practice areas, standard rate, weekly chargeable target, utilisation, WIP and two rate card lines" width={1440} height={900} caption="A demo fee earner with a department rate for Civil Litigation and a general rate." />

The **Rate Card** (Configuration, Rate Card, or the tab on the fee earner) holds dated lines. Each line has the fee earner, an optional practice area, an optional client under **Agreed With**, the rate, a start date and an optional end date. When a time entry is priced, the module looks for a line valid on the date the work was done, most specific first:

1. agreed with this client, for this practice area
2. agreed with this client, any area
3. this fee earner, this practice area
4. this fee earner, any area
5. the practice area's default hourly rate
6. the fee earner's standard rate

Two lines for the same person, area and client that overlap in dates are refused, because nobody could say afterwards which one the client should have paid. To change a rate, set an end date on the old line and add a new one. The old line stays, so a bill raised today for work done last year still comes out at last year's rate.

## Recording time

Fee earners work in **Legal, Time and Costs, Time Recording**. The list opens on their own time for the current month. Each entry carries the matter, fee earner, date, an activity code (attendance, drafting, research, telephone, correspondence, reviewing documents, at court, travel and waiting, negotiation, file administration), the narrative, hours, rate, amount, written-off value and what is left to bill.

There are two ways to capture hours:

- **Timer.** Create the entry, click **Start Timer**, and **Stop Timer** when done. Stopping rounds up to the firm's unit (6 minutes by default, set in Legal settings), with a minimum of one unit, so a two minute call is charged as six minutes. A fee earner can have only one timer running; starting a second is refused so the same minutes are not charged twice.
- **Typed.** Enter the hours directly. They are kept as typed.

The rate fills in from the rate card when the matter, fee earner and date are set. Click **Record** to commit the entry as work in progress. Recording is refused if the timer is still running, if there are no hours, or if the matter is not open or on hold.

### The rules on an entry

These are checked when the entry is recorded, not while it is a draft:

- **Narrative.** A chargeable entry needs a narrative of at least twelve characters. "Attendance" alone is refused; the client has to be able to read what was done.
- **Non-billable time.** Untick billable and pick a reason: internal work, file administration, training, dealing with a complaint, business development, or courtesy to the client.
- **No time on a closed matter.**

Two more rules apply at all times: an entry cannot be dated in the future, and one entry cannot hold more than 24 hours.

Once an entry is on a bill it is locked. Hours, rate, matter, date, fee earner, narrative and the billable flag cannot be changed, and it cannot be deleted. To change it, the bill has to go back to draft first, and once a bill is delivered the route is a credit note.

### Timers left running

The **Legal: stop timers left running** job runs every four hours. Any timer running for more than ten hours is stopped, the entry is capped at eight hours, put back to draft, and a note on it tells the fee earner to check the time before recording it.

## Write-offs and write-downs

Both buttons sit on the time entry and both need a **reason** from a fixed list: work took longer than agreed, over the agreed cap, client goodwill, trainee learning time, duplicated work, our error, not recoverable from the client, or other.

- **Write Off** takes the whole entry off. It becomes Written Off, its full value goes into the written-off column, and the matter chatter records the hours and the reason. It is refused on a billed entry.
- **Write Down** reduces what will be billed and keeps what was recorded. Type the amount coming off in **Written Off**, pick the reason and click the button. It is refused if the amount is the whole entry (use Write Off) or if the entry is already billed.

Any write-off or write-down over the firm's **Write-off Limit** (250 by default) is refused unless the user is in the Supervising Partner group, and the message names the person to ask: the fee earner's supervisor, then the matter's supervising partner. The same check applies when somebody types a figure straight into the Written Off field, so the list view is not a way round it. The entry records who wrote it off.

<Callout title="Written-off value stays visible">A write-off does not delete anything. The recorded amount, the written-off amount and the reason stay on the entry, and roll up to the matter, the bill and the Realisation report. That is deliberate: realisation is only honest if the write-offs that explain it are on the record.</Callout>

## Work in progress

**Legal, Reporting, Work in Progress** is a pivot of time entries by fee earner and practice area, with hours, amount, written off and to bill. The time list also has a graph of hours by week. On each matter, **WIP** is billable recorded time not yet on a bill plus billable recorded disbursements, and **Time Recorded**, **Written Off Amount** and **Realisation (%)** (fees billed as a percentage of time recorded) sit beside it.

A weekly job, **Legal: chase unbilled work in progress**, raises an activity on the responsible fee earner for any open file whose oldest unbilled time is older than the lock-up target. It ships switched off.
