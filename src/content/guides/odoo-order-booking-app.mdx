An order booker in FMCG visits 30 to 60 shops a day. Each visit is short: look at the shelf, ask what the shop needs, write the order, move on. Paper order books make that fast at the shop and slow everywhere else. Someone types the orders in at night, prices are wrong because last month's sheet was used, and nobody can tell which shops were visited and did not order. The phone app turns each visit into an Odoo record at the moment it happens.

This chapter follows one salesman's visit. It is part of the [FMCG distribution guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-distribution-management/). The Order Taker app in Base and Van Sales and the Enterprise field app work the same way; where Enterprise adds a step, it is marked.

## Before the first visit: the day and the route

In Enterprise, the home screen opens with **Start my day**. Once on duty, the phone records visits and position until **End my day**; position is only recorded while on duty. Lower on the home screen the salesman sees sales, units, orders and shops against this month's target, what is left, and where he should be by today.

Tapping the route shows shops to visit, orders, no-orders and sales so far. **Next stop** says where to go. Each shop in the list shows what it owes, its credit band (Enterprise) and buttons to call, message on WhatsApp or navigate in the phone's map app.

In Enterprise, **More tools, Best order** reorders today's shops from where the salesman is, or from the depot, around opening hours, and shows the kilometres saved. It works offline.

## At the shop

Opening a shop checks the salesman in, with the time and GPS position. The shop card shows what it owes, its credit limit, its last order, the credit band and the field note from the office. From here he can record **No order today**, take a **Shelf photo**, run a **Store check** (Enterprise), collect older dues or start the order.

### The suggested order (Enterprise)

The suggested order is the fastest way to a sensible order. For every product the shop has bought, the app measures how many it sells a day from its confirmed orders over the last **Sales History** days (60 on the demo). It multiplies that by the days until the next visit plus the **Spare Stock** days, takes off what the salesman counts on the shelf, and rounds to cartons where a carton unit exists.

Each line says why, for example "sells about 1.2 a week, next visit in 3 days, plus 1 day spare". The salesman types what is still on the shelf to lower a line, then **Use suggestion** fills the cart. Because every quantity has a reason, he can explain it to the shop owner instead of guessing. The calculation runs on the phone from the saved copy of the day, so it works offline too.

### The catalogue

Focus products come first, with the message the office set. Each product shows the price, the carton price and stock. The salesman can filter by category, focus or the shop's **usual order**, search, or scan a barcode with the camera. In Base, a stepper on every product shows "usual 11" for what this shop normally takes.

In Enterprise, the brand row filters the catalogue to one brand, and any line can switch between **Unit** and **Carton**. Scanning a carton barcode adds a carton, and receipts print "2 Carton of 24 (48 Units)".

### Review and confirm

The review screen lists each line with quantity and a discount field. The salesman can give a line or order discount up to the allowed maximum and add a note for the warehouse. Totals include tax as Odoo computes it. **Save draft** keeps it open; **Confirm order** sends it. Promotions that apply are added automatically (see [promotions and claims](/odoo-guides/odoo-trade-promotions-principal-claims/)).

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-order-booking-app/o06-review.webp" alt="Order review for Sweet Tooth Confectionery with three lines, Cola 1.5L, Chocolate Bar 50g and Chewing Gum, each with unit, quantity stepper and discount field, and Save draft and Confirm order buttons" width={800} height={1720} caption="Review: quantities, discounts within the limit, then confirm." />

Two rules stop bad orders here. A discount above the Maximum Discount setting is refused. A credit order that would take the shop over its credit limit, counting orders not yet invoiced, is refused when Enforce Credit Limit is on.

Once placed, the order is in Odoo at once with its number and total. The salesman can share the confirmation on WhatsApp, print a receipt or go to the next stop. If Confirm Orders Immediately is off, the order waits as a quotation for the office to check.

## When the shop does not order

A visit without an order is still information. The salesman picks a reason: still has stock, shop closed, owner not available, credit hold, price objection, buying from a competitor, or other with a note. The visit counts toward the strike rate either way. Ten "price objection" visits on one route in a month is a pricing problem the manager can now see in Visit Analysis.

## New shops, orders and follow up

- **A new shop on the road.** **+ Shop** on the route takes the name, type, phone, address and notes, and saves the GPS position. The shop joins the route and the visit starts. In Enterprise the salesman can add the owner's name and phone and photos of the trade licence and ID, and a new shop buys cash only until a manager approves it.
- **My orders.** The Orders tab filters by draft, confirmed or cancelled and by today, 7 or 30 days. A draft can be edited, sent or cancelled; **Reorder** copies an old order into a new cart.
- **Search.** Finds any shop on the salesman's routes by name, code or phone, for a shop that calls in outside its day.
- **WhatsApp reorder link** (Enterprise). The WhatsApp button sends the shop its own reorder page with its usual products, quantities to change and one Send button, no login. It stops working after the Reorder Link Lifetime (7 days on the demo). What the shop sends lands in More tools, Shop orders for the salesman to confirm.
- **Stock.** What the warehouse can supply, with low and out of stock products, before promising a shop.

## Working without signal (Enterprise)

Order bookers in markets and basements lose signal all day. In the Enterprise field app, routes, shops, prices and stock are kept as a saved copy on the phone. Check-ins, no-orders, photos, orders, collections, returns, store checks, promises and Ask manager requests are saved at once, each with its own id, and sent oldest first when the signal comes back. A failure is retried, never lost, and nothing is saved twice. If the server sees something different from the phone, such as a new price or a credit limit reached, the salesman sees it under Check what changed.

At the end of the day, **My sales today** shows sales, cash and bank collected, credit sales, debts collected and returns, and **End my day** stops tracking.
