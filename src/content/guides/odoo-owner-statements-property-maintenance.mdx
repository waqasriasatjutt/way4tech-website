An owner who hands a property to an agency to manage wants one thing at the end of each month: a statement that says what rent came in, what the agency kept, what was spent on the flat, and the figure that will land in his account. If that statement is built from what was planned rather than what happened, it will not match his bank, and the agency spends the next week explaining the difference. The usual culprits are a cheque that was counted but bounced, and a plumber's bill that was paid but never charged back. This chapter covers both halves in the Real Estate Management Enterprise add-on for Odoo 19: maintenance requests that know who pays, and owner statements built from cleared cheques and finished work only.

## Maintenance requests

Open **Management, Maintenance**. A request is one fault on one property.

**What and where.** The property is required. When you pick it, the add-on looks for an active rent or lease agreement on that property and fills in the tenant as **Reported By** and the lease, so you do not have to look them up. The owner comes from the property and cannot drift from it. Add a **Category** (Plumbing, Electrical, Air Conditioning, Appliances and General / Handyman ship with the add-on; add your own under Configuration) and a **Priority** from low to urgent.

**The fault and the work.** The **Fault** tab holds what was reported and is required. The **Work Done** tab is where the technician or the office writes what was actually done.

**Handling.** Reported date, a scheduled date, the member of staff it is **Assigned To** and, if the job is sublet, the outside **Contractor**.

**Cost and who pays.** Enter the **Cost** and choose **Bill To**: Owner, Tenant, or Agency (absorbed). Only owner-billed costs reach the owner statement.

### The request board

Requests move through **New**, **Assigned**, **In Progress** and **Done**, with Cancelled to the side. Assign is refused until the request has a person or a contractor on it. Mark Done stamps the completion date, and that date decides which owner statement the cost lands on. The board opens on the **Open** filter, and there are filters for Urgent, Unassigned and Billed to Owner, with grouping by property, category or who is billed.

On each lease, a **Maintenance** button and tab list the requests raised against it, and the portfolio dashboard counts open and urgent requests.

## Building an owner statement

Open **Management, Owner Statements** and create one. Pick the **Owner**, the period **From** and **To** (it defaults to the first of this month to today), and check the **Management Fee (%)**, which defaults from the company. Then press **Build / Rebuild**.

The add-on fills the lines from two sources:

- **Rent collected**: every cheque for this owner with purpose Rent, status Cleared and a clear date inside the period. Each line names the property and the cheque number.
- **Maintenance**: every request on this owner's properties that is billed to the owner, marked done with a completion date inside the period, and has a cost above zero.

From the lines it works out:

| Figure | How |
|---|---|
| Rent Collected | Sum of the rent lines |
| Management Fee | Rent collected times the fee percentage |
| Maintenance Total | Sum of the maintenance lines |
| Other Deductions | Typed in by you |
| Net Payable | Rent collected less fee, maintenance and other deductions |

Because the statement only counts cleared cheques, a cheque that bounced or is still sitting in the drawer does not make the owner look richer than his bank says. Because it only counts finished, owner-billed repairs, a job still in progress waits for next month.

### Confirm, pay and print

1. Check the lines. If something is missing, fix the source (clear the cheque, finish the request) and press **Build / Rebuild** again. Rebuilding is allowed only in draft.
2. Press **Confirm**. Confirmed statements show on the dashboard as statements to pay, and their net payable adds up to the **Owed to owners** figure.
3. Transfer the money from your bank as usual, type the **Payout Reference**, and press **Mark Paid**. The payout date is stamped.
4. Press **Print** for a PDF statement with the owner, period, the rent collected lines, the maintenance charged to the owner, the totals and, once paid, the payout date.

Statements get references like OST/2026/0001 and can be filtered by draft, to pay and paid, and grouped by owner.

<Callout title="Limits on the money side">The statement does not post anything to the accounts: there is no vendor bill to the owner, no payment and no fee income entry. Tenant-billed and agency-absorbed repairs are recorded on the request but not invoiced for you. Build / Rebuild deletes the lines and makes them again, so a line you added by hand is lost on rebuild; put a one-off charge in Other Deductions instead. The statement is in the company currency, and there is no owner portal, so the PDF is what you send.</Callout>

## A month-end routine

1. Make sure every cheque that cleared this month is marked cleared, with the [cheque register](/odoo-guides/odoo-post-dated-cheques-rent/).
2. Mark finished maintenance done and check the cost and Bill To on each one; the **Billed to Owner** filter helps.
3. Create one statement per owner for the month and press Build / Rebuild.
4. Review, confirm, pay, mark paid and print.
5. Open the dashboard: **Owed to owners** should be back to zero once every statement is paid.
