A project board earns its place when anyone in the team can open it and answer three questions in ten seconds: what is stuck, who has it, and what is due this week. In standard Odoo those answers are spread across a kanban, a list and the task forms. This chapter sets up a board in Project Pro that puts them on one screen, then covers the two planning views that a Community server does not have on its own: a timeline and a workload grid.

Everything here happens under the **Work** menu. If you have not read the [hub page](/odoo-guides/odoo-project-management/), the short version is that a project has one board, each row is an item and each column is a fact about it.

## Step 1: create the board

On Home, press **New project board**. The same button is on Work, Boards. One dialog creates the project, the board, its statuses and columns, and switches on the recommended rules.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-project-boards-timeline-workload/new-board.webp" alt="New Project Board dialog in Odoo with Start a new project selected, project name Spring Campaign, Marketing Campaign template chosen and its statuses Idea, Planned, In Production, Live and Done listed" width={1400} height={875} caption="Choosing a template shows its statuses before you commit." />

1. Under **What**, leave **Start a new project** selected. Choose **Add a board to an existing project** only when the project is already in Odoo.
2. Type a **Project Name** the team will recognise, such as "Spring Campaign" or "Villa 12 Fit-out".
3. Pick the **Project Manager**. Automations report to this person, so pick whoever should hear about finished and stuck work.
4. Pick a **Board Template**. The grey box under the field describes it and lists its statuses in order. Leave it empty for the four starter statuses: Not Started, Working on it, Stuck and Done.
5. Keep **Switch on the recommended automations** ticked. For this project only, it tells people when they are put on an item, tells the manager when something is done, and raises a flag when something is stuck.
6. Press **Create and open the board**.

Projects that existed before the module was installed already have a board with the starter columns. To set them all up at once, use **Set up a board for every project** on Work, Boards.

## Picking a template

Work, Templates lists eight boards that ship with the app. Each brings its own statuses and columns, so the choice is mostly about which lanes match the way your work flows.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-project-boards-timeline-workload/templates.webp" alt="Board Templates screen in Odoo with eight cards: Sprint Board, Client Projects, Marketing Campaign, Construction Site, Recruitment Pipeline, Bug Tracker, Event Planning and Employee Onboarding, each listing its statuses" width={1400} height={875} caption="The eight templates, each with its statuses listed in order." />

- **Sprint Board:** Backlog, To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done, with story points that add up per status and a Sprint column.
- **Client Projects:** a Waiting on Client status so delays are visible, a budget per item and an Invoiced tick for month end.
- **Marketing Campaign:** Idea, Planned, In Production, Live, Done, with channel, budget against spend and launch date.
- **Construction Site:** Mobilised, In Progress, Inspection and On Hold before Complete, with a permit tick and cost per item.
- **Recruitment Pipeline:** one item per candidate from Sourced to Hired or Rejected, with a score that averages per status.
- **Bug Tracker:** severity, component and the version a fix shipped in. Closed and Won't Fix both count as done.
- **Event Planning:** one line per vendor with the cost and whether the deposit is paid.
- **Employee Onboarding:** a checklist per new starter with department, buddy and start date.

Once a board works the way you like, open **Board settings** on it and press **Save as Template**. Columns, options and colours are copied; items are not.

## Step 2: put work on the board

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-project-boards-timeline-workload/board.webp" alt="Website Relaunch board in Project Pro with coloured lanes for Backlog, To Do, In Progress, In Review and Done, columns for people, due date, priority, hours, story points, sprint, type and progress, and totals at the bottom of each lane" width={1400} height={875} caption="Each coloured lane is a status, each row an item. Totals sit at the bottom of every lane." />

Press **Add item** at the top right, the **+ Add item** line at the bottom of a lane, or the small **+** at the end of a lane title. Type a short name, such as "Write the pricing page copy", and press Enter. The cursor stays ready for the next one, so a list of ten items takes a minute. Keep one outcome per item: "Homepage" is a project, "Write the homepage hero text" is an item.

To move an item, drag the row by its name into another lane, or click its status pill and pick a status from the coloured menu. The lane totals update as you go.

To see everything about one item, click the expand icon next to its name. A dialog shows every column with a large status at the top. **Edit details** opens a compact form, and **Comments, files, subtasks** opens the full task with its conversation and attachments.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-project-boards-timeline-workload/item.webp" alt="Item dialog in Project Pro for the task Write the pricing page copy, showing project, board status, assignees, priority, deadline, allocated time, tags, customer and a description tab" width={1400} height={875} caption="One item on one card, with a link to the full task." />

When a sprint ends or a colleague goes on leave, tick the box on the left of several rows. A bar appears with **Status**, **Assign**, **Delete** and **Clear**, acting on every ticked row at once.

## Step 3: fill in and add columns

There is no Save button on the board. Click a cell, change it, click away, and the change is kept.

| Column | What it holds | How you edit it |
|---|---|---|
| Status | Where the item stands | Click the pill, or drag the row |
| People | Who does the work, one or more | Type a name and pick it |
| Due | The deadline, shown red when overdue | Pick a date |
| Priority | Low, Medium, High or Urgent | Click the stars |
| Timeline | Planned start and end | Set two dates in the small panel |
| Progress | Percent complete | Type a number, press Enter |
| Notes and Tags | Short text and your own labels | Type, or pick and create |

Project administrators can add their own. Press **Add column** at the top left or the **+** at the far right of the header row, give it a name and a type (status, dropdown, people, date, timeline, number, progress, checkbox, text or priority), add options with colours for status and dropdown columns, and choose whether a number column shows a lane total. It appears on every row of that board immediately. The **Columns** button hides columns you do not use and changes their order. A board with five columns is read faster than one with twelve, so hide before you add.

## Step 4: give the work to people

An item with nobody on it does not get done. Click the People cell and pick one or more names. The person is told at once in the Odoo inbox, and by email or phone push if they chose those. From then on they hear when the item changes status, moves, gets a comment, or when someone mentions them with @name. Nobody is told about their own actions. How each person chooses what reaches them is in the [approvals and automation chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-project-approvals-automation/).

## Step 5: plan with Timeline and Workload

Four screens answer four questions. **Home** answers "what do I do today". **All Items** lists every item across projects, with filters for mine, overdue and unassigned. The other two are the planning views.

**Timeline** draws each item as a bar from planned start to due date, coloured by status, with day, week and month scales and grouping by assignee. Drag the end of a bar to move a deadline.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-project-boards-timeline-workload/timeline.webp" alt="Timeline view in Project Pro for the week of 24 to 30 August 2026, items grouped by assignee as coloured bars with a red line marking today" width={1400} height={875} caption="Timeline grouped by assignee. Colours follow the status." />

**Workload** shows hours per person per week against their capacity, with a team total at the bottom and a line at the top that says when nobody is over capacity. Move work away from anyone shown over capacity before the week starts, not after.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-project-boards-timeline-workload/workload.webp" alt="Workload view in Project Pro showing hours per person per week from 24 August to 18 October 2026 with percentage of capacity and a team total of hours against 200 hours per week" width={1400} height={875} caption="Workload: hours per person per week, and the team total against capacity." />

<Callout title="How Workload counts, and what it does not">Hours are spread evenly over a task's working days, a task with several assignees is split between them, and capacity is a flat eight hours per working day. A task with no estimated hours counts as one day of work. It does not read leave, public holidays or part-time contracts, so a person on leave still shows full capacity. Fill in Allocated Time on items, or the grid will undercount.</Callout>

Every board also has a list view, from the icon at its top right, for sorting, exporting to Excel and printing.

## On the phone

The whole app works in the phone browser. To get an icon on the home screen, open the same address in Chrome on Android and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**, or in Safari on iPhone use the share icon and **Add to Home Screen**. Open it once from the icon and allow notifications. On a phone a board shows one card per item; tap a card to open it, tap the status to change it, and tap **Edit details** for the rest.

## If something looks wrong

- **The Project Pro icon is missing.** The user has no Project rights. The administrator sets Project to User or Administrator on that user.
- **A project has no board, or the board is empty.** It was created outside Project Pro. Use **Set up a board for every project**.
- **You cannot add a column.** Adding columns, rules and templates needs the Administrator level of Project.
- **"The page appears to be out of date."** The app was updated while you were in it. Refresh once.
