Hiring is where most small companies lose track first. The job goes out as a picture on WhatsApp, CVs arrive in three inboxes and a personal phone, the interview notes are on paper, and the offer is a Word file somebody edits from the last one. Three weeks later nobody can say who was interviewed, what salary was offered or whether the candidate ever said yes. When the person finally joins, HR types their name, salary and start date again into the HR system, and the laptop is not ready on day one because nobody told IT.

Odoo 19 Community already covers most of the hiring path for free: a careers page on your Odoo website, an apply form, an applications board with stages, interview scheduling and refusal emails. This chapter shows that path end to end on the sample company used across this guide, Crescent Textiles in Lahore, hiring one Accounts Officer. It also shows the three things Community leaves out and the Recruitment Plus module adds: one apply link with share buttons, an offer letter the candidate accepts or declines online, and a hire that fills the employee record and starts onboarding by itself.

## What you need installed

| Module | Where it comes from | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| Recruitment (`hr_recruitment`) | Odoo Community | Job positions, the applications board, stages, refusals, talent pool |
| Online Jobs (`website_hr_recruitment`) | Odoo Community | The careers page at `/jobs` and the apply form |
| Interview Forms (`hr_recruitment_survey`) | Odoo Community | Interview scorecards and the Send Interview questionnaire |
| Recruitment Plus (`wt_hrms_recruitment_plus`) | Way4Tech, USD 39 | Share links, the Offer tab, offer letter PDF, online Accept or Decline, hire to onboarding |

Online Jobs installs the Odoo Website app if you do not have it yet. Set the website's name, logo, phone and email once under **Website, Configuration**, because the careers page and the candidate's offer page both use them.

<Callout title="Two recruitment modules, pick one">The WT HRMS Suite includes a separate module, HRMS Recruitment (`wt_hrms_recruitment`), with its own vacancies, candidates, interviews and offers inside WT HRMS. It has no public careers page and no online acceptance. This chapter uses Odoo's own Recruitment app with Recruitment Plus on top. Recruitment Plus is not listed on the Odoo Apps Store yet; ask for it if you want this flow.</Callout>

## Step 1: Set up the job position

1. Open **Recruitment**. Each card is a job position, with how many people you want to recruit, new applications and applications in progress.
2. Click **New**, or open an existing position, and fill in the title and **Department**.
3. On the **Details** tab set the **Recruiter** (the HR person who owns the hiring), the **Interviewers** (usually the hiring manager), the **Interview Form** and the **Target**, the number of people you want.
4. Write the text candidates will read. Click **Go to Website**, then **Edit** on the job page, type the description, and **Save**.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-recruitment-job-offer-online-acceptance/job-position-apply-link.webp" alt="Accounts Officer job position in Odoo Recruitment with the Careers page switch on, the apply link to share with a copy button, share buttons for LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Facebook and X, and the recruiter, interviewer and interview form below" width={1440} height={900} caption="The job position. Recruitment Plus adds the Careers page switch, the apply link and the share buttons at the top." />

Interviewers only see the applications of the jobs they are named on, so naming the manager here is what lets them read CVs without giving them all of HR.

A simple interview scorecard is worth building once before the first interview. In **Recruitment, Configuration, Interview Forms**, make a form with a few rated questions (technical skills, communication, attitude and team fit), a recommendation (hire, second interview, do not hire) and a free text box for strengths and concerns. Pick it as the Interview Form on every job.

## Step 2: Publish it and share one link

Switch **Careers page** on. The job is now on your careers page and the form shows the **apply link**. Click the copy icon to paste the link into a job board, an email or a LinkedIn post, or click **LinkedIn**, **WhatsApp**, **Facebook** or **X** to open that site's share window with the text "We are hiring" and the link already in it. **Open page** shows you what the candidate will see.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-recruitment-job-offer-online-acceptance/careers-page.webp" alt="Crescent Textiles careers page on the Odoo website listing Machine Operator, Sales Executive and Accounts Officer with open positions, location and department" width={940} height={900} caption="The careers page at /jobs: every published position, with the number of openings, location and department." />

The candidate reads the job, clicks **Apply Now** and fills in a short form: name, email, phone, LinkedIn profile, a CV and an optional introduction. Most people apply from a phone, and the form works on one. After **Submit Application** they see a thank you page and the application appears on the board straight away, in the first column.

<Callout title="Closing a job">When the position is filled, switch Careers page off. The job disappears from the careers page and the link stops taking applications. Applications already received stay where they are. The share buttons refuse to work while the job is unpublished, so nobody shares a dead link by mistake.</Callout>

## Step 3: Work the applications board

Click **Applications** on the job card. Each application is a card and each column is a step: New, Qualification, First Interview, Second Interview, Contract Proposal and Contract Signed. Drag a card to the next column as the candidate moves forward. Stars on the card are your own evaluation.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-recruitment-job-offer-online-acceptance/applications-pipeline.webp" alt="Odoo applications kanban for Accounts Officer with columns New, Qualification, First Interview and Second Interview, each card showing the candidate name and star evaluation" width={1440} height={900} caption="The applications board for one job. Drag cards between columns; the stars are HR's evaluation." />

Open a card to see the contact details, the CV, notes and the full history. Along the top:

- **No Meeting** opens the calendar. Pick a slot, add the interviewer as an attendee and save: the candidate and the interviewer both get an invitation, and the meeting shows on the card.
- **Send Interview** emails the candidate a short online questionnaire with a deadline. Use it as a first screening before you meet.
- **Refuse** asks for a reason and, if **Send Email** stays ticked, sends a polite refusal. The card leaves the board; the Refused filter finds it again.
- **Add to Pool** keeps a good candidate in the talent pool for the next opening.

After each interview the interviewer fills in the scorecard and adds a short log note. HR decides the next step from those, not from memory.

## Step 4: Make the offer

Move the chosen candidate to **Contract Proposal** and open the **Offer** tab that Recruitment Plus adds.

1. Fill in **Offered Start Date** and **Monthly Salary**.
2. Under **Terms printed on the letter**, write what the letter should say besides salary and date: probation, notice, working hours, leave.
3. Click **Print Offer** to check the letter first if you like.
4. Click **Send Offer**. The email opens with the subject "Job offer: Accounts Officer at Crescent Textiles (Pvt) Ltd", the offer letter attached as a PDF and a button to the candidate's offer page. Click **Send**.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-recruitment-job-offer-online-acceptance/offer-tab.webp" alt="Offer tab on an Odoo applicant with offer status Not sent, offered start date 1 December, monthly salary 90,000 and three terms printed on the letter, with Send Offer and Print Offer buttons at the top" width={1440} height={900} caption="The Offer tab before sending: start date, monthly salary and the terms the letter will print." />

Send Offer refuses to run until four things are filled: the candidate's email, the job position, the monthly salary and the start date. That check is there because a letter with a blank salary is worse than no letter.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-recruitment-job-offer-online-acceptance/offer-letter-pdf.webp" alt="Offer of Employment PDF on the Crescent Textiles letterhead addressed to the candidate, with position, department, start date, monthly salary in rupees, contact, the terms, the online confirmation link and two signature lines" width={1191} height={1300} caption="The offer letter PDF: company letterhead, the offer in a table, the terms, the link to answer online and two signature lines." />

The letter carries your company logo and address from the company settings. The status on the Offer tab changes to **Sent**, with the time it went and the **Offer Link**, which you can copy and send on WhatsApp if the email lands in spam.

## Step 5: The candidate answers online

The button in the email opens a private page that only that link can open. It shows the position, department, start date, monthly salary and terms, with **Accept the offer** and **Decline the offer**. Declining asks for a reason in a text box.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-recruitment-job-offer-online-acceptance/candidate-offer-page.webp" alt="Candidate's job offer page on the company website showing position, department, start date and salary with an Accept the offer button and a Decline the offer link" width={1440} height={660} caption="What the candidate sees: the offer and two choices. No login needed." />

- **Accepted:** the application moves to the hired stage by itself, shows a green HIRED ribbon, and the recruiter gets a message saying the candidate accepted the job offer online.
- **Declined:** the status becomes Declined, the candidate's reason shows on the Offer tab, and the recruiter gets a message with the reason.
- **Need to change it?** Click **Reset offer** on the Offer tab, change it and send again. Resetting clears the old link, so an answer can only come for the offer that is current.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-recruitment-job-offer-online-acceptance/applicant-hired.webp" alt="Odoo applicant form with a green HIRED ribbon, hire date filled, stage Contract Signed and a Create Employee button at the top left, with the offer letter shown in the attachment preview" width={1440} height={900} caption="After the candidate accepts: Contract Signed, HIRED, and Create Employee ready." />

## Step 6: Hire and start onboarding

Click **Create Employee** on the accepted application. The employee record opens with the job and department from the application and the CV attached. Recruitment Plus then fills in what standard Odoo leaves blank:

| On the new employee | Taken from |
|---|---|
| Work email | The candidate's own email, until they get a company address |
| Wage | The monthly salary on the offer |
| Contract start date | The offered start date |
| Manager | The manager of the job's department |

It also starts the onboarding plan. It looks for an activity plan on employees whose name contains "onboarding" (Odoo ships one called Onboarding, under **Employees, Configuration, Onboarding / Offboarding**) and schedules every step of it, dated from the start date, each one assigned to HR or to the manager as the plan says.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-recruitment-job-offer-online-acceptance/new-employee-onboarding-plan.webp" alt="New Odoo employee record for the hired candidate with department Accounts, manager set, and eight planned onboarding activities on the right dated from the start day, from collecting documents to the probation review" width={1440} height={900} caption="The new joiner one click later: manager from the department, and eight onboarding activities already dated from 1 December." />

Edit the plan once so it matches how you actually bring people in. The sample company uses eight steps:

| When | Task | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 2 days before start | Collect ID copy, documents and bank details | HR |
| 1 day before start | Prepare desk, laptop and work accounts | Manager |
| First day | Create the HR login and send it | HR |
| First day | Register on the attendance device and set the Badge ID | HR |
| First day | Check wage, working schedule and leave on the record | HR |
| First day | Introduce to the team and explain the first tasks | Manager |
| After 1 week | First week check-in | Manager |
| After 3 months | Probation review | Manager |

Each person sees their own steps in their activities (the clock icon at the top) and clicks **Mark Done** when finished. On the first day, click **Create User** on the employee to give them a login, then add their ID number, bank account and Badge ID. The rest of the joiner's first months, letters, documents and confirmation, are in the [onboarding and offboarding chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-employee-onboarding-offboarding/).

<Callout title="Two onboarding lists">The plan started on hire is a set of Odoo activities. If you also use the WT HRMS lifecycle checklist on the employee's Onboarding tab, decide which one is the real list, or HR ticks the same task twice. Many companies keep the activity plan for the first week and the WT HRMS checklist for what must be done before confirmation.</Callout>

## Common problems

**A candidate says the apply link shows nothing.** The job is not on the careers page. Switch Careers page on for that job.

**Send Offer gives an error.** It names what is missing: the candidate's email, the job, the monthly salary on the Details tab, or the start date on the Offer tab.

**The candidate accepted but Create Employee filled no manager.** The manager comes from the job's department. Set a manager on the department, or set it on the employee by hand.

**The onboarding activities did not appear.** No activity plan on employees has "onboarding" in its name, or it has no steps. Check **Employees, Configuration, Onboarding / Offboarding**.

**Two new joiners share the company email.** They do not with this module: the work email starts as the candidate's own address. Change it on the record once they get a company mailbox, before you import or create logins that match on email.

With the person hired, the next step is their leave rules. The [leave policy chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-leave-policy-late-deduction/) sets monthly limits, half days, unpaid leave and late arrivals, all of which the offer letter's terms should agree with.
