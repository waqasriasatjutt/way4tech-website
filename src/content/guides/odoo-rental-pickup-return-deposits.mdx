At a hire counter the paperwork goes wrong in the same three places every time: a machine promised to two customers for overlapping days, a late return nobody charged because the price for the extra day was not on the contract, and a deposit refunded in full when it should have covered the late fee. None of this needs complicated software. It needs the order to know the dates, the price table to cover any period, and the handover to be recorded when it happens. This chapter covers how the Rental Management module does that on the ordinary Odoo 19 Community sales order.

## Rentable products and their rates

Tick **Can be Rented** on a product and its Rental tab opens up:

- **Rental Rates**: so many hours, days, weeks or months for so much money. A product usually has a day, a week and a month rate.
- **Security Deposit** per unit.
- **Units for Rent**: a fixed number, for a hire fleet that is not stock managed, or the quantity on hand, for goods you also buy and sell.
- **Late Penalty (%)**: an optional percentage added on top of a late return charge.

**Rental, Configuration, Rental Rates** shows every rate across products, with the per hour equivalent so you can see at a glance whether the week rate is really cheaper than seven days.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-rental-pickup-return-deposits/rental-rates.webp" alt="Rental Rates list in Odoo with day, week and month rates for a table saw, scissor lift, diesel generator, party tent, pressure washer and PA system, an hourly rate for a camera kit, and a per hour equivalent for each" width={1600} height={1000} caption="Rate rows per product, with the per hour equivalent beside each price." />

Pricing follows one rule. The single rate that covers the requested period for the least money wins; part periods round up to whole blocks; a rate priced at zero is ignored. Ten days with a day rate of 35 and a week rate of 120 is charged as two weeks, 240, because that is cheaper than 350. Rental lines are priced from these rates rather than the pricelist, through Odoo's normal price computation, so tax included rates and the rest of the order work as usual. Rates held in the company currency are converted when the order is in another currency.

Combo products cannot be made rentable, because a bundle has nothing of its own to hand over.

## The rental order

**Rental, Operations, Rental Orders** is a filtered view of sales orders with **Rental Order** ticked. A rental order has a **Pickup** date and an **Expected Return** date. Each line takes those dates, and you can change them on one line when an item goes out early. Moving the return date reprices the line. The order also shows the total security deposit and the rental status: Draft, Reserved, Out, Partly Returned or Returned. The order is only as far along as its least advanced line.

### No double booking

When you confirm, the module counts the units of each product already promised to confirmed rentals that overlap the exact window, not the day. Windows are half open, so a return at 10:00 frees the unit for a pickup at 10:00. If the order would oversell, confirmation is refused and the message names the product, the free quantity and the orders holding the rest. The count is done with full rights, so a salesperson who cannot see another team's order is still stopped from promising the same item.

**Check Availability** on the order reports the same without confirming. **Rental, Operations, Availability** answers "have we got four of these next Tuesday" without an order: pick a product, a window and a quantity, and it shows units owned, already promised, free, whether there are enough and which orders hold them.

## The schedule

**Rental, Operations, Schedule** lists every rental line with its customer, product, quantity ordered, pickup, return, duration, how many were picked up and returned, and the rental status, as a list, calendar or pivot. It is the counter's view of what goes out and comes back today.

## Pickup

**Pickup** on a confirmed order opens a wizard already filled with everything still to go out. Set the time it was collected, correct any quantity that differs, and add a note such as a serial number or the name of whoever collected it. **Confirm Pickup** records it. Partial pickups are allowed; the line keeps what is still to go out for the next pickup.

## Return and late charges

**Return** opens a wizard with each line's picked up quantity, how many are already back, when it was due and how many are coming back now. Set the time it came back.

If that time is after the expected return, the wizard shows the late charge before anything is saved, so the counter can tell the customer what the delay costs. The charge is the late hours priced from the product's own rate table with the cheapest covering rate, times the quantity, plus the late penalty percentage. Untick **Charge for Late Return** to waive it. **Confirm Return** records the return and puts the charge on the order as a line of its own. A second late return on the same line updates that fee line rather than adding another.

Lateness is settled from the recorded return, not the clock, so an item that is late but not yet back has no late charge until it returns. The overdue flag covers that gap.

## The movement log

Every pickup and every return writes a movement record: reference, date, direction, order, customer, product, quantity and, for returns, how many hours late. Movements are never edited afterwards, so the history stands up when a customer disputes a late charge. **Rental, Operations, Movements** lists them all.

## Security deposits

The deposit is the product's deposit per unit times the quantity on each rental line, totalled on the order and converted to the order's currency.

1. **Invoice Deposit** raises an invoice for the deposit alone, against a deposit service product, so it never mixes with hire revenue. It can only be raised once and only if there is a deposit.
2. **Refund Deposit** opens a wizard with the deposit held and a deduction that starts at the order's late charges. Change the deduction if there is damage or anything else to keep. The refund is raised as a credit note for the deposit less the deduction. It cannot be zero and cannot be more than the deposit taken.

The refund is a credit note of its own rather than a reversal of the deposit invoice, because the amount going back is rarely the amount that came in.

## Overdue rentals and reminders

A daily scheduled action looks for rental lines whose return date has passed while goods are still out, flags the order as Overdue and puts one to-do on it for whoever is following it. It does this once per order, not every night, and clears the flag when everything is back. **Send Reminder** on the order emails the customer that the goods are due back, using the shipped return reminder template.

A **Rental Agreement** prints from the order.

<Callout title="Where this module stops">Availability is a pool per product, not a tracked unit with its own serial, meter or inspection record; the note on a pickup is where a serial number goes. Pickup and return are recorded in the module's own wizards and movement log and are separate from Odoo's delivery orders, so if your rentable products are storable goods, decide with your warehouse how standard deliveries are handled. One rate prices a hire; rates are not combined. There is no damage assessment workflow: damage is a deduction you type on the deposit refund.</Callout>

## Setting it up

1. Install Rental Management; it needs Sales and Inventory.
2. Tick Can be Rented on each product you hire out, add its rate rows and deposit, and choose a fixed pool or quantity on hand.
3. Set a late penalty percentage on products where late returns hurt, such as items booked back to back.
4. Create a rental order with pickup and return dates, confirm it, and try a second overlapping order that would oversell.
5. Record a pickup, a partial return and a late return, then invoice and refund the deposit, and check the figures with whoever does the books.
6. Check the return reminder email template and the person who should receive overdue to-dos.

For bookings taken on the website, add the [Online Rental Shop](/odoo-guides/odoo-rental-website-booking/); with both installed, the shop reads these rate tables and counts these rentals. The [rental management hub](/odoo-guides/odoo-rental-management/) compares the two modules.
