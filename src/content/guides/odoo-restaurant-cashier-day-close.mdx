Restaurants rarely lose money in one big theft. They lose it in small, deniable amounts at the till: a void after the food went out, a friend's discount, a wallet payment that was a screenshot of an old transfer, a drawer that is a few hundred short every Saturday. Each is easy to explain away on the night and impossible to find a month later. The fix is not more suspicion, it is a name and a reason on every one of them, recorded at the moment it happens.

This chapter covers the cashier's side: taking cash, cards and mobile wallets, what the receipt carries, closing the register, the Z report and cash count, and the roles, PINs and audit log that sit behind every one of those steps.

## Taking payment

1. **Open the table.** The order shows its lines, the customer if one is set, and the loyalty points the guest will earn. Only a cashier, manager or owner can go further: the Waiter role has no Payment button, and the server refuses a payment from a waiter login.
2. **The payment screen.** It shows the total, the amount per guest, and the payment methods of this register, for example Cash, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank Transfer, Card and Customer Account. Tip and Invoice are here too.
3. **Cash with change.** Tap Cash, tap the notes received (+10, +20, +50) or type the amount, and the change is shown. Validate.
4. **Mobile wallet.** Tap JazzCash. A window shows the merchant account and a large QR for the guest to pay from their app. Type the transaction reference from the guest's screen. Validate is refused until the reference is there.

A wallet reference that was already used on the same wallet is flagged with the earlier order. The usual cause is a guest showing an old screenshot, so the flag is the check that matters most in practice.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-cashier-day-close/jazzcash-payment.webp" alt="JazzCash payment dialog in the Odoo POS with a merchant QR code, the merchant account number and name, the amount 18.30, a required transaction reference of at least 8 characters and an optional payer phone" width={1600} height={1000} caption="A wallet payment: merchant QR for the guest, and a transaction reference the cashier must type." />

Set the wallets up in **Restaurant, Configuration, Wallet Methods**: one method each for JazzCash, EasyPaisa, SadaPay, NayaPay or a bank transfer, with the merchant account the guest pays to and whether a reference is required. Later, **Reporting, Wallet Payments** lists every wallet payment by method; the manager ticks the ones found in the merchant portal and presses Mark verified.

<Callout title="Two known issues on the payment side">On the current Odoo 19 build the payment screen and the printed receipt show the change as a negative number (for example "-20.00" when 20.00 is due back). The amount is right; only the sign is wrong, and it comes from Odoo itself, not from the suite. Separately, on orders created on the server rather than rung up at the till (the demo's sample orders are made this way), the "Need an invoice?" block on the receipt prints without a valid code. If a guest needs an invoice for such an order, make it from the backend.</Callout>

## What the receipt carries

The receipt prints the logo, ticket number, time, cashier, table and number of guests; the guest and their tax number (NTN) when set; each dish with its unit price and note; the payment and change, with the wallet reference under a wallet payment; loyalty points earned; and a **Rate your meal** QR that opens a one-page rating form on the guest's phone.

With Fiscal Compliance installed, the receipt also carries the fiscal block: the FBR invoice number and QR, or "Fiscal invoice pending" with a reference while the authority is offline. The till never waits for the tax authority. If it is slow or down, the order is queued and retried after 1, 5 and 15 minutes and then every hour, and **Reporting, Fiscal Queue** shows what is still waiting. What FBR and the provinces require from that receipt is covered in the [FBR POS integration guide](/odoo-guides/fbr-pos-integration-odoo/); this chapter does not repeat it. Saudi receipts print as a ZATCA Phase 1 simplified tax invoice and UAE receipts as a tax invoice with the TRN; neither has an API connection.

## Clock in and out

Staff clock in at the till: menu, **Clock in / out**, tap your name, type your PIN. A shift left open is closed on its own after 16 hours, and a manager can correct the times, which marks the shift "Edited by hand". **Reporting, Shifts** shows hours per person per day. With Odoo's Attendance app installed, every punch also goes there.

## Closing the register

Menu, **Close Register**. For each payment method the screen shows what the system expects, what was counted and the difference. Count the cash in the drawer and type it; wallets and cards are checked against their own reports.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-cashier-day-close/closing-register.webp" alt="Closing Register dialog in the Odoo POS for 33 orders with expected, counted and difference for Cash, Card, Customer Account, JazzCash, EasyPaisa and Bank Transfer, and Close Register, Cash In/Out and Daily Sale buttons" width={1600} height={1112} caption="Closing the register. The cash difference shows in red because nothing has been counted yet." />

The cash count works like this:

- **Expected in drawer** = opening float + cash payments after change + cash put in − cash taken out.
- **Counted** is typed by denomination (5000 × 3, 1000 × 12 and so on) or as one figure. The notes and coins come from **Configuration, Cash Denominations**, which ships with PKR from 5000 down to 1.
- **Difference** = counted − expected.

Cash in and cash out during the day always need a manager PIN, so every movement of the drawer has a name on it.

## The Z report

Closing the register creates the branch's **Z report** on its own, under **Reporting, Day Reports**. It holds sales, tax, refunds, net sales, payments by method, orders and covers, discounts, voids and tips, sales by service and by category, and the cash side: float, cash sales, cash in and out, expected, counted note by note, and the difference. **Mark as Done** freezes it, and only an owner can reopen it. An **X report** is the same report taken during the day without closing. Day reports print as a PDF or on the receipt printer and export to Excel.

## Roles, PINs and the audit log

Three rules sit behind everything above. **Branches are walls:** a manager, cashier or waiter sees only their own branches, on every screen and report. **A PIN on every risky action:** voids of sent dishes, price changes, discounts over the limit, refunds, reopening a paid order, cash in and out, and transfers, all checked on the server. **Every action logged:** who, when, on which registered device, the value before and after, and who approved.

| Action at the till | Waiter | Cashier | Manager |
|---|---|---|---|
| Take payment | No | Yes | Yes |
| Remove a sent dish | PIN | PIN | PIN |
| Discount above the limit, price change | PIN | PIN | PIN |
| Move or merge a table | PIN | PIN | Yes |
| Cash in or out | No | PIN | PIN |
| Mark a dish 86 | PIN | PIN | Yes |

These are the defaults. Each register can change them, as the [setup chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-pos-setup/) explains.

**Reporting, Audit Log** lists every sensitive action with user, employee, branch and device. **Reporting, PIN Approvals** shows each request, approved or refused, by whom and for what amount. Filter the audit log on void once a week and look at the names; that habit alone changes behaviour at the till.
