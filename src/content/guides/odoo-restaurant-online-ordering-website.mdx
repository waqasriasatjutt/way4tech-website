Most restaurants pay a delivery app a share of every order, and the customer who orders through it belongs to the app. Restaurant Website Ordering puts the ordering on the restaurant's own Odoo website instead. Customers order for pickup, delivery or at their table, and every order lands in the same [Online Orders inbox](/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-delivery-online-orders/) as the aggregators, then goes to the till and the [kitchen screen](/odoo-guides/odoo-kitchen-display-system/) like any other order.

<Callout title="An add-on to the suite">

It is an add-on to the Restaurant Suite, not part of the suite install. It needs Restaurant Online Orders, Menu and Delivery. You can try every step below at [restro.way4tech.com/order](https://restro.way4tech.com/order).

</Callout>

## What the customer sees

The landing page carries a rotating offer banner, the restaurant name and line, and the dishes that really sell most. Banners can be A/B tested: two versions are shown in turn and the clicks counted.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/welcome.webp" alt="Restaurant ordering website landing page in Odoo with a weekend family deal banner, the restaurant name, Order online and Our locations buttons, and a Popular right now row" width={1366} height={900} caption="The landing page: offers, the restaurant, and what sells most." />

The menu is one page per branch, with an open or closed badge, pickup or delivery, categories, a search over names, descriptions and tags, and diet chips. Each dish shows its photo, price, allergens and calories. A dish marked 86 at the branch shows as sold out.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/menu.webp" alt="Restaurant menu page on the Odoo website with the branch name, open now badge, delivery mode, category chips, a search box, vegetarian, vegan and halal filters, and dishes with photos and prices" width={1366} height={900} caption="The menu for one branch, with search and diet filters." />

### The item customiser

Tapping a dish opens the customiser: sizes, flavours, sauces and extras with live prices, required and optional groups with their limits, "first N included free", choices ticked by default, a note for the kitchen, and a "make it a meal" offer when the dish is part of a combo.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/customiser.webp" alt="Item customiser on the ordering website with size, sauce and extra choices, each with its price, a note for the kitchen and an Add to basket button with the live total" width={1366} height={900} caption="Every option prices itself as the customer picks." />

Picking an option can open another group, up to three levels deep: naan, then which naan. Each rule is set in **Restaurant, Configuration, Nested Modifiers**. A pizza style item can be **half and half**, with a flavour per half, priced by the dearer half or by the average of the two.

## Basket and checkout

The basket shows quantities, options and notes, suggests what is "often ordered together" from the restaurant's own sales, and checks every price and item again before checkout.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/checkout.webp" alt="Checkout page with pickup or delivery, customer details, delivery address, tip choices, time slot, payment choice and the order total with tax and fees" width={1366} height={1180} caption="Checkout: how, when, tip, and how to pay." />

At checkout the customer chooses pickup (or curbside, with an "I'm here" button), delivery with the zone found from the address or from "Use my location", or the table they are sitting at. Tips, tax as the till computes it, service and bag fees, and an allergy note printed in capitals for the kitchen are all here, with ASAP or a time slot that respects lead time, cut-off, holidays, orders per slot and kitchen load.

### Points, gift cards and wallet

Loyalty points are spent only after a one-time code is sent to the customer's phone, so nobody can spend another person's points. Gift card codes and the eWallet balance come off the total. If the order is rejected or cancelled, all of it is given back.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/rewards.webp" alt="Checkout in French with the loyalty points applied after a phone code, a gift card code accepted and an allergy note for the kitchen" width={1280} height={860} caption="Points, a gift card and an allergy note, here in French." />

### Card payment

A card order opens Odoo's own payment page, with whatever provider the restaurant enabled. The order reaches the kitchen only once the payment is confirmed. A customer who confirmed their phone can save a card for next time.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/pay.webp" alt="Odoo payment page for a restaurant website order with the order summary and the payment method choice" width={1280} height={860} caption="Odoo's payment page; the card details never touch the restaurant's server." />

<Callout title="Prices stay honest">

The browser never sets a price. Every dish, size, extra and half is priced again on the server from the chosen options, using the branch menu and its pricelist. Sending the same order twice creates it once.

</Callout>

## After the order

The status page moves through received, accepted, preparing, ready and on the way the moment the kitchen moves the order, and shows the rider on a map for a delivery. Texts and phone notifications go out at each step.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/tracking.webp" alt="Order status page with the received, accepted, preparing, ready and completed steps and a map with the rider and the delivery address" width={1366} height={900} caption="Live status, with the rider on the map." />

Below it the customer can message the restaurant (staff reply from the order), see the pickup shelf, rate the meal, see the rider's photo at the door, and turn the order into a weekly repeat. While it is allowed, they can cancel or move the order, and a card payment is refunded through the gateway.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/after-order.webp" alt="Order confirmation in French with the steps, a reply from the restaurant, a five star rating and a repeat weekly option" width={1280} height={860} caption="Messages, rating and a weekly repeat." />

**My orders** opens after a code to the phone or a sign in: past orders with track and order again, favourites, weekly repeat orders, a referral code that earns points, and the customer's data to download or erase. Gift cards can be bought online too, with the code emailed to the recipient.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/my-orders.webp" alt="My orders page with a referral code, favourite dishes, past orders with Track and Order again buttons, and download or erase my data" width={800} height={801} caption="My orders: history, favourites, referral code and the customer's data." />

## Dine-in by table QR

Each POS table gets its own ordering link. **Restaurant, Configuration, Table QR Codes** lists them; select tables and print one QR code per page. Scanning it opens the menu on that table, with no phone or email needed, and the order goes to the till on that table.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/dinein.webp" alt="Ordering website opened from a table QR code showing the dine-in badge with the table name above the menu" width={1280} height={860} caption="The menu opened from a table." />

## Setting up a branch

**Restaurant, Configuration, Branches**, tab **Website ordering**, holds everything the site does for that branch.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/branch-settings.webp" alt="Branch form in Odoo with the Website ordering tab: ordering modes, minimum order, scheduling, dine-in and rewards, kitchen and hours settings" width={1440} height={900} caption="The Website ordering tab on a branch." />

| Section | What you set |
|---|---|
| Website ordering | Turn the branch on, pickup and delivery, minimum order, tagline |
| Scheduling | Lead time, cut-off, slot length, orders per slot, kitchen load per slot, busy mode |
| Dine-in and rewards | Table ordering, points and gift cards on the website, the one-time code |
| Kitchen, hours and safety | Delivery hours, minimum age for alcohol, how long an order may wait before it is cancelled and refunded |
| After the order | Ratings, messages, customer cancel and move, the pickup shelf |
| Accounts and rewards | Favourites, saved cards, referral points, weekly repeat orders |
| Text messages | Which steps send a text, and a manager phone alerted when an order waits too long |
| Courier service | DoorDash Drive or Uber Direct, booked by a button or automatically |

## Running it

Website orders sit on the native **Website** channel beside the aggregators, with a bell, and are accepted the same way. Orders for later appear in **Operations, Scheduled Orders** and go to the kitchen in time by themselves.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/inbox.webp" alt="Online Orders inbox in Odoo with New, Accepted, Preparing and Ready columns holding website orders" width={1440} height={900} caption="Website orders in the same inbox as every other channel." />

Refunds, cancellations and goodwill discounts on a placed order are requested by anyone and approved by a manager, so the two steps stay in different hands. On the order, a Customer tab shows the rating, messages, shelf, minutes to accept and to ready, and the tip split. **Reporting** adds Order Timings, Website Tips, Promo Usage, Customer Ratings, Referral Codes, Customer Data Requests and the Message Outbox.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-online-ordering-website/override.webp" alt="Online order form in Odoo with the Override tab: requested change, amount and reason, and the approval state with who requested it" width={1440} height={900} caption="Manager approval for refunds and discounts." />

## The rest of the platform

The site is in English and French, installable on the phone as an app with an offline page, keyboard and screen reader friendly (checked against WCAG 2.2 AA), and sends GA4 and Meta Pixel events. Search engines get structured data for the restaurant and its menu.
