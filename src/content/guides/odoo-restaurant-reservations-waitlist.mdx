The door is the first and last thing a guest remembers. A booking that was lost, a family of six told "ten minutes" and left standing for forty, a regular who is treated like a stranger, a bad meal that only shows up as a one-star review a week later. Each one costs a returning guest, and returning guests are where a restaurant's margin is.

This chapter covers the guest side: bookings and the walk-in queue, run from the till the host already has, and then loyalty tiers, birthday offers and feedback. Standard Odoo 19 has a Booking button on the POS when booking is enabled, as the [restaurant features documentation](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/point_of_sale/restaurant.html) notes. Restaurant Reservations adds the rules a busy restaurant needs around it: table suggestions, deposits, no-show tracking, reminders and a fair waitlist.

## The booking calendar

**Restaurant, Reservations, Calendar** shows the week coloured by branch, with list and kanban views by status. Each branch sets its own booking rules on the **Reservations** tab of the branch form: booking length, slots, no-show grace, reminders, deposits and waitlist times.

## Making a booking

1. **Guest, party size, time.** Fill in the guest, phone, party size, occasion and source (phone, walk-in and so on), and the start time. The branch time and duration follow from the branch rules.
2. **Availability.** The button lists the free tables for that window, with the smallest table that fits already ticked.
3. **Deposit.** A branch or a single booking can require a deposit before it is confirmed. The amount and its reference are recorded on the booking.
4. **Confirm.** Confirm sends the confirmation message. **Seat**, **Complete**, **No-show** and **Cancel** follow as the evening goes on.

### The rules behind a booking

| Rule | What it does |
|---|---|
| No double booking | Tables never overlap, with 15 minutes between bookings; the refusal names the other booking |
| Party size | Larger than the seats warns; more than twice the seats is refused |
| Reserved ring | 30 minutes before, the tables turn Reserved on the floor plan with a chip such as "11:45 Omar (6)" |
| No-show | Released after the grace time, 20 minutes in the demo; from three no-shows, the guest's next booking shows a warning |
| Deposits | Required per branch or per booking, recorded with a reference |
| Closed days | Configuration, Booking Blackouts refuses bookings for a private event or a holiday |
| Messages | Confirmation, a reminder 3 hours before, and table-ready, by SMS or a WhatsApp webhook, or logged only |

<Callout title="Messages need a provider">Confirmations and reminders go out only once a sending method is chosen in Settings: Odoo SMS or a WhatsApp webhook. Until then they are logged on the booking and nothing reaches the guest, as in the screenshot above. Deposits are recorded with their reference; taking the deposit online is not part of the module.</Callout>

## The walk-in waitlist

The **Waitlist** button in the till opens the queue. Each party shows its position, size, phone and arrival time, and the real wait against the quoted wait in green, amber or red. The strip at the top counts parties and guests waiting, how many are over their quote, and the next quote to give.

- **Add party** takes three taps: name, size, phone.
- **Notify** sends "your table is ready".
- **Seat** opens a table picker with the best table already ticked.
- **Left** records a party that gave up, which is the number to watch on a Friday.

Bookings due in the next two hours sit beside the queue, so the host sees both at once and does not give away a table that is held for 9:45.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-reservations-waitlist/waitlist-in-pos.webp" alt="Waitlist in the Odoo POS with 5 waiting, 17 guests, 5 over quote and the next quote 30 minutes, parties with position, size, phone, arrival time, minutes waited against quoted, notify, seat and left buttons, and bookings due with times, table numbers and Seat buttons" width={1600} height={773} caption="The waitlist in the till: real wait against quoted wait, and the bookings due beside it." />

For a host desk without a till, **Reservations, Host Stand** is the same queue on a tablet at the door, with its own Booking button. **Waitlist History** keeps every party, and **Reporting, Guest Messages** logs every SMS and WhatsApp message sent.

## Loyalty tiers and tier prices

**Restaurant, Configuration, Loyalty Tiers** sets Bronze, Silver and Gold, each reached by points earned in the last 12 months, with a discount that becomes a price list in one click. When a member is set on an order, their tier badge shows and their prices apply. The dish card in the till shows the Silver and Gold price next to the normal one, so a waiter can tell a regular what they will pay.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-reservations-waitlist/loyalty-tiers.webp" alt="Loyalty Tiers in Odoo: Bronze from 0 points with 8 members, Silver from 500 points in 12 months with 5 percent off and a Silver members price list, Gold from 1,500 points with 10 percent off and a Gold members price list" width={1600} height={395} caption="Three tiers, each reached by points in the last 12 months, each with its own price list." />

Underneath, points, eWallet, gift cards and coupons are Odoo's own loyalty programs. Starter programs are created at install: 1 point per 100 spent, and 100 points give 5 off. **Operations, Loyalty Members** shows guests grouped by tier with their points and balance, and the customer display shows the guest's name, tier and points on the screen facing them.

**Birthday offer.** A few days before a guest's birthday the module issues a coupon, once a year, valid for 14 days, sent by message or noted on the contact. The amount and the days before are set in Settings, and **Reporting, Birthday Offers** lists the coupons sent.

## Rate your meal

Every receipt carries a **Rate your meal** QR. It opens a one-page form on the guest's phone with stars for overall, food and service, and a comment box. No website is needed; the page is served by Odoo.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-reservations-waitlist/rate-your-meal.webp" alt="Rate your meal page on a phone for Lahore Grill House Gulberg with the order number, star ratings for overall, food and service, a comment box and a Send my rating button" width={1400} height={1292} caption="The page the receipt QR opens on the guest's phone." />

**Reporting, Guest Feedback** shows ratings by branch, waiter and day. A rating of 2 or less creates a to-do for the branch managers the same day and, with the call center installed, opens a complaint. That is the point of the feature: the manager hears about the cold biryani that evening, while the guest can still be called, rather than reading it on a review site.
