A distributor sends forty quotations a month. Ten get chased because a salesperson happened to remember. The customer who never opens email answers WhatsApp in ten minutes, but those chats live on a personal phone, so nobody else can see them. A web enquiry asks for a price list and hears nothing for a week. At month end the sales manager works out commission in a spreadsheet and two people dispute it. None of this is a missing feature in the quote itself. It is follow-through, and on Odoo Community most of it is left to people.

This guide covers six Way4Tech modules that sit on Odoo 19 CRM and Sales and do the chasing: WhatsApp messages from quotations and invoices, a WhatsApp code on website signup, email drip campaigns, commission plans, automatic quotation reminders and an AI assistant on leads, quotes and products. All six run together on one live demo, an invented Dubai supplier of PPE, cleaning chemicals and packaging called Dunecrest Industrial Supplies, with five months of leads, quotes, invoices and payments behind it.

## What standard Odoo Community already does

Community is a good base for selling. CRM gives you leads, a pipeline by stage, activities, lost reasons, expected revenue and a predicted probability. Sales gives you quotations with online signature, sales orders and invoicing from the order. Any record can send an email and keep it in the chatter, and the Email Marketing app sends one-off mailings to a list.

What it does not do on its own:

- **Chase a quotation.** A quote sent on Monday stays exactly as it is until somebody opens it again.
- **Talk WhatsApp.** Odoo's WhatsApp app is Enterprise only. On Community the conversation happens outside Odoo.
- **Run a drip.** Odoo's Marketing Automation app, the one with flows of emails triggered by time and opens, is Enterprise only.
- **Pay commission.** There is no commission model at all. Teams use spreadsheets.
- **Write for you.** There is no assistant that reads a lead and drafts the reply.

The six modules fill those gaps without moving you to Enterprise, and they share the same records. One lead can get a welcome email from a campaign, a lead score from the assistant, a quote on WhatsApp, reminders at two, five, ten and fifteen days, and once it is paid, a commission line for the salesperson.

## Quotation follow-up in two minutes

The smallest module is often the one that pays first. Quotation Follow Up adds a rule list under Sales, Configuration. Four rules ship with it: a gentle nudge at two days, a check in at five, "still interested?" at ten and a last call at fifteen, each with its own email template. You can change the days, swap the templates, copy a sales manager on a rule, or skip quotes below a minimum. On the demo the last call only goes to quotes of AED 1,000 or more.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-sales-messaging-tools/qf-rules.webp" alt="Quotation Follow-Up Rules list with four rules at 2, 5, 10 and 15 days, their email templates, the sales manager copied on the last one and an active toggle on each" width={1440} height={900} caption="The four follow-up rules, editable in the list." />

Once a day the sweep looks at every quotation still in draft or sent. For each one it picks the latest rule whose day has been reached and that is later than the last one already sent, sends that email and writes a note in the chatter. A quote that nobody touched gets each reminder in turn. On the quote you see how many reminders went out, when the last one was and which rule fired, and a Send Reminder Now button does the next one on the spot. Tick Disable follow-up on a quote you are negotiating by phone.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-sales-messaging-tools/so-form-draft-rem.webp" alt="Quotation S00020 in Odoo showing Disable follow-up, the number of reminders sent, the date of the last reminder and the last rule fired, with Send Reminder Now, Send on WhatsApp and Ask Claude in the header" width={1440} height={900} caption="A quotation with the reminder fields, and the buttons the other modules add." />

<Callout title="Two limits worth knowing">
In this version, unticking the master switch in Settings does not stop the daily sweep, because Odoo removes the setting when it is unticked and the sweep reads a missing setting as on. To pause reminders, archive the rules or switch off the scheduled action. The global minimum amount in Settings is applied by the daily sweep only, not by Send Reminder Now.
</Callout>

## WhatsApp codes on signup

If customers create their own portal or webshop accounts, a fake phone number is a follow-up call that never connects. WhatsApp OTP Verification adds a required phone field with a Send OTP button to Odoo's signup page, sends a six digit code with your Meta approved template, and only enables Sign up once the code checks out. The server checks the code again on submit, codes expire after ten minutes by default, and an hourly job clears old ones. If an existing contact has the same email and no login yet, the new account is linked to that contact instead of creating a duplicate.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-sales-messaging-tools/signup-otp.webp" alt="Odoo website signup page with name, email, a phone number field and a Resend OTP button, a red message saying WhatsApp API is not configured, password fields and a greyed out Sign up button" width={1440} height={900} caption="The signup page with the OTP fields. With no Meta token set, the module says the API is not configured." />

The settings sit in the Website settings: the switch, the Meta token and phone number id, the template name and the expiry. These credentials are separate from WhatsApp Integration.

<Callout title="What the code shows in this version">
A failed WhatsApp send is ignored, so the page reports success even when Meta refused the message. The verified phone is meant to be saved on the new contact, but the write also targets a mobile field Odoo 19 no longer has, so it fails quietly. There is no limit on how often a visitor can request or try a code. And the setting is installed as the text "False", which the signup code reads as switched on, so save the setting once to set it the way you want.
</Callout>

## How the demo is set up

Dunecrest Industrial Supplies LLC is invented, and so is everyone in it: four salespeople, a reseller and an introducer who earn commission, 22 customer companies with example.com addresses, 20 products, 44 leads, 57 quotations at every stage, 35 invoices and five months of history from June to November 2026. Prices are in AED with 5% VAT.

Nothing on it reaches a real person. WhatsApp and the AI assistant are in simulation mode, which both modules switch on at install: WhatsApp messages are recorded and marked sent without calling Meta, and Ask Claude returns a fixed placeholder answer without calling Anthropic. The message history and the AI answers you see were stored when the demo was built, to show what live use looks like. No outgoing mail server is connected, so campaign and reminder emails stay on the server, and the daily follow-up sweep is paused there for that reason.
