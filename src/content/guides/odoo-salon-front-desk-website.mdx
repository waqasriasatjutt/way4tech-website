Most salons buy too much software or too little. A two chair studio does not need a spa room grid, and a day spa with a hammam will outgrow a booking diary in a week. The Way4Tech salon modules are sold separately so you can pay for what the business actually does. The [main guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-spa-management/) explains the four-module ladder of base, till, spa layer and loyalty add-on. This chapter covers the two modules that sit beside it, a fast front desk screen and a website theme, and then gives a straight answer on which combination fits which salon.

## Salon Quick Booking: a front desk without Odoo lists

Some receptionists never want to open an Odoo list view. Salon Quick Booking gives them five large screens, under an **Open Salon** app: Quick Booking, Appointments (the board), Quick Order, Dashboard and Reports. The bookings are the same appointments as the base module, so nothing is kept twice. It installs on WT Salon Management and WT Enterprise Salon (its manifest depends on both), so the client's loyalty tier, membership and packages load as soon as they are picked.

### Open the session

The front desk works in sessions, one per day. On any screen, press **Open Session**, enter the opening cash counted into the drawer and confirm. From then on every quick order and booking counts toward that session with running totals. Only a receptionist or manager can open or close a session.

### Serve a walk-in with Quick Order

Quick Order is for a client standing at the counter. Tap one or more services and each goes into the cart. Search the client by name or phone, or add a new one with just a name, or leave it blank for a walk-in customer. Pick a chair from the live map and a stylist if the service needs one, then press **Pay**: the sale is created, moved straight to invoiced, and the payment screen opens.

### Book ahead with Quick Booking

For later today or another day, pick the services, client, stylist, date and time. A discount, if you give one, applies to every line. **Book Appointment** puts it in the New stage.

### Chairs that look after themselves

A chair is added once by an administrator, with a short code such as C1 and optionally a preferred stylist. Its status is never set by hand: it becomes occupied when an active appointment is put on it and free again the moment that appointment is done, invoiced or cancelled. A second active appointment on an occupied chair is refused, and the error names the one already there.

### The board, payment and the day's figures

**Open Salon, Appointments** shows the day full width, grouped by stage and refreshing itself every thirty seconds, with a stats row counting new, confirmed, in progress, done, invoiced and paid visits and today's revenue. Drag a card to another stage or use its action button. Dragging straight to Invoiced is blocked on purpose: payment goes through **Pay** so no invoice is created without it.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-front-desk-website/front-desk-board.webp" alt="Appointments board in the Open Salon front desk with totals for new, confirmed, in progress, done, invoiced and paid and today's revenue, and appointment cards in stage columns with client, service, stylist, price and Confirm, Start or Complete buttons" width={1600} height={1000} caption="The board refreshes every thirty seconds and counts the day across the top." />

The payment screen lists the service lines, subtotal, tax, total and your configured payment journals. Because Enterprise Salon is installed, it also shows the client's points, what a redemption is worth and a gift card field, both applied as a discount line before the invoice posts. Pick a journal and confirm, and the invoice is posted and the payment created and reconciled in one step. Once an invoice is posted, editing the appointment is blocked.

The Dashboard shows bookings, cancellations, paid visits and walk-ins, total and pending revenue, a breakdown by stage and stylist, the top five services and who is active now. Reports gives a date range sales summary by date, stylist or service. It is a front desk summary, not a replacement for the base module's pivot views. The gear button on every screen sets one of seven colour themes, dark mode, font size, a brand name and logo, and whether a receipt prints after payment, saved per device.

At the end of the day press the lock button, enter the closing cash counted and confirm. The session's totals are fixed, and a session cannot be closed twice.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-front-desk-website/close-session.webp" alt="Close Session dialog in the Open Salon front desk showing the session reference, revenue so far, appointment count, a closing cash field and a closing note" width={1600} height={1000} caption="Closing the day: revenue and appointments so far, then the counted cash." />

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Stylist | Quick Order, Quick Booking, the board and the Dashboard |
| Receptionist | All of that, plus opening and closing a session and reading Reports |
| Manager | All of that, plus Appearance Settings and full access to chairs and sessions |

Before the team starts, add at least one cash or bank journal under **Accounting, Configuration, Journals**, because the payment screen lists whatever is configured there.

<Callout title="What Quick Booking does not do">A chair's status is whether an appointment is on it right now, not a calendar for the day: two appointments cannot share a chair at the same moment, but nothing stops the same chair being assigned to two bookings later in the day. There is no cash drawer, card reader, barcode scanner or receipt printer driver, and no SMS channel.</Callout>

## Salon and Spa Theme: the public website

The theme gives a salon or spa a finished looking site on day one. It is nine drag and drop sections (Odoo calls them snippets) for a hair salon, nail studio, barbershop, spa or aesthetic clinic, plus six published pages built from them: Home, About, Service Menu, Style Gallery, Team and Contact. It needs Odoo 19 with the Website app and nothing else, and it is Community.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-front-desk-website/theme-home.webp" alt="Homepage of the Salon and Spa Theme on an Odoo website for a demo salon, with a headline, Book Now and Service Menu buttons, three stat figures and a generated abstract hero graphic" width={1600} height={1000} caption="The homepage as installed, with the demo salon's text ready to replace." />

The nine snippets are Salon Hero, Salon Services (a menu in six categories with prices), Salon Features, Salon Style Gallery, Salon Team, Salon Testimonials, Salon FAQ, Salon Booking CTA and Salon Contact with a working contact form. They are registered in the website builder's own Blocks panel under the usual categories, so you can drop any of them onto any page.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-front-desk-website/theme-snippets.webp" alt="Odoo website editor open on the salon homepage with the Blocks panel on the right listing categories such as Intro, Content, People and Contact and Forms" width={1600} height={1000} caption="The theme's sections sit in the normal Blocks panel, so they work on any page." />

To make it yours:

1. Press **Edit** on any page. Changes stay private until you press **Save**.
2. Replace the photos first. The hero, gallery and team images are generated abstract graphics in the theme's gold and charcoal, not photographs. Click an image and the editor's image panel opens.
3. Change colours and fonts once in **Website, Configuration, Theme**. Every snippet uses the same palette, so one change applies everywhere.
4. Edit services and prices directly on the Service Menu. They are plain text on the page, not linked products, so type over them and copy or delete rows as needed.
5. Edit the team by copying a whole stylist card and changing the copy.
6. Check the **Book Now** links. They point at `/salon/book`, the booking page from WT Salon Management. Without that module, repoint each button (hero, booking band and the Contact page's Book Online link) to your own booking address.
7. Rewrite the FAQ, then check the page's Published switch in the editor's top bar.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-front-desk-website/theme-service-menu.webp" alt="Service Menu section of the salon theme with six category cards such as Hair Studio, Aesthetic, Spa, Brow and Lash, Nail Studio and Bridal, each listing services with prices in AED" width={1600} height={1000} caption="The service menu is text on the page: type over the names and prices." />

With demo data on, the theme renames your company to the demo salon so the header matches the pages. Change it back under **Settings, General Settings, Companies**; the theme sets it only once and never overwrites it again. Your page edits survive later theme updates, as with any Odoo website page.

<Callout title="What the theme does not do">It has no booking form of its own, no payment or shopping cart, no blog or events calendar (add Odoo's Blog or Events apps if you want them), and no real photography. The demo text ships in English only; translate it with Odoo's own translation tools.</Callout>

## Which modules to buy

All prices are as listed on each module, in USD, for Odoo 19.

| Your business | Buy | Total |
|---|---|---|
| One or two chairs, diary and invoices from the back office | WT Salon Management | 10 |
| A salon taking money at a Point of Sale till | Salon Management + POS Salon | 959 |
| A day spa with rooms, therapists and couples | Salon Management + POS Salon + Spa POS | 2,258 |
| Packages, memberships, gift cards and deposits | Add WT Enterprise Salon to any of the above | + 1,299 |
| A receptionist who wants speed over Odoo screens | Salon Management + Enterprise Salon + Quick Booking | 1,458 |
| A public website that books into the salon | Salon and Spa Theme with Salon Management | 49 |

Spa POS does not replace POS Salon, it extends it, so a spa buys the three in order. A plain salon stops at POS Salon. Both can take the loyalty add-on, and its features show in either till. Quick Booking is a front desk screen beside the till, not a payment terminal; a salon that needs a card reader or receipt printer wants POS Salon. None of these modules replace Odoo accounting, inventory or payroll; they post into them, and none include SMS or WhatsApp credit.
