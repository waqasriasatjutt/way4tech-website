A salon's best clients rarely pay the full price in cash on the day. They hold a ten session package bought in March, a gift card from a birthday, points from last year and a deposit paid when they booked the colour. Each is money the salon has already taken, and each has to come off the right bill exactly once, or the salon is either out of pocket or charging twice. WT Enterprise Salon, sold as Salon Loyalty, Packages and Memberships, is the add-on that handles that money, plus the features that bring clients back. It sits on WT Salon Management, covered in the [main guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-spa-management/).

## Do you need it?

The base module runs the day: bookings, services, commission, the online booking page and invoices. This add-on handles everything that is not a straight cash sale. If none of the following matters to you, the base module alone is the right purchase:

| What you want | Base module | With this add-on |
|---|---|---|
| Points for spending, in tiers | No | Four tiers, a QR card, points that can be spent |
| Sell ten sessions up front and count them down | No | Yes, and the session is not charged again |
| Monthly membership with a minimum term | No | Yes, and the term is enforced |
| Gift cards with balance and expiry | No | Yes, and overspending is refused |
| Deposit at booking carried onto the invoice | Advance Due as a figure only | Taken into a journal and deducted once |
| Waitlist, consent forms, before and after photos | No | Yes |
| Reminder, review, birthday and win-back emails | Confirmation email only | Yes |
| Owner's dashboard and reports on money held | No | Yes |

It installs on Odoo 19, Community or Enterprise, and adds items to the existing Salon menu rather than a second app.

## Step 1: switch on what you use

Open **Settings** and scroll to the Salon section. The add-on's settings sit below the base module's, in eight groups: booking, deposits and payments, loyalty, packages and memberships, gift cards, reviews, messaging and automation. A feature that is off does nothing: its menu and its tab disappear, and anything that would create one of its records is refused.

Out of the box, loyalty, packages, gift cards, reviews and the waitlist are on. Memberships, deposits, consent forms, photos, products used, SMS, WhatsApp, the referral bonus and the automatic emails are off until you turn them on. Text messages go through Odoo SMS and need SMS credits on your Odoo account. WhatsApp needs your own Meta Business account, approved templates, an access token and a phone number ID.

## Step 2: set the loyalty rate and tiers

Open **Salon, Loyalty, Program Setup**. Two numbers decide what you give away. **Points per Currency Unit** is what each unit spent earns; **Points to Redeem 1 Currency Unit** is what buys one unit of discount. The demo runs 2 and 40: a 300 bill earns 600 points, worth 15, which is five per cent back.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-loyalty-packages-memberships/loyalty-program-tiers.webp" alt="Salon Loyalty Program form in Odoo with 2 points per currency unit, 40 points to redeem one unit, and a tiers table of Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum with minimum lifetime points and tier discount percentages" width={1600} height={1000} caption="Two numbers set the value of a point; the Tiers tab sets the ladder." />

The Tiers tab holds the ladder, each tier with a minimum of lifetime points, a tier discount and a badge colour. A client sits in the highest tier their lifetime points reach.

Earning needs no action. When an appointment is invoiced, the module opens a loyalty card for the client if they do not have one and posts the points. Every movement is a row in **Points Transactions**: earned, redeemed, returned (a cancelled visit gives points back), manual adjustment and expired. Unlike a redemption, a manual adjustment moves the lifetime total too, so a client does not keep a tier that a mistake bought them.

To spend points, open the appointment and press **Redeem Points**, type how many and confirm. The discount lands on the ticket. More points than the card holds is refused, and so is a redemption worth more than the bill, a second redemption on the same visit, and one on a visit that is cancelled or already invoiced.

## Step 3: sell and use a package

A package is a bundle of sessions paid up front. In **Salon, Packages, Package Templates** set the sessions included, the price, the validity in days (0 means no expiry), the product used to invoice the bundle, and on the Included Services tab which services count as a session. Sell one from **Customer Packages**: pick the client and package, set the purchase date, and the expiry works itself out.

On the appointment, open the Enterprise tab and pick the client's package. Only their active packages are offered. The visit counts as a session used, the covered value shows as Covered by Package and the balance due drops by the same amount. Anything on the ticket the package does not cover is still billed.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-loyalty-packages-memberships/customer-package.webp" alt="Customer package record in Odoo for a Colour Care Package with 4 sessions used, 1 remaining, an expiry date and the four appointments that used the sessions" width={1600} height={1000} caption="Sessions Used is counted from real visits; the Appointments tab is the audit trail." />

Cancel a visit and its session goes back. A package with no sessions left is refused by name, so it cannot go below zero.

## Step 4: memberships with a minimum term

In **Salon, Memberships, Membership Plans** set the monthly price, duration in months, minimum term in months (0 means the member can leave any time), service discount and free services per month. Sell one from **Customer Subscriptions**; the subscription shows Committed Until and Inside Minimum Term.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-loyalty-packages-memberships/membership-minimum-term.webp" alt="Customer membership record in Odoo for a Platinum VIP plan with start and end dates, Committed Until date, Inside Minimum Term ticked and an Early Exit Approved switch" width={1600} height={1000} caption="Inside the minimum term, Cancel is refused until a manager approves an early exit." />

Cancelling a subscription inside its minimum term is refused, and the message names the date the commitment ends. Only a manager can tick **Early Exit Approved** with a reason, and then the cancellation goes through. A receptionist cannot tick it; Odoo refuses the write itself, not just the button.

## Step 5: gift cards

In **Salon, Gift Cards** press New. A code is generated, or type your own; two cards cannot share one. Fill in the initial value, who bought it, who it is for and the expiry date. Initial value, redeemed and remaining balance are separate columns, so you can see what is still out there against you.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-loyalty-packages-memberships/gift-cards.webp" alt="Gift Cards list in Odoo with code, purchaser, beneficiary, initial value, redeemed, remaining balance, expiry date and state Active or Fully Used" width={1600} height={1000} caption="Gift cards with what was issued, what was spent and what you still owe in services." />

To redeem, pick the card on the appointment's Enterprise tab and type the amount for this visit. More than the card holds, a card past expiry, a cancelled card and more than the bill are all refused. Cancel the visit and the amount goes back on the card.

## Step 6: deposits and the balance due

A deposit comes from one of two places. If the service carries an advance in the base module (the demo's full colour takes a quarter up front), the booking's Deposit Amount is exactly that. If not, and **Ask for a deposit at booking** is on in Settings, confirming the booking works the deposit out from the total at your percentage. The service's own advance always wins, and the client is never asked for both.

Nothing is collected until the client pays. Press **Take Deposit** on the appointment, check the amount and pick the journal, cash drawer or bank. A payment is posted and the balance due drops. When you invoice the visit, the deposit goes on as its own line, once. Invoice the same appointment again for an extra service and the deposit is not taken off a second time. If the invoice was already posted when the deposit lands, the module refuses to touch it and tells you to register the deposit as a payment against that invoice instead.

The Enterprise tab on any appointment is the one screen to learn. It keeps the total, what a package covered, what a gift card covered, what points covered and what was collected as a deposit as separate figures, and works out **Balance Due** from them. That is the number to read out at the desk. If the credits together come to more than the bill, the save is refused and the message says by how much.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-loyalty-packages-memberships/balance-due.webp" alt="Salon appointment form in Odoo with Redeem Points and Deposit Paid buttons, an Advance Due of 70, and the Enterprise tab showing loyalty tier Gold, service package, loyalty points and gift card discount fields" width={1600} height={1000} caption="The Enterprise tab on an appointment, where every credit meets the bill." />

With a Stripe secret key in Settings, the online booking page can send the client to Stripe to pay the deposit as they book.

## The waitlist, consent, photos and notes

When a day is full, add the client to **Salon, Waitlist** with their preferred stylist, services, date and time window. When a booking on that day is cancelled, the module notifies the first person who asked. Someone who wanted a different stylist is not called, and someone whose treatment needs a room is only called if that room is still free inside their hours. **Book This Slot** creates the appointment from the entry. Entries whose day has passed expire overnight.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-loyalty-packages-memberships/waitlist.webp" alt="Salon waitlist in Odoo with clients, preferred stylist, preferred date, time window, requested services and a Notify button on each row" width={1600} height={1000} caption="The waitlist: who wants which stylist, on which day, between which hours." />

**Consent forms** are written once under **Salon, Configuration, Consent Form Templates**. On the appointment's Consent Forms tab, staff pick the form, type the client's name in Signed By and tick Signed, and the date and time are recorded. **Before and after photos** go on the Photos tab of the visit. **Stylist Notes** on the appointment hold the colour formula for this visit with the last three visits underneath, while **Customer Notes** hold lasting facts about the person, such as an allergy, with a type and date.

## The owner's figures

**Salon, KPI Dashboard** opens on the current month. Two money figures are deliberately separate: **Collected** is money actually received against posted invoices, and **Booked, not yet collected** is the rest of the diary's value. Use Collected when working out anyone's share of takings. The dashboard also shows appointments, average rating from published reviews only, top stylist, late cancellations, occupancy counted from your rooms and stations and opening hours, and **Held for Customers**: gift card balances and deposits not yet taken off a bill.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-loyalty-packages-memberships/kpi-dashboard.webp" alt="Salon KPI Dashboard in Odoo with today's appointments and money collected, period appointments, collected and booked not yet collected, average rating, room occupancy, gift card balances and deposits held, top stylist and late cancellations" width={1600} height={1000} caption="Collected and booked are kept apart, and money held for clients has its own row." />

Under **Salon, Reports** four pivots cover points issued and redeemed (never netted), packages sold against sessions used, gift card liability and deposits held. They are figures for your accountant, not journal entries.

## Who sees what

Stylists see their own appointments and the client's loyalty tier, but not points balances, gift cards or memberships. Reception runs everything except settings, the dashboard and reports. Managers see it all. Clients in their online account see only their own cards, packages and memberships. With [POS Salon](/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-pos-till/) installed too, points, sessions, gift cards and deposits can be taken off the bill at the till under the same rules.

<Callout title="What the add-on does not do">It is not a salon system on its own and will not install without WT Salon Management. It does not sell packages, memberships or gift cards on the website, does not collect the monthly membership fee, and does not pay commission (it can confirm it and mark it paid on a schedule). It does not post gift cards as a liability in the books. It does not refund a deposit: a cancelled client's deposit stays recorded as paid, and refunding it is a normal Odoo refund. WhatsApp needs your own Meta Cloud API token and phone number ID, and nothing is published from reviews until a person presses Publish.</Callout>
