Most schools that come to Odoo already have the records. They sit in an admissions register, a fee ledger kept by the accountant, attendance registers per class and a spreadsheet of marks the class teacher fills in the week before results. The trouble starts when those have to agree with each other: a report card that says ninety eight per cent attendance when the register says otherwise, a family chased for fees already paid, a student promoted without anyone checking the last term's marks. Putting the school on one system fixes that only if the office and the teachers both work in it every day. This guide explains what that system has to do, what standard Odoo gives you, and how to set up School Management on Odoo 19 so the year runs from admission to the final report card.

## What a school office actually needs

Leave the feature lists aside. A school needs a small number of things to be true at all times:

1. **One record per student, one enrollment per year.** The student's details, parents, medical notes and photo stay on the student. Grade, section, subjects and fees belong to the year, so last year's class does not overwrite this year's.
2. **Fees that follow the grade.** Grade 5 pays what Grade 5 pays. The office should pick a fee structure, not type amounts for each child.
3. **Attendance taken once, used everywhere.** If the class teacher marks the day, the report card should count it without anybody adding up a register.
4. **Marks tied to a term.** A test written in October has to land on the First Term card and nowhere else.
5. **A timetable that shows clashes.** One teacher in two rooms at the same period is a planning error that should be visible before Monday.
6. **Clear roles.** Teachers mark attendance and enter marks. The office handles admissions and fees. Only the administrator changes the grade scales and fee structures.

## What standard Odoo does and does not do

Odoo 19 Community has several apps a school will use: Employees for staff, Attendances and Time Off for staff presence and leave, Invoicing for the books and Fleet for vehicles and drivers ([Fleet documentation](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/hr/fleet.html)). It also has [eLearning](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/websites/elearning.html), which publishes online courses on the website for people to follow.

None of these knows what a student in Grade 5 section A is. There is no admission application, no enrollment by session, no subject list per grade with credit values, no fee structure per grade, no daily student register, no term with dates and weightage, no grade scale that turns a percentage into a letter and points, and no report card. eLearning tracks course progress for website visitors, not term marks for a class. You could build some of this with custom fields, but the parts that matter (a mark counting only in its term, attendance counted inside term dates, a position in class) need code.

School Management fills that gap and leans on the standard apps for the rest. Teachers are Employees. Staff attendance and leave stay in Attendances and Time Off. Bus routes take their vehicles and drivers from Fleet. All of these install with it.

[School Management](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_school_management/) is USD 10. [School Academic Reports](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_school_academic_reports/) is a USD 49 add-on that prints the three academic reports from the marks and attendance the base app already keeps. [School Management Enterprise](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_enterprise_school/) is a USD 800 add-on that needs both of the others and adds the wider campus records listed above, along with parent meetings, installment plans, budgets, visitor management and events. All three run on Odoo 19 Community. This guide covers the base app and the Academic Reports add-on.

## Give people access before anything else

Open **Settings, Users and Companies, Users**, pick the person and set the WT School Management role:

- **Administrator** sets up sessions, grades, subjects, fee structures and grade scales, confirms applications and prints report cards.
- **Officer** is the office: applications, students, enrollments, fee slips, transport and notices. An officer cannot delete configuration.
- **Staff** is a teacher: attendance, assignments for their subjects, marks, exams and lesson plans.

<Callout title="No role means no access">A user without one of the three roles gets an Access Error on every school screen, and that includes whoever installed the app. Give yourself Administrator first, then the others.</Callout>

The top menu follows the order the office works in: Applications, Students, Enrollments, Academics, Exams, Assignments, Fees, Attendance, Transport, Other and Configuration.

## Set up the school year once

Each step feeds the next, so work in this order.

1. **Session.** In **Academics, Sessions**, create the school year the way the office names it, for example 2026/2027, with start and end dates.
2. **Academic year and terms.** In **Academics, Academic Years**, add the same year with its terms: dates and a weightage for each, such as three terms at 33.33, 33.33 and 33.34, or two semesters at 50. Auto Divide Weightage splits it evenly. Terms matter more than they look: marks are entered against a term and report cards are printed for one.
3. **Grade scales.** In **Academics, Grade Scales**, set the bands that turn a percentage into a letter and points. The [report cards chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-school-report-cards/) goes through this in detail.
4. **Grades, sections and subjects.** In **Academics, Grades**, make one record per class level (Pre-K, KG, Grade 1 to 12) with its grade scale, its sections and its subjects. Each subject has a code unique inside the grade, a credit value that weights it in the GPA, an optional flag and the teachers who take it.
5. **Fee structures.** In **Fees, Fee Structures**, one per grade or per grade and year. The [admissions and fees chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-student-admission-fees/) covers this.
6. **The small lists.** Configuration holds classrooms, the periods of the day, assignment types (class test, assignment, examination, project) and discipline types. Fill them once and the forms offer them as choices.

## Teachers and staff

Teachers are employees in the Employees app. On the employee, tick Is Teacher (or Is Supervisor) and pick the subjects they teach; the timetable, assignments and subject records then offer them. Staff attendance and leave use Odoo's own Attendances and Time Off apps, so if staff clock in on a biometric terminal, the [ZKTeco ADMS guide](/odoo-guides/zkteco-adms-odoo/) shows how those punches reach Odoo. For staff salaries in Pakistan, see the [Odoo payroll Pakistan guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-payroll-pakistan/).

## Daily attendance

**Attendance, Student Attendance** holds one line per student per day, Present or Absent, with optional check in and check out times. A teacher marks a whole class from the list: group by grade or section, select the rows and set the status. Lock the day once it is final; staff cannot edit locked lines. Dates in **Attendance, Public Holidays** are skipped when attendance is counted.

This is the register the report card reads. It counts school days, present, absent and the percentage from the lines inside the term's dates, so a school that marks every day gets a correct card without anyone adding up a register at term end.

## Timetable, classes and lesson plans

Set up classrooms and time slots (for example 08:00 to 08:45) under Configuration. In **Academics, Classes**, make a class for each grade and section in the session; the timetable and class assignments are built on it. **Academics, Timetables** holds one entry per subject, teacher, classroom, weekday and time slot. Group the list by teacher or by classroom to spot a double booking before the week starts. Under **Other, Lesson Plans**, a teacher writes what they plan to cover for a grade, section and subject, visible to everyone or only to its author, and approved by the person responsible.

## Transport

**Transport, Locations** holds the stops. A route in **Transport, Routes** has a transport manager, a vehicle and driver from Fleet, an ordered list of stops with pickup and drop off times, and the students who ride it; switch Transport Enabled on the student. A trip in **Transport, Trips** is one run of a route on a date: tick Picked up and Dropped off per student with the times for the morning and afternoon checks.

## The other records

Under **Other**, discipline records hold the incident date, type, summary and action taken; those dated inside a term print in the Conduct block of the report card. Service hours log community service or club time per student with a supervisor, and the total shows on the enrollment. The notice board holds notices with dates and an audience: all, students only or teachers only. Archive a notice to take it down early.

## A school year on the system

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| Before the year | Session and academic year with terms, grade scales, grades, sections, subjects and teachers, fee structures, classrooms, time slots, timetable |
| Admissions | Applications, Confirm, Enroll. Check subjects on each enrollment, confirm it, make the fee slips |
| Every day | Attendance per class, then lock the day |
| During the term | Assignments, marks and Evaluate, each with its term. Fee slips marked paid as money comes in, chase overdue ones |
| End of term | Last examination evaluated, attendance locked, report cards printed for the class with remarks |
| End of year | Whole year report cards with a promotion note, promote enrollments, new session and academic year, new enrollments |
