Results week is where a school's credibility with parents is won or lost. A report card with a wrong percentage, a position that does not match the class list or an attendance figure the parent knows is wrong gets talked about at the gate. Most of those errors come from the same place: marks copied from a teacher's register into a spreadsheet, and from the spreadsheet into a Word template, by hand, late at night. In School Management the marks are entered once, by the teacher, against a term, and the report card is worked out from them every time it is printed. This chapter follows a mark from the grade scale to the printed card, the class result sheet and the transcript.

The marks, assignments, exams and seating plans are in the base School Management app. The three printed reports come with the School Academic Reports add-on, which installs on top and adds the buttons and menus described below.

## Step 1: the grade scale

A grade scale turns a percentage into a letter, points and a remark. In **Academics, Grade Scales**, add one line per band: the symbol, Min %, Max %, the points used for the GPA and a short description. The description prints on the report card as the remark for each subject, so write it the way you want parents to read it.

Bands are inclusive and must cover 0 to 100 with no gaps: A from 80 to 100, B from 70 to 79.99, and so on down. Tick **GPA Calculation** on the scale used for the overall grade. Each grade (class level) picks its own scale, and a subject can have its own if it is marked differently.

## Step 2: assignments and marks by term

Marks reach the report card in three steps. The third one is the one the card reads.

1. **Grade Assignments.** Under **Assignments, Grade Assignments**, the teacher defines a piece of work for a grade and subject: a name, a type such as class test, assignment or examination, a description and attachments. Approve it.
2. **Class Assignments.** Under **Assignments, Class Assignments**, schedule it for a class with start and end dates and the total marks, and start it. The app creates one student assignment for every student taking that subject. Per type weightages can be set on the class.
3. **Student Assignments.** Each student's copy. The student or the teacher submits it with an attachment, the teacher enters Marks Obtained, picks the **Term** and presses **Evaluate**. The percentage and the grade scale line are worked out.

<Callout title="What counts on a report card">An assignment counts only when it is in the Evaluated state, has a Term set, and is not marked Exempted. If a subject shows Not assessed on a card, one of those three is missing. Teachers who forget the term are the most common cause.</Callout>

## Step 3: exams and seating plans

**Exams, Exams** holds an exam for a grade and subject with a date, duration, pass marks and instructions. Add questions of three kinds: multiple choice with the correct choice ticked, true or false, and subjective free text. Each question carries its marks, and the exam total is their sum. Publish the exam, start it on the day and complete it afterwards.

Each student's paper is a submission under **Exams, Submissions**, with one answer line per question. The teacher enters the marks for each answer and presses **Grade**. The score, the percentage and a pass or fail against the pass marks are worked out from those marks. Multiple choice answers are not marked for you; the teacher confirms the marks on each line.

For a written exam in a hall, **Exams, Seating Plans** lays out the room. Give the number of rows and columns and press **Generate Seats**; the app labels the seats A1, A2 and so on. Place a student on each seat and finalise the plan.

<Callout title="Exam scores do not flow to the card by themselves">The report card reads assignments, not exam submissions. To put an exam on the card, create an Examination type assignment for the term and enter the marks there. That is deliberate: the teacher decides which result counts.</Callout>

## Step 4: print the report card

The Student Academic Report Card is a one page A4 PDF per student, for one term or the whole academic year.

- **One student:** open the enrollment or the student and press **Report Card** in the header.
- **A whole class:** open **Enrollments**, filter the grade and section, tick the students and use **Actions, Print Report Card**. You get one PDF with one page per student.

In the dialog, pick the term, or leave it empty for the whole year. Add the class teacher's remarks and, at year end, a promotion note such as "Promoted to Grade 6". Press **Print**.

### What is on the card and where it comes from

- **School header:** company name, logo, address, phone and email from **Settings, Companies**. Change the logo there and every card follows.
- **Student block:** photo, name, enrollment number, grade and section, academic year, date of birth, parents and the period covered.
- **One row per subject:** teacher, code, credits, number of evaluated assessments in the period, marks obtained, marks out of, percentage, grade and points from the subject's scale, and the scale's description as the remark.
- **Total:** all marks added, the overall percentage, the overall grade from the grade's own scale (or the GPA scale when the grade has none) and the GPA weighted by credits.
- **Position in class:** rank among students of the same grade, section and session by overall percentage, for example "3rd of 28". Students with equal percentages share a rank, and cancelled or terminated enrollments are left out.
- **Attendance:** school days, present, absent and percentage from the attendance lines inside the period.
- **Conduct:** discipline records dated inside the period. Nothing prints when there are none.
- **Remarks and signatures:** the text from the dialog, then lines for the class teacher, principal and parent.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-school-report-cards/report-card.webp" alt="Printed student academic report card for a demo student at Greenfield International School, First Term 2026/2027, with eight subjects, marks, percentage, grade A, points, overall 87.7 per cent, GPA 4.00, position 1st of 8, attendance 98.6 per cent and class teacher's remarks" width={952} height={920} caption="A First Term report card from the demo school." />

The percentage per subject is the marks obtained over the marks available across all counted assessments, not an average of percentages. So a 60 mark exam weighs three times a 20 mark test.

Print the cards after the last assessment of the term is evaluated and the term's attendance is locked. You can print again at any time; the card always shows the current records, and nothing on it is stored.

## Step 5: the class result sheet

The Class Result Sheet puts the whole class on one landscape page for the staff room or the principal. Open **Academics, Reports, Class Result Sheet**, or select enrollments and use **Actions, Print Class Result Sheet**. Pick the academic year, grade, section and term; the dialog shows how many students it covers. Leave the section empty for the whole grade and the term empty for the whole year.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-school-report-cards/class-result-sheet-dialog.webp" alt="Class Result Sheet dialog in Odoo with academic year 2026/2027, Grade 5, section A, term First Term and 8 students" width={1366} height={800} caption="The result sheet dialog counts the students before you print." />

The sheet has a row per student and a column per subject with the marks and the grade, then the total, percentage, grade, GPA, attendance and position. The class is ranked by overall percentage. A student with no evaluated assessment yet sits at the bottom without a rank. Under every subject are the class average, the highest and the lowest percentage, which is the quickest way to spot a subject where the whole class struggled.

## Step 6: the transcript

Press **Transcript** on the student form, or use **Print, Transcript** on the student list. There is nothing to fill in. The transcript has a block per academic year on record, oldest first: grade and section, status, the full year marks per subject, the year total, position and attendance. At the bottom are the cumulative credit weighted GPA, the overall average, the credits and the span of years. Cancelled enrollments stay off it.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-school-report-cards/transcript.webp" alt="Printed academic transcript for a demo student with two academic years, Grade 4 in 2025/2026 completed and Grade 5 in 2026/2027 in progress, marks per subject, and cumulative GPA 4.00, overall average 88.5 per cent and 28 credits" width={952} height={910} caption="A transcript with two years on record and the cumulative GPA." />

<Callout title="Limits worth knowing">The term weightage on the academic year is stored but not applied by the reports: a whole year card adds up every evaluated mark in the year. Online exam results reach the card only through an Examination assignment. The card layout is a fixed template; changing colours or adding blocks is a customisation.</Callout>

## When a card looks wrong

- **The grade letter is wrong or empty.** The subject's scale bands do not cover the percentage. They must run from 0 to 100 without gaps. The overall grade uses the grade's scale, or the one with GPA Calculation ticked.
- **Attendance shows 0 days.** No attendance lines fall inside the term's dates. Check the term dates on the academic year.
- **Position is missing.** Only students with at least one evaluated assessment in the period are ranked. A student alone in a section shows 1st of 1.

Before marks, the students have to be enrolled with their subjects, which the [admission and fees chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-student-admission-fees/) covers. The [School Management overview](/odoo-guides/odoo-school-management/) explains the year and term setup and the daily attendance the card counts.
