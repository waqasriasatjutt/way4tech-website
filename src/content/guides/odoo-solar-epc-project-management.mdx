A solar job is sold on one spreadsheet and built from another. The design engineer's string calculation lives on a laptop, the price lives in an email, the grid application lives on the utility's portal, the commissioning readings are on a sheet in the van, and the meter readings that would show whether the site is making what it was sold as making are nowhere at all. When a customer calls in year two because the system feels low, nobody can put the design, the test sheet and the generation side by side. This guide shows how a solar installer or EPC contractor runs the whole job in Odoo 19 with our Solar Project Management module.

## What a solar EPC job involves

An EPC contractor takes a system from an empty roof to a certified, metered installation and often looks after it for years afterwards. The work splits into nine stages, and each one ends with a piece of paper:

| Stage | What happens | What proves it |
|---|---|---|
| Survey | Area, obstructions, orientation, tilt, shading, structure, a year of bills | Site survey report |
| Design | Module and inverter, strings, yield, price | Single line diagram |
| Permit Application | Building or roof permit where the site needs one | Permit |
| Grid Connection | Connection or net metering application to the utility | Grid approval |
| Procurement | Materials bought and called off to site | Supplier purchase order |
| Installation | The crew builds | Installation photographs |
| Inspection | Somebody checks it | Inspection report |
| Commissioning | Every string measured, the system energised | Electrical safety certificate |
| Handover | Warranties and documents to the customer | Handover certificate |

## What standard Odoo does

Odoo Project can hold the stages and Inventory can move the panels, but neither knows what a string is, what a performance ratio means or why a voltage reading on a cold morning matters. Offices that try to make standard Odoo fit end up with a project per job, a spreadsheet per design and a folder of PDFs per commissioning. What Odoo does give you underneath is what this module builds on: products and the warehouse for components, Invoicing for milestone bills, activities for the to do list and the chatter for the audit trail. The [inventory overview](/modules/inventory/) and [accounting overview](/modules/accounting/) cover what ships.

## How the module fits together

[Solar Project Management](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_solar_epc) (USD 349) runs on Odoo 19 Community and installs Inventory and Invoicing. It adds one app, **Solar EPC**, with Delivery, Compliance, Operations and Maintenance, Billing, Reporting and Configuration menus.

1. **Configure.** Modules and inverters are ordinary products with a Solar tab carrying their electrical data. Utilities hold the export cap, the export tariff, the application fee and how long they usually take; incentive schemes pay per kWp, as a percentage or a fixed amount within a size range; crews carry their daily capacity and an accreditation that expires.
2. **Settings.** Default irradiation and performance ratio, degradation, tariff escalation, lifetime and the grid carbon factor for the business case; the coldest cell temperature for string sizing; the Voc tolerance and insulation floor for commissioning; and three switches: structural note required, the stage gate, and grid approval before handover.
3. **Survey and design.** The survey gives the usable area and a rough system size; the design sizes the strings, works out the yield, builds the parts list, prices the job and walks the payback.
4. **Contract.** Send Proposal, then Contract Signed against a payment schedule that has to add up to exactly 100 percent. The target handover date comes from the stage durations.
5. **Build.** Grid application, materials, the daily site diary, then commissioning string by string.
6. **Hand over and after.** Warranties per component, an O&M contract with its visit calendar, and the monthly generation log against the design.

### The design carries the sale

The design holds the module and the inverter as real products, so the strings are sized against the inverter's DC window at the coldest morning the site sees, and the yield is stated plainly: system size times irradiation times performance ratio, less the shading measured on the survey. The business case walks every year of the system's life rather than dividing the price by the first year's saving.

### Commissioning that means something

The commissioning test sheet is laid out from the approved design, one row per string with the voltage it should read. A string passes when the measured open circuit voltage is within the tolerance in Settings, the insulation is above the floor, and polarity and earth continuity are confirmed. Nothing is handed over until every string has passed and the safety certificate number is recorded.

### After the handover

Hand Over opens a warranty for each component on the parts list, a performance warranty for the modules and the installer's own workmanship cover. An O&M contract lays out its visits across the term, and a monthly generation line per site compares the meter with the design, flagging any site that falls further behind than the threshold.

### Who sees what

Three roles keep the money where it belongs. A **Site Technician** sees the jobs, daily reports, maintenance visits and commissioning tests of their own crew and builds from the array, the strings and the parts list, without contract values, costs or prices. A **Design Engineer** sees every job, designs and prices them, runs the payment schedule and the O&M contracts. A **Solar Manager** also sees margins, project costs and reporting, and owns the configuration.

## Limitations

We would rather you read these before you buy:

- **No drawing or shading model.** Area, obstructions and shading loss come from the survey; layout drawings and shading studies are filed as documents.
- **No monitoring connection.** Meter readings are entered or imported; the module does not read inverter or monitoring portals.
- **Applications are recorded, not filed.** The grid application holds the dates, fee and approval number; lodging it with the utility is still done on the utility's own portal.
- **Call Off Materials fails in this version.** The button that creates the delivery to site stops with an error on Odoo 19, so the delivery is created in Inventory by hand until the fix ships.
- **No customer portal, no battery sizing.** Customers do not log in, and a battery can go on the parts list by hand but is not sized by the design.
- **Two mail jobs ship switched off.** Maintenance visit reminders and the underperforming site review are scheduled actions to switch on once outgoing mail is set up.
