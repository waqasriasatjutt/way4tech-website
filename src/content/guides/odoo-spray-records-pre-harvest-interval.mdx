A spray record kept on a clipboard in the tractor is fine until somebody needs it. A residue query from a buyer, a farm assurance inspection or a recall on a batch of product all ask the same questions: what went on which field, when, at what rate, out of which container, and was the crop cut after the interval had run. This chapter of the [farm management series](/odoo-guides/odoo-farm-management/) shows how Farm Management makes the record as the work is booked and uses it to stop mistakes.

## Setting up the inputs

Any Odoo product becomes a farm input on its **Farm Input** tab:

| Field | Used for |
|---|---|
| Farm Input | Seed, fertiliser, plant protection product, lime, feed, veterinary or other. Puts it in the input picker on a pass |
| Pre-Harvest Interval (days) | The days between application and cutting, off the label |
| Active Substance | Printed on the spray record; an inspector asks for the active, not the trade name |
| Maximum Dose / ha | The label rate; an application above it is refused |

## The input line is the record

On a pass, the Inputs Used tab holds one line per product: the quantity, the unit, the rate per hectare worked out over the area worked, the cost, the interval copied from the product, the batch number off the container, the target (septoria, blackgrass, aphids), and the operator certificate number, visible to farm managers only.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-spray-records-pre-harvest-interval/act-spray.webp" alt="Spray pass inputs used tab with two lines, a growth regulator and a fungicide, showing active substance, quantity in litres, rate per hectare, cost, pre-harvest interval, batch number, target and operator certificate" width={1440} height={900} caption="A spray pass: two products, each with its batch, rate and interval." />

When the pass is booked as done the line becomes evidence. It cannot be deleted any more: correct the quantity or cancel the pass. The pass itself cannot be deleted either, because the database would take the lines with it. With stock tracking on, each line is meant to draw the product out of the farm's input store. In the current build that move is not made on Odoo 19, so the store is kept in Inventory for now.

## Three label rules, enforced

| Rule | When it bites | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| No input, no booking | Booking a sowing, fertiliser, spray or lime pass with no line | Record what went on the ground first |
| Maximum dose | Saving a line whose rate per hectare is above the label | The rate, the label rate, and "an over-dose is a rejected load" |
| Wind limit | Booking a spray as done when the wind reading is above the company limit | The reading, the limit, and to correct it or move the pass |

The wind reading on a pass is filled from the weather log for that field on the planned day. The limit is a company setting, 15 kph by default, because it is a licence condition and differs by scheme.

## When is the field clear?

Each booked line has a clear date: the application date plus the interval. The field and the crop cycle both show **Clear to Harvest From**, the latest clear date still running on that ground. Compliance, Intervals Running lists every booked application still inside its interval, grouped by field.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-spray-records-pre-harvest-interval/phi-list.webp" alt="Intervals running list grouped by field with each plant protection application, its date, interval and clear date" width={1440} height={900} caption="Read this before the combine goes out." />

Confirming a harvest is refused while any booked application on that field, since the crop was drilled, is still inside its interval on the harvest date. The refusal names the latest one, because that is the one that decides. The interval belongs to the ground, not the paperwork, so a spray booked without a cycle on that field still counts.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-spray-records-pre-harvest-interval/h-draft.webp" alt="Draft harvest of leeks dated today with a note that it will not confirm because the rust spray interval is still running on the field" width={1440} height={900} caption="A leek load booked for today: it cannot be confirmed until the rust spray interval runs out." />

The flag that says an interval has elapsed is stored so it can be searched. A nightly job, Farm: expire pre-harvest intervals, refreshes it, so a field blocked this morning is free this afternoon without anyone editing a record. The rule can be switched off with Enforce Pre-Harvest Intervals in the settings; it is on by default and an assurance scheme will expect it on.

<Callout title="Back-dated harvests">
The check compares every application since drilling with the harvest date. A harvest entered late, dated before a later spray on the same field, is still refused by that later spray. Enter harvests as they come off.
</Callout>

## The weather log

Compliance, Weather Log keeps one line per field per day: rainfall, minimum and maximum temperature, wind, humidity and condition. A date in the future is refused, because it records what happened, not a forecast. The wind feeds the spray check and the rainfall gives the 30 day total on the field, which is what explains a yield a year later. Filters for Rain Recorded and Windy, and a rainfall graph by field.

## Who books what

The people who do the spraying book their own passes. A Farm Worker sees only the passes they are named on, and passes nobody is named on, with their input lines; the Compliance menu with every application, the intervals and the operator certificate stay with the agronomist and the manager. That keeps the record honest at the source, where the batch number is read off the container, while the farm manager keeps the overview. An agronomist can plan a spray as a draft or planned pass days ahead, with the products and rates, and the driver books it as done on the day, when the wind reading for that field is checked.

## On paper

The Spray Record prints from a pass in A4 landscape: field, date, operator and wind, then every product with active substance, batch, quantity, rate, target, interval and clear date. The Field Record Sheet prints every application ever made on a field beside its cropping history, and the Harvest Passport lists everything under a load.
