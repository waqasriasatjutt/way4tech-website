Turkish VAT rarely stops at amount times rate. On a cleaning contract the buyer keeps seven tenths of the KDV and pays it to the tax office himself. On rent paid to a person the tenant withholds income tax before paying. An export line carries no KDV but must say which exemption applies. Get any of these wrong and the e-invoice is refused, or worse, accepted with the wrong figure. This chapter of the [Turkish localization series](/odoo-guides/odoo-turkey-localization/) sets up the rules in the Turkish Tax Engine and follows them onto real invoices and bills on the demo.

## Why the rules are records, not built-in rates

Tevkifat ratios, stopaj rates and exemption codes are set by GİB communiqués and they change. A module that hard-codes them is right until the next communiqué and wrong afterwards, without telling anyone. The Tax Engine ships no rates at all. Every rule is a record with a code, the figures, the dates it applies from and to, and a **Verified** tick that records who confirmed it. In Development and Staging you can work with unverified rules; a company in Production refuses to send a document that uses one.

## The taxes Odoo already has

The Türkiye chart (`l10n_tr`) loads KDV at 20%, 10% and 1% for sales and purchases, a 0% exempt tax and a group tax per tevkifat ratio, named like **WH 20% (2/10)**: the 20% KDV plus a negative child of -4%, the two tenths of the KDV that the buyer withholds. The Tax Engine keeps those and adds a Türkiye tab to each tax: the Turkish tax type (KDV, KDV exempt, tevkifat, stopaj, ÖTV or other), the underlying KDV rate, the GİB tax code used in the UBL and, on a zero-rated tax, a default exemption code.

## Tevkifat rules

Open **Türkiye, Configuration, Taxes, Tevkifat Rules**. A rule has:

- a **code** and name, plus a separate GİB code if you keep one;
- the **ratio** as numerator and denominator, for example 2/10, shown both as a ratio and a decimal; it must be above zero and no more than one;
- whether it may be used on **sales**, on **purchases** or both;
- the **sales tax** and **purchase tax** it puts on the line, which are the `l10n_tr` group taxes;
- optional seller and withholding accounts, a service category and product categories;
- **effective from** and **to** dates, and **Mark Verified**.

The demo has four illustrative rules, 2/10 machinery maintenance, 5/10 construction, 7/10 cleaning and 9/10 consultancy, all left unverified on purpose.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-tevkifat-stopaj-kdv/tev-form.webp" alt="Tevkifat rule DEMO-210 Makine ve teçhizat bakım onarım with ratio 2 over 10, applying to sales and purchases, with the WH 20% (2/10) sales and purchase taxes and the Mark Verified button" width={1440} height={900} caption="A tevkifat rule: ratio, taxes, scope and dates." />

Put the rule on a product (Invoicing, Products, the Türkiye group) and it is filled in on every line that uses the product, without overwriting a choice somebody already made. The same group holds a stopaj rule, an exemption code and the GTİP customs code.

## A sales invoice with tevkifat

INV/2026/00043 on the demo bills Marmara İnşaat for one installation and maintenance service at 18,000 TRY. The product carries the 2/10 rule, so the line took the WH 20% (2/10) tax. Choosing a rule swaps the tax rather than adding one, which is what stops the KDV being charged twice. Under the totals the invoice shows the Turkish summary.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-tevkifat-stopaj-kdv/inv-tevkifat.webp" alt="Customer invoice INV/2026/00043 to Marmara İnşaat for an 18,000 TRY installation service, with the Turkish summary showing KDV in full 3,600 and 720 withheld by the buyer, with a total of 20,880" width={1440} height={1250} caption="The split on the invoice: charged, withheld by the buyer, collected by you." />

| Figure | Amount (TRY) |
|---|---|
| Service, untaxed | 18,000 |
| KDV calculated at 20% | 3,600 |
| Withheld by the buyer (2/10) | 720 |
| KDV collected (8/10) | 2,880 |
| Customer pays | 20,880 |

The invoice type becomes TEVKIFAT by itself and the split goes into the UBL-TR, which the [e-Fatura chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-e-fatura-e-arsiv/) shows. On the buying side it works the same way: the demo's October cleaning bill is 30,000 plus 6,000 KDV, of which the company withholds 4,200 as the buyer under the 7/10 rule and pays the supplier 31,800. That 4,200 is what the KDV-2 dataset reports.

## Stopaj

Stopaj is income tax withheld at source when you pay rent to a person or a fee to a self-employed professional. A stopaj rule holds the rate, the **withholding account** where the withheld amount posts, sales or purchases (purchases by default), who it applies to (any payee, legal entities only, or natural persons only) and the **Muhtasar code** of the declaration line. **Create Matching Tax** makes a negative-rate Odoo tax from the rule, once.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-tevkifat-stopaj-kdv/stp-form.webp" alt="Stopaj rule DEMO-STJ Kira stopajı at 20 percent with withholding account 360, Muhtasar code 041 demo and the Create Matching Tax and Mark Verified buttons" width={1440} height={856} caption="A stopaj rule, with its tax created from it." />

<Callout title="Account 360 in the Turkish chart">
Odoo's `l10n_tr` chart types 360 Ödenecek Vergi ve Fonlar as a payable account, and Odoo requires a due date on every line of a payable account. A withholding tax that posts there will not save. On the demo we changed 360 to a current liability before creating the stopaj taxes. Do the same, or pick another account, before you press Create Matching Tax.
</Callout>

On the demo the landlord, Kemal Örnekçi, is a natural person with a TCKN. October's rent is 45,000 TRY gross; 9,000 is withheld and 36,000 is payable to him. The accountant's monthly fee from Elif Deneme carries KDV and a 20% freelance stopaj, so the KDV and the stopaj cancel and 18,000 is payable. Both land on the Muhtasar dataset in the [reports chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-mizan-bilanco-turkish-reports/).

## Exemptions and ÖTV

A **KDV exemption code** has a code, the reason, the tax it exempts from, the type (full, partial, export or other) and dates. Full exemption keeps the right to reclaim input KDV; partial does not, which decides where the amount lands on the return. A line with a 0% exempt tax but no code is flagged. When every product line on an invoice is exempt, the invoice type becomes ISTISNA.

**ÖTV rules** record the annexed list (I petroleum, II vehicles, III alcohol and tobacco, IV other goods), the method (a percentage, a fixed amount per unit, or a percentage with a per-unit minimum), the rate or amount, whether the KDV base includes the ÖTV, product categories or a GTİP prefix, and dates.

## What the engine checks before anything is sent

The engine lists Turkish tax problems on the invoice in plain sentences:

- an exempt tax with no exemption code;
- an exemption code, tevkifat rule or stopaj rule outside its dates;
- a tevkifat rule with no tax for this kind of document;
- a stopaj rule with neither an account nor a tax, or used for a payee it does not apply to;
- on a Production company, any rule that is not Verified.

<Callout title="Limits in this version">
- **ÖTV is configuration only.** Nothing on an invoice line reads the ÖTV rules yet.
- **No rates ship.** You enter every ratio, rate and code, and confirm them with your mali müşavir.
- **The dashboard's stopaj tile reads customer invoices.** Stopaj is normally on vendor bills, so read it from the Muhtasar dataset instead.
</Callout>
