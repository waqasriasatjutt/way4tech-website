Not every retailer waits for the salesman. Many FMCG distributors have a trade counter at the warehouse where shopkeepers come in, pick up a few cartons, pay and leave. It is often the busiest and least controlled part of the business: a clerk with a cash box, a paper bill book and a habit of letting regulars "pay next time". Returns are the same story. A carton comes back from a shop, nobody records why, and it is either sold again after its date or quietly written off.

This chapter covers the counter, returns from every channel, and the cash count that closes the day. All of it is in the Enterprise edition. It is part of the [FMCG distribution guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-distribution-management/).

## Why the counter belongs in the same app

A shopkeeper who buys from the van on Tuesday and from the counter on Thursday is one customer with one balance. If the counter runs on a separate till, that balance splits in two. The salesman sees a shop under its limit while the counter has already given it credit, and the van's promotion is not applied when the same shop walks in. Running the counter in the field app keeps one customer record, one credit limit, one risk band and one set of schemes across every channel. The counter is simply a third way to sell, beside pre-sales and van sales, and its sales, payments and returns are normal Odoo records like every other.

## Setting up the counter

1. In **Distribution, Configuration, Settings**, under Warehouse Counter, choose the **Counter Warehouse**. The counter sells from this warehouse and takes returns into it. Each company has one counter warehouse.
2. Give counter staff the **Counter** role: Settings, Users, open the user, Warehouse Counter.
3. In **Configuration, Notes and Coins**, list your currency's notes and coins once, so the drawer is counted by note rather than typed as a total.
4. Set the **Cash Count Tolerance**, the cash journal and the shortage and overage accounts in Settings.

The counter runs on a phone, a tablet or a counter PC, or a SUNMI handheld with a scanner.

## A counter sale

The counter home shows today's counter with Sell, Scan, Shops, Collect money, Take back goods, My sales today, Ask manager and Finish day, plus sales today and the cash in the drawer.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-trade-counter-cash-and-carry/c01-counter-home.webp" alt="Warehouse counter home screen for Karim with the counter open, selling from the main stock, tiles for Sell, Scan, Shops, Collect money, Take back goods, My sales today, Ask manager, Finish day and More tools, and sales today and cash in drawer" width={800} height={1720} caption="The counter home: one screen for the clerk's whole day." />

1. **Sell** opens the catalogue with stock from the counter warehouse, by unit or carton. Scan with a keyboard or handheld scanner, or search.
2. Pick **Walk-in customer** or a named shop. A named shop brings its credit limit, band and promotions with it.
3. **Checkout** shows the payment options: Cash, Bank or card, Cheque, or On credit within the shop's limit. A walk-in pays in full.
4. **Confirm** posts the delivery, invoice and payment in the same step. Every sale has a receipt with Print, Share and PDF.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-trade-counter-cash-and-carry/x-ctr-payment.webp" alt="Counter sale payment screen with Cash, Bank or card, Cheque and On credit options, cash received 18.86, the sale lines Cola 330ml Can 24 pack and Mineral Water 500ml 12 pack, subtotal, tax, total 18.86 and Confirm button" width={800} height={1720} caption="Cash, card, cheque or credit within the limit, then one confirm." />

**Shops** lists every customer with what it owes and an Over limit warning, and **New** creates a customer on the spot. **Collect money** takes cash, bank or cheque against old debts, oldest invoice first, part payments allowed.

## Returns, from any channel

Returns work the same from the counter, the van and the order booker: open the customer, then **Return** on the sale the goods came from. The screen lists the open invoices and the recent sales that goods can be taken back against.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-trade-counter-cash-and-carry/x-ctr-return.webp" alt="Counter screen for Al Baraka Catering with three overdue open invoices and a Take goods back list of four recent counter sales, each with a Return button" width={800} height={1720} caption="Returns are always made against the sale the goods came from." />

For each line, the clerk or driver sets the quantity and a reason: damaged, expired or near expiry, wrong product delivered, customer refused part of the order, or other, with a note and a photo.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-trade-counter-cash-and-carry/x-van-return-sheet.webp" alt="Take back goods sheet for Al Noor General Store with quantity steppers for Orange Juice 1L and Potato Chips 150g and reasons Damaged, Expired or near expiry, Wrong product delivered, Customer refused part of the order and Other" width={800} height={1720} caption="Each returned line needs a reason, and the reason decides where it goes." />

The reason decides where the goods go:

- **Good goods** go back to stock: the counter warehouse, or the van they left from.
- **Expired or near expiry** goes to a **Quarantine** location and is kept for the claim to the principal, so it is never resold.
- **Damaged** goods are scrapped.

A credit note is posted for what was already invoiced, so the shop is not charged twice. At the counter, the refund can be paid in cash or left as credit on account. A return worth more than the **Returns Needing Approval** amount goes to the manager first. Damaged and expired goods flow into the [claims to principals](/odoo-guides/odoo-trade-promotions-principal-claims/).

## Closing the drawer

**Finish day** shows the arithmetic: cash sales plus cash debts collected minus cash refunded gives the cash expected in the drawer. The clerk counts it, by notes and coins if he likes, adds a note for any gap and presses **Close the day**.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-trade-counter-cash-and-carry/x-ctr-close.webp" alt="Close the day screen with sales, of which on credit, bank card and cheques, cash sales, cash debts collected, cash refunded and cash expected in the drawer, a Cash counted field, Count notes and coins and Close the day button" width={800} height={1720} caption="Cash expected, cash counted, then close." />

Counting by note is the same on the van at night. The app adds up each note and coin and shows Balanced, Short or Over against what is expected. The count then goes to the manager, who recounts on the phone or in the back office. A shortage above the tolerance is booked as money the salesperson owes; an overage is booked as a gain.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-trade-counter-cash-and-carry/x-bo-cashcount.webp" alt="Cash count record for a van settlement with cash expected 125.24, counted 130.24, difference 5.00, result Over, tolerance 2.00, a notes and coins table with counted and recount columns, and the chatter showing the overage booked" width={1600} height={1000} caption="A verified cash count: counted, recounted, and the overage booked." />

**Operations, Counter Days** lists each counter day with sales, cash, bank and credit sales, debts collected, cash expected, counted and the variance. A red row is a day that did not balance.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-trade-counter-cash-and-carry/bo-op-counter-days.webp" alt="Counter Days list with one row per day for Karim Counter showing sales, sales total, cash, bank, on credit, debts collected, cash expected, cash counted, variance and state open or closed, with two short days in red" width={1600} height={1000} caption="Counter Days: every drawer, expected against counted." />

With **Settle Before Selling** on, a counter cannot sell while an earlier day with sales is not closed, and a van cannot sell on a new day while an earlier day is not settled. A manager can let one person sell for one day from the approvals.

<Callout title="Limits on the counter">Counter sales need signal; they are not part of the offline queue. Each company has one counter warehouse. Lots and expiry dates are not tracked, so "expired" is a reason on the return, not a batch record. On the current build, the counter login's More, Expense claims shows "Claims not available, you do not have access": counter staff cannot file expense claims from the app yet.</Callout>

**Reporting, Sales by Channel** splits sales between the warehouse counter, field orders and each van, so the counter's share is visible beside the routes. The van side of the evening count is in the [van sales guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-van-sales/).
