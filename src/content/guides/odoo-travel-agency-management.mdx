A travel shop rarely loses money on the trip it sold. It loses it on the details around the trip: a passenger whose passport runs out four months after the holiday and gets turned away at check-in, a hotel that was never actually confirmed, a cancellation refunded in full when the supplier kept thirty per cent, a balance that should have come in six weeks before departure and did not. Each of those is a fact somebody knew at the time, written in a notebook or an email, not on a record anybody could check. This guide covers what a travel agency or tour operator needs from Odoo, what standard Odoo leaves out, and how the Travel Agency Management module runs the whole file on Odoo 19 Community.

## What a travel agency actually needs

Take away the brochure and a travel business needs a handful of things from its system:

1. **One file per trip.** The airline PNR, the IATA agency code it was sold under, the consultant, the customer and every passenger, in one place that everyone answers the phone from.
2. **Passports checked before money changes hands.** Many destinations want six months of validity past the travel date. The system should name the passenger who fails that, before the file is confirmed, not at the airport.
3. **Every part of the trip as its own line.** Flight, hotel, transfer, excursion, cruise, rail and insurance, each with its supplier, its net cost, its sell price and the locator the supplier gave back.
4. **Margin that is real.** Markup over net plus the commission the supplier pays back, per line, per file and per consultant.
5. **Seats that cannot be oversold.** A tour operator selling a coach of forty five cannot let three consultants sell forty eight.
6. **Money in before tickets go out.** A deposit before ticketing, a balance by a set number of days before departure, and a list of who is late.
7. **Cancellations priced by the rules you publish.** The agency fee and the supplier penalty both depend on how close to departure the change comes in.

## What standard Odoo does and does not do

Standard Odoo gives a travel business good general tools: contacts, sales quotations, invoices, payments and the accounting behind them. You can put a flight and a hotel on a quotation as two service products and invoice them.

What it does not know is anything specific to travel. There is no PNR, no passenger list with passport expiry, no supplier confirmation number that has to exist before a segment counts as booked, no net cost against sell price per line with the supplier's commission on top, no fixed departure with a seat count, no visa checklist and no cancellation fee by days before departure. You could add some fields with Studio, but the controls (refusing to confirm a file because one passport is short, refusing to oversell a departure) need code. That gap is what this module fills. The [travel agency solution page](/solutions/travel-agency/) describes the wider setup.

### How a file moves

A file opens as an **Enquiry**, moves to **Quoted** once something is on it, then **Confirmed**, **Ticketed** and **Travelled**, with Cancelled to the side. Each step has a gate. Confirm refuses a file with no travellers, nothing booked, no departure date, a passenger with no passport expiry, or a passport that runs out inside the validity window. Ticket refuses a file whose segments are still waiting on a supplier, or whose deposit has not come in. Mark Travelled refuses a file that still owes money while that block is switched on. The status cannot be written around the buttons: dragging a card across the board or importing a status is refused with an explanation, because those are the same write.

### Who sees what

There are three access groups. **Travel Consultant** sees the Sales menu and only their own files, travellers, receipts, visas and changes. **Operations and Ticketing** sees every file and the Operations menu. **Travel Manager** adds Reporting and Configuration. Every model carries a multi-company rule, and amounts on a file follow the currency of the company whose office sold it.

### Reporting

Three manager screens sit under Reporting. **Margin by Consultant** is a pivot of sold files with sell, supplier cost and gross margin by consultant and month. **Balance Due** lists every sold file past its balance date and still owing, grouped by departure month. **Load Factor** graphs seats booked against seats total on each departure.

## Setting it up, in order

1. Install Travel Agency Management. Installing with demo data gives you a sample office (Meridian Travel, City Centre) with consultants, suppliers, packages and files at every stage.
2. Open **Travel, Configuration, Settings**: IATA agency code, default markup (12 per cent out of the box), passport validity window (six months), deposit percent (20), balance due days before departure (42), the block on ticketing without a deposit and travel without the balance, the visa chase window and, if you are a tour operator, the margin scheme and its tax.
3. Add an office per branch under **Configuration, Offices**, with its code, its own IATA code and licence number if it has them, and the sales journal its invoices go to.
4. Add consultants under **Configuration, Consultants**, linked to their Odoo user, with a margin target and a commission percent.
5. Check **Configuration, Service Types** (flight, hotel, transfer, activity, cruise, rail, insurance, other): each needs the revenue product it invoices to, plus a default markup and commission.
6. Add suppliers under **Configuration, Suppliers** with how they are settled, settlement days, credit limit and the commission they pay you.
7. Load your visa rules and edit the fee schedule bands to match your booking conditions.
8. Turn on the five nightly jobs under Scheduled Actions once outgoing mail works. They ship switched off.
9. Run one real file end to end, enquiry to travelled, before going live.

<Callout title="A back office, not a booking engine">Nothing in this module talks to a GDS, an airline, a bed bank or a cruise line, and it does not import the BSP billing file or enter supplier bills. PNRs, locators and net costs are typed in from the supplier's own system, and vendor bills are entered in Accounting as you do now. If you want live fares or an online booking page, this module is not it.</Callout>

## Limitations

These are the module's own statements about what it does not do:

- **No GDS or supplier connection.** No live availability, fares or ticket issuing.
- **No online booking or customer portal.** The itinerary goes out as a PDF.
- **No BSP or ARC reconciliation.** The supplier record shows what is unsettled; it does not match the billing file line by line.
- **No supplier bills.** What you owe a supplier is tracked on the segment and marked settled by hand.
- **No dynamic packaging or seat maps.** Package prices are per adult and per child with a single supplement, and seats on a departure are a count.
- **Not a tax filing.** The margin scheme figure is written on the invoice for the accountant; nothing is posted or filed.
