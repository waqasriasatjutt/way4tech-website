A Turkish invoice is not a PDF you email. It is a UBL-TR file with a sixteen-character number and a UUID, it goes through the Revenue Administration (GİB, Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı), and whether it goes as an e-Fatura or an e-Arşiv depends on whether your customer is on GİB's list of registered users. The KDV on it may be split, with the buyer keeping part of it under tevkifat. When you pay rent to a person you withhold stopaj. Goods on the road need an e-İrsaliye, and the month closes on a Mizan laid out in the Tekdüzen chart. This guide explains what Odoo 19 gives you for all that, and then walks through the Way4Tech Turkish Localization Suite on a demo company in İstanbul.

## What a Turkish company needs from its ERP

| Need | Turkish term | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| Two kinds of identity number | VKN, TCKN | A company has a 10-digit VKN (Vergi Kimlik Numarası), a person an 11-digit TCKN (T.C. Kimlik Numarası). Routing and the UBL party block depend on which one it is. |
| Address and tax office | il, ilçe, vergi dairesi | Province, district and tax office on every partner and on the company. |
| VAT that is not just a rate | KDV, tevkifat, istisna | Standard 20%, reduced 10% and 1%; withholding VAT on listed services; exempt lines that must carry a reason code. |
| Income tax withheld at source | stopaj | Withheld on rent paid to people and on fees to self-employed professionals, reported on the Muhtasar return. |
| The right e-document | e-Fatura, e-Arşiv | e-Fatura when both sides are registered users, e-Arşiv for everybody else, with a daily report of e-Arşiv invoices. |
| Delivery notes | e-İrsaliye | The vehicle, the driver and the despatch time, for goods leaving the warehouse. |
| Month-end reports | Mizan, Bilanço, Gelir Tablosu | Trial balance, balance sheet and income statement in the Tekdüzen code ranges. |
| Monthly notices and returns | Form Ba/Bs, KDV-1, KDV-2, Muhtasar | Purchases and sales per counterparty above a threshold, and the figures for each return. |
| Electronic books | e-Defter | The journal (yevmiye) and general ledger (kebir), signed with the mali mühür. |

## What standard Odoo gives you

Odoo 19 ships `l10n_tr`, the Turkish chart of accounts with its taxes, including KDV at 20%, 10% and 1% and the tevkifat group taxes, one per ratio. That is the base the suite builds on, and you still load it yourself: the suite never installs or replaces a chart.

For e-invoicing, Odoo's own Turkish modules (`l10n_tr_nilvera` and its e-invoice and e-dispatch extensions) are tied to Nilvera as the integrator. If you already work with another özel entegratör, or you want an engine that is not tied to one provider, that is the gap. Odoo's single Tax ID field also accepts a VKN or a TCKN without saying which, and Odoo Community has no rule records for tevkifat, stopaj or exemption codes, no Mizan at a chosen Tekdüzen depth, and no Ba/Bs.

### How it is set up

1. **Load the Türkiye chart** on the company from Invoicing settings, before anything else.
2. **Install the suite**, then open **Türkiye, Configuration, Setup Wizard**: company VKN or TCKN, tax office, MERSİS, trade registry, province, district and environment, then the cost method (7/A or 7/B, deliberately with no default), e-documents, the integrator, document series, the GİB user list and a connection test.
3. **Check the health board** under **Configuration, Localization Health**. Every line is Healthy, Warning or Critical, with a button to the screen that fixes it.
4. **Clean the partners.** Each contact gets a Türkiye tab with VKN, TCKN, tax office, province and district, a checksum button and a GİB registration check. A server action moves numbers typed into the wrong box after an import.
5. **Keep your tax rules.** Tevkifat ratios, stopaj rates and exemption codes are records with effective dates, confirmed with your mali müşavir and then marked Verified.
6. **Post invoices as usual.** The e-document type, scenario and invoice type are worked out, numbered and sent from a background queue.

### Environments and the Verified tick

Each company runs in Development, Staging or Production. Only in Development and Staging may the mock integrator and the demo loader be used. A company in Production refuses to transmit any document that relies on a rule nobody has marked Verified, whether a tevkifat rule, an exemption code, a stopaj rule or the UBL-TR schema version. The suite ships no rates at all, so the figures you rely on are always ones somebody in your company confirmed.

<Callout title="Before you buy: what this version does not do">
- **It does not connect to GİB out of the box.** The only integrator adapter shipped is a mock that never leaves the database. A real özel entegratör needs its adapter written against the suite's interface. Everything up to that point, routing, numbering and the validated UBL-TR, works now.
- **Credit notes do not build as e-documents** in this version, and invoices to foreign buyers are refused because they have no VKN, so export invoicing needs a fix first.
- **It files nothing.** The declaration screens give the figures; KDV, Muhtasar and Ba/Bs are filed in GİB's systems.
- **It does not sign the e-Defter.** It builds and validates the books; signing and the berat go through a certified integrator.
- **ÖTV rules are configuration only.** Nothing on an invoice line reads them yet.
- **It is not certified by GİB** or affiliated with it.
</Callout>
