A Labrador comes in limping on a Tuesday. The vet needs to see, before she touches him, that he reacted badly to meloxicam last spring, that he has put on three kilos since his booster, and that the owner's insurer wants the full history for any claim. Reception needs to know he is due his leptospirosis booster next month, and the kennel team needs to know he is booked in for a week's boarding over half term, which he cannot do without that booster. Every veterinary practice runs on this kind of joined-up record. This guide shows what that takes in Odoo, what standard Odoo does not give you, and how our Veterinary Clinic Management module fills the gap.

## What a practice needs from its system

1. **The animal as the record.** Species, breed, sex, neuter status, age, microchip, temperament and weight, linked to the owner who pays.
2. **A diary that refuses clashes.** One vet cannot be in two consultations at once, and one theatre cannot hold two surgeries.
3. **Clinical notes in the format vets use.** Subjective, objective, assessment, plan, with the vitals beside them.
4. **Preventive care that runs itself.** A puppy course that schedules its own boosters, every dose with a traceable batch, and a reminder that brings the client back.
5. **Medicines with their rules.** Controlled drugs recorded, stock taken out of the dispensary, and withdrawal periods on food-producing animals.
6. **The rest of the building.** Boarding, day care, in-patients and grooming, by kennel.
7. **The money.** Invoices from the consultation and the stay, and insurance claims with the excess and the shortfall.

## What standard Odoo does and does not do

Standard Odoo gives you contacts for owners, products and services with prices, invoicing and Inventory for medicine stock. That is the base the module builds on. What it does not have is any notion of an animal. There is no species, breed or microchip, no weight history, no consultation with SOAP notes and vitals, no vaccination protocol, no booster, no controlled drug list, no withdrawal period and no kennel. Most practices that try to run on plain Odoo end up with the animal's name in the contact's notes, which works until a client has three cats.

It adds a Veterinary app with six menus: Clinical, Preventive Care, Boarding, Billing, Reporting and Configuration.

## The patient and the consultation

Each animal is a **patient** with its owner, species, breed and the breed's watch-out note, sex, neuter status, date of birth, temperament, microchip and pet passport. Allergies, chronic conditions and a diet note sit on the Clinical tab, where the vet sees them before treating. Every weight taken at a consultation lands in the weight history, and a line chart shows the trend.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-veterinary-clinic-management/patients.webp" alt="Veterinary patients in Odoo as cards with pet name, species, breed and owner" width={1440} height={900} caption="Patients by animal, each linked to its owner." />

A **consultation** is booked in the diary for a vet and a room, and moves from scheduled to arrived, in progress, finished and invoiced, with no show and cancelled beside them. The vet writes the SOAP notes, records the vitals, adds the charges and presses Finish, which files the weight on the patient and puts a follow-up to-do on the vet if a date was set. Invoice raises the owner's invoice from the charges, and the discharge note prints or goes to the owner by email.

## Preventive care

Vaccination protocols per species schedule the course. When a dose is recorded as given, with its batch number, expiry and site, the next dose or the annual booster is scheduled straight away. Overdue doses have their own list, the vaccination card prints from the patient, and a daily job emails owners before a dose falls due.

## Medicines, labs and stays

A **prescription** names the medicine, dose, route, frequency and days, and dispensing it takes the quantity out of the clinic's dispensary stock. Products flagged as controlled drugs appear in the controlled drug register. On a food-producing animal, the withdrawal days on the line give the date meat or milk can be sold again, and the animal shows it. **Lab requests** go from requested to sample taken, sent and result in, with abnormal results flagged in red. **Boarding** covers boarding, day care, hospitalisation and grooming in kennels with feeding and medication plans, billed per night.

## How to set it up

1. Install Veterinary Clinic Management. Inventory and Accounting come with it.
2. In **Settings, Veterinary**, set the reminder window and the default appointment length.
3. Create the **clinic** with its code, licence number, emergency phone, journal and dispensary location, then its **rooms** and **team**, ticking who may prescribe.
4. Check the **species** list, add **breeds**, and set the **vaccination protocols** you follow.
5. Load your price list as products: consultations, vaccines, medicines and boarding.
6. Import owners and patients, apply the protocols, and turn on the reminder job.

<Callout title="Two settings that do nothing yet">Controlled Register Header and Check Vaccination Before Boarding appear in Settings but are not read by any screen in this version. Admitting a boarder always requires the Vaccination Checked tick on the stay, whatever the setting says.</Callout>

## Limitations

These come from reading the module's code:

- **No client portal or online booking.** The diary is for the practice.
- **Email reminders only.** No SMS.
- **The controlled drug register is a filtered list** of dispensed prescriptions with a controlled product, without a running balance or witness.
- **The allergy check on dispensing is a text match** of the product name against the allergy note.
- **No product screen for the veterinary flags.** Whether a product is a medicine, a vaccine or a controlled drug, and its withdrawal days, are set on import; withdrawal days can be edited on the prescription line.
- **Reception can read clinical notes.** The Reception and Nursing role is described as unable to read confidential clinical notes, but no field or record rule enforces it.
- **The vaccination card is the only vaccination document.** There is no separate rabies certificate layout, though each dose has a certificate number field.
- **An abnormal lab result can error.** Saving a result already ticked Abnormal fails when the requesting vet has an Odoo user; record the result, then tick Abnormal.
- **No lab or insurer link.** Results and claims are recorded by hand.
