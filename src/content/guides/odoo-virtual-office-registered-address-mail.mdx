A virtual office looks like the easiest money in a coworking space: a monthly fee for an address and a pigeonhole. It is also the part with a legal duty behind it. In most countries an operator that lets a business use its address as a registered office or trading address has to know who that business is, check identity before the service starts, keep it in date, and be able to show what happened to every letter, and on court papers and tax letters that record is the whole service. This chapter of the [coworking space management series](/odoo-guides/odoo-coworking-space-management/) shows how the module handles it.

## Four kinds of address service

**Virtual Office, Registered Addresses** (coordinators and managers) lists every service. A service belongs to a company account or to a member, sits at one building, and has a **type**:

| Type | Use |
|---|---|
| Registered address | the official address of a company on the public register |
| Trading address | the address a business trades from and prints on its letters |
| Mail forwarding | post sent on to another address at a set frequency |
| Mail holding | post kept for collection |

For a registered or trading address the **exact legal name** of the entity using it is required, with its company number, because that is what goes on the register you keep. Forwarding services carry the forwarding address and a frequency: daily, weekly or on request.

## The identity gate

Each service has an identity block: the **document type** (passport, national ID, driving licence or company certificate), its reference and expiry, and who checked it and when. The flow:

1. **Ask for Identity** moves a new service to Waiting on Identity.
2. The document is seen. Its type, reference and expiry are typed in.
3. A **space manager** presses **Identity Checked**, which records them and today's date. It is refused without a document and reference, or when the document has already expired. Nobody else can write the checked-by or checked-on fields, even by editing the record.
4. **Activate** puts the service live. While the company setting **Check Identity for Address Services** is on (the default), it is refused unless the identity is checked against a current document.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-virtual-office-registered-address-mail/live-service.webp" alt="Registered office address service RA/2026/0010 for Halden Bloom Ltd, company number 15873312, at Northlight Ancoats, identity verified against a passport expiring in 2031, checked on 6 November, state Active, with the post tab" width={1440} height={900} caption="A live registered office: who uses it and what was checked." />

The same gate sits on the add-on that bills the service. An add-on such as Registered Office Address can be marked as needing an identity check, and then it cannot start for anybody without a live address service whose identity has been checked.

**Suspend** pauses a live service by hand. **Terminate** ends it with today's date, and is refused while any post for it is still in the building.

## The post

**Front Desk, Post** opens on what is still here. Each item is logged against its service, with the received date and time, the sender, the **kind** (letter, parcel, legal, tax or bank), **statutory** for official correspondence such as court papers and tax letters, a tracking reference for parcels, and the days it has been waiting. Filters: Still Here, Official Correspondence, Member Not Told Yet.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-virtual-office-registered-address-mail/post.webp" alt="Post list showing items still in the building, with received date, the address service, who it is billed to, sender such as Companies House or HM Revenue and Customs, kind Legal or Court Papers, Tax Authority, Parcel or Bank, official correspondence ticked, days waiting and state Received or Member Told" width={1440} height={900} caption="What is waiting at reception, with official post flagged." />

| Button | Does | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Tell the Member | sends the Post Waiting email and stamps the time | a live service |
| Collected | records the hand-over and the time | the name of who collected it |
| Forwarded | records it as sent on | a forwarding address on the service |
| Return to Sender | records it as returned | nothing |

A letter can only leave the building once: collected, forwarded and returned items cannot be handled again.

## Setting up a virtual office, step by step

1. **Plan.** Create a Virtual Office plan with its monthly fee, any meeting room credits, and Valid At set to the buildings that sell it. On the demo it is 45 a month with two hours of meeting room time, Ancoats only.
2. **Add-ons.** Create Registered Office Address and Mail Handling as monthly add-on services, and tick Needs Identity Checks on the registered office one.
3. **Member or account.** Sign the customer up as a member on the plan, or under a company account if a company pays.
4. **Address service.** Create the service for that member or account, choose the type, and for a registered or trading address type the exact legal name and company number.
5. **Identity.** Ask for Identity, record the document seen, and have a space manager press Identity Checked.
6. **Go live.** Activate the service, then Start the add-on. Both are refused until step 5 is done.
7. **Every day.** Log the post as it arrives, tell the member, and record each item collected, forwarded or returned.
8. **Every month.** The billing run picks up the plan fee and the running add-ons for the month and puts them on one invoice per payer.

On the demo, open Virtual Office, Registered Addresses and compare a live service, one Waiting on Identity that cannot be activated yet, and one suspended because its document ran out. Then open Front Desk, Post with the Official Correspondence filter to see the letters that matter most.

## What it does not do

The module files nothing with Companies House or any regulator, does no electronic identity verification, and does not scan the post. It keeps the record an operator has to be able to produce: who uses the address, what document was seen, who checked it and when, and what happened to every item. Billing for the address and the mail handling goes through add-ons on the monthly run, covered in [memberships, company accounts and the monthly billing run](/odoo-guides/odoo-coworking-membership-billing/).
