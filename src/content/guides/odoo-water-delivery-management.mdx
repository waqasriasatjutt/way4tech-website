A water distributor counts its bottles once a year and finds four hundred missing. Some are in customers' store rooms with no deposit behind them, some cracked on the truck, some went to a kiosk that closed. Nobody can say which, because the business recorded what it sold and not what it lent. Meanwhile the coupon books sold last winter are a liability nobody has sized, and the health inspector wants to know which batch the bottle in a customer's kitchen came from. This guide covers what a bottled water business has to track and how to run one on Odoo 19 with our Water Delivery Management module.

## What a water business has to track

A water business looks like a delivery company, but its real asset is the returnable container. Every full bottle that leaves the truck is yours, sitting in somebody else's kitchen, and every one should have a deposit behind it.

| What | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Containers out, per customer | The asset you cannot see, and the number that turns into write offs |
| Deposit held against them | Money you owe back, and the cover for bottles that never return |
| Coupons sold and not redeemed | Water paid for and not yet delivered: a liability |
| Standing orders | Which customers are due which day, so the run builds itself |
| What went out on the truck and what came back | Bottles walk off trucks; the run has to balance |
| Cash expected against cash counted | Per run and per driver |
| Batch and test log | What an inspector asks first, and what a recall needs |
| Coolers on rental | A monthly fee and a sanitisation cycle |

## What standard Odoo does and does not cover

Standard Odoo has no water delivery app. People try:

- **Sales and Inventory.** Good for selling cartons, but stock in Inventory is stock in your warehouse. A bottle in a customer's kitchen is not modelled, so neither is its deposit.
- **Subscriptions (Enterprise).** Recurring invoices, but no bottle allowance consumed by deliveries and no coupons.
- **Van sales or distribution modules.** Routes and cash collection, but no returnable container ledger. See the [van sales guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-van-sales/) if cartons are all you deliver.

Odoo gives you the parts underneath: customers, Invoicing with VAT, lot numbers in Inventory, and the chatter for an audit trail. The module builds the water business on top.

## The module

[Water Delivery Management](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_water_delivery) (USD 199) runs on Odoo 19 Community and needs Inventory and Invoicing, both part of Community. It adds one app, **Water**, with six menus:

| Menu | Screens |
|---|---|
| Operations | Deliveries, Daily Runs, Runs to Reconcile, Missed Deliveries |
| Customers | Accounts, Standing Orders, Container Ledger, Coupon Books, Subscriptions |
| Rentals | Dispensers, Sanitisation Due, Service Visits |
| Quality | Production Batches, Water Quality Log |
| Reporting | Revenue by Round, Deposit Liability, Container Losses |
| Configuration | Settings, Plants and Depots, Rounds, Container Types, Price Plans, Subscription Plans, Drivers, Vehicles |

Four roles split the work: **Driver** (own runs and stops, no pricing, no ledger edits), **Water Quality Officer** (the test log and batches), **Dispatcher** (rounds, runs, reconciliation and accounts) and **Water Delivery Manager** (everything). The records that carry money, runs, deliveries, batches, coupon books and subscriptions, only change status through their buttons, so no check can be skipped by editing a field.

## How a day works

1. **Build the runs.** Each round's standing orders say which customers are due which day. A button or the nightly job builds tomorrow's run with one planned stop per customer, priced from the customer's plan.
2. **Load out.** Count the containers onto the truck and name the released batch for each. Load Out refuses a load that will not fit on the vehicle or a batch the lab has not released.
3. **Deliver.** At each stop the driver records bottles left, empties back, coupons taken, deposit charged, cash collected and who signed. The delivery moves the customer's ledger, redeems coupons and uses up the subscription allowance.
4. **Miss with a reason.** A missed stop needs a reason, and a reschedule date builds the return visit.
5. **Reconcile.** Back at the plant, loaded less delivered less brought back has to be zero, within your tolerance, before the run closes.
6. **Bill.** Coupon books are invoiced when sold, subscriptions at the end of each period, account customers once a month from their deliveries.

### The container ledger

Every movement writes a line: opening balance, delivered, empties collected, written off, count adjustment or deposit only. The running balance is what the customer holds. Deposit required is the containers held times the deposit; when it is more than the deposit held, the account shows a shortfall, and a delivery that would leave the customer short is refused unless the driver takes the difference.

### Coupons and subscriptions

Coupon books are sold in advance at the plan's coupon price, invoiced up front, and redeemed one coupon per bottle at the door, oldest book first. A driver has no rights to edit a book. Subscriptions carry a monthly allowance and an overage price; with no overage price, the round stops at the allowance.

### Quality and recalls

Every fill is a batch with its source, shift and operator, sent for testing with pH, dissolved solids, turbidity, chlorine and coliform. A batch with a failed or missing test cannot be released. Every delivery line names its batch, so quarantining a batch lists every customer who already has it.

### Dispensers

Coolers are tracked from stock to rental, service, collection and scrap. Installing one starts the sanitisation cycle, a job raises the visit when it is due, and a monthly job bills the rental, skipping customers whose subscription includes the cooler.

## Limitations

These come from reading the module's code, and we would rather you know them before you buy:

- **No portal, ordering page or separate driver app.** Drivers use Odoo in a phone browser and see only their own runs and stops.
- **No stock moves.** Plants name a warehouse and a released batch can create a stock lot, but deliveries do not move stock in Inventory.
- **Deposits are not posted to the accounts.** The Deposit Liability report gives the figure; booking it is for your accountant.
- **Delivery times ignore the time zone.** Times built from the standing order windows are stored as if in UTC, so in Riyadh a 07:00 window shows as 10:00.
- **Rental invoices carry no tax.** Dispenser rental lines have no product, so VAT has to be added by hand.
- **Chargeable service visits are not invoiced.** The flag is recorded only.
- **Routine test lines start at a passing value.** Sending a batch for testing fills each reading with its lower limit; enter the real readings before release.
- **Every scheduled job ships switched off,** from building runs to billing, until you turn them on.
