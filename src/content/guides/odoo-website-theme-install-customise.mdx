A theme is a head start, not a finished site. It arrives full of sample names, sample prices and sample numbers, and the job is replacing every one with yours before a customer sees it. All of that happens in Odoo's own website editor; nothing here needs code. This chapter takes a Way4Tech theme from the Apps screen to a site you can publish, using the clinic theme demo for the screenshots. The same steps apply to every theme in the [series](/odoo-guides/odoo-website-themes/).

## Install the module and pick it as the website theme

1. Put the `wt_theme_<name>` folder in your addons path (on Odoo.sh, push it to your repository), open **Apps**, click **Update Apps List** in developer mode, and clear the default Apps filter in the search bar so themes are listed. Install it. The only dependency is Website.
2. Open the website editor, go to the **Theme** tab and click **Switch Theme**, or open the theme chooser. Find your theme's card and click **Use this theme**.
3. Check the result in **Website, Site, Pages**: the theme's four pages (About, a services page, a showcase page and Contact) are there, published and indexed, and the homepage shows nine sections.

Step 2 is the one people skip. Odoo loads a theme module's stylesheet, palette and fonts only for a website whose theme is set to that module. Installed from Apps alone, the pages and menu entries appear but the opening banner, the buttons and the coloured call to action band lose their styling. If your Theme tab still shows Odoo's purple palette and the Inter font after install, the theme is installed but not picked.

## The editor in three tabs

Open **Website** and click **Edit**. The panel on the right has three tabs.

**Blocks** lists Odoo's own sections by category (Intro, Columns, Content, Images, People, Text, Contact and Forms, Social, Catalog) and the smaller inner content pieces such as text, button, image, video, accordion, card, form and map. Drag one onto the page to add it.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-theme-install-customise/ed-editor-blocks.webp" alt="Odoo 19 website editor open on the clinic theme homepage with the Blocks tab listing section categories and inner content blocks" width={1440} height={900} caption="The Blocks tab. The theme's own sections are not in this list, except on the salon theme." />

**Style** shows the options of whatever you clicked. Click inside a section and it gets a blue frame with arrows to move it up or down and a small toolbar to duplicate or delete it, and the Style tab shows its layout, background, content width, height and visibility. Duplicating is how you reuse a theme section: a second services grid, or the call to action band on another page.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-theme-install-customise/ed-editor-section.webp" alt="A section of the clinic theme selected in the Odoo editor with a blue frame, move arrows and the Style tab showing layout, background, content width, height and visibility" width={1440} height={900} caption="A selected section: move it with the arrows, duplicate or delete it from its toolbar." />

**Theme** holds the site wide settings, covered below.

## Replace the words and pictures

Click any heading or paragraph and type. Select words for the text toolbar: bold, links, colour, alignment. Start at the top. The opening banner on every theme has an eyebrow line, a headline with one coloured word, a paragraph and three stat figures such as "1.2K+ Members" or "250+ Cases Won". Those numbers are samples. Publishing a figure you cannot back up is worse than having none, so change them or delete the stats row.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-theme-install-customise/ed-editor-text.webp" alt="The clinic theme banner in edit mode with the headline selected for typing" width={1440} height={900} caption="Click and type. The stat figures under the buttons are sample text too." />

Then images. Click one and use **Replace** in the Style tab to upload yours or pick from the media library. Most of the themes use icons and Odoo's placeholder images rather than photographs, so this step does more for how the site looks than any colour change. The clinic's four doctor cards, for example, point at Odoo's default team pictures and need real photos.

Your logo and company name come from **Settings, Companies**. The phone number, Sign in link and Contact Us button in the header are Odoo's own header elements: click the header to change its template, background, scroll effect and which elements show. Click the footer for its layout, colours and the copyright bar. The footer's Useful Links and About us text are Odoo defaults and need editing like anything else.

## Colours, fonts and buttons in the Theme tab

The Theme tab has the palette (Main, Light and Dark, Color Presets), Switch Theme, languages, page layout and background, then size, family, line height and margins for paragraphs and headings, button style, font, padding and corner radius, link style, input fields, and Advanced options such as header visibility and code injection.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-theme-install-customise/ed-editor-theme.webp" alt="Odoo editor Theme tab with the colour palette, Switch Theme button, language and page layout options and paragraph and heading font settings" width={1440} height={900} caption="The Theme tab. Switch Theme is in the Website group." />

Each Way4Tech theme sets these to its own defaults: a palette named after the theme (`wt-gym`, `wt-lawfirm`), its Google fonts for headings, body, menu and buttons, square, pill or slightly rounded buttons, and the header and footer layouts.

<Callout title="Palette changes do not reach the theme's own sections">Changing a palette colour recolours Odoo's blocks, links, the header and the footer. The theme's nine sections take their colours from the theme stylesheet and from the coloured word typed into each headline, so they keep the theme's colours. To change them, set a text colour or a section background in the editor, or have the stylesheet edited.</Callout>

Check every page on a phone before publishing: the phone icon in the top bar switches the preview. Each theme section stacks to one column at phone width. A section's visibility option can hide it on mobile only.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-theme-install-customise/ed-mobile-preview.webp" alt="Mobile preview of the clinic theme homepage in the Odoo website app showing the banner stacked in one column" width={1440} height={900} caption="Mobile preview from the phone icon in the top bar." />

**Save** at the top of the panel writes your changes; **Discard** throws them away. Nothing is saved as you type.

## Pages and the menu

The **Site** menu has Homepage, Menu Editor, Pages, Properties, Optimize SEO and the HTML / CSS Editor.

- **New, Page** adds a page. Fill it from the Blocks tab, or duplicate sections from a theme page and move them across.
- **Site, Properties** sets the current page's title, URL, menu entry, published and indexed flags.
- **Site, Optimize SEO** sets each page's search title, description and sharing image. Theme pages are published and indexed from the start with titles like About Us and Services; give each a real title and description.
- **Site, Menu Editor** reorders the header links by drag, makes sub menus by dragging right, and renames, relinks or deletes entries.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-theme-install-customise/ed-menu-editor.webp" alt="Odoo Edit Menu dialog listing Home, About, Services, Doctors, Contact and Contact us with edit and delete icons" width={1440} height={900} caption="If both Contact and Contact us are listed, delete Contact us: it is Odoo's own page." />

Each theme removes Odoo's Contact us menu entry once, at install. On the demos it is still there next to the theme's Contact, so check your menu and delete it if it survived.

## Make the contact form work

Every theme ends its homepage and its Contact page with a contact block: address, phone, email and hours on the left, a message form on the right. In the code, that form posts to `/website_form/`, an address Odoo 19 does not answer, and it is not built as Odoo's form block. A visitor can fill it in, but the message does not reach you.

Replace it before you publish:

1. Edit the Contact page, click the form column and delete the form.
2. From **Blocks, Inner content**, drag **Form** into the empty column.
3. In the Style tab pick the action: **Send an E-mail** to your address, or **Create a Lead** if CRM is installed.
4. Keep the fields you want (name, phone, email, subject, message), set the thank you page and Save.
5. Repeat on the homepage, which has the same block at the bottom.

## Switching to another theme

The theme chooser changes the look in one click, but these themes create ordinary website pages and menu entries that stay behind. Several share addresses: `/about-us`, `/services` and `/contact` on all of them, `/cases` on both consulting and law firm. Installing a second theme next to the first gives you two homepages competing and duplicate addresses.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-theme-install-customise/ed-theme-kanban.webp" alt="Odoo Pick a Theme screen with theme cards and Use this theme buttons" width={1440} height={900} caption="The theme chooser. Use it after the old theme is uninstalled, not before." />

The clean way: try the new theme on a copy of the database, note the edits you want to keep, uninstall the old theme module in Apps (that removes its pages, menu entries and homepage content), then pick the new one in the chooser and check Site, Pages and the Menu Editor for anything left over. To go back to a plain Odoo site, uninstall the theme and choose one of Odoo's own themes.

<Callout title="One theme per database">The Way4Tech theme modules do not block each other, and their pages and homepage content are written for the whole database rather than one website. Keep a single Way4Tech theme installed, even if you run several websites.</Callout>
