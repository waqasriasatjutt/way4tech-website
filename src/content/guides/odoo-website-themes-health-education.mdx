Clinics, gyms, schools and charities have one thing in common online: the visitor is deciding whether to trust you with their health, their body, their child or their money. The pages that win that are the people (doctors, coaches, faculty, the team), proof (stats, graduates, impact figures) and an easy first step. These four themes are built around that. Each costs USD 39, needs only the Website app on Odoo 19 Community, and is edited in the normal Odoo editor, as shown in the [install and customise chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-website-theme-install-customise/).

The gym, education and NGO themes share the common skeleton (banner, six services, four reasons, a six card showcase, four people, three testimonials, four questions, a call to action band, contact). The clinic theme has its own section set.

## Clinic and Hospital

For clinics, hospitals, dental practices, vet clinics and physiotherapy centres. Teal and blue, soft and rounded, and the most finished looking of the themes.

- **Palette:** teal `#00A8A8` primary, blue `#0066CC` secondary, green `#4ADE80` accent, `#1E293B` and `#F0F9FF`.
- **Fonts:** Plus Jakarta Sans headings, Inter for body, menu and buttons. Pill shaped buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About (`/about-us`), Services, Doctors (`/doctors`), Contact.
- **Sections:** a banner with Book Appointment and Call Us buttons and an opening hours card; Care For Every Stage Of Life (six specialities: cardiology, paediatrics, dental care, ophthalmology, gynaecology, lab and diagnostics); Healthcare With Heart (four reasons); Specialists You Can Trust (four doctors with speciality, credentials and a See All Doctors button); a stats band; a Book An Appointment Online band; testimonials; a five question accordion with an Ask A Doctor button; and contact, whose form includes a Department choice.

What to change first: the four doctor cards point at Odoo's default team images, so on the demo they show empty photo placeholders. Upload real photos, and only list credentials your doctors hold. Point Book Appointment at whatever takes bookings. If you run the clinic itself in Odoo, the [clinic management guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-clinic-management/) covers patients and appointments; the theme does not read that data.

## Gym and Fitness

For gyms, fitness studios, CrossFit boxes, yoga and pilates studios and personal trainers. Lime on near black, condensed type, square buttons.

- **Palette:** lime `#84CC16` primary, `#0F172A` secondary, yellow `#FACC15` accent, `#020617` and `#F7F8F3`.
- **Fonts:** Anton headings, Barlow body and buttons, Barlow Condensed for the menu.
- **Pages and menu:** About, Train With Us (`/services`), Membership (`/membership`), Contact.
- **Services:** strength, conditioning, yoga and mobility, CrossFit, boxing and one to one coaching.
- **Showcase:** six membership plans, each with a tag and price line (Drop-In $25, Open Gym $89/mo and so on), a title and a sentence.
- **Other sections:** Why People Stay, Coaches (four), member quotes, questions on trials, beginners, hours and contracts, a Free Week Trial band.

What to change first: your real plans and prices in the six cards, and Free Week Trial pointed at your sign up page. For memberships, class booking and check in run inside Odoo, see the [gym management guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-gym-management/).

## School and Education

For schools, colleges, academies and training centres. Royal blue with amber, a serif for headings.

- **Palette:** blue `#1E40AF` primary, amber `#F59E0B` secondary, cyan `#06B6D4` accent, `#1E293B` and `#F0F9FF`.
- **Fonts:** Merriweather headings, Nunito for body, menu and buttons. Pill buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About, Programmes (`/services`), Recent Graduates (`/programmes`), Contact.
- **Programmes:** STEM, humanities, business, arts, languages and athletics.
- **Showcase:** six recent graduates, each with the university and subject they went on to.
- **Other sections:** Why Families Choose Us, Faculty (four), testimonials, questions on applications and scholarships, an Apply Now band.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-themes-health-education/education-sec04.webp" alt="Recent Graduates section of the education theme with six cards naming universities and subjects" width={1440} height={538} caption="Education: the Recent Graduates showcase." />

What to change first: the URLs. The Programmes menu entry opens `/services` and the Recent Graduates page lives at `/programmes`. Set them straight in Site, Properties. Then replace the sample graduate destinations with real ones, with permission. The [school management guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-school-management/) covers admissions and report cards inside Odoo.

## NGO and Nonprofit

For charities, foundations and community organisations. Warm orange and teal, a serif for headings.

- **Palette:** orange `#EA580C` primary, teal `#0F766E` secondary, yellow `#FACC15` accent, `#1F2937` and `#FEF7F0`.
- **Fonts:** Source Serif Pro headings, Source Sans Pro for body, menu and buttons. Pill buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About, Our Programmes (`/services`), Recent Impact (`/impact`), Contact.
- **Programmes:** education, healthcare, clean water, food security, women and girls, climate action.
- **Showcase:** six impact figures such as schools built or meals served, each with a line of detail.
- **Other sections:** Why People Give, The Team (four), testimonials, questions on where donations go and tax, a Donate Now band.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-themes-health-education/ngo-sec04.webp" alt="Recent Impact section of the NGO theme with six figures such as 12 New Schools and 38 Wells" width={1440} height={538} caption="NGO: six impact figures, all sample numbers." />

<Callout title="No donations built in">Donate Now is a link and the theme has no payment of its own. Point it at your donation page. The sample impact figures and the "per dollar" claim in Why People Give are placeholders; publish only numbers your accounts support.</Callout>

## Set up checklist

1. Install, pick the theme as the website theme, and check the Theme tab palette.
2. Company name and logo, so the header and footer match.
3. Banner text and the three stat figures.
4. People with real photos: doctors, coaches, faculty or team.
5. The showcase: plans, graduates or impact figures you can stand behind.
6. Buttons pointed at booking, sign up, applications or donations.
7. The contact form replaced with Odoo's Form block, the stray Contact us menu entry removed, SEO titles set.

The salon and spa theme, the one theme with photographs and its sections in the Blocks panel, is covered with the salon modules in [which salon module do I need](/odoo-guides/odoo-salon-front-desk-website/).
