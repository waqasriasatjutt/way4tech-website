Hospitality sites sell a feeling first and a booking second. A visitor looks at rooms, dishes or trips, decides they want it, and then needs one obvious button. These four themes put the showcase in the middle of the homepage and a booking style button at the top, in the call to action band and in the contact block. What they do not do is take the booking. Every Book, Reserve and Get Tickets button is a link you point at the system that actually holds availability. Each theme costs USD 39 and needs only Odoo 19 Website; editing is covered in the [install and customise chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-website-theme-install-customise/).

All four use the common skeleton: banner, six services, four reasons, a six card showcase, four people, three testimonials, four questions, a call to action band and contact.

## Hotel and Resort

For hotels, resorts, B and Bs, lodges and holiday rentals. Deep teal and gold, Cormorant Garamond headings, square buttons, a quiet tone ("A Quieter Coast.").

- **Palette:** teal `#1A4D5C` primary, gold `#C9A961` secondary, `#E2D9C9` accent, `#0F2D38` and `#F8F5EE`.
- **Fonts:** Cormorant Garamond headings, Inter for body, menu and buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About, Stay With Us (`/services`), The Story (`/story`), Contact.
- **Stay With Us:** six room types with size and features, from Garden Rooms to Whole-Property hire.
- **Showcase:** a six step story of the property, from the purchase to the latest addition.
- **Other sections:** On Property (restaurant, gardens, treatment room, service), The People (four), guest quotes, questions on cancellation and children, a Book A Stay band.

What to change first: point Book A Stay and See Rooms at your booking channel, replace the property story with yours, and put your real cancellation and children policies in the questions. If the hotel runs its front desk in Odoo, see the [hotel management guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-hotel-management/).

## Restaurant

For restaurants, cafes, bistros and pizzerias. Tomato red and navy, an elegant serif with a round sans.

- **Palette:** red `#C0392B` primary, navy `#2C3E50` secondary, yellow `#F1C40F` accent, `#1B2631` and `#FFF8F0`.
- **Fonts:** Cormorant Garamond headings, Poppins for body, menu and buttons. Pill buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About, On The Menu (`/services`), From The Kitchen (`/menu`), Contact.
- **On The Menu:** six categories: wood fired pizzas, hand rolled pasta, garden salads, a tiramisu bar, the wine cellar and an espresso bar.
- **Showcase:** six signature dishes, each with a course, a name and a line of description.
- **Other sections:** Why Diners Stay, In The Kitchen (four), testimonials, questions on reservations and vegetarian options, a Reserve A Table band, and a contact block titled Find Us. Book Us.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-themes-hospitality-events/restaurant-sec04.webp" alt="From The Kitchen section of the restaurant theme with six dish cards" width={1440} height={1128} caption="Restaurant: six dishes from the kitchen." />

What to change first: your real dishes and prices, and Reserve A Table pointed at your reservation page or phone number. The menu on the site does not follow your Point of Sale menu, so keep it short and keep it current. Table reservations and online orders inside Odoo are in the [restaurant management guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-restaurant-management/).

## Travel Agency and Tour

For travel agencies and tour operators. Cyan with amber and orange.

- **Palette:** cyan `#0891B2` primary, amber `#F59E0B` secondary, orange `#F97316` accent, `#0F172A` and `#F0FDFA`.
- **Fonts:** Playfair Display headings, Poppins for body, menu and buttons. Pill buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About, Trip Styles (`/services`), Featured Trips (`/trips`), Contact.
- **Trip Styles:** adventure, beach escapes, culinary, wildlife and safari, honeymoon, long haul tours.
- **Showcase:** six featured trips, each with a destination, nights and a price line, a name and a sentence.
- **Other sections:** Why Travellers Pick Us, Trip Designers (four), testimonials, questions on whether you are an agent or operator and on custom trips, a Browse Trips band.

What to change first: the six trips, with real durations and prices, and Plan A Custom Trip linked to your enquiry form. Bookings, deposits and supplier costs inside Odoo are covered in the [travel agency guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-travel-agency-management/).

## Event and Conference

For conferences, festivals, summits and corporate events. Violet and pink, a geometric sans.

- **Palette:** violet `#7C3AED` primary, amber `#F59E0B` secondary, pink `#EC4899` accent, `#0F172A` and `#FAFAFA`.
- **Fonts:** Sora headings, Inter for body, menu and buttons. Pill buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About, What's On (`/services`), Schedule (`/schedule`), Contact.
- **What's On:** keynotes, workshops, builder sessions, the hallway track, after hours and the awards.
- **Showcase:** a schedule of the event days and workshops as six cards with dates.
- **Other sections:** Why You'll Come Back, Speakers (four), attendee quotes, questions on refunds and sponsor passes, a Get Tickets band.

<Callout title="No ticketing or countdown in the code">The listing for this theme mentions a countdown, but the module has none, and Get Tickets is a plain link. Odoo Events sells tickets and publishes the agenda; Odoo's own Countdown block can be dragged in from the editor's inner content.</Callout>

If you run the event's suppliers, budget and guest list in Odoo, the [event management guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-event-management/) covers that side.

## Set up checklist

1. Install, pick the theme as the website theme, and check the Theme tab palette.
2. Company name and logo.
3. Banner headline, paragraph and stat figures.
4. The showcase cards: rooms, dishes, trips or schedule, with real prices and dates.
5. Book, Reserve, Browse and Get Tickets buttons linked to the system that holds availability.
6. The contact form replaced with Odoo's Form block.
7. SEO titles and descriptions set, and every page checked in the mobile preview.
