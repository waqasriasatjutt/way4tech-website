A services firm sells trust before it sells anything else. The visitor wants to know what you do, who will do it, and whether you have done it before for someone like them. These four themes are laid out around exactly that: a services grid, a showcase of past work, the people, and a direct way to get in touch. All four cost USD 39, need only the Odoo Website app and run on Odoo 19 Community. Install and editing steps are in the [install and customise chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-website-theme-install-customise/); this chapter is about what each theme contains and what to change first.

Consulting, Law Firm and Real Estate share the common Way4Tech skeleton: banner, six services, four reasons, a six card showcase, four people, three testimonials, a four question accordion, a call to action band and a contact block. Construction has its own section set.

## Consulting and Agency

For consulting firms, agencies, strategy practices and creative studios. The voice is blunt and senior: the sample headline is "We Don't Write Decks."

- **Palette:** black `#000000` primary, amber `#F59E0B` secondary, green `#10B981` accent, with `#1F2937` and `#F9FAFB`.
- **Fonts:** IBM Plex Serif headings, IBM Plex Sans for body, menu and buttons. Square buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About (`/about-us`), What We Do (`/services`), Selected Engagements (`/cases`), Contact (`/contact`).
- **Showcase:** six selected engagements, each with a practice tag, a title and a one line result, for example SaaS Re-Pricing or Manufacturing Redesign.
- **Other sections:** What We Do (growth strategy, operating model and four more), Why Boards Hire Us, The Partners (four), testimonials, questions about scoping and retainers, Start A Conversation band.

What to change first: rewrite the six engagements as your own anonymised results, with numbers you can stand behind, and put your real partners in The Partners. The buttons Start A Conversation and Case Studies jump to the contact block and the services section by default.

## Law Firm

For law firms, advocates, solicitors and legal consultancies. Navy and gold, serif throughout.

- **Palette:** gold `#B8860B` primary, navy `#1A2E4D` secondary, `#D4AF37` accent, `#0F1A2E` dark and `#FAF7F0` light.
- **Fonts:** Playfair Display headings, Lora for body, menu and buttons. Square buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About, Practice Areas (`/services`), Recent Wins (`/cases`), Contact.
- **Practice Areas:** six areas: corporate, litigation, tax, family, real estate and criminal defence.
- **Showcase:** six recent wins, each a short case summary.
- **Other sections:** Why The Firm, Senior Partners (four), testimonials, questions on consultations and fees, a Free Consultation band.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-themes-professional-services/lawfirm-sec02.webp" alt="Practice Areas section of the law firm website theme with six cards: Corporate Law, Litigation, Tax Law, Family Law, Real Estate and Criminal Defence" width={1440} height={558} caption="Law Firm: the Practice Areas section, also the main block of the /services page." />

What to change first: the practice areas to the ones you actually take, and the Recent Wins to matters you may publish. Many bar councils restrict advertising results, so check before you fill that page. The sample FAQ answers about consultation and fees also need your real terms.

## Real Estate

For estate agencies, brokers and developers. Deep blue with amber and green accents.

- **Palette:** blue `#1E3A8A` primary, amber `#F59E0B` secondary, green `#10B981` accent, `#0F172A` and `#F8FAFC`.
- **Fonts:** Manrope headings, Inter for body, menu and buttons. Slightly rounded buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About, Property Types (`/services`), Featured Listings (`/listings`), Contact.
- **Property Types:** family houses, apartments, commercial, land, rentals and investment.
- **Showcase:** six featured listings, each with a type, a line of beds, size and price, and a name such as Riverside Penthouse.
- **Other sections:** Why Buyers Choose Us, Our Brokers (four), testimonials, questions about buyer fees and viewings, a Search Listings band.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-themes-professional-services/realestate-sec04.webp" alt="Featured Listings section of the real estate website theme with six property cards such as Riverside Penthouse and Oakwood Lane Family Home with size and price lines" width={1440} height={538} caption="Real Estate: featured listings are typed cards, not property records." />

<Callout title="Listings are text">The theme does not read Odoo products or any property records. Each listing is a card you type. Keep the page to a handful of featured properties and point Search Listings and Book A Viewing at wherever your full listings and viewing requests are handled.</Callout>

## Construction

For builders, contractors, engineers, architects and fit out firms. This theme was built separately from the others, with its own section set and the boldest look.

- **Palette:** orange `#FF6B35` primary, slate `#2B3A55` secondary, yellow `#FFB627` accent, `#1F2937` and `#F8F9FA`.
- **Fonts:** Bebas Neue headings, Roboto Condensed for body, menu and buttons. Square buttons, filled header links, and the **sidebar** header template.
- **Pages and menu:** About, Services, Projects (`/projects`), Contact.
- **Sections:** banner with a large years on site badge, Services That Build Cities (six), Built On Trust (four reasons and an About the Firm button), Projects We're Proud Of (six cards with photos from Odoo's image library and a Browse All button), a stats band, People Who Build It (four), testimonials, a Request a Quote band and contact. It has no questions section.

<Shot src="/odoo-guides/odoo-website-themes-professional-services/construction-sec02.webp" alt="Services That Build Cities section of the construction website theme with six cards such as Commercial Construction, Residential Projects and Industrial and Plant" width={1440} height={636} caption="Construction: the services grid." />

What to change first: the six project photos, which are Odoo library placeholders, then the team. The years badge and the stats band are sample figures.

## Set up checklist for all four

1. Install and pick the theme as the website theme, then confirm the Theme tab shows its palette.
2. Company name and logo in Settings, Companies.
3. Banner: eyebrow line, headline, paragraph and the three stat figures.
4. The showcase cards and the team, with real names and photos.
5. Every button pointed at a real page: by default they jump to the contact block or another section.
6. The contact form replaced with Odoo's Form block, set to email you or create a CRM lead.
7. Odoo's Contact us menu entry deleted if it is still in the header, and an SEO title on each page.

For a law firm that also wants matters, time and billing in Odoo, see the [law firm management guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-law-firm-management/); for property records, the [real estate guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-real-estate-management/); for project costing, the [construction guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-construction-management/). The themes and those apps are separate: the theme does not read their data.
