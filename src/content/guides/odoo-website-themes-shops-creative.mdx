A brand site and a shop are two different jobs. The brand site tells people why your things are worth buying; the shop takes the order. These four themes do the first job. They give a shop, a fashion label, a photographer or a software company a homepage, a categories page, a showcase and a contact page in the right voice. They do not sell anything: none of them installs Odoo eCommerce or reads products, and every Shop or Start Free button is a link. Each costs USD 39 and needs only Website on Odoo 19 Community; editing is in the [install and customise chapter](/odoo-guides/odoo-website-theme-install-customise/).

All four use the common skeleton: banner, six category or service cards, four reasons, a six card showcase, the team, three testimonials, four questions, a call to action band and contact.

## eCommerce

For online shops, boutiques and retail brands. Black and amber with a red accent, a characterful grotesque for headings ("Better Things, Worn Often.").

- **Palette:** black `#000000` primary, amber `#F59E0B` secondary, red `#EF4444` accent, `#0A0A0A` and `#FAFAFA`.
- **Fonts:** Bricolage Grotesque headings, Inter for body, menu and buttons. Pill buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About, Categories (`/services`), Bestsellers (`/shop`), Contact.
- **Categories:** apparel, footwear, bags, accessories, home and gifts.
- **Showcase:** six bestsellers, each with a category, a name and a price and origin line.
- **Other sections:** Why People Buy From Us, About Us (four people), reviews, questions on returns, sizing, repair and sustainability, a Shop New band.

<Callout title="The /shop clash">The theme's Bestsellers page lives at /shop, which is also the address of Odoo's eCommerce shop. Odoo answers a real route before a website page, so once eCommerce is installed the shop wins and the Bestsellers page can no longer be opened, while its menu entry sits next to eCommerce's own Shop entry. On the demo, where eCommerce is installed, the header shows both. Change the Bestsellers page URL in Site, Properties, or delete it and keep the shop.</Callout>

What to change first: the six categories and bestsellers to yours, Shop New pointed at the real shop, and your returns and sizing policy in the questions.

## Fashion and Boutique

For fashion brands, ateliers, designers and boutiques. Black, gold and cream, Bodoni for headings, square buttons ("Quiet Luxury, Loud Craft."). There is no live demo of this theme; this section is written from the module code.

- **Palette:** black `#000000` primary, gold `#C8A45C` secondary, white `#FFFFFF` accent, `#0A0A0A` and `#F5F2EE`.
- **Fonts:** Bodoni Moda headings, Inter for body, menu and buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About, The Collections (`/services`), Lookbook (`/lookbook`), Contact.
- **The Collections:** outerwear, eveningwear, daywear, atelier, accessories and archive.
- **Showcase:** a six piece lookbook, from a camel wool coat to a couture gown.
- **Other sections:** The House Codes (in house atelier, natural fibres, lifetime repair, limited runs), The Atelier (four people), testimonials, questions on sizing, made to measure, press and sustainability, a Shop AW26 band, and contact titled Visit The Atelier.

What to change first: the lookbook pieces, with your own photographs, and Shop AW26 renamed for your current season and linked to the shop.

## Photography and Portfolio

For photographers, studios and portfolio sites. Black and white with one hot pink accent, Marcellus headings, square buttons ("Light. Frame. Story.").

- **Palette:** black `#000000` primary, white `#FFFFFF` secondary, pink `#FF3366` accent, `#0A0A0A` and `#F5F5F5`.
- **Fonts:** Marcellus headings, Inter for body, menu and buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About, Categories (`/services`), Recent Work (`/work`), Contact.
- **Categories:** weddings, portrait, brand campaigns, editorial, travel and motion.
- **Showcase:** six recent projects, each with a category and a short description.
- **Other sections:** Working Together (discovery call, production, edit and deliver, prints and album), Studio (two people, not four), testimonials, questions on travel, turnaround, editing style and backups, a View Portfolio band.

## SaaS and Startup

For software products, apps and startups. Violet, sky blue and pink, Space Grotesk headings, slightly rounded buttons ("Ship Faster With Less Friction."). This section is written from the module code.

- **Palette:** violet `#7C3AED` primary, sky `#0EA5E9` secondary, pink `#EC4899` accent, `#0F172A` and `#F8FAFC`.
- **Fonts:** Space Grotesk headings, Inter for body, menu and buttons.
- **Pages and menu:** About, Product Features (`/services`), Pricing (`/pricing`), Contact.
- **Product Features:** real time sync, AI assist, integrations, analytics, security and a developer API.
- **Showcase:** a pricing grid of six tiers: Free, Pro at $12 a seat, Business at $32 a seat, Enterprise, a startup discount and an open source plan.
- **Other sections:** Why Teams Switch, Engineered By (four), testimonials, questions on the trial, cancelling, self hosting and compliance, a Start Free band, and contact titled Talk To Sales.

What to change first: the tiers and prices to yours, every Start Free and tier button linked to your real sign up, and the Why Teams Switch claims (such as "5x Cheaper" and "Used By Top Teams") replaced with ones you can prove. The questions on SOC2 and GDPR are samples; answer them only with what your product actually holds.

## Set up checklist

1. Install, pick the theme as the website theme, check the Theme tab palette.
2. If Odoo eCommerce is or will be installed with the eCommerce theme, move the Bestsellers page off /shop.
3. Company name and logo.
4. Your categories and showcase cards, with your own photographs on fashion and photography.
5. Every Shop, Book and Start Free button pointed at a real page.
6. The contact form replaced with Odoo's Form block.
7. The stray Contact us menu entry removed and SEO titles set.

If your shop already runs on Shopify, the [Shopify connector guide](/odoo-guides/odoo-shopify-connector/) covers syncing orders and stock into Odoo; the theme is only for an Odoo hosted site.
