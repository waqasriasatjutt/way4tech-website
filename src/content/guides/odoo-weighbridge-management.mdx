At most weighbridges the operator still reads the indicator and types the number. That is where the money leaks: a digit misread at the end of a long shift, a truck weighed while still rolling onto the deck, a tare remembered from last week, a friendly driver and a rounder number. A scrap yard pays for what the ticket says came in, a mill invoices for what it says went out, and nobody can prove afterwards which figure was real. This guide covers what a weighbridge ticket has to record, where standard Odoo stops, and how our WT Weighbridge module takes the weight from the indicator itself in Odoo 19 Community.

## What a weighbridge ticket has to record

Every truck is weighed twice. An inbound truck bringing goods in is weighed loaded on arrival, which is the gross, and empty on the way out, which is the tare. An outbound truck is the other way round. Net is gross less tare, and a deduction for moisture, soil or impurities brings it down to the final net that is paid or invoiced.

| On the ticket | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Vehicle, driver, partner, product | Who and what the weight belongs to |
| Direction | Decides which weighing is the gross and which the tare |
| First and second weighing with time, operator and method | The evidence, and who stands behind it |
| Deduction with a reason | The difference between weight and value |
| Final net in the product's unit | What goes into stock and onto the bill |

The weak point is always the method. A weight read from the indicator by a machine at the moment the reading is stable is evidence. A weight typed by a person is an opinion.

## What standard Odoo does and does not do

Odoo Inventory has receipts, deliveries, products counted in kilograms or tonnes, and lots. It does not have a weighbridge ticket, two weighings, gross and tare, a vehicle's stored tare, deductions, a scale connection or an audit trail of how each weight was obtained. Without a module, weighbridge sites keep a separate ticket book or a standalone weighbridge program and retype the net into the receipt, which brings back exactly the error the scale was meant to remove.

## How the weight gets in

The free WT Weighbridge agent runs on the Windows PC in the weighbridge office and reads the indicator over RS232, a USB serial adapter or a serial-to-Ethernet converter. A manager pairs that PC with Odoo once, from a four step checklist in the Weighbridge app. After that, the ticket form on that PC shows the indicator reading in large figures, green when it is stable, and **Capture weight** works only on a stable reading.

Each capture is signed by the agent with a key only Odoo knows. Before recording it, Odoo checks the signature, that it is stable, that it was taken for this ticket, that the PC clock is within the allowed minutes of the server, that it has never been used before and that any camera images match it. Then it records the weighing with the station, the indicator and the raw value read.

## Vehicles and stored tare

Every truck is a vehicle record with its plate, owner or haulier, usual driver and **stored tare**. With the setting on, every done ticket whose tare was captured from the scale refreshes the stored tare. It stays valid for the number of days in the settings, and while it is valid, **Use stored tare** can stand in for the empty weighing so the truck crosses once. A manager can set a stored tare by hand, with a reason, for a truck weighed empty elsewhere.

## Deductions and manual weights

A deduction is a percentage or a fixed number of kilograms with a reason, such as rain soaked scrap or pallets picked out of the load. When the indicator cannot be used at all, a manager can type the weight with a written reason. The ticket gets a red Manual weight ribbon, the chatter records who and why, and the Manual overrides report lists every case.

## Into stock and onto paper

A done ticket sets its final net as the quantity on the linked receipt or delivery, in the product's own unit, and can create the transfer and validate it if you choose. Tickets print on A5 or on an 80 mm roll, with both weighings, the deduction, the final net, the camera snapshots on A5, and signature lines for the driver and the operator.

## Who uses it

The module was written for sites where trucks are weighed for money or for stock:

- **Scrap and recycling yards** buying mixed loads from dealers and dismantlers and selling sorted grades to mills, where deductions for dirt, oil and water are a daily argument.
- **Grain and feed mills** receiving farm loads and deducting moisture.

## Limitations

These come from the module's code and the demo, and we would rather you read them before you buy:

- **It needs the Windows agent on the weighbridge PC.** The live weight is read through the browser on that PC; another computer sees "Not paired" and cannot capture.
- **Only continuous output indicators are read.** The agent listens to what the indicator sends; it does not poll indicators that only answer on request.
- **It does not bill.** Receipts and deliveries get the quantity; purchase bills and customer invoices are raised elsewhere in Odoo.
- **Automatic validation can stop on an Inventory prompt.** With Odoo's SMS on delivery confirmation installed, the first validated delivery asks a question the ticket cannot answer, and the ticket notes that the transfer needs attention. Answer it once in Inventory.
- **Quantities in tonnes are rounded to the unit's precision.** On the demo this left 0.01 tonne remainders as backorders when receipts were validated automatically; check the rounding of your weight unit.
- **There is no driver kiosk or mobile screen.** Tickets are run from the weighbridge PC.
