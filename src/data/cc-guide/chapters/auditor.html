The auditor role is for internal or external auditors, and for anyone who must check the records without changing them. You read every construction record on every project, costs and margins included, and you can export. You cannot create, change or delete anything.

The audit trail

Finance › Controls › Audit Trail records who changed which field on which document, from what to what, and when. Number fields also show the change in money.

Useful ways to read it:

  1. Group by Document to see the full history of one certificate or variation.
  2. Group by User to see what one person changed.
  3. Filter on the field Segregation of Duties to see every time a duty override was granted and used.

Why it matters

The audit trail cannot be edited. It is the answer to "who changed this figure, and when".

Approvals

Commercial & Billing › Approvals › Waiting on Me shows open approvals. Remove the filter to see all of them, including those signed and refused, with each signature by round.

Finance › Controls › Approval Matrix and Approval Limits show the rules: which document types need approval, at what amounts, by whom.

Access review

Configuration › Access Control › Access Review lists every user with their roles, project memberships, last sign-in, two-factor status, whether they see cost and margin, and flags:

  • inactive 90 days or more,
  • missing two-factor where the role needs it,
  • duty conflicts (for example Procurement and Finance on the same person),
  • overrides used.

You can read reviews; the administrator works and signs them.
