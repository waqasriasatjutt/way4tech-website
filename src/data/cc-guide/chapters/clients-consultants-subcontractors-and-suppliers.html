People outside the company can use Construction Cloud through the portal: a simple web page that shows only their own records. This chapter is written for them. If you work for the contractor, read it to know what your clients and subcontractors see.

Portal user Typical person Sees
Client The employer's representative Their contracts, certificates, variations, retention, inspection sign-offs, document reviews and defects
Consultant Engineer, architect, client's quantity surveyor RFIs, submittals, inspections and drawings on the projects they are invited to
Subcontractor A subcontract firm Their subcontract orders and valuations
Supplier or vendor A supplier or subcontractor Their compliance documents, to upload and renew

Note

Portal users never see anyone else's records. A client cannot see another client's job; a subcontractor cannot see another firm's order. Trying to open someone else's page gives an error.

Logging in to the portal

  1. You receive an invitation email from the contractor. Click the link and choose a password.
  2. Next time, go to the address the contractor gave you and log in with your email and password.
  3. The portal home opens with one card for each thing you can see.

Tip

Connection & Security on the portal home lets you change your password and set up two-factor sign-in.

For clients

Contracts, certificates and variations

  1. Click Construction Contracts on the portal home.
  2. Open a contract. You see the contract sum, the certificates issued with the amounts certified and paid, the variations and the retention held.
  1. Open a certificate to see it line by line, and download the PDF.

Inspection sign-off

When an inspection or a hold point needs your sign-off:

  1. Click Inspection Sign-off.
  1. Open the point, read the inspection results and the photos.
  2. Click Sign off, or Reject with a comment. A rejection raises a non-conformance report for the contractor.

Document reviews

Document Reviews lists drawings and documents sent to you for review. Open one, add comments, and answer Approve, Approve with Comments or Reject.

Defects after handover

During the defects period:

  1. Click Defects. You see each defect with its status and fix-by date.
  2. To report a new one, click Report a defect: project, where, what is wrong, details and a photo.
  3. When the contractor reports a defect fixed, open it, compare the before and after photos, and click Accept the repair or Not fixed with a comment.

For consultants

  1. Click Consultant Workspace on the portal home. Only the projects you are invited to are shown.
  1. RFIs: open an RFI and write your answer.
  2. Submittals: stamp submittals that are submitted or under review.
  3. Inspections: read the inspection and send a comment to the site team.
  4. Drawings: download the current revision of each drawing.

Why it matters

Every answer is dated. Late answers to RFIs are the start of many delay claims, so the date you answer is recorded on both sides.

For subcontractors

  1. Click Subcontracts (or the construction card) on the portal home.
  1. Open your subcontract order: the order value, variations, what has been certified, retention held and paid.
  2. Open a valuation to see what was certified line by line and any contra charges.

For suppliers and vendors: compliance documents

The contractor needs certain documents from you before it can place orders or pay: trade licence, tax registration, insurance certificates and others.

  1. Click Compliance Documents on the portal home.
  1. The page shows your company's status and every document needed, with what the contractor holds and its status.
  2. To add a missing document, click Upload. Choose the file and enter the number, issuer and dates.
  3. To replace one that is expiring, click Renew on it.
  4. Your upload shows as Waiting for Review until the contractor checks it. If it is rejected, the reason is shown.

Watch out

If a required document expires, the contractor's system may stop new orders and payments to you until a valid one is uploaded and approved. Renew before the expiry date.

For the contractor: giving portal access

  1. Clients: on the contract, click Share with Client (the portal access window opens for the employer's contact).
  2. Consultants: on the project's Members tab, add the person with the role External Consultant.
  3. Subcontractors and suppliers: give portal access to their contact from the contact form (Actions › Grant portal access).
