The end of a job: commissioning, handover packages, completion certificates, the defects period with the client on the portal, warranties, the closeout checks and the final account.

Tip

Every section follows the same pattern: where the menu is, what it is for, who uses it, a picture of the list, a picture of a typical record, the important fields, the stages, the buttons and the linked records. On the live screens, the ? button next to the pager opens the same kind of help for the screen you are on.

Closeout

Where: Closeout

What it is for: One tile per job with closeout and handover progress, systems commissioned, open defects and the end of the defects period.

Who uses it: Project Manager, Director

Key steps:

  1. Open a job tile.
  2. Work through its closeout checklist.
  3. Follow the links to commissioning, handover and defects.

A typical record. Click any line to open it. This is "Aldercar Lane Culvert and Highway Works":

Field What to enter Where it comes from

Defects Period Ends The latest end of a running defects period on the project. Worked out by the system.
Closeout Checklist Add lines in the table. Lines you add here.
Lessons Learned What went well, what did not, and what the next job should do differently. Filling it in ticks the closeout check. You enter it.
