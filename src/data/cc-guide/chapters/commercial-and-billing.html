Commercial & Billing

Where: Commercial & Billing

What it is for: Quantities of work done, measured on site against the bill items. A certified measurement is locked and is what valuations are paid on.

Who uses it: Site Engineer, Commercial Manager

Key steps:

  1. Press New, pick the contract and add the bill items and dimensions.
  2. Press Book as Measured.
  3. Agree it with the client's surveyor and press Mark Agreed.
  4. Press Certify; the quantities now feed the next valuation.

The list shows: Reference, Measured On, Project, Measured By, Items, Measured Value, Days Awaiting Certification, Status.

Actual Cost to Date The ledger at the period end: materials issued, labour worked, subcontract value billed and approved site expenses, each one counted only if its own date falls on or before the period end. A subcontract carries no dated history in the base module, so the billed figure on a live order counts whole. You enter it.
Accrued Cost Work done and goods delivered that nobody has invoiced yet. This is the single most common reason a reconciliation lies, and it always lies in the same direction: margin too high. You enter it.
