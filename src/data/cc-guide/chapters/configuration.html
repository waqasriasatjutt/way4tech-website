The important fields

Field What to enter Where it comes from
Name Nothing, it fills in by itself. Worked out by the system.
Employee Leave empty for a rate that applies to everybody of the trade (and grade) who has no rate of their own. The Employees list.
Trade The trade this rate prices. Required when no employee is set. The Construction Labour Type list.

Basis The period the amounts below are stated for. You enter it.
Basic Basic pay for the period. You enter it.
Allowances Housing, transport, food and other fixed allowances paid with the wage for the period. You enter it.
Employer Contributions % Added on top of basic plus allowances: social security, pension, gratuity or end-of-service accrual, as a percentage. You enter it.
Employer Fixed Costs Fixed employer costs for the period not tied to pay, such as a flat insurance premium. You enter it.
Paid Hours in the Period Hours the period pays for. Empty uses the company figure for a month or the standard work day for a day. You enter it.
Pay Per Hour Basic plus allowances divided by the paid hours: what the person is paid for an hour, before overtime. Worked out by the system.
Cost Per Hour Pay per hour plus the employer contributions: what an hour costs the job, before overtime. Worked out by the system.
