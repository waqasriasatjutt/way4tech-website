Once a tender is won it becomes a project with a contract and a contract bill. The contract sets the rules for every later screen: retention, advance, payment terms, liquidated damages and the standard form whose notice clauses drive the time bars. Around it sit the notices register, claims, bonds and insurance.

Tip

Every section follows the same pattern: where the menu is, what it is for, who uses it, a picture of the list, a picture of a typical record, the important fields, the stages, the buttons and the linked records. On the live screens, the ? button next to the pager opens the same kind of help for the screen you are on.
