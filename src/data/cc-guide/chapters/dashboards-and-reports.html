Tip

Every section follows the same pattern: where the menu is, what it is for, who uses it, a picture of the list, a picture of a typical record, the important fields, the stages, the buttons and the linked records. On the live screens, the ? button next to the pager opens the same kind of help for the screen you are on.

Dashboards & Reports

Where: Dashboards & Reports

What it is for: The whole portfolio on one screen: contract value, certified, cash received, retention, margin, tender pipeline, safety and RFIs, with every figure linked to its records.

Who uses it: Director, Administrator

Key steps:

  1. Pick the period and projects in the toolbar at the top.
  2. Read the tiles and charts.
  3. Click any figure to open the records behind it.

How to use it:

  1. Open Dashboards & Reports.

Margin by Job (CVR)

Where: Dashboards & Reports › Cost and Margin › Margin by Job (CVR)

What it is for: Margin by job from the latest cost value reconciliation.

Who uses it: Directors, Commercial Managers.

Why it matters: Profit by job.

Key steps:

  1. Group by job.
  2. Compare periods.
  3. Open the reconciliation behind a figure.

Stages

The bar at the top right shows where the record is. The stages are:

Stage What it means
Draft New and still being prepared. You can change anything.
For Review Being looked at by the reviewer or the client.
Signed Off Signed off and closed.
Cancelled Cancelled. Kept for the record but ignored in totals.

Buttons

Button What it does When you see it
Pull Figures Re-read the certified valuations, the measurement, the variations, the ledger and the accrual register. Only at Draft
Raise Accruals Draft an accrual for everything delivered or certified and not yet invoiced at this period end. Only at Draft
Send for Review Sends the record to the reviewer. Only at Draft
Sign Off Signs the record off. A signed record is locked. It asks you to confirm first: "Signing off locks this reconciliation for good." Only at For Review
Cancel Cancels the record. It stays in the list for the record. Not at Signed Off, Cancelled
Reset to Draft Puts the record back to draft so it can be changed. Only at For Review, Cancelled
Export to Excel Downloads this record as an Excel file. Always
