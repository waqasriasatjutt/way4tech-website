As a director you see every job, every figure and every approval. Your work in the system is short and regular: read the dashboard, sign what is waiting, and look closer at any job that is going red.

What you see when you log in

  • approvals waiting on you: documents that need your signature now.
  • Your dashboard: Executive Dashboard: the whole company on one screen.
  • Quick actions: Executive Dashboard, Approvals (your approval queue) and Cash (the cash position by job).

Note

You can read every setting, but you cannot change configuration. That is the administrator's job. This protects the controls you rely on.

The Executive Dashboard, tile by tile

Open it from Home, or Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Executive.

At the top: the company, a project picker (all projects, or one), the period buttons, and refresh. Every tile and chart opens the records behind it when you click it.

The charts below:

  • S-Curve: Planned vs Earned vs Actual: whether the work is ahead of or behind the plan, and whether it costs more than it earns.

Tip

Pick one project in the picker to see the whole dashboard for that job only.

Daily tasks

Sign what is waiting

  1. On Home, click approvals waiting on you (or the Approvals quick action).
  1. Open a line. It shows the document, its value when the approval was raised, who has already signed and who can sign.
  2. Click the document link to read it in full.
  3. Click Approve, or Refuse and write the reason.

Watch out

If the document's value changes after you sign, the approval starts again. This stops a price being raised after it was approved.

Why it matters: A requisition or variation waiting on you is usually a site or a client waiting on you. Clear the queue every day.

Check the alerts

Dashboards & Reports › Alerts › Alerts lists everything the alert rules have raised, worst first: time bars, expiring bonds and insurances, retention not chased, overdue certificates.

Weekly tasks

Look at each job on one screen: Project 360

Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Project 360, then pick the job at the top right.

  • Value and cost: contract value, revised value, budget, committed, actual, forecast cost at completion, forecast margin, and progress against time elapsed.
  • Cash and open items: certified, invoiced, received, retention held, open variations, RFIs, NCRs and incidents.
  • S-curve, top 5 overspent bill items and the programme: critical path delay and the next activities due.
  • The status badge says Healthy, Watch or At Risk.

See every job on a map: Portfolio Map

Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Portfolio Map. Each job is a pin: red at risk, amber watch, green healthy.

Colour Rule
Red Critical path more than 14 days late, forecast margin below zero, SPI below 0.85, or more than two incidents open
Amber Any delay, margin under 8 percent, SPI under 0.95, or any open incident
Green None of the above

Click a pin, then Open Project 360.

Margin by job

  • Dashboards & Reports › Cost and Margin › Live Margin by Job: today's budget, committed, actual and margin for every job.
  • Dashboards & Reports › Cost and Margin › Margin by Job (CVR): the margin from the signed monthly cost value reconciliations (CVR).

Note

The two can differ. Live margin moves every day. CVR margin is the figure the commercial team signed off at month end, with accruals and provisions included.

Monthly tasks

Cash

Finance › Cash › Cash Position shows each job's certified, paid and outstanding amounts. Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Cash Flow, 13 Weeks shows money in and out week by week; watch the Lowest Running Position tile.

Earned value

Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Earned Value compares planned work, earned work and actual cost.

  • SPI (schedule performance index) below 1.00 means behind programme.
  • CPI (cost performance index) below 1.00 means the work costs more than it earns.

Chapter 70 explains how each figure is worked out.

Get it by email: My Digests

  1. Open Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › My Digests and click New.
  2. Choose weekly or monthly, tick the dashboards to include and, if you want, limit it to some projects.
  3. Save. The system emails you a PDF on the Next Send date. Preview PDF shows it now.

Tip

A Monday morning digest of the Executive Dashboard and Cash Flow, 13 Weeks is a good start.

Reports

Report Centre

Dashboards & Reports › Report Packs › Report Centre lists every report, export and dashboard in the system, grouped by subject, each with a line on what it shows and an Open button. You only see what your role may open.

Monthly project report packs

Each month the project managers produce a branded PDF pack per job: summary, progress and S-curve, commercial and cost position, CVR and earned value, 13-week cash flow, programme, quality and safety, risks, photos and the look-ahead. Read them under Dashboards & Reports › Report Packs › Monthly Report Packs; packs can also be emailed to you automatically (Chapter 11).

Company portfolio report

Dashboards & Reports › Report Packs › Company Portfolio Report: pick a date, then Print PDF or Export to Excel. One line per job: contract value, physical and time percentage, margin, cash received, cash position (received less actual cost) and the red, amber or green status.

WIP schedule

The Dashboards & Reports › Report Packs › WIP Schedule shows over and under billing per job: how much has been billed compared with the revenue earned on a cost-to-cost basis. Finance prepares it at each period end (Chapter 18).

My Dashboard

Your own dashboard starts from the director template. Add the widgets you check most (Chapter 1, "My Dashboard").
