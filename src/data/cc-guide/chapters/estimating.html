Estimating prices a tender: estimate versions with their work breakdown, a resource library and regional price books, rate build-ups, quantity takeoff, subcontractor and supplier enquiries, the markup sheet and approval. On award the estimate becomes the job budget.

Tip

Every section follows the same pattern: where the menu is, what it is for, who uses it, a picture of the list, a picture of a typical record, the important fields, the stages, the buttons and the linked records. On the live screens, the ? button next to the pager opens the same kind of help for the screen you are on.

Estimates

Where: Sales & Tenders › Estimating › Estimates

What it is for: Estimates: the detailed price of a tender, version by version, with its structure, cost items, takeoff, enquiries, markup sheet and tender summary.

Who uses it: Estimators; Commercial Managers approve.

Why it matters: The approved estimate becomes the job budget on award.

The list shows: Estimate, Tender, Client, What Changed, Estimator, Direct Cost, Tender Price, Margin %, State.

The bar at the top right shows where the record is. The stages are:

Stage What it means
Draft New and still being prepared. You can change anything.

Resource Library

Where: Sales & Tenders › Estimating Library › Resource Library

What it is for: Resource library: labour, crews, plant, materials and subcontract rates used in build-ups.

Who uses it: Estimators.

Why it matters: One library keeps every rate consistent.

The list shows: Code, Name, Type, Price Book, Unit, Currency, Unit Cost, Unit Cost (company).

A typical record. Click any line to open it. This is "Carpenter, formwork":

The important fields

Field What to enter Where it comes from
Code Your library code, for example LAB-MASON. You enter it.
Name Type it. You enter it.
Type Choose one: Labour, Crew, Plant, Material, Subcontract, Other. You enter it.
Unit What one unit of this resource is: hr, day, m3, bag, tonne, No. You enter it.
Price Book The regional book this rate belongs to. Leave empty for a rate used everywhere. The Regional Price Book list.
Company Pick from the list. The Companies list.
Unit Cost Cost of one unit in the resource currency: labour or payroll rate; crew members added up; plant hire plus fuel plus operator; material at the chosen supplier price. Worked out by the system.
Unit Cost (company) Unit cost times the location factor, in the company currency. This is the figure a build-up uses. Worked out by the system.
Exchange Rate Used Company currency per unit of the resource currency, fixed on the last refresh so a build-up does not move with every market tick. Set by the system.
Trade Pick from the list. The Construction Labour Type list.
Labour Rate / hr All-in cost of one hour of this trade, used when no payroll cost rate is available. You enter it.
Use Payroll Cost Rate Take the hourly cost from the labour cost rates kept by HR (Labour Cost Rates) for this trade, when that module is installed. Press Refresh Rates to pick up a new figure. You enter it.
Note Write the details. You enter it.

Buttons

Button What it does When you see it
Refresh Rates Re-read the payroll cost rate and the exchange rate. Always

Build-Up Templates

Where: Sales & Tenders › Estimating Library › Build-Up Templates

What it is for: Reusable rate build-up templates (unit rate analyses).

Who uses it: Estimators.

Why it matters: Price common items the same way every time.

The list shows: Reference, Description, Work Type, Unit, Net Cost per Unit, Margin %, Sell Rate per Unit.

A typical record. Click any line to open it. This is "[CC-EXC-BULK] Bulk excavation, load and cart on site":

The important fields

Field What to enter Where it comes from
Description What this rate buys, in the words the bill will use. You enter it.
Reference Your own library reference, for example CIV-CONC-C30. You enter it.
Work Type Pick from the list. The Construction Work Type list.
Unit The unit the rate is quoted per, for example m3, m2, No., L.S. You enter it.
Reusable Template A standard analysis kept to be applied to many bill items, for example "RCC M25 slab per m3". Templates are offered first in Apply Build-Up. You enter it.
Price Book The regional book this analysis is priced from. The Regional Price Book list.
Output per Hour Productivity of the gang: units of the build-up produced in one working hour. With gang hours, sets the output per cycle. You enter it.
Gang Hours per Cycle Hours in the cycle the components describe, usually one working day. Labour and plant components with a number set are charged for these hours. You enter it.
Output per Cycle How many units the components below produce. A gang placing 18 m3 of concrete in a shift has an output of 18, and the components are the cost of that whole shift. You enter it.
Company Pick from the list. The Companies list.
Overhead % Site preliminaries and head office recovery carried on this rate. Kept apart from margin so a rate can be cut in a negotiation without anyone pretending the overhead went away. You enter it.
Margin % Mark-up applied to cost plus overhead. You enter it.
Margin per Unit Nothing, it fills in by itself. Worked out by the system.
Sell Rate per Unit The rate that goes on the bill. Four decimals on purpose: the bill line rounds it for print, this one does the arithmetic. Worked out by the system.
Components Add lines in the table. Lines you add here.
Labour Nothing, it fills in by itself. Worked out by the system.

Buttons

Button What it does When you see it

Refresh from Library Re-reads every resource rate from the library. Always
Apply to Items Puts this build-up on the chosen estimate items. Always

What to fill in

Field What to enter Where it comes from

Tender Pick from the list. The Tender list.
Project Pick from the list. The Projects list.
