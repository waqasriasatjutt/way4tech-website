This chapter is for the Construction Administrator and for experts who need to know how the controls work. It covers the setup wizard, the brand, users and roles, project memberships, approval limits, segregation of duties, the access review, plan limits, period lock and month-end close, alerts, imports and exports.

Watch out

Configuration changes affect everyone at once. Make them outside working hours where possible, and write in the record's chatter what you changed and why.

The setup wizard

The setup wizard sets up a new company in six steps. Open it from Home (Setup Wizard quick action, or Continue setup on the checklist) or from Settings › Brand › Getting Started.

Each step has Save and Continue, Back and Skip.

  1. Company: name, logo, country, currency, tax ID, email and phone.
  2. Brand: the preset and the product name.
  3. Team: one row per person with name, email and construction role. Each gets a login with the right roles and an invitation email.
  4. First project: name, code, client, contract value, start and finish dates, site address.
  5. Bill of quantities: Import the Bill from Excel opens the bill import for the first project.
  6. Sample data (only while the sample jobs exist): Hide the Sample Data archives the sample projects, employees and logins. Nothing is deleted.

Progress is kept. Until the first five steps are done, Home shows the setup checklist to administrators.

Brand settings

Settings › Brand holds the product name, login tagline, vendor name and website, three colours (primary, secondary, accent) and the logo.

  1. Choose a preset, or type your own colours and upload a logo (the brand becomes Custom).
  2. Change the product name or tagline if needed.
  3. Save and reload. The top bar, buttons, login page, browser tab, app icon, mail footers and printouts all follow.

Users and roles

Creating a user

  1. Open Settings › Users & Companies › Users, click New.
  2. Enter the name and the email (the login).
  3. In the construction section, choose the role on the ladder (Site Worker to Construction Administrator) and tick any extra roles: Estimator, Procurement and Stores, QHSE Officer, Finance.
  1. Save. Click Send an Invitation Email if it was not sent automatically.

The user lands on Home with the dashboard and quick actions of their role.

Tip

Give each person the lowest role that lets them do their job. Add an extra role rather than moving them up the ladder. A buyer who also checks deliveries needs Procurement and Stores, not Project Manager.

Configuration › Access Control also has:

  • Construction Roles: the roles and what each includes.
  • Access Matrix: every record type with read, write, create and delete for each role.
  • Who Has Which Role: every user and their roles, direct and inherited.

What site roles never see

Cost, rate, margin and wage fields are removed for site roles at the server, not only hidden on the screen. Exports, reports and the site app follow the same rule.

Project memberships

One person can hold a different role on each job: project manager on one, view only on another.

  1. Switch it on: Settings › Construction › Access Control › Enforce Project Membership. Choose the Company wide roles that always see every project (default Commercial Manager, Director, Administrator; Auditors always do).
  2. Open a project, tab Members, and add each person with their project role.
  1. Click Who can see this project to check: every user who can open it, why, their project role, whether they can change it and whether they see cost.

All memberships: Configuration › Access Control › Project Memberships.

Note

A project role never adds rights beyond the company role. A Site Engineer made Project Manager on a job is shown "Company role is lower" and still does not see cost.

Approval limits by amount

  1. Finance › Controls › Approval Limits (also Finance › Controls › Approval Limits).
  1. Open a document type: Material Requisition, Purchase / RFQ Award, Subcontract Order, Variation, Petty Cash, Site Expense or Payment Certificate.
  2. Add each role with the amount it approves alone (0 = no ceiling). The Routing box describes the result in words.
  3. Click Enforce Limits.

How it works: the role with the lowest limit approves alone up to that limit. Above it, the approve button sends the document to the next role up. It appears in their Waiting on Me, and the form shows a yellow banner naming who signs next.

Note

Limits are written as approval matrix rules marked "From Approval Limits". Your hand-written rules for that document type are archived while limits are in force and come back when you switch limits off. The full matrix is under Finance › Controls › Approval Matrix; Delegations lets an approver lend their authority for a period.

Segregation of duties

The person who created or requested a document cannot approve, award, agree, issue or sign it. The rule is on by default for each document type (a toggle on the Approval Limits screen).

When it is truly needed, an administrator can override once:

  1. On the refusal message, click Override and log it.
  2. Enter the reason.
  3. The person can then decide that one document once. The grant and its use are written to the audit trail and the document's chatter.

All overrides: Configuration › Access Control › Duty Overrides.

Access review

Review who can do what every quarter.

  1. Configuration › Access Control › Access Review. A daily job opens one review each quarter and gives every administrator a to-do with a 14-day deadline.
  2. Click Refresh.
  3. Use the Flagged smart button: inactive 90 days or more, missing two-factor, duty conflicts (for example Procurement and Finance together), overrides used, too many administrators.
  4. Fix each finding (archive the user, remove a role, set up two-factor) and write a note.
  5. Click Mark Reviewed.

Sign-in controls

  • Sign-In Networks (Configuration › Access Control › Sign-In Networks): per role, the addresses or ranges sign-in is allowed from.
  • Session Timeout (Settings): minutes of inactivity before the screen locks.
  • Two-Factor Sign-In (Settings): roles that must use it.

Plan limits

Your hosting plan may limit internal users and active construction projects. Settings › Brand › Plan shows the plan read only. At 80 percent Home shows "Your plan is nearly full"; at the limit "Your plan is full", and creating another internal user or construction project is refused. Archiving a user or project frees a place. Portal users do not count.

Period lock and month-end close

Why it matters

Once a month is reported, its figures must not move. The period lock stops anyone changing records dated in a closed month.

  1. Finish the month: valuations and certificates issued, invoices raised, CVRs signed, labour costed, petty cash approved, diaries posted.
  2. Open Finance › Controls › Close the Month.
  1. Pick the close-up-to date. Choose whether to lock commercial documents, site records, or both.
  2. Read the count of records still open in the period. Fix them, or tick to close over them if you must (they stay open).
  3. Confirm. Records dated on or before the date can no longer be created, changed or deleted.

Note

Labour costing is also refused on or before the site lock date (except for an administrator or director) and in a closed payroll period.

Alerts

Configuration › Approvals and Alerts › Alert Rules decides what the system chases.

Each rule has the record type, which records (a filter), a date test (falls due within, or older than, so many days), who to tell (project manager, record owner, named people or a group), the summary, what to do, the severity, the repeat interval and a ceiling per run.

  1. Click What Does It Catch? to preview the records before relying on a rule.
  2. Click Run It Now to try it.

A daily job runs every rule. Each alert becomes an activity on the person told and shows in Dashboards & Reports › Alerts › Alerts, where it can be marked Actioned or Dismissed.

Shipped rules cover time bars, bond and insurance expiry, supplier documents, retention due, overdue certificates and RFIs, among others.

Importing a bill of quantities from Excel

  1. Open the bill (Contracts & Setup › Bills of Quantities › Bills of Quantities) and click Import from Excel, or use step 5 of the setup wizard.
  2. Click Download Template.
  3. Fill one row per item: section, item code, description, unit, quantity, rate (and cost or budget if you have it). Sections can nest.
  4. Upload the file. The preview marks rows with problems (unknown unit, missing quantity).
  5. Fix the file and upload again, or click Confirm when the preview is clean.

Tip

Import the client's bill exactly as issued, item codes included. Valuations and certificates then match the client's bill line for line.

Other imports: Configuration › Contracts and Indices › Import Index Values (price indices for escalation) and the standard Import records option in the favourites menu of most lists.

Exports

  • Any list: select records, Actions › Export (Chapter 1).
  • Pivots: the download button gives Excel.
  • Documents › Drawings › Document Register (Excel): drawing register and revision history.
  • Dashboards & Reports › Cost and Margin › Job Cost Sheet: one sheet per job, PDF or Excel.
  • Labour › Payroll › Payroll Export: hours for the payroll.
  • Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › My Digests: dashboards as a PDF by email.

Project templates

Configuration › Jobs and Bills › Project Templates: a template holds the job type, form of contract, phases (as offsets from possession), bill sections, cost codes, a document register, an inspection plan, expected permits and the approval chain. Apply a Template on a project shows what it will create before writing anything.

Navigation and help settings

Settings › Brand › Navigation and Help has:

  • What's Next Banners: on or off for everyone.
  • User Guide Link: where the help panel's guide link points. {chapter} is replaced by the chapter of the screen.

Under Configuration › Help and Process Map:

Menu What you maintain
Process Flows The processes shown in How it works and their steps: who does each step, the screen Open goes to, and what is counted as waiting
Help Topics One topic per screen: what it is, who uses it, key steps (one per line) and the process step it belongs to
What's Next Guides One record per document stage: meaning, next step, who, the roles allowed to press the button, and the button itself
Lifecycle Menu Map Every moved menu entry with where it came from

Tip

Write help topics in the words your own people use. If the site calls a requisition an "indent", say so in the topic.

The guided tours (Tour for your role and Show me how to on Home) need no setup.

My Dashboard templates

Five templates ship: Director, Commercial Manager, Finance, Project Manager and Site Engineer. A user's first My Dashboard is copied from the template of their role.

To change a template:

  1. Open Home › My Dashboard and click Customise.
  2. Arrange the widgets the way the role should see them.
  3. In the yellow Templates bar, pick the role and click Publish this layout as the template.

To reset users to a template:

  1. Open Configuration › My Dashboard › Dashboard Templates and the template.
  2. Click Apply to Users, pick the users, click Apply. Widgets a user's role may not see are left out.

User Dashboards lists every personal dashboard; Widget Library lists every widget with the roles allowed to add it. Archive a widget to take it out of the library.

Report Centre and report settings

The Report Centre fills itself at install and every night from the installed modules. Report Centre Entries under Configuration lists them; Action › Refresh Report Centre does it now; archive an entry to hide it for everybody. Settings › Construction › Management Reports sets the pack day for the monthly report packs.
