Finance turns certificates into invoices, keeps track of money owed in and out, manages retention releases and petty cash, posts labour cost to the jobs, owns the cash flow forecast and closes the month.

What you see when you log in

Your dashboard is the Finance Dashboard. Quick actions: Certificates to Invoice, Retention and Petty Cash.

Note

Invoicing a certificate

Why it matters

A certificate is the client's promise to pay. The invoice is your demand. Every day between the two is a day longer before you are paid.

  1. Click Certificates to Invoice on Home (or open Commercial & Billing › Valuations and Certificates › Payment Certificates and filter on issued, not invoiced).
  2. Open the certificate. Check the amount payable, retention and advance recovery.
  1. Click Create Invoice. A draft customer invoice opens: work certified on the income line, retention and advance recovery on their own lines, with the taxes set in Settings.
  2. Check it and post it.
  3. When the payment arrives, register it on the invoice. The certificate becomes paid.

Finance › Invoices and Bills › Client Invoices lists every invoice raised from a certificate.

Subcontractor bills

  1. The commercial team certifies the subcontract valuation.
  2. Click Create Bill on it. A draft vendor bill is created with retention and contra charges deducted on their own lines.
  3. Post it and pay it as normal. Finance › Invoices and Bills › Subcontractor Bills lists them.

Watch out

If supplier compliance blocks payment, Mark Paid on a subcontract valuation is refused for a vendor whose documents have expired. The message names the missing documents.

Supplier bills and the three-way match

Supplier bills for construction orders appear under Finance › Invoices and Bills › Supplier Bills to Check when the bill does not match the order and the goods received. Do not pay until the buyer has resolved the difference.

Retention

Commercial & Billing › Retention › Retention Ledger: every amount held and released, with due dates.

  1. When practical completion is certified, the first moiety falls due and you get a to-do to apply for it.
  2. Write to the client, then click Release on the ledger line when the money comes back.
  3. Nobody Chased This lists releases past due and not paid.

Petty cash

Site staff hold small cash floats for minor purchases.

  1. Site › Site Cash › Petty Cash Floats: each float, who holds it, its limit and balance.
  1. Transactions: each spend with its receipt, project and cost code.
  2. To Approve: approve or reject transactions.
  3. Floats Needing Top-Up: floats below their level. Top them up.

Labour cost to the jobs

Each month:

  1. Open Labour › Payroll › Payroll Periods, New, for the month.
  2. Load from Attendance. Supervisors then allocate and approve the hours (Chapter 19).
  3. Select the approved hours in Time Allocation and click Cost to Job.
  4. Enter camp and welfare costs on the Camp and Overheads tab.
  5. Click Close Period and Post. One journal entry is posted per job.

Labour › Payroll › Payroll Export gives an Excel file of the hours to help prepare the payroll (generic, WPS-friendly or Pakistan-friendly layouts).

Cash flow

  • Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Cash Flow, 13 Weeks: four weeks of actuals, then thirteen weeks forecast. Watch the Lowest Running Position tile.
  • Finance › Cash › Cash Flow Forecast: a detailed forecast per job.

WIP schedule (over and under billing)

  1. Open Dashboards & Reports › Report Packs › WIP Schedule and click New. Set the as-of date (the period end).
  2. Click Compute.
  3. Check each job's line: - Contract value = original contract sum + agreed variations. - Estimated cost = forecast cost at completion. - % complete = cost to date / estimated cost (capped at 100). - Earned revenue = contract value x % complete. - Billed to date = gross certified to date. - Over billing (a liability) = billed - earned, when positive. Under billing (an asset) = earned - billed, when positive.
  4. Export to Excel or Print WIP Schedule.
  5. Click Finalise to keep that period's figures.

Plant hire accruals and hire-outs

  • Commercial & Billing › Cost Value Reconciliation › Accrue Plant Hire: choose the date to accrue up to (default the end of last month). Each live hire gets one accrual for the charge to that date, minimum hours and days included, less what earlier accruals took. Nothing is accrued twice. Reverse an accrual to redo it.
  • Procurement & Stores › Plant and Tools › Hire-Out of Own Plant: when your company hires its own machines to others, Create Invoice raises the customer invoice for each period.

Month-end close

  1. Make sure certificates are invoiced, bills posted, petty cash approved and labour costed.
  2. Accruals: the commercial team raises them in the CVR. Check Commercial & Billing › Cost Value Reconciliation › Accruals to Reverse.
  3. Lock the period: Finance › Controls › Close the Month (Chapter 80). After that nobody can change records dated in the closed period.
