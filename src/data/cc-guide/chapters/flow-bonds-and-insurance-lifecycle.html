Bonds tie up bank limits and must be returned. Insurance must be in force every day of the job. Both are tracked from request to release, with expiry warnings.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Blue boxes are steps. Orange boxes are checks or holds that can stop the flow.

Who does what

Step Who
Bonds Commercial Manager, Finance
Insurance Commercial Manager, Finance, Procurement for certificates

Step 1. Request the bond

  1. Open Sales & Tenders › Bond Register and press New. Choose type (performance, advance payment, retention, bid) and direction (Given by Us or Received by Us).
  2. Link the contract and set the value as an amount or a percentage.
  3. Press Request from Bank. When issued, enter number and dates and press In Force.

Step 2. Reductions and facility use

  1. Add the Reduction Schedule lines. Press Apply Due Reductions, or let the nightly check do it.
  2. See the bank limits used in Contracts & Setup › Contract Administration › Bank Facilities.

Why it matters: Released limit is bonding capacity for the next tender.

Step 3. Release

  1. When the obligation ends (for example the performance certificate), press Request Release, then Released when the original comes back.

Step 4. Insurance policies

  1. Open Contracts & Setup › Contract Administration › Insurance Policies, press New: cover, insurer, limit, deductible, period, projects. Attach the schedule and press In Force.
  2. Within the warning period the status becomes Expiring Soon, after the end date Expired.

Step 5. Subcontractor certificates

  1. Record certificates in Contracts & Setup › Contract Administration › Subcontractor Insurance Certificates against the subcontract, tick We Are Named if applicable, and press In Force.

Why it matters: An expired certificate can block payment through supplier compliance.
