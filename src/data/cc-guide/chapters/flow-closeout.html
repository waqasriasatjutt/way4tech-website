The end of the job: test the systems, hand over the documents, issue the taking-over certificate, fix defects in the defects period with the client on the portal, issue the final certificate, release retention and bonds, and close the job out.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Blue boxes are steps. Orange boxes are checks or holds that can stop the flow.

Who does what

Step Who

Defects Site team; client on the portal

Step 1. Commission the systems

  1. Open Closeout › Commissioning › Systems, open a system and press New Test Record.
  2. Record Pass, Fail or N/A on each check, name the witness and attach the signed certificate. Press Complete Test.
  3. Each failed check becomes a punch item (snag) assigned to the installer. When fixed, press Retest.

Why it matters: Systems are handed over working, with proof.

Step 2. Assemble the handover package

  1. Open Closeout › Handover › Handover Packages, press New, pick the project and template. Items fill in.
  2. For each item press Received when it arrives (or N/A), and Accepted by Client when signed for.
  3. At 100 percent press Mark Ready, then Issue to Client. Download Document Set gives a zip with an index.

Step 3. Taking-over certificate

  1. Open Closeout › Handover › Completion Certificates, press New, kind Taking-Over, pick project and contract section.
  2. Press Issue Certificate. Open snags become the outstanding list, the contract moves to Defects Liability and the first retention moiety falls due.

Why it matters: This one certificate starts the defects period and releases half the retention.

Step 4. Defects period with the client portal

  1. Defects appear in Closeout › Defects Period › Defects. The client reports them on the portal at /my/defects.
  2. Assign and press Being Fixed; when repaired, attach the after photo and press Fixed.
  3. The client presses Accept the repair on the portal (or Sign Off in the office). Chase Closeout › Defects Period › Overdue Defects.

Why it matters: Defects closed on time bring the final retention home.

Step 5. Final certificate

  1. On the taking-over certificate press Raise Final Certificate.
  2. Work through the End-of-Period Checklist and press Issue Certificate. It is refused while any defect is open.
  3. The final retention moiety falls due and the performance bond release is requested.

Step 6. Release retention and bonds

  1. On the contract press Release First Moiety / Release Final Moiety, or confirm the rows in Commercial & Billing › Retention › Retention Ledger.
  2. On the bond press Request Release, then Released.
  3. Check Commercial & Billing › Retention › Retention Due, Not Chased for anything still unpaid.

Step 7. Final account and closeout checklist

  1. Agree the final account in Closeout › Final Account › Final Accounts (Run the Checklist, Pull Figures, Submit, Agree the Account, Issue Final Certificate).
  2. Open Closeout › Closeout Dashboard, open the job and press Refresh.
  3. Tick bonds returned and insurances cancelled, write the lessons learned, and press Archive Project when every check is done.
