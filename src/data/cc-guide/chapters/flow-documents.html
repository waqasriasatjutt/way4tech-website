The site must always build from the current revision, and you must always be able to prove who received what and when. This flow follows a drawing from its first revision to the register.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Who does what

Step Who
Drawings and revisions Document controller
Reviews Reviewer (internal or portal)
Distribution Project Manager

Step 1. Register the drawing

  1. Open Documents › Drawings › Drawing Register and press New. Enter number, title, discipline and revision scheme.
  2. Press New Revision to add the first revision with its file.

Why it matters: One register for every drawing on the job.

Step 2. Set the status code and issue

  1. On the revision set the status code (S0 to S4, A, B, C, AB; see Configuration › Documents › Document Status Codes).
  2. Press Issue. The earlier revision is superseded automatically and shows Superseded By.

Why it matters: Old revisions cannot be mistaken for current ones.

Step 3. Review

  1. Press Request Review on the revision, or open Documents › Drawings › Document Reviews.
  2. Name the reviewer and due date, press Send for Review.
  3. The reviewer adds comments and answers Approve, Approve with Comments or Reject. The answer sets the revision code to A, B or C.

Step 4. Distribute

  1. Set who gets which disciplines in Documents › Issue and Distribution › Distribution Matrix.
  2. In Documents › Drawings › Drawing Revisions select revisions and press Build Transmittals. One draft transmittal is built per recipient.
  3. Open each in Documents › Issue and Distribution › Transmittals and press Issue. Record Acknowledged and Response Received.

Why it matters: Proof of who received what and when.

Step 5. Answer "did they have the latest drawing"

  1. Open Documents › Issue and Distribution › Transmittal Log (who had what) and filter by the drawing or recipient.
  2. Download the register from Documents › Drawings › Document Register (Excel).

RFIs and submittals

Step 6. Requests for information

  1. Open Documents › Queries and Approvals › Requests for Information, press New, write the question and the date needed, and press Raise.
  2. Record the answer with Mark Answered. If it changes the work, press Route to Variation.
  3. Chase open ones in Documents › Queries and Approvals › RFIs Awaiting Answer.

Why it matters: Late answers cost time and support claims.

Step 7. Submittals

  1. Open Documents › Queries and Approvals › Submittals, press New, attach the shop drawing or sample and press Submit.
  2. Record the stamp: Stamp A: Approved, Stamp B: As Noted, Stamp C: Resubmit or Stamp D: Rejected.

Why it matters: Nothing is built from an unapproved submittal.
