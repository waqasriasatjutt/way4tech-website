Three plant money flows: fuel from tank to job cost with a check on consumption; hired-in plant from on-hire to the month-end accrual; and your own plant hired out to a customer and invoiced.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Blue boxes are steps. Orange boxes are checks or holds that can stop the flow.

Fuel

Step 1. Tanks and deliveries

  1. In Procurement & Stores › Plant and Fleet › Fuel Tanks set up each site tank with its capacity and reorder level.
  2. Record each delivery: date, supplier, delivery note, litres and price per litre. A delivery that would overfill the tank is refused.
  3. Record dips. The variance against the book stock shows missing fuel.

Step 2. Issue fuel to a machine

  1. In Procurement & Stores › Plant and Fleet › Fuel Issues create a fuel issue: plant or vehicle, the machine, the job charged and the source (tank, card or station).
  2. Enter the litres and the meter reading, and press Post.
  3. Consumption since the last fill is set against the norm. The issue is Flagged if it is too high, the meter did not move, there is no reading, or the litres exceed the tank of the machine.
  4. An approved site expense of kind Fuel is raised on the job.

Why it matters: Fuel is one of the easiest costs to lose. The check catches it the same day.

Step 3. Review flags

  1. Open Procurement & Stores › Plant and Fleet › Fuel Anomalies. Check each flag with the operator and press Mark Reviewed.

Hired-in plant

Step 4. Daily logs drive the hire

  1. Each day record a plant daily log in Procurement & Stores › Plant and Fleet › Plant Daily Logs: working, idle and breakdown hours, meter, fuel and work done. Press Confirm.
  2. For hired plant, the hours go onto the plant sheet of the live hire. For owned plant, they go onto the hour meter.
  3. On an hourly hire, each day is charged the sheet hours raised to the minimum hours per day; a breakdown day is credited.

Step 5. Off-hire on time and accrue at month end

  1. Check Procurement & Stores › Plant and Tools › Off-Hire Due for hires near or past their planned off-hire date.
  2. At month end run Commercial & Billing › Cost Value Reconciliation › Accrue Plant Hire up to the last day of the month. Each hire gets one accrual for the charge not yet accrued.
  3. Check the result in Commercial & Billing › Cost Value Reconciliation › Plant Hire Accruals. Reverse the latest accrual to redo it.

Why it matters: Hire cost is in the month it belongs to, before the supplier bill arrives.

Hire-out of your own plant

Step 6. Hire out and invoice

  1. In Procurement & Stores › Plant and Tools › Hire-Out of Own Plant create a hire-out: customer, owned machine, rate per hour, day, week or month, minimum hours and days.
  2. Press Send Out. Certificates are checked first.
  3. Enter the hours on the Hours tab and press Create Invoice each period.
  4. Press Return when the machine comes back, raise the last invoice, then Close.

Why it matters: Idle owned plant earns money instead of costing it.

Step 7. Certificates

  1. Keep lifting, pressure and insurance certificates in Procurement & Stores › Plant and Fleet › Certificates and Inspections. Record Renewal opens the next one prefilled.
  2. Depending on Settings, an expired certificate warns or blocks daily logs, putting a hire on hire, sending out a hire-out and dispatching a transfer.
