People's hours become job cost without counting anybody twice. Attendance is loaded into time entries, the supervisor allocates them to the job and bill item, the costs are worked out with overtime, and the month is closed with one journal entry per job. Camp and welfare costs are shared out to the jobs as well.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Who does what

Step Who
Cost rates, overtime rules Project Manager, Finance
Allocate, approve Site User (supervisor)
Cost to job, close period Project Manager, Finance

Step 1. Set the cost rates

  1. Open Configuration › Site and Labour › Labour Cost Rates. Add a rate per trade (and grade) and one per employee where pay differs.
  2. Enter basic, allowances and employer contributions per month, day or hour. Or press Take from Contract Wage.
  3. Cost per hour = (pay x (1 + employer %) + employer fixed) / paid hours.

Why it matters: This is the rate the job is charged for an hour.

Step 2. Set the overtime rule

  1. Open Configuration › Site and Labour › Overtime Rules. Normal hours per day (8) and week (48), overtime multiplier (1.25), rest days and public holidays.

Step 3. Load attendance

  1. Open Labour › Time and Gangs › Load from Attendance, pick the dates and run it. Every closed attendance becomes a time entry once.
  2. Site attendance wins over office attendance for the same person and day.

Why it matters: Nobody types hours twice.

Step 4. Allocate, split and approve

  1. Open Labour › Time and Gangs › Time Allocation. Entries are grouped by day.
  2. Set the bill item and cost code. For a person on two jobs press Split and point the copy at the other job.
  3. Select rows and press Approve.

Step 5. Cost to job

  1. Select approved rows and press Cost to Job (Project Manager or Finance).
  2. One labour log is written per entry. Diary and gang logs for the same person and day are marked as covered, so nothing counts twice.
  3. Back to Allocate undoes the costing.

Why it matters: The job cost sheet, the CVR and earned value all read the labour log.

Step 6. Camp and welfare

  1. Open Labour › Payroll › Camp and Welfare Costs. Enter the camp, transport, food, visa, medical and PPE costs per worker or as a total.
  2. Press Allocate to Jobs. Each worker's share goes to the jobs he worked on in proportion to his hours.

Why it matters: Hidden labour costs reach the job.

Step 7. Close the payroll period

  1. Open Labour › Payroll › Payroll Periods and the month.
  2. Press Close Period and Post. It is refused while any entry is not costed. One journal entry per job is posted.
  3. Press Payroll Export for the payroll file (generic, WPS-friendly or Pakistan-friendly).

Why it matters: The month's labour cost lands in the accounts by job.

Step 8. Read the results

  1. Open Labour › Manpower › Manpower Dashboard: heads, hours, overtime %, cost per hour, absenteeism and labour budget used.
