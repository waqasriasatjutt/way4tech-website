Once a month each job is reconciled: what it is worth against what it cost. Earned value and the cash flow forecast are checked, then the month is locked so reported figures cannot change.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Step 1. Accrue cost not yet billed

  1. Open Commercial & Billing › Cost Value Reconciliation › Accruals and add cost incurred without a bill. Press Post.
  2. When the real bill arrives, check Commercial & Billing › Cost Value Reconciliation › Accruals to Reverse and press Reverse Now.

Why it matters: An invoice not yet received is still cost. Missing it makes margin look better than it is.

Step 2. Build the CVR

  1. Open Commercial & Billing › Cost Value Reconciliation › Reconciliations, press New, pick the job and month.
  2. Press Pull Figures: value (certified, measured not certified, agreed variations, provision), cost (ledger, accruals, provisions) and commitment.
  3. Press Raise Accruals if needed. Read the lines by cost code.

Why it matters: The monthly truth about each job.

Step 3. Sign off

  1. Press Send for Review, then Sign Off. A signed CVR is locked.
  2. See Dashboards & Reports › Cost and Margin › Margin by Job (CVR) and Dashboards & Reports › Cost and Margin › Jobs Losing Money.

Step 4. Earned value

  1. Open Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Earned Value. Snapshots are taken every Monday; press Take Snapshot Now for today.
  2. Read SPI (time) and CPI (cost). Below 1.00 means late or over cost.

Step 5. Cash flow

  1. Update the job forecasts in Finance › Cash › Cash Flow Forecast (Generate, Issue).
  2. Read Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Cash Flow, 13 Weeks.

Why it matters: Watch the Lowest Running Position tile.

Step 6. The monthly report pack

  1. Open Dashboards & Reports › Report Packs › Monthly Report Packs and press New. Pick the project and any day of the month.
  2. Write the executive summary, risks and look-ahead notes.
  3. Press Generate: the figures of the month are frozen in the pack and a branded PDF is made. Check it with Open PDF, then press Send.
  4. To make it automatic, tick Monthly Report Pack on the Reporting tab of the project and add the recipients.

Why it matters: The client and the board get the same figures, frozen at month end.

Step 7. The WIP schedule (over and under billing)

  1. Open Dashboards & Reports › Report Packs › WIP Schedule, press New and set the as-of date to the period end.
  2. Press Compute. For each job: contract value, estimated cost, percent complete (cost to cost), earned revenue, billed to date, and over or under billing.
  3. Check the lines, use Export to Excel, then Finalise to keep the period.

Why it matters: Over billing is a liability and under billing an asset. Finance needs both at every month end.

Step 8. Close the month

  1. Open Finance › Controls › Close the Month. Set the commercial lock date (valuations, certificates, measurements, CVRs) and the site lock date (diaries, labour, attendance, expenses).
  2. On or before the lock, records cannot be created, changed or deleted except by an Administrator or Director.

Why it matters: Reported figures cannot move after the board has seen them.
