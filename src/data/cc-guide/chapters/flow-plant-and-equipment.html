Owned and hired plant, from putting it on hire to off-hire, with daily sheets, hour meters, services and breakdowns.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Who does what

Step Who
Plant hire, off hire Project Manager, Quantity Surveyor
Daily sheets, meters Site User
Maintenance Plant Manager

Step 1. Register the machine

  1. Open Procurement & Stores › Plant and Tools › Equipment and press New: category, owned or hired, rates, the project it is on.
  2. Use Set In Use, Set Available, Send to Maintenance as its status changes.

Step 2. Hire in

  1. Open Procurement & Stores › Plant and Tools › Plant Hire, press New, pick supplier, machine and rate, and press Put On Hire.

Why it matters: Hired plant costs money every day it is on site.

Step 3. Daily sheet and meter

  1. Each day press Today's Sheet on the hire, or open Procurement & Stores › Plant and Tools › Daily Plant Sheets: hours worked, idle and down.
  2. Record readings in Procurement & Stores › Plant Maintenance › Hour Meter Readings.

Why it matters: Hire bills are checked against these sheets.

Step 4. Services and breakdowns

  1. Check Procurement & Stores › Plant Maintenance › Due for Service.
  2. A breakdown is reported with Report Breakdown; it opens a request in Procurement & Stores › Plant Maintenance › Maintenance Requests.

Step 5. Off hire

  1. Check Dashboards & Reports › Site and Time › Idle and Overdue Plant every week.
  2. Press Off Hire on the hire record the day the machine leaves.

Why it matters: Every day late off-hiring is paid for.

Step 6. Review utilisation

  1. Open Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Plant Utilisation.

Tip

Fuel, plant daily logs, hourly hire with minimum hours, the month-end hire accrual, hire-out and certificates are walked through in the flow chapter "Fuel, plant hire and hire-out".
