From the site asking for material to the supplier being paid. Each step leaves a record linked to the one before, so a bill can always be traced back to the bill item and the person who asked for it. Supplier compliance can hold the order or the payment when the supplier's papers are not in order.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Blue boxes are steps. Orange boxes are checks or holds that can stop the flow.

Who does what

Step Who
Requisition Site User, Site Engineer
Approve requisition Project Manager
RFQ, comparison, award, order Procurement and Stores
Goods received Storekeeper, Site User
Bill check and payment Finance

Step 1. Raise the requisition

  1. Open Procurement & Stores › Buying › Requisitions and press New (or use the New Requisition quick action on Home, or the Requests tab of the phone app).
  2. Pick the project, the date needed and, on each line, the material, quantity and bill item.
  3. Press Submit.

Why it matters: The requisition ties the cost to the job and the bill item from the start.

Step 2. Approve it

  1. The approver opens it from Waiting on Me (Commercial & Billing › Approvals › Waiting on Me) or the requisition list.
  2. Check the quantities against the bill and press Approve.
  3. If approval limits are on, a yellow banner says who signs next when the amount is above your limit.

Why it matters: Only approved needs are bought.

Watch out

The person who raised a requisition cannot approve it. This is the separation of duties rule.

Step 3. Ask suppliers for quotes

  1. On the approved requisition press Request Quotes, or open Procurement & Stores › Buying › Requests for Quotation and press New.
  2. Add the vendors and press Send to Vendors.
  3. Record each vendor's prices against the same lines and press Record as Received, or Vendor Declined.
  4. When all are in, press Quotes In.

Why it matters: Several quotes give the best price and a record of the choice.

Step 4. Compare and award

  1. Open Procurement & Stores › Buying › Quote Comparison. The lowest compliant price on each line is marked.
  2. On the RFQ press Award. Award the whole request to one vendor or split it line by line.
  3. If you choose a price that is not the lowest, write the justification. It is kept on the request.
  4. Use Print Comparison for the file.

Why it matters: The auditor's question "why this supplier" is answered on the record.

Step 5. Compliance check on the order

  1. The award creates one purchase order per vendor in Procurement & Stores › Buying › Purchase Orders.
  2. If the vendor is not compliant, a red banner shows what is missing and a Vendor Compliance button.
  3. Depending on the setting, Confirm Order is blocked, or allowed with a warning written on the order. In the demo, orders above 25,000 are warned.

Why it matters: Money does not go to firms without valid licences, tax papers or insurance.

Note

Holds and the vendor statuses are explained in chapter 37, Supplier compliance.

Step 6. Receive the goods

  1. When the delivery arrives, open the order and press Receive Goods, or open Procurement & Stores › Buying › Goods Received.
  2. Enter the quantity received and the condition, then press Confirm Receipt.
  3. The material is now in the site store and its cost reaches the job when issued to work.

Why it matters: Only goods received can be billed.

Step 7. Check the bill

  1. Finance enters the supplier's bill in Invoicing › Vendors › Bills.
  2. Open Finance › Invoices and Bills › Supplier Bills to Check. Each bill is matched against the order and the goods received (three-way match).
  3. On the goods received note press Match Invoice. On the bill press Mark Match Reviewed once differences are explained.

Why it matters: You pay for what was ordered and received, at the agreed price.

Step 8. Pay

  1. Confirm the bill and register the payment in Invoicing as usual.
  2. For subcontract valuations, the Mark Paid step is checked against compliance too (blocked in the demo for non-compliant subcontractors).

Why it matters: The job's cash out is now recorded.

Tip

The Procurement dashboard (Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Procurement) shows requisitions to buy, RFQs out and orders not yet received.
