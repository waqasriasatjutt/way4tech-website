Quality is planned before the work starts with an inspection and test plan (ITP). Inspections are requested at each point. A hold point stops measurement of the work it covers until the engineer releases it. Failures become non-conformance reports, and small defects become snags that hold retention.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Step 1. Set up the ITP

  1. Open Quality & Safety › Quality › Inspection and Test Plans and press New.
  2. Give it a title, the project and a template. The points are copied from the template.
  3. Under Covers, pick the bill sections and items it covers.
  4. Name the releasing engineer and the consultant, then press Activate.

Why it matters: The hold points now protect the covered bill items.

Step 2. Request the inspection

  1. On the plan's Points tab, press Request Inspection on a point (or use Quality & Safety › Quality › ITP Hold Points by Plan).
  2. An inspection request is raised in Quality & Safety › Quality › Inspections and the engineer gets a to-do.

Why it matters: The inspection proves the work met the specification.

Step 3. Inspect

  1. On site, open the Checks tab of the app, or the inspection on the computer.
  2. Record Pass, Fail or N/A and an observation for each check. Use Pass Remaining Checks for a clean inspection.
  3. Press Mark Complete.

Step 4. Hold point and release

  1. While a hold point is open, measuring a covered bill item is refused (or warned, depending on the setting). The bill item shows a red banner "Held by an ITP hold point".
  2. The releasing engineer, a QHSE Officer or a Project Manager presses Release Hold Point on the point.
  3. The consultant can sign off on the portal (Inspection Sign-off). A consultant rejection fails the point and raises an NCR.

Why it matters: You cannot be paid for work you could not prove was inspected.

Step 5. Raise an NCR for failures

  1. On a failed point press Fail and Raise NCR. The NCR opens in Quality & Safety › Quality › Non-conformance Reports.
  2. Press Investigate, record the cause, then Agree Disposition (repair, replace, accept).
  3. Press Raise Re-inspection, and Close when it passes. Back on the point, press Inspect Again.

Why it matters: Defective work is fixed and the cause removed.

Step 6. Snags

  1. Report a snag from the app (photo, location, trade, priority) or in Quality & Safety › Quality › Snags.
  2. Assign it, press Being Fixed, then Fixed with an after photo, then Sign Off.
  3. Snags ticked Holds Retention appear in Commercial & Billing › Retention › Snags Holding Retention.

Why it matters: Open snags hold retention, so closing them brings money in.
