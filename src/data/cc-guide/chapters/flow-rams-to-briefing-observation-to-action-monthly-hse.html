Three safety management flows. A risk assessment and method statement (RAMS) is prepared, approved and briefed to the workers who sign it. A safety observation becomes a corrective action that is closed out. Each month the HSE statistics are worked out and printed.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Blue boxes are steps. Orange boxes are checks or holds that can stop the flow.

RAMS to briefing

Step 1. Prepare the RAMS

  1. Open Quality & Safety › Safety › RAMS and press New. Pick the project and a template. Hazards, controls, PPE, training and method are copied in.
  2. Adjust each hazard's likelihood and severity before and after controls. The band follows the thresholds in Settings.
  3. Link the permits it covers, the programme activity and the bill section.

Why it matters: Everyone works to the same agreed method.

Step 2. Approve

  1. Press Submit for Review. The approver presses Approve. A very high residual risk blocks approval.

Step 3. Brief and sign on

  1. Press New Briefing, list the workers and read the RAMS out.
  2. Press Sign on each line; the worker signs on a small form.
  3. Print RAMS with Sign-on Sheet from the Print menu. If the method changes, press New Version.

Why it matters: A signed sheet proves each worker was told the risks.

Observation to action

Step 4. Report an observation

  1. Anyone uses Quality & Safety › Safety › Report an Observation: project, type, a sentence and a photo.
  2. The supervisor presses Add Corrective Action with an owner and due date. A serious near miss goes to Raise as Incident.

Step 5. Close the action

  1. The owner opens Quality & Safety › Safety › Corrective Actions (filter Mine), writes what was done or adds a photo, and presses Close Out.
  2. Overdue actions get a daily activity. The observation closes itself when all its actions are closed.

Monthly statistics

Step 6. Work out and print the month

  1. A statistics period per running job and one for the company are made on the 1st of each month in Quality & Safety › HSE Statistics › Monthly Statistics.
  2. Open it, press Recalculate if needed, write the commentary and print the Monthly HSE Report.
  3. LTIFR = lost-time injuries x base / man-hours. TRIR = recordable cases x 200,000 / man-hours.
  4. Read the trends on Quality & Safety › HSE Dashboard.

Why it matters: These are management figures, not a statutory return.

Step 7. Training, audits and drills

  1. Keep training cards in Quality & Safety › Training and Competence › Training Records. Quality & Safety › Training and Competence › Training Matrix shows days to expiry; negative means expired.
  2. Run audits and drills from their checklists, then press Raise Actions to turn every No into a corrective action.
