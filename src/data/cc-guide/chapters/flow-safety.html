Safety on site runs on four records: the permit to work before high risk work, the toolbox talk before the shift, the incident report when something goes wrong, and the safety inspection.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Who does what

Step Who
Permits QHSE Officer (issues), Site User (holds)
Toolbox talks Site User, QHSE
Incidents Anyone reports, QHSE investigates

Step 1. Permit to work

  1. Open Quality & Safety › Safety › Permits to Work and press New. Pick the permit type (hot work, confined space, height, excavation), location and times.
  2. Answer the type's checks. The issuer presses Issue, the holder Accept (Holder).
  3. During the work use Suspend / Resume or Extend. At the end press Close Out. Renew for Today copies it for the next day.

Why it matters: No high risk work starts without a permit.

Step 2. Watch live permits

  1. Open Quality & Safety › Safety › Permit Board: cards by status.

Step 3. Toolbox talk

  1. Open Quality & Safety › Safety › Toolbox Talks and press New. Pick the topic (Configuration › Quality and Safety › Toolbox Talk Topics).
  2. Use Copy Last Talk's Crew for attendance. Press Held, then All Signed the Sheet and Sign Off.

Why it matters: Proof that people were briefed.

Step 4. Report the incident

  1. Anyone reports it from the app (Requests tab) or in Quality & Safety › Safety › Incidents and Near Misses.
  2. QHSE presses Start Investigation, records the cause, then Raise Corrective Actions and Close.

Why it matters: Near misses reported today prevent injuries tomorrow.

Step 5. Safety inspection

  1. Raise an inspection in Quality & Safety › Quality › Inspections with a safety checklist and record the results as in the quality flow.
  2. Read the totals on Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › QHSE.

Tip

Risk assessments and method statements, safety observations, corrective actions, monthly HSE statistics, training and audits are walked through in the flow chapter "RAMS to briefing, observation to action, monthly HSE statistics".
