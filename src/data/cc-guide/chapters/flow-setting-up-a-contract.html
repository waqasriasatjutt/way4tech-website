Once a job is won, set it up so that every later screen has what it needs: the contract terms, the contract bill, the budget by cost code, the programme and the team who may see the job.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Who does what

Step Who
Project and team Project Manager, Administrator
Contract and bill Commercial Manager, Quantity Surveyor
Programme Planner, Project Manager

Step 1. Check the project

  1. Open Contracts & Setup › Projects and open the job.
  2. Check the client, the project manager, the start and end dates and the contract value.
  3. On the project you can also Apply a Template (Configuration › Jobs and Bills › Project Templates) to set stages and settings in one go.

Why it matters: The project is the key every other record hangs on.

Step 2. Fill in the contract terms

  1. Open Contracts & Setup › Contracts and open the contract.
  2. Set the Standard Form (for example FIDIC 2017 Red). The notice clauses and time bars come from it.
  3. Set retention percentage and limit, advance payment and recovery rate, payment terms and liquidated damages.
  4. Add the contract sections if the job is handed over in parts.

Why it matters: Every valuation, certificate and notice follows these rules. A wrong retention rate here is wrong on every certificate.

Step 3. Agree the contract bill

  1. Open Contracts & Setup › Bills of Quantities › Bills of Quantities and open the job's bill.
  2. Check sections, items, quantities and rates. Use Import from Excel or Export to Excel for big changes.
  3. Press Agree as Contract. The bill is now the contract bill that measurement and valuations read.

Why it matters: Measurement is only possible against an agreed bill.

Watch out

Change an agreed bill with New Revision, never by editing the old one. The old revision stays as the record.

Step 4. Set the budget and cost codes

  1. On each bill item, set the cost code and the budget cost (from the estimate or the rate build-up).
  2. Check the codes in Configuration › Jobs and Bills › Cost Codes.
  3. Open Dashboards & Reports › Cost and Margin › Budget vs Actual to see the budget per line.

Why it matters: Cost and value can only be compared month by month if both use the same codes.

Step 5. Load and freeze the programme

  1. Open Planning › Programmes and open the job's programme.
  2. Add or import the activities with durations and links.
  3. Press Recalculate Critical Path, then Freeze as Baseline.
  4. Look at it on Planning › Programme Gantt.

Why it matters: Every delay and every extension of time claim is measured against the frozen baseline.

Step 6. Name the team

  1. On the project, open the Team tab and add the people who work on the job.
  2. Where one person needs a different role on this job, add a membership in Configuration › Access Control › Project Memberships.
  3. Press Who Can See This Project on the project to check the result.

Why it matters: Site staff see only their jobs, and the phone app lists only those jobs.

Step 7. Set the site location

  1. On the project, open the Construction tab, Site Geofence.
  2. Type the latitude and longitude, or press Locate from Address.
  3. Set the radius. The same point drives the phone check-in and the Portfolio Map pin.

Why it matters: Check-ins outside the radius are refused or flagged.

Step 8. Start on site

  1. On the contract, press Start on Site.
  2. Open Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Project 360 to see the job in one screen.

Why it matters: The contract is now live: valuations, notices and time bars start counting.
