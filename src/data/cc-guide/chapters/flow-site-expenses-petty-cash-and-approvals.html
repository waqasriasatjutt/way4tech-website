Small costs on site and how every document finds its approver. The approval matrix and approval limits route a document to the right person; delegations cover leave; the audit trail records every change.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Blue boxes are steps. Orange boxes are checks or holds that can stop the flow.

Step 1. Petty cash float

  1. Open Site › Site Cash › Petty Cash Floats, press New, name the holder and amount, press Activate.

Step 2. Record a spend

  1. Open Site › Site Cash › Petty Cash Transactions, press New, attach the receipt, pick the project and category, press Submit.

Step 3. Approve

  1. The approver opens Site › Site Cash › Petty Cash to Approve or Waiting on Me and presses Approve (or Reject).
  2. Above your limit, the banner names who signs next. Finance then presses Post.
  3. Floats running low are listed in Site › Site Cash › Floats Needing Top-Up; press Replenish.

Step 4. Site expenses

  1. Open Site › Site Cash › Site Expenses, press New, enter the cost, Submit, Approve, Mark Paid or Create Vendor Bill.

Step 5. How approvals route

  1. Rules are in Finance › Controls › Approval Matrix; amount limits per role in Finance › Controls › Approval Limits.
  2. Away? Set a delegation in Finance › Controls › Delegations.
  3. Every change is in Finance › Controls › Audit Trail.

Why it matters: Big amounts reach senior people, and every change can be traced.
