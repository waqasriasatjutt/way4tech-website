A package of work is let to a subcontractor against an allowance in the bill. Each period your own engineer values the work at the subcontractor's rates, the valuation is certified with retention and contra charges deducted, and the subcontractor's bill is paid. Certificates are cumulative: everything is stated to date and the payment is the movement since the last one.

Step 1. Set up the package

  1. Open Procurement & Stores › Subcontracting › Subcontract Packages and press New.
  2. Write the scope and what is excluded, pick the bill items it covers. The allowance fills from the bill.
  3. Set the enquiry and return dates and the date needed on site, then press Enquiries Sent.

Why it matters: Letting within the allowance protects the margin. The buying gain is allowance less award.

Step 2. Level the quotes

  1. Add each firm's quote as received. Keep their figure untouched and add a levelling adjustment for anything they excluded.
  2. Check each bidder's compliance in the quote list.
  3. Press Quotes In.

Why it matters: Levelled quotes compare like with like.

Step 3. Award

  1. Press Award on the package and pick the firm.
  2. If the firm is not compliant and awards are set to Block (as in the demo), the award is refused with a message listing the missing documents and the Compliance Officer. The block is written in Procurement & Stores › Supplier Compliance › Compliance Log.
  3. On award the subcontract order is created. Open it and press Confirm, then Start Work.

Why it matters: Work never starts with a firm whose insurance has expired.

Note

In the demo, the award of package SP/HC/013 (wall and floor tiling, Harlow Court) to Ashworth Drylining was blocked because its public liability certificate had expired.

Step 4. Record insurance and bonds

  1. Enter the subcontractor's insurance certificates from the Insurance button on the subcontract, or in Contracts & Setup › Contract Administration › Subcontractor Insurance Certificates.
  2. Enter any performance bond received in Sales & Tenders › Bond Register with direction Received by Us.

Why it matters: A subcontract whose certificates have all expired shows a warning banner.

Step 5. Value the work

  1. Open Commercial & Billing › Subcontractor Accounts › Subcontract Valuations and press New. Pick the subcontract and the period.
  2. Press Value from Measurement. The lines fill from your own measured quantities at the subcontractor's rates.
  3. Reduce a line if the work is not complete or not free of defects. A line can never be valued above what was measured.
  4. Press Mark Valued.

Why it matters: You pay for what your engineer measured, not what the subcontractor applied for.

Step 6. Deduct contra charges

  1. Record costs caused by the subcontractor (damage, cleaning, attendance) in Commercial & Billing › Subcontractor Accounts › Contra Charges.
  2. Press Agree to Charge. Agreed charges are deducted on the next certificate.

Why it matters: Costs others caused are recovered, not absorbed.

Step 7. Certify and hold retention

  1. Press Certify on the valuation. Retention is deducted at the subcontract rate.
  2. If certifying is set to warn for non-compliant firms, a note is written on the valuation.
  3. Order changes go through Commercial & Billing › Subcontractor Accounts › Subcontract Order Variations and change the subcontract value.

Why it matters: The certificate fixes what is owed this period.

Step 8. Bill and pay

  1. Press Create Bill on the certified valuation. The bill appears in Finance › Invoices and Bills › Subcontractor Bills.
  2. Finance pays the bill and presses Mark Paid on the valuation. In the demo, paying a non-compliant subcontractor is blocked.

Why it matters: The subcontract payment notice period is often shorter than the main contract one. Pay on time.
