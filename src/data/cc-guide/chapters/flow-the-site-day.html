One normal day on site, from check-in to posting the diary. Most of it can be done on a phone with the Site App at /site, which works without signal and sends everything when the connection returns.

Tip

Open Home › How it works to see the processes as step cards with what is waiting for you at each step, and press Open to jump to the screen. Each document also shows a What's next banner that says what the current stage means and who acts next.

The flow at a glance

Who does what

Step Who
Check in Everyone on site
Roll call Foreman, supervisor
Diary and post Site User
Approve attendance Site User, Project Manager

Step 1. Open the Site App

  1. On the phone open the address /site (or Site › Open the Site App on a computer).
  2. Sign in once. In the browser menu choose Add to Home screen.
  3. The Today tab shows the site, the weather, who is checked in and the open items (inspections, hold points, snags, RFIs).

Why it matters: Everything the site needs, on the phone in the pocket.

Step 2. Check in

  1. Open the Check in tab and press the big button.
  2. The phone position is compared with the site geofence. Outside it, the check-in is refused or flagged, depending on the setting.

Why it matters: Attendance is proven by place and time.

Note

Check-in needs a connection, because the time must be the server's.

Step 3. Roll call for crews without phones

  1. The foreman opens Site › Attendance › Crew Roll Call and records the crew present in one go.

Why it matters: Fast attendance for the whole gang.

Step 4. Write the diary

  1. Open the Diary tab in the app.
  2. Tap the weather condition, enter the temperature and rain.
  3. Add manpower by trade with the +/- headcount and plant with hours.
  4. Write the work done, one activity per line, and any delays with cause and hours.
  5. Press Save to today's diary.

Why it matters: The diary is the daily record that wins disputes about delay and dayworks.

Step 5. Take photos

  1. Open the Photos tab and tap Take a photo.
  2. The photo is stamped with date, time, project and GPS position.
  3. Add a caption, the bill item and the location, and save.

Why it matters: Dated, placed photos are strong evidence.

Step 5b. Send requests from site

  1. Open the Requests tab.
  2. Choose Material requisition (goes straight to procurement), Raise an RFI (saved as a draft RFI for the document controller) or Incident or near miss (lands on the QHSE incident register).
  3. Fill in the short form and send it.

Why it matters: The site does not wait until it is back in the office to ask for things.

Step 6. Post the day

  1. In the app press Save and post the day, or on the computer open the diary in Site › Daily Records › Site Diaries and press Post Day.
  2. Posting creates the labour logs and the delay events from the diary lines.
  3. Tomorrow, press Copy Yesterday on the new diary to start from today's crews and plant.

Why it matters: Posting turns the record into cost and delay evidence.

Watch out

A posted diary cannot be edited in the app. Later entries are added as annotations so nothing is lost.

Step 7. Offline and the queue

  1. Without signal a grey bar says Offline. Keep working: entries go into a queue on the phone.
  2. The cloud button in the top bar shows how many entries are waiting. They are sent automatically when the signal returns, or press Send now.
  3. An entry the server cannot take stays marked Needs you with the reason.

Why it matters: No entry is lost and none is recorded twice.

Step 8. Approve attendance and check exceptions

  1. The supervisor opens Site › Attendance › Attendance to Approve and approves the day's records.
  2. Open Site › Attendance › Attendance Exceptions for check-ins outside the geofence or missing check-outs and fix them.
  3. In the office, Site › Daily Records › Site App Sync Log lists every entry received from phones.

Why it matters: Only approved hours go to cost and pay.
