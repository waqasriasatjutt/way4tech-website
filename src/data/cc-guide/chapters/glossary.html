Construction and system words used in this guide, in plain language.

Construction and commercial terms

Accrual
A cost that has happened (goods delivered, work done) but has not yet been billed. Booked at month end so the margin is not overstated.
Advance payment
Money the client pays at the start of a job, recovered by deducting a percentage from later certificates.
Advance payment guarantee
A bank guarantee that protects the client's advance until it is recovered.
Application for payment
The contractor's monthly claim to the client, made from the valuation.
As-built drawing
A drawing updated to show what was actually built.
Bid bond
A bank guarantee given with a tender, showing the bidder will sign if they win.
Bill of quantities (BoQ)
The list of every item of work with its quantity, unit and rate. The spine of the job.
Bill item
One line of the bill of quantities.
Budget
What a bill item, or the whole job, is expected to cost.
Buying gain
The difference between the allowance in the bill for a package and the price it was let at.
Certificate (payment certificate)
The client side's statement of what it will pay this period, after retention and earlier payments.
Claim
A request for more time, more money or both, under the contract.
Commitment (committed cost)
Money promised and not yet spent: approved requisitions, open orders, subcontract orders, plant hire booked.
Contra charge
A cost the contractor paid on a subcontractor's behalf, deducted from the subcontractor's next certificate.
Contract value (contract sum)
What the client agreed to pay at the start.
Cost code
A code that groups cost by kind of work (for example Substructure, Piling), used in reports and the CVR.
Cost value reconciliation (CVR)
The monthly comparison of value earned against cost incurred, which gives the true margin.
Daywork
Work paid by the time and materials used rather than by measured quantity, recorded on a signed sheet.
Defects liability period (defects period)
The time after handover, often 12 months, during which the contractor must fix defects.
Delay event
A record of something that delayed the work, with its cause and dates. The basis of a time claim.
Earned value (EV)
The budgeted cost of the work actually done.
Extension of time (EOT)
Extra time added to the completion date because of a delay the contractor is not responsible for.
Final account
The final statement of the contract value after all variations, claims and adjustments.
Final certificate
The certificate at the end of the defects period, which releases the last retention.
Form of contract (standard form)
A published set of contract conditions, such as FIDIC, NEC or AIA.
Hold point
A check where work must stop until an inspector releases it.
Inspection and test plan (ITP)
The list of checks to be done on a piece of work, with who does them and which are hold points.
Liquidated damages (LDs)
A fixed amount per day or week the contractor pays the client for finishing late.
Margin
Value minus cost. Profit.
Measurement
Recording the quantity of work built, item by item, against the bill.
Moiety
Half. Retention is usually released in two moieties.
Near miss
An event that could have hurt someone but did not.
Non-conformance report (NCR)
A record that work does not meet the specification, with the fix agreed.
Notice
A formal letter the contract requires, often within a strict number of days.
Package
A part of the work let to one subcontractor.
Permit to work
Written permission to do dangerous work, with the precautions in place.
Practical completion
The date the work is finished enough for the client to take it over.
Preliminaries (prelims)
The cost of running the site: management, welfare, cabins, cranes, scaffolding.
Provisional sum
An allowance in the bill for work not yet designed, adjusted when it is spent.
Quantity surveyor (QS)
The person who measures and values the work and manages the money chain.
Requisition
A request from site for materials.
Retention
A percentage the client holds back from each certificate until completion and the end of the defects period.
Revised contract value
Contract value plus agreed variations.
RFI (request for information)
A formal question to the design team.
RFQ (request for quotation)
A request to suppliers to give a price.
Site instruction
An instruction from the client's side, given on site.
Snag
A small defect to fix before or after handover.
Submittal
A document or sample sent for approval before use, such as a material data sheet.
Taking-over certificate
The certificate that records the client taking over the works; it starts the defects period.
Time bar
The last date a notice can be given. Miss it and the right may be lost.
Toolbox talk
A short safety briefing on site.
Transmittal
A record of which documents were sent to whom, when and why.
Valuation
The value of all work done to date, worked out each month.
Variation
A change to the work the client asked for, which changes the contract value.

Earned value terms

BAC
Budget at completion: the total cost budget.
PV
Planned value: the budgeted cost of the work that should be done by now.
AC
Actual cost of the work done.
SPI
Schedule performance index, EV divided by PV. Below 1.00 means behind programme.
CPI
Cost performance index, EV divided by AC. Below 1.00 means the work costs more than it earns.
EAC
Estimate at completion: what the job will cost when finished.

System terms

Action (Actions menu)
The gear menu on a list or form with Export, Archive, Duplicate and other actions.
Activity
A task with a due date linked to a record, shown in the clock menu at the top right.
Archive
Hide a record without deleting it. Archived records can be found with the Archived filter and restored.
Breadcrumb
The trail at the top left showing the screens you came through.
Chatter
The panel on a record with its history, messages, notes, activities and attachments.
Dashboard
A screen of tiles and charts summarising your work. Click any figure to open the records behind it.
Favorite
A saved search.
Filter
A ready-made search that narrows a list.
Follower
A person who receives the messages posted on a record.
Form view
The screen showing one record in full.
Group by
Showing a list in folders, one per value of a field.
Kanban
Cards in columns, usually one column per stage.
Log note
An internal note on a record. Only colleagues see it.
Period lock
A date before which records can no longer be changed.
Pivot
A summary table that adds up numbers by rows and columns.
Portal
The web page where clients, consultants, subcontractors and suppliers see their own records.
Quick action
A button on Home that opens a screen your role uses often.
Role
What a user is allowed to see and do.
Segregation of duties
The rule that the person who raised a document cannot approve it.
Smart button
A button on a form showing a count of linked records; click it to open them.
Status bar
The stages at the top right of a form, with the current one highlighted.
Two-factor sign-in
Logging in with a password and a code from your phone.
