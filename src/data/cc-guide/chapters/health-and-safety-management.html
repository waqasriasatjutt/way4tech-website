HSE Plus adds the safety management system: the HSE dashboard, risk assessments and method statements with briefings, safety observations, corrective actions, monthly HSE statistics, the injury and illness log, training and competence, and audits and drills.

Tip

Every section follows the same pattern: where the menu is, what it is for, who uses it, a picture of the list, a picture of a typical record, the important fields, the stages, the buttons and the linked records. On the live screens, the ? button next to the pager opens the same kind of help for the screen you are on.

HSE Dashboard

Where: Quality & Safety › HSE Dashboard

What it is for: HSE dashboard: man-hours, LTIFR, TRIR, days since the last lost-time injury, actions, observations and training.

Who uses it: QHSE Officers, Project Managers, Directors.

Why it matters: Safety performance at a glance.

How to use it:

  1. Open Quality & Safety › HSE Dashboard.
  2. Pick the company, the project and the period at the top, if the screen has them.
  3. Read the tiles and charts. Click a tile, a bar or a table row to open the records behind it.
  4. Press the refresh button to read the latest figures.

Report an Observation

Where: Quality & Safety › Safety › Report an Observation

What it is for: A quick form to report a safety observation: project, type, a sentence and a photo.

Who uses it: Anyone on site.

This menu opens a small window (a wizard). Fill it in and press the main button to run it, or close it to do nothing.

RAMS

Where: Quality & Safety › Safety › RAMS

What it is for: Risk assessments and method statements (RAMS) with hazards, controls, PPE and briefings.

Who uses it: Site Engineers prepare; QHSE Officers approve.

Why it matters: Everyone works to an agreed safe method.

The list shows: Reference, Title, Project, Version, Risk Before, Residual, Residual Band, Workers Signed On, Review By, State.

Stages

The bar at the top right shows where the record is. The stages are:

Stage What it means
Draft New and still being prepared. You can change anything.
In Review Being looked at by the reviewer or the client.
Approved Approved. The next step can start.
Superseded Replaced by a later version. Kept for the record.
Cancelled Cancelled. Kept for the record but ignored in totals.

Buttons

Button What it does When you see it
Submit for Review Sends it to the reviewer for approval. Only at Draft
Approve Approves the record so the next step can start. Only at In Review
New Briefing Starts a briefing of this RAMS with a list of workers. Only at Approved
New Version Copies this into the next version and marks the old one superseded. Only at Approved
Load Template Hazards Copies the hazards of the template. When it applies
Back to Draft Puts it back to draft so it can be changed. Only at In Review, Cancelled
Cancel Cancels the record. It stays in the list for the record. Not at Cancelled, Superseded

Related records (smart buttons)

The buttons along the top of the form count the linked records. Click one to open them:

  • Briefings: opens the briefings linked to this record.
  • Signed On: opens the signed on linked to this record.
  • Permits: opens the permits linked to this record.

Tabs on the form: Hazards and Controls, Method Statement, PPE and Training, Briefings.

The demo record shows a warning banner: "In review: the safety officer or project manager checks the controls and approves it. Work may not start on an unapproved RAMS." Banners like this tell you what is wrong or what to do next.
