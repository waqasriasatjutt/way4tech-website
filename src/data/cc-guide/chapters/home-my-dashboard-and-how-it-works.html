The Home app is where every user starts: the Home page for your role, your own dashboard of figures, and How it works, the process maps that show each job process step by step with what is waiting for you at each step.

Tip

Every section follows the same pattern: where the menu is, what it is for, who uses it, a picture of the list, a picture of a typical record, the important fields, the stages, the buttons and the linked records. On the live screens, the ? button next to the pager opens the same kind of help for the screen you are on.

Home

Where: Home

What it is for: Your landing page. It greets you, shows the approvals waiting on you, a large button to the dashboard for your main role and quick actions for the roles you hold.

Who uses it: Every user.

Why it matters: It is the shortest path from logging in to the work that needs you today.

Key steps:

  1. Click the approvals box to sign what is waiting on you.
  2. Open your dashboard from the large card.
  3. Use the quick actions for the jobs you do every day.
  4. Use How it works to see where each screen fits in the job.

What you see, from top to bottom:

  1. A greeting with your name, your company and your construction role or roles.
  2. Approvals waiting on you. Click it to open the documents that need your signature.
  3. Your dashboard. A large button to the dashboard of your main role (for example Executive for a director, Site for a project manager, Finance for finance staff).
  4. Quick actions for each role you hold, such as New Diary, New Requisition, Check In, Valuations or Petty Cash. A quick action only appears when you are allowed to open what is behind it.
  5. For administrators only: the setup checklist while setup is not finished, and a banner when the plan is nearly full.

My Dashboard

Where: Home › My Dashboard

What it is for: My Dashboard: your own figures, charts and lists picked from every construction dashboard.

Who uses it: Every user.

Why it matters: The numbers you check every day, in one place.

Key steps:

  1. Press Edit to change the layout.
  2. Add widgets from the library; you only see those your role may use.
  3. Drag to reorder and use the arrows to resize.
  4. Press Save; Reset brings back your role template.

What you see: tiles (one figure, click it to open the records), charts and short lists, picked from all the construction dashboards. A figure your role may not see shows "Hidden".

How to use it:

  1. Pick the period (All Time, This Month, This Quarter, This Year) and the job at the top. Your choice is remembered.
  2. Press Customise to change the dashboard. The widget library opens on the right.
  3. Press Add next to a widget, drag it by its title to move it, and use the size buttons to make it wider, narrower, taller or shorter. The bin removes it.
  4. Press Save, or Discard to throw the changes away. Reset to template puts back the template of your role.

How it works

Where: Home › How it works

What it is for: Every process of a construction job as a chain of steps: who does each step, what happens and what is waiting there now. Start here when you are not sure what comes next.

Who uses it: Everyone

Key steps:

  1. Pick a process on the left, for example Measure to Cash.
  2. Read the steps from left to right: each card says who does it and what happens.
  3. The number on a card is what is waiting at that step for you.
  4. Press Open on a card to go straight to that screen.

What it is for: How it works shows each construction process as a chain of steps, in the order the work is done: Tender to Contract, Contract Setup, Procure to Pay, Subcontract, Measure to Cash, Variations and Claims, Site Day, Quality, Documents, Labour Cost and Closeout.

Who uses it: Everyone. Each person only sees counts of records they could open.

How to use it:

  1. Open How it works.
  2. Pick a process on the left. The badge next to it is the total waiting for you.
  3. Read the step cards from left to right. Each card says who does the step, what happens, and what is waiting now (for example "3 measurements to agree").
  4. Press Open on a step to go straight to its screen, already filtered. A step done by another role says so instead of showing a button.
