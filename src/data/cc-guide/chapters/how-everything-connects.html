Every record in Construction Cloud is made from, or points to, another record. This chapter draws those links, then explains where every important number comes from. Read it when a figure surprises you: the answer is always in the record one step back.

Earned value figures

Figure Meaning Formula
BAC Budget at completion The BoQ cost budget
PV Planned value: the work that should be done by now BAC x planned % from the baselined programme
EV Earned value: the work actually done BAC x physical %
AC Actual cost Labour, approved site expenses, certified subcontract valuations, issued materials
SPI Schedule performance: ahead or behind EV / PV (below 1.00 = behind)
CPI Cost performance: earning more than spending or not EV / AC (below 1.00 = over cost)
EAC Estimate at completion BAC / CPI
ETC Estimate to complete EAC - AC
VAC Variance at completion BAC - EAC
TCPI Cost performance needed on the rest of the work to finish on budget (BAC - EV) / (BAC - AC)

Example

A job has a budget (BAC) of 1,000,000. By now the programme planned 40 percent (PV = 400,000). Measurement shows 35 percent built (EV = 350,000) and the actual cost is 380,000 (AC). SPI = 350 / 400 = 0.88: behind programme. CPI = 350 / 380 = 0.92: costing more than it earns. EAC = 1,000,000 / 0.92 = about 1,087,000.

Note: A snapshot is taken every Monday. Each Earned Value screen has a ? button listing every formula.

Project health colours

Colour Rule
Red (At Risk) Critical path more than 14 days late, forecast margin below zero, SPI below 0.85, or more than two incidents open
Amber (Watch) Any delay, margin under 8 percent, SPI under 0.95, or any open incident
Green (Healthy) None of the above

Margin and SPI count only for roles that see cost.

When a number looks wrong

  1. Click the number. Every dashboard figure opens the records behind it.
  2. Look one step back in the chains above. A missing actual cost is usually a delivery not issued to work, hours not costed, or a subcontract valuation not certified.
  3. Check the dates. Period locks, valuation dates and the dashboard's period buttons all filter by date.
  4. Check your role. Roles that do not see cost show Hidden instead of a figure.
