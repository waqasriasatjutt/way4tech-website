This chapter is for the people who look after the workforce: labour supervisors and foremen who confirm hours, and HR or payroll staff who turn hours into job cost and prepare the payroll. The goal is simple: every hour worked is charged once, to the right job and bill item, at the right cost.

Attendance

  • Site › Attendance › My Site Check-In: check in from a phone or computer. The site area (geofence) is checked.
  • Site › Attendance › Crew Roll Call: the supervisor ticks who is present.
  • Site › Attendance › All Attendance and Attendance by Site: everyone's records.
  • Site › Attendance › Attendance Exceptions: check-ins outside the site area, missing check-outs, late arrivals.

To approve: Site › Attendance › Attendance to Approve, select the lines, Approve (or Reject with a reason).

Loading hours

  1. Open Labour › Time and Gangs › Load from Attendance.
  2. Pick the dates and click Load.
  3. Every closed attendance in the dates becomes a time entry, once. Site attendance wins over office attendance for the same person and day, because it says which site.

Note

People who check in at the office go to their Usual Site (employee form, Job Costing tab) or the site of their gang.

Time Allocation (supervisor screen)

Labour › Time and Gangs › Time Allocation opens on the entries still to allocate, grouped by day.

  1. Check each person's project.
  2. Set the bill item and cost code the hours went to.
  3. If a person worked on two jobs or items, click Split. Point the copy at the other job or item and correct the hours.
  4. Select the rows and click Approve.
  5. Project manager or finance: select the approved rows and click Cost to Job.

Normal, overtime and rest-day or holiday hours and the cost are worked out on every change.

Watch out

A costed entry cannot be changed. Click Back to Allocate first. Costing is refused for a day on or before the site lock date and in a closed payroll period.

Why it matters: Hours booked to the right bill item are what make the labour productivity figures and the earned value true. Hours dumped on one item make every report wrong.

No double counting

The diary, gang allocations and attendance approvals also record labour. When a person's hours are costed through Time Allocation, those other records are marked Covered for that person and day, so the cost is counted once.

Watch out

Do not type labour logs by hand for people whose hours come through time entries.

Cost rates and overtime

Configuration › Site and Labour › Labour Cost Rates: a rate per trade (and grade), or per employee where it differs. A rate is basic pay plus allowances plus the employer's contributions, per month, day or hour, from a date.

  • Cost per hour = (pay x (1 + employer %) + employer fixed costs) / paid hours. This is what the job is charged.
  • The lookup for a person on a day: their own rate, then their trade's rate, then the trade's default day wage.

Configuration › Site and Labour › Overtime Rules: normal hours per day (8) and per week (48), the overtime multiplier (1.25), rest days with their multiplier (1.5) and the public holiday multiplier (2.0). A rule on a project overrides the company rule.

Payroll periods

  1. Labour › Payroll › Payroll Periods, New: name (for example November 2026) and dates.
  2. Load from Attendance, let the supervisors allocate and approve, cost to the jobs.
  3. Enter the month's camp and welfare costs on the Camp and Overheads tab.
  4. Close Period and Post. Refused while any entry is not costed. One journal entry is posted per job.
  5. Reopen cancels the period's entries if you must correct something.

Camp and welfare costs

Labour › Payroll › Camp and Welfare Costs: camp and accommodation, transport, food, visas and permits, medical and insurance, PPE and tools. Enter per worker per month, or a total shared by the workers listed. Allocate to Jobs shares each worker's amount over the jobs he worked on, by hours.

Manpower dashboard

Labour › Manpower › Manpower Dashboard: one row per job per month with heads, person-days, hours, overtime %, labour cost, cost per hour, absent days, absenteeism %, the labour budget and Labour Budget Used %. Over budget shows red; absenteeism over 10 percent shows amber.

Manpower Analysis gives the same as a pivot and graph by job, trade, person, week and month.

Payroll export

Labour › Payroll › Payroll Export: an Excel file of costed hours per employee, with a sheet of hours by job. Choose Generic, WPS-friendly or Pakistan-friendly columns.

Watch out

The file helps you prepare the payroll. It is not a certified bank salary file or a statutory return. Check it against your bank's or authority's current format.

Weekly routine for supervisors

  1. Every day: roll call or check that the crew checked in.
  2. Approve attendance exceptions.
  3. By Monday: allocate and approve last week's hours.

Monthly routine for HR and finance

  1. Cost all approved hours.
  2. Enter camp and welfare costs and allocate them.
  3. Close the payroll period and post.
  4. Export the payroll file.
