People's hours become job cost through labour logs, gangs, time entries and payroll periods.

Tip

Every section follows the same pattern: where the menu is, what it is for, who uses it, a picture of the list, a picture of a typical record, the important fields, the stages, the buttons and the linked records. On the live screens, the ? button next to the pager opens the same kind of help for the screen you are on.

Labour

Where: Labour

What it is for: Daily record of who worked on site: trade, headcount, hours and wage cost, per project.

Who uses it: Site Engineer, Project Manager

Key steps:

  1. Press New, pick the project and date.
  2. Enter the trade, the number of workers and the hours.
  3. Save. The wage cost lands on the project cost.

The list shows: Date, Project, Phase, Labour Type, Headcount, Days Worked, Daily Wage, Covered, Total Cost.

A typical record. Click any line to open it. This is "Labour Logs":

Labour Logs

Where: Labour › Time and Gangs › Labour Logs

What it is for: Labour logs: hours of a trade on a project and day, at a rate. They are the labour cost of the job.

Who uses it: Site Users, Project Managers.

Why it matters: Every labour cost figure reads these logs.

Note

This is the same screen as Labour ("Labour"). The fields and buttons are described there.

Gangs

Where: Labour › Time and Gangs › Gangs

What it is for: Gangs: named crews with a trade, headcount and a normal output per day.

Who uses it: Site Users, Project Managers.

Why it matters: Productivity is measured per gang.

Key steps:

  1. Press New and add the trades and headcount.
  2. Check the daily cost.
  3. Press Allocate Today to raise today's allocations.

The list shows: Gang, Project, Ganger, Trade, Headcount, Cost per Gang-Day, Productivity %.

A typical record. Click any line to open it. This is "Drylining gang A (7)":

The important fields

Field What to enter Where it comes from
Gang Type it. You enter it.
Code Type it. You enter it.
Project Leave empty for a gang that moves between jobs. The Projects list.
Ganger Who answers for the output. The Employees list.
Trade The kind of work this gang does. Filters the bill items it is offered. The Construction Work Type list.
Headcount Nothing, it fills in by itself. Worked out by the system.
Cost per Gang-Day The whole gang for one day at the wage rates on the make-up. Every allocation of this gang costs a multiple of this. Worked out by the system.
Productivity % Output achieved against the norm across every confirmed allocation. 100 is the estimate; below is money. Worked out by the system.
Make-Up Add lines in the table. Lines you add here.

Buttons

Button What it does When you see it
Allocate Today Creates today's labour allocation for this gang. Always

Related records (smart buttons)

The buttons along the top of the form count the linked records. Click one to open them:

  • Allocations: opens the allocations linked to this record.

Tabs on the form: Make-Up, Notes.

Tip

Allocate Today drafts one allocation per gang for today on its last bill item.

Labour Allocations

Where: Labour › Time and Gangs › Labour Allocations

What it is for: Labour allocations: a gang's day booked to a bill item, with output.

Who uses it: Site Users, Quantity Surveyors.

Why it matters: Compares output with the norm and books the cost.

Key steps:

  1. Press New, or select gangs and press Allocate Today.
  2. Enter the bill item and output.
  3. Press Confirm; use Copy to Tomorrow for the next day.

The list shows: Date, Project, Gang, Bill Item, Gang-Days, Headcount, Output, Unit, Norm per Gang-Day, Productivity %, Labour Cost, Labour Cost per Unit, Status.

A typical record. Click any line to open it. This is "Labour Allocations":

Stages

The bar at the top right shows where the record is. The stages are:

Stage What it means
Draft New and still being prepared. You can change anything.
Confirmed Confirmed and in force.
Cancelled Cancelled. Kept for the record but ignored in totals.

Buttons

Button What it does When you see it
Confirm Confirms the record. It now counts in totals and reports. Only at Draft
Copy to Tomorrow Copies this allocation to tomorrow. Not at Cancelled
Cancel Cancels the record. It stays in the list for the record. Not at Cancelled
Reset to Draft Puts the record back to draft so it can be changed. Not at Draft

Tabs on the form: Labour Logs, Notes.

Manpower Requests

Where: Labour › Time and Gangs › Manpower Requests

What it is for: Manpower requests: the site asks for people of a trade for dates.

Who uses it: Site Users, Project Managers.

Why it matters: People are planned like materials.

Key steps:

  1. Press New, pick the project, trades, headcount and dates.
  2. Press Submit, and Request Approval if needed.
  3. When the gang arrives, press Mark Fulfilled.

The list shows: Reference, Project, Trade, Headcount, Date From, Date To, Requested By, Fulfilled With, Status.

A typical record. Click any line to open it. This is "MR/2026/00002: 3 Steel Fixer":

The important fields

Field What to enter Where it comes from
Reference Nothing, it fills in by itself. Set by the system.
Project Pick from the list. The Projects list.
Phase Pick from the list. The Construction Phase list.
Requested By Pick from the list. The Users list.
Company Pick from the list. The Companies list.
Trade Pick from the list. The Construction Labour Type list.
Headcount Enter the number. You enter it.
Estimated Wage Cost Headcount times calendar days times the trade's day rate. The value the approval matrix reads. Worked out by the system.
Date From Pick the date. You enter it.
Date To Pick the date. You enter it.
Shift Choose one: Day, Night. You enter it.
Reason Write the details. You enter it.

Stages

The bar at the top right shows where the record is. The stages are:

Stage What it means
Draft New and still being prepared. You can change anything.
Submitted Sent for a decision. Waiting on the other side or an approver.
Approved Approved. The next step can start.
Fulfilled The request has been met.
Rejected Turned down. It can be reworked or cancelled.

Buttons

Button What it does When you see it
Submit Sends the record on for review or approval. Only at Draft
Approve Approves the record so the next step can start. Only at Submitted
Reject Turns the record down. Write the reason in the chatter. Only at Submitted, Approved
Mark Fulfilled Records that the request was met. Only at Approved
Reset to Draft Puts the record back to draft so it can be changed. Only at Submitted, Rejected
Request Approval Sends the document into the approval matrix. The approver sees it in Waiting on Me. When it applies

  • Approvals: opens the approvals linked to this record.

Time Allocation

Where: Labour › Time and Gangs › Time Allocation

What it is for: Time allocation: time entries still to be given a job, bill item and cost code, grouped by day.

Who uses it: Site Users (allocate, approve), Project Managers and Finance (cost to job).

Why it matters: Hours become job cost only once allocated and approved.

Key steps:

  1. Press Load from Attendance to bring in this week's hours.
  2. Use Split to share hours across jobs or bill items.
  3. Press Approve.
  4. Close the payroll period to cost them to the jobs.

The list shows: Date, Employee, Trade, Job, Bill Item, Cost Code, Hours, Normal Hours, Overtime Hours, Labour Cost, State.

A typical record. Click any line to open it. This is "Time Allocation":

Stages

The bar at the top right shows where the record is. The stages are:

Stage What it means
To Allocate Hours loaded but not yet given a job and bill item.
Approved Approved. The next step can start.
Costed to Job The hours are charged to the job as labour cost.
Cancelled Cancelled. Kept for the record but ignored in totals.

Buttons

Button What it does When you see it
Split Splits the entry in two, for a person on two jobs. Only at To Allocate
Approve Approves the record so the next step can start. Only at To Allocate
Cost to Job Charges the approved hours to the job as labour cost. Only at Approved
Back to Allocate Undoes the costing so the entry can be changed. Not at To Allocate
Cancel Cancels the record. It stays in the list for the record. Not at Cancelled

Related records (smart buttons)

Payroll Periods

Where: Labour › Payroll › Payroll Periods

What it is for: Payroll periods: a month of costed hours, closed with one journal entry per job.

Who uses it: Finance, Project Managers.

Why it matters: The month's labour cost lands in the accounts by job.

Key steps:

  1. Press New for the month.
  2. Press Load from Attendance and allocate the hours.
  3. Enter camp and welfare costs.
  4. Press Close Period and Post.

The list shows: Name, Date From, Date To, Hours, Overtime Hours, Labour Cost, Overhead Cost, Not Costed Yet, State.

Camp and Welfare Costs

Where: Labour › Payroll › Camp and Welfare Costs

What it is for: Camp and welfare costs (camp, transport, food, visas, medical, PPE) shared out to the jobs.

Who uses it: Finance.

Why it matters: Hidden labour costs reach the job instead of sitting in overhead.

Key steps:

  1. Press New and pick the period and cost type.
  2. Enter the cost per worker or as a total.
  3. Press Allocate to Jobs.

The list shows: Period, Name, Category, Basis, Amount, Recharged to Jobs %, Allocated Amount.

A typical record. Click any line to open it. This is "Camp beds, Kestrel Lodge":

The important fields

Field What to enter Where it comes from
Name Type it. You enter it.
Period Pick from the list. The Payroll Period list.
Category Choose one: Camp / Accommodation, Transport, Food / Mess, Visa and Work Permit, Medical and Insurance, PPE and Tools, Other. You enter it.
Camp Which camp or bus route, for the record. You enter it.
Basis Per worker: the amount is what one worker costs for the month. Total: the amount is shared equally by the workers listed. You enter it.
Amount Enter the amount. You enter it.
Recharged to Jobs % Share of the cost the jobs carry. The rest stays a company overhead and is not allocated. You enter it.
Allocated Amount Nothing, it fills in by itself. Worked out by the system.
Unallocated Amount The share of workers with no hours on any job in the period, plus whatever is not recharged. Worked out by the system.
Workers Leave empty for everybody with hours costed in the period. The Employees list.
Allocation Nothing, it fills in by itself. Set by the system.

Buttons

Button What it does When you see it
Allocate to Jobs Shares the cost out to the jobs by hours worked. Always
Workers from Labour Camp Fills the workers from the labour camp occupants. When it applies

Tabs on the form: Allocation, Project Expenses.

Payroll Export

Where: Labour › Payroll › Payroll Export

What it is for: A wizard that exports costed hours to Excel for payroll (generic, WPS-friendly or Pakistan-friendly).

Who uses it: Finance.

Why it matters: Payroll starts from the same hours as job cost.

Key steps:

  1. Pick the payroll period.
  2. Press Download.
  3. Load the file into your payroll.

This menu opens a small window (a wizard). Fill it in and press the main button to run it, or close it to do nothing.

What to fill in

Field What to enter Where it comes from
Period Pick from the list. The Payroll Period list.
Date From Pick the date. You enter it.
Date To Pick the date. You enter it.
Column Set Choose one: Generic, WPS-friendly (UAE salary file layout), Pakistan-friendly (CNIC, EOBI). You enter it.
Include Approved, Not Yet Costed Normally only hours costed to a job are exported. You enter it.

Buttons in the window: Download: Loads lines from other records so you do not type them.; Close: Closes the record.
