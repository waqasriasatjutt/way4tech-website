The report centre lists every report in one place. The monthly project report pack, the WIP schedule (over and under billing) and the company portfolio report are the documents a board reads.

Tip

Every section follows the same pattern: where the menu is, what it is for, who uses it, a picture of the list, a picture of a typical record, the important fields, the stages, the buttons and the linked records. On the live screens, the ? button next to the pager opens the same kind of help for the screen you are on.

Report Centre

Where: Dashboards & Reports › Report Packs › Report Centre

What it is for: Report centre: every report, export and dashboard, grouped by subject, with an Open button.

Who uses it: Everyone.

Why it matters: One place to find any report.

Monthly Report Packs

Where: Dashboards & Reports › Report Packs › Monthly Report Packs

What it is for: Monthly project report packs: the figures of the month frozen in a branded PDF and sent to the recipients.

Who uses it: Project Managers; Commercial and Finance read.

Why it matters: The same monthly report for the client and the board.

The list shows: Month, Project, Contract Revised, Physical %, Margin Pct, Rag, Date Generated, State.

A typical record. Click any line to open it. This is "Harlow Court, Levels 3 to 5 Fit-Out October 2026":

The important fields

Field What to enter Where it comes from
Name Nothing, it fills in by itself. Worked out by the system.
Project Pick from the list. The Projects list.
Month Any day in the month; it is moved to the 1st. You enter it.
Date To Nothing, it fills in by itself. Worked out by the system.
Recipients Who gets the pack by email. Starts from the project's report recipients. The Contacts list.
Rag Nothing, it fills in by itself. Set by the system.
Contract Revised Nothing, it fills in by itself. Set by the system.
Certified Nothing, it fills in by itself. Set by the system.
Forecast Cost Nothing, it fills in by itself. Set by the system.
Margin Pct Nothing, it fills in by itself. Set by the system.
Physical % Nothing, it fills in by itself. Set by the system.
PDF Nothing, it fills in by itself. Set by the system.
Date Generated Nothing, it fills in by itself. Set by the system.
Date Sent Nothing, it fills in by itself. Set by the system.
Executive Summary Your words at the top of the pack. Written by the project manager each month; kept when the pack is regenerated. You enter it.

Stages

The bar at the top right shows where the record is. The stages are:

Stage What it means
Draft New and still being prepared. You can change anything.
Generated The record is at the "Generated" stage.
Sent Sent to the other party.

Buttons

Button What it does When you see it
Generate Builds the lines from the current data. Only at Draft
Regenerate Builds the PDF again after a correction. Only at Generated
Open PDF Opens the PDF made for this record. When it applies
Send Sends it to the recipients by email. Only at Generated
Back to Draft Puts it back to draft so it can be changed. Only at Generated

Tabs on the form: Executive Summary, Risks and Look-ahead, Photos.

WIP Schedule

Where: Dashboards & Reports › Report Packs › WIP Schedule

What it is for: WIP schedule: earned revenue against billing per job, showing over and under billing.

Who uses it: Finance, Commercial Managers.

Why it matters: A month-end figure finance needs for the accounts.

The list shows: As Of, Contract Total, Earned Total, Billed Total, Over Billing, Under Billing, Net Over (Under), State.

A typical record. Click any line to open it. This is "WIP 2026-10-31":

The important fields

Field What to enter Where it comes from
Name Nothing, it fills in by itself. Worked out by the system.
As Of Pick the date. You enter it.
Company Pick from the list. The Companies list.
Include Completed Jobs Completed jobs with retention or final account still open are often kept on the schedule until closed. You enter it.
Over Billing Nothing, it fills in by itself. Worked out by the system.
Under Billing Nothing, it fills in by itself. Worked out by the system.
Net Over (Under) Nothing, it fills in by itself. Worked out by the system.
Projects Add lines in the table. Lines you add here.

Stages

The bar at the top right shows where the record is. The stages are:

Stage What it means
Draft New and still being prepared. You can change anything.
Final The record is at the "Final" stage.

Buttons

Button What it does When you see it
Compute Works out the adjustment from the indices. Only at Draft
Export to Excel Downloads this record as an Excel file. When it applies
Finalise Keeps this period as final. When it applies
Back to Draft Puts it back to draft so it can be changed. Only at Final

Company Portfolio Report

Where: Dashboards & Reports › Report Packs › Company Portfolio Report

What it is for: Company portfolio report: one line per job with value, progress, margin, cash and status.

Who uses it: Directors, Commercial, Finance.

Why it matters: The whole company on one page.

This menu opens a small window (a wizard). Fill it in and press the main button to run it, or close it to do nothing.

What to fill in

Field What to enter Where it comes from
Company Pick from the list. The Companies list.
As Of Pick the date. You enter it.
Include Completed Jobs Tick when it applies. You enter it.

Buttons in the window: Print PDF: Prints the record as a PDF.; Export to Excel: Downloads this record as an Excel file.; Close: Closes the record.
