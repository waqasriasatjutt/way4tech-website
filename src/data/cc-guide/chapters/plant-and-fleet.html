Plant and Fleet adds fuel management, driver logs, plant transfers, hourly plant hire with accruals, hire-out of your own plant, plant daily logs and inspection certificates.

Tip

Every section follows the same pattern: where the menu is, what it is for, who uses it, a picture of the list, a picture of a typical record, the important fields, the stages, the buttons and the linked records. On the live screens, the ? button next to the pager opens the same kind of help for the screen you are on.

Plant Daily Logs

Where: Procurement & Stores › Plant and Fleet › Plant Daily Logs

What it is for: Plant daily logs: one per machine per day with working, idle and breakdown hours, meter, fuel and work done.

Who uses it: Site Engineers, foremen.

Why it matters: Hire charges and services follow the real hours.
