The buyer turns approved site requests into orders at the right price, from suppliers who are allowed to supply, delivered on time. You also look after the supplier list and their compliance documents.

1. Pick up approved requisitions

  1. Open Procurement & Stores › Buying › Requisitions.
  1. Filter on approved ones. Check the required date and the delivery address.

2. Ask for prices (RFQ)

  1. Select the requisitions to buy together and use Actions › Request Quotes (or New under Procurement & Stores › Buying › Requests for Quotation).
  2. Choose the suppliers to ask and the date replies are due.
  3. Send the RFQ. Each supplier gets the list of items.
  1. Enter each supplier's prices as they arrive.

3. Compare and award

  1. Open Procurement & Stores › Buying › Quote Comparison.
  1. Compare price, delivery time and terms line by line.
  2. On the RFQ, click Award. Choose the supplier per line if you split the order. Purchase orders are created.

Watch out

Above your approval limit, the award goes to the next person up for signature first. The form shows a yellow banner saying who signs next.

4. Purchase orders

Procurement & Stores › Buying › Purchase Orders lists the orders from construction requisitions. Confirm and send them to the supplier.

Note

If the supplier is not compliant (for example an expired licence), confirming a large order may be blocked or warned, depending on the company setting.

5. Delivery and the bill

The storekeeper records the delivery as Goods Received (Chapter 16). When the supplier's bill arrives, Finance › Invoices and Bills › Supplier Bills to Check shows bills where the bill, the order and the delivery do not agree (the three-way match). Resolve each difference with the supplier before finance pays.

Supplier compliance

You keep the approved vendor list and the documents each supplier must hold.

Vendor compliance status

Procurement & Stores › Supplier Compliance › Vendor Compliance shows each vendor's status: Compliant, Expiring Soon, Non-Compliant, Suspended or Blacklisted, and what is missing in plain words.

Prequalifying a vendor

  1. Procurement & Stores › Supplier Compliance › Prequalifications, New.
  2. Pick the vendor, the questionnaire and the trades they applied for.
  3. Enter their answers. The score and a recommendation appear.
  4. Check the Documents Checklist tab.
  5. Click Send for Review. The reviewer approves, approves with conditions, or rejects.

Compliance documents

  1. Procurement & Stores › Supplier Compliance › Compliance Documents, New: vendor, document type, number, issuer, dates and the file.
  2. Submit for Review; the reviewer approves or rejects.
  3. Renew before expiry. Vendors can also upload their own documents on the portal (Chapter 21).

Documents to Review lists uploads waiting for you.

Why it matters

An uninsured subcontractor's accident can become your claim. The compliance holds stop awards, orders and payments to vendors whose documents have lapsed.

Plant and tools

  • Procurement & Stores › Plant and Tools › Plant Hire and Daily Plant Sheet: hired plant and its daily hours.
  • Procurement & Stores › Plant and Tools › Tools and Custody Register: who holds which tool.
  • Dashboards & Reports › Site and Time › Idle and Overdue Plant: hired plant standing idle or past its off-hire date.

Plant and fleet

The plant manager (often in procurement) also keeps:

  • Fuel Tanks, Tank Deliveries and Tank Dips: site tank stock and the variance between book and measured fuel.
  • Procurement & Stores › Plant and Fleet › Fuel Anomalies: fuel issues flagged against the norm. Check each with the operator and click Mark Reviewed.
  • Procurement & Stores › Plant and Fleet › Certificates and Inspections: thorough examinations, third-party inspections, registrations and insurance for each machine, with Record Renewal.
  • Procurement & Stores › Plant and Tools › Off-Hire Due: hired plant whose off-hire date is close or past. Off-hire it or extend the hire.
