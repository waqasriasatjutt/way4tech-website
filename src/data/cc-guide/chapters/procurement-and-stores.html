From the site asking for material to the goods on site: requisitions, requests for quotation, quote comparison, purchase orders, goods received, subcontract packages and orders, site stores, plant and tools, and supplier compliance.

Procurement & Stores

Where: Procurement & Stores

What it is for: Requests from site for the materials a job needs. A requisition is the start of buying: once approved it goes out for quotes or is issued from the store.

Who uses it: Site Engineer, Site User, Procurement

Key steps:

  1. Press New, pick the project and add the materials and quantities.
  2. Press Submit, then Request Approval if the amount needs a signature.
  3. Once approved, procurement uses Request Quotes to ask suppliers for prices.
  4. When goods arrive, press Receive Goods to record the delivery.

The list shows: Reference, Project, Phase, Request Date, Total Cost, Status.
