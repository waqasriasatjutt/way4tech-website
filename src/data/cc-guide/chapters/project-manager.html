As project manager you run one or more jobs. You see the site's records and the job's costs, you approve what the site asks for, and you keep the programme, the information flow and the budget under control.

What you see when you log in

Your Home shows the Site Manager Dashboard as your dashboard, and these quick actions:

Quick action Opens
Projects Your construction projects
Work Orders Work handed to gangs and subcontractors
Approvals Documents waiting for your signature
New Diary A new site diary for today

Note

If your company restricts users to their project team, you see only the projects you manage or are on the team of.

Your project form

Open Contracts & Setup › Projects and click your job.

What to check on it:

  • Smart buttons across the top: costs, tasks, status and, under More, the bill items, subcontracts, requisitions, diaries, inspections, closeout and Project 360.
  • Tabs: Map (the site), Description, Settings, Construction, Team and Members.
  • Construction tab: contract value, budget, committed, actual cost, margin and progress, and the Site Geofence (the site position used for check-in).
  • Team tab: the people on the project team. Only they see the job when team restriction is on.
  • Members tab: each person's role on this job (see Chapter 80).

Tip

Project 360 on the project form gives the whole job on one screen: value, cost, cash, open items and programme.

Daily tasks

1. Approve what the site needs

  1. On Home, click approvals waiting on you.
  2. Open each requisition, job sheet or expense. Check quantities and the bill item it is booked to.
  3. Click Approve, or Refuse with a reason.

Why it matters

An approved requisition becomes a committed cost against the bill line. If the line is over budget, the system warns you (or stops you, depending on the company setting) before the money is promised.

2. Read the site diaries

Site › Daily Records › Site Diaries. Each diary is one day on one site: weather, manpower, plant, work done, delays, visitors and instructions. Check that every site has today's diary and that delays are written down with their cause.

3. Approve attendance exceptions

Site › Attendance › Attendance to Approve. Approve the ones with a good reason; reject the rest. Approved attendance becomes labour hours on the job.

Weekly tasks

Work orders and the schedule

A work order hands part of the bill to a gang or subcontractor with dates.

  1. Open Planning › Work Orders › Work Orders to see them all.
  1. Planning › Work Orders › Work Order Gantt shows them as bars on a time line. Drag a bar to move its dates.
  1. Planning › Work Orders › Delayed Work Orders lists the ones past their end date.
  2. To create work orders from the bill in one go, use Planning › Work Orders › Generate Work Orders from Bill.

Manpower requests

When you need more people, raise a manpower request: Labour › Time and Gangs › Manpower Requests, then New. Give the trade, headcount, dates and project.

RFIs and instructions

  • Documents › Queries and Approvals › Requests for Information: questions to the design team. Check which are overdue and which are holding up work (Dashboards & Reports › Site and Time › RFIs Holding Up Work).
  • Commercial & Billing › Variations and Claims › Site Instructions: every instruction received on site. Make sure verbal ones are confirmed in writing (Dashboards & Reports › Site and Time › Unconfirmed Verbal Instructions).

Programme and delays

  • Planning › Programmes holds the baselined programme.
  • Site › Daily Records › Delays and Stoppages records each delay with its cause, dates and the activities it hits. Raise a contract notice from the delay event so the time bar is not missed (Chapter 13).

Watch out

Many contracts lose the right to extra time if the notice is late. Raise a delay event the day the delay starts.

Monthly tasks

Budget against cost

  1. Dashboards & Reports › Cost and Margin › Budget vs Actual: every bill item with budget, committed, actual and remaining.
  1. Dashboards & Reports › Cost and Margin › Lines Over Budget: only the items already over budget.
  2. Explain each overspend to the commercial team before the monthly cost value reconciliation (CVR).

Earned value and Project 360

Open Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Project 360 for your job and check the forecast margin, the S-curve and the programme. Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › Earned Value gives SPI and CPI (see Chapter 70).

Closeout (end of the job)

When the job nears completion use the Closeout app: commissioning systems and tests, handover packages, the taking-over certificate, defects and warranties, and the closeout checklist. Chapter 70 shows how they link.

The monthly report pack

Why it matters

The pack is the one document the client, the director and the commercial team all read. Its figures are frozen at the month end, so everyone discusses the same numbers.

  1. Open Dashboards & Reports › Report Packs › Monthly Report Packs (or the Report Packs button on the project) and click New.
  2. Pick the project and any day of the month (it moves to the 1st).
  3. Write the executive summary, risks and look-ahead notes.
  4. Click Generate. The month's figures are collected as at the month end and the branded PDF is made.
  1. Click Open PDF and check it. Correct anything and Generate again.
  2. Click Send. The PDF is emailed to the recipients.

To make it automatic, tick Monthly Report Pack on the project's Reporting tab and add the recipients. Last month's pack is then made and sent on the pack day (the 5th, unless Settings say otherwise).
