The QHSE officer looks after quality, health, safety and environment: permits to work, toolbox talks, incidents, inspection and test plans, inspections, non-conformance reports (NCRs) and snags. You also keep the libraries behind them: permit types, toolbox topics and inspection plans.

Permits to work

A permit to work controls dangerous work: hot work, work at height, confined spaces, excavation, live electrical work.

  1. Quality & Safety › Safety › Permits to Work, New.
  2. Choose the Permit Type. The precautions to check are copied from the type.
  3. Enter the project, location, the work, who does it and the valid from and to times.
  4. Tick each precaution as it is in place.
  5. Issue the permit. When the work is finished, Close it.

Quality & Safety › Safety › Permit Board shows all live permits on one screen.

Watch out

A permit left open overnight means nobody knows if the area is safe. The dashboard counts live permits; check it every afternoon.

Toolbox talks

Quality & Safety › Safety › Toolbox Talks, New: the topic (from the topic library), project, date, who gave it, and the attendees. Attendees can sign.

Incidents and near misses

  1. Quality & Safety › Safety › Incidents and Near Misses. Site staff report from the phone; you can also click New.
  1. Open the incident. Complete the type, severity, people involved, whether time was lost, and the immediate action.
  2. Investigate: write the root cause.
  3. Add Corrective Actions, each with an owner and a due date.
  4. Close the incident when the actions are done.

Inspection and test plans (ITP)

An ITP lists the checks that must be done on a piece of work, and which are hold points (work stops until released).

  1. The library of plans is under Configuration › Quality and Safety › ITP Templates (concrete, reinforcement, formwork, blockwork, waterproofing, steel, drainage, MEP first fix).
  2. To use one on a job: Quality & Safety › Quality › Inspection and Test Plans, New.
  3. Give it a title, the project and the template. The points are copied.
  4. Under Covers, pick the bill sections and items it applies to.
  5. Name the Releasing Engineer and the Consultant.
  6. Click Activate.

For each point, Request raises an inspection. While a hold point is open, the bill items it covers cannot be measured. Release it from the plan or Quality & Safety › Quality › ITP Hold Points by Plan.

Why it matters

Hold points stop the company being paid for work that was covered up before it was checked.

Inspections

Quality & Safety › Quality › Inspections: each inspection with its checks. Record Pass, Fail or N/A. A failed check raises an NCR.

Hold Points Awaiting Release lists the hold points waiting for you.

Non-conformance reports (NCRs)

  1. Quality & Safety › Quality › Non-conformance Reports.
  1. Each NCR records what is wrong, the trade responsible, the agreed fix and the due date.
  2. When the fix is done, inspect again and close the NCR.

Snags

Quality & Safety › Quality › Snags: defects found at any time, with photo, location, trade and priority. Assign each to the subcontractor responsible. A snag can be marked to hold retention.

Locations

Configuration › Quality and Safety › Locations: the areas of each site (block, level, room). Use them on inspections, snags and NCRs so problems can be found again.

Risk assessments and method statements (RAMS)

Site engineers and supervisors prepare RAMS; the QHSE officer or project manager approves them; workers are briefed and sign.

  1. Open Quality & Safety › Safety › RAMS and click New. Pick the project and a template (the activity). Hazards, controls, PPE, required training and the method are copied in.
  2. For each hazard, set the likelihood and severity before and after controls. The score is likelihood times severity, and the band (low, medium, high, very high) follows the thresholds in Settings.
  3. Link the permits it covers, the programme activity and the bill section.
  4. Click Submit for Review. The approver clicks Approve. A very high remaining risk blocks approval.
  5. Click New Briefing: list the workers, read the RAMS out, then each worker signs on their line.
  6. If the method changes, click New Version. The old version is kept with its briefings.

Print the RAMS with Sign-on Sheet from the Print menu.

Note

Settings can rename RAMS to SWMS (safe work method statements) if that is what your company calls them.

Safety observations and near misses

Anyone on site can report an observation with Quality & Safety › Safety › Report an Observation: project, type, one sentence and a photo. Supervisors and the QHSE officer action and close them in Quality & Safety › Safety › Safety Observations.

  1. Open the observation and click Add Corrective Action with an owner and a due date.
  2. A near miss worth investigating goes to Raise as Incident, which creates an incident and links the two.
  3. The observation closes itself when every action is closed out.

Corrective actions

Quality & Safety › Safety › Corrective Actions (filters Open, Overdue, Mine). The owner writes what was done, adds a photo if useful, and clicks Close Out. A daily job reminds the owner of every overdue action.

HSE statistics

Quality & Safety › HSE Statistics › Monthly Statistics holds one period per running job and one for the company, made on the 1st of each month.

Figure How it is worked out
Man-hours From HR time entries, site diary manpower or labour logs, whichever is fullest (never added together)
LTIFR Lost-time injuries x 1,000,000 / man-hours (base set in Settings)
TRIR Recordable cases x 200,000 / man-hours
Severity rate Days lost x 200,000 / man-hours
Days since last LTI At the end of the period

Write the commentary, click Recalculate if records changed, and print the Monthly HSE Report.

Note

These are management figures. They are not a statutory return.

The Injury and Illness Log lists every recordable case (set on the incident's Injury and Illness Log tab), and the Annual Summary counts cases and days per year with the hours worked.

Training and competence

  • Quality & Safety › Training and Competence › Training Records: each worker's card with its expiry date (filled from the training type's validity).
  • Quality & Safety › Training and Competence › Training Matrix: a pivot of workers against training with the days to expiry. Negative means expired.

Training can be required by the job position, the permit type and the RAMS. When workers are listed on a permit, missing training shows as a warning, or stops the permit being issued if Settings say Block. A nightly job chases expiring cards to the worker's manager.

Audits and emergency drills

  1. Open Quality & Safety › Safety › Audits and Drills, New. Pick the kind and the checklist; the questions load.
  2. Walk the site and answer Yes, No or N/A, with a finding for each No.
  3. Click Complete. The score is the weight of Yes answers over all applicable questions, against the checklist's pass mark.
  4. Raise Actions turns every No into a corrective action.
  5. A drill also records the scenario, the time to muster against the target and the headcount.
