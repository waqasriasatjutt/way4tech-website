Quality & Safety

Where: Quality & Safety

What it is for: The inspection and test plan for a piece of work on a job. Its hold points stop measurement of the bill items it covers until released.

Who uses it: QHSE Officer, Site Engineer

Key steps:

  1. Press New, pick the ITP template, the project and the bill sections.
  2. Press Activate; the hold points are created.
  3. Inspect and release the points as work goes on.
  4. Press Close Plan when the work is finished.

The list shows: Reference, Title, Project, ITP Template, Bill Sections, Releasing Engineer, Points, Open Holds, Failed, Completion %, State.

Stages

The bar at the top right shows where the record is. The stages are:

Stage What it means
Reported Reported and waiting to be dealt with.
Under Investigation Being investigated.
Actions Open The investigation is done and corrective actions are being carried out.
Closed Closed. Kept for the record.
Cancelled Cancelled. Kept for the record but ignored in totals.

Buttons

Button What it does When you see it
Start Investigation Starts the investigation. Only at Reported
Raise Corrective Actions Records the actions that stop it happening again. Only at Under Investigation
Close Closes the record. Only at Reported, Under Investigation, Actions Open
Cancel Cancels the record. It stays in the list for the record. Not at Closed, Cancelled
Reopen Opens the record again. Only at Closed, Cancelled

Tabs on the form: Investigation, Corrective Actions, Injury and Illness Log.

Tip

A daily job raises the statutory report before its deadline and chases the actions.
