Short answers to the questions people ask most. Each points to the chapter with the full explanation.

Logging in and access

I forgot my password.

Click Reset Password on the sign-in page and follow the link in the email. If no email arrives, check your spam folder, then ask your administrator.

The invitation link does not work.

Links work once and expire after a few days. Ask your administrator to send a new invitation.

I am asked for a code after my password.

Your role uses two-factor sign-in. Open your authenticator app and type the current six-digit code (Chapter 1).

I lost the phone with my authenticator app.

Ask your administrator to switch two-factor off for your user. Then set it up again on the new phone.

The system keeps sending me to "Set up two-factor sign-in".

Your role requires it. Click Set it up now and follow the steps in Chapter 1. The page will not go away until it is done.

My screen locked while I was away.

Your company has a session timeout. Sign in again; nothing you saved is lost.

I cannot sign in from home.

Your role may be limited to sign-in from the office network (Sign-In Networks). Ask your administrator.

A menu in this guide is missing on my screen.

Your role does not include it. Check your role on the Home greeting. If you need it for your work, ask your administrator (Chapter 2, "Roles: who sees what").

I cannot see one of our projects.

Your company restricts users to the projects they work on. Ask the project manager to add you to the project team, or the administrator to add a project membership (Chapter 80).

I see "Hidden" instead of a figure.

Your role does not see costs or margins. The figure is not sent to your screen. This is deliberate.

Everyday use

How do I get back to Home?

Click the square app icon at the top left, then Home.

I changed a record by mistake. How do I undo it?

If it is not saved yet, click the discard button (curved arrow) next to the breadcrumb. If it is saved, change the field back; the chatter keeps the history of both changes.

I cannot change a record: the fields are grey.

The record has moved past the stage where it can be edited (for example, a certified valuation), the period is locked, or your role can only read it. Look at the status bar and the role table in Chapter 2.

The system says the period is locked.

Records dated on or before the lock date cannot be created or changed. Ask the administrator or finance if a correction is really needed (Chapter 80).

How do I find records from last month?

Open the search panel, choose a date filter (for example Last Month), or group by month.

How do I save a list layout I use every day?

Set the filters and grouping, then Favorites › Save current search, and tick Default filter if you want it every time (Chapter 1).

How do I send a record to a colleague?

Open the record, click Log note, type @ and their name, and write your message. They get a notification with a link.

How do I export a list to Excel?

Select the rows, Actions › Export, choose the fields, Export (Chapter 1).

Can I change the language?

Yes. Preferences › Language (Chapter 1).

Site and the phone app

The site app says Offline.

You have no signal. Keep working: entries wait on the phone and are sent automatically when the signal returns (Chapter 12).

An entry in the app says "Needs you".

The office could not accept it, for example because the inspection was already closed. Read the reason, then discard the entry.

Check-in is refused.

You are outside the site area, or there is no connection. Move inside the site boundary and make sure you have signal. If the site position is wrong, tell the project manager; it is set on the project's Construction tab.

I cannot release a hold point from the phone.

Hold points are released in the office by the releasing engineer, a QHSE officer or the project manager (Chapter 17).

I posted the diary and then remembered something.

Add it in the app. On a posted day it is kept as an annotation; the posted diary is not changed (Chapter 12).

Why can I not record measurement on a bill item?

An inspection and test plan has an open hold point on that item. Release the hold point first (Chapter 17).

Approvals and money

What does "Waiting on Me" mean?

Documents you can sign now, including ones delegated to you (Chapter 10).

Why can I not approve my own requisition?

Segregation of duties: the person who raised a document cannot approve it. Ask the next approver, or the administrator for a logged override in a real emergency (Chapter 80).

I approved it but it went to someone else.

The amount is above your approval limit. The yellow banner on the form says who signs next.

The approval started again after I signed.

The document's value changed after you signed, so the approval chain was raised again. Check the new value and sign again.

The measurement sheet is refused because it exceeds the bill quantity.

Extra quantity is a change to the contract. Raise a variation for the extra (Chapter 13).

The award, order or payment to a supplier is blocked.

The supplier is not compliant: a required document is missing or expired, or the vendor is suspended. The message names what is missing and who to contact (Chapter 15).

Why is the certificate amount less than the application?

The certifier agreed less. Each line shows the difference and the reason. The shortfall is the starting point for the next valuation (Chapter 13).

Why is retention not released?

It is not yet due, or open snags are holding it. See Commercial & Billing › Retention › Snags Holding Retention (Chapter 13).

Labour cost seems counted twice.

It should not be. Costed time entries cover diary and gang labour for the same person and day. Check the Covered column on the labour logs, and that nobody typed labour logs by hand for those people (Chapter 19).

Dashboards and reports

A dashboard figure looks wrong.

Click it to open the records behind it, then follow Chapter 70, "When a number looks wrong".

The dashboard shows a different margin from the CVR.

Live margin moves every day; CVR margin is the figure signed at month end, with accruals and provisions (Chapter 10).

Why is a project red on the Portfolio Map?

It meets one of the red rules: more than 14 days late on the critical path, negative forecast margin, SPI below 0.85, or more than two open incidents (Chapter 70).

A project is not on the map.

It has no site position. Set it on the project's Construction tab, Site Geofence, or press Locate from Address.

Can I get the dashboard by email?

Yes. Dashboards & Reports › Dashboards › My Digests (Chapter 10).

Portal

Our client cannot see the contract on the portal.

The contract has not been shared. Click Share with Client on the contract (Chapter 21).

A consultant cannot see a project.

Add them to the project's Members tab with the role External Consultant.
