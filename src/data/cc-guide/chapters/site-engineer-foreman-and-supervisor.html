This chapter is for the people who run the work on site: site engineers, foremen, supervisors and site managers. You record what happens on site: the diary, attendance, photos, inspections, snags and material requests. You do most of it on a phone, with the site app, and some of it on a computer.

Note

Site roles never see prices, rates, wages or margins. The figures are not sent to your screen at all. You record facts; the office puts the money on them.

Your dashboard is the Site Manager Dashboard. It shows the same tiles as for the project manager (Chapter 11) but only for your projects, and without any cost figures.

The site app on your phone

The site app is a simple phone screen at the system address followed by /site. It works without signal and sends everything when the connection comes back.

Install it

  1. On your phone, open the address followed by /site and sign in.
  2. Use the browser menu, Add to Home screen (iPhone) or Install app (Android).
  3. Open it from the new icon from now on.

The app shows only the projects you work on. Tap the site name at the top to switch project.

Today

Today shows the site name and address, the latest weather on record, people checked in now and in today's diary, and the open items: inspections, hold points, snags, RFIs, NCRs and ITP completion. Below that is today's diary status and the current drawings (current revision only).

Tip

Every tab has a ? button with a short explanation.

Diary (Site User and up)

  1. Tap Diary.
  2. Weather: tap the condition, enter the temperature and any rain.
  3. Manpower: add each trade and use + and - for the headcount.
  4. Plant: each machine with its hours.
  5. Work done: one activity per line.
  6. Delays: what stopped work, the cause and the hours lost.
  7. Notes: anything else, such as visitors or instructions received.
  8. Tap Save to today's diary to add the lines, or Save and post the day to post the diary.

Why it matters

The diary is the evidence for every delay and extra-cost claim. A delay that is not in the diary did not happen, as far as the client is concerned.

Note: If the day is already posted, what you add is kept as an annotation. The posted diary is never changed.

Photos

  1. Tap Photos, then Take a photo.
  2. The camera opens. Take the photo.
  3. The app stamps it with the date, time, project code and GPS position.
  4. Add a caption, the bill item and the location.
  5. Save. It goes on today's diary, or into the site photo library.

Checks: inspections and snags

  1. Tap Checks. Inspections waiting on the site are listed, including those from inspection and test plans.
  2. Tap one. For each check, tap Pass, Fail or N/A and add an observation.
  3. Tap Save results. A fail raises a non-conformance report (NCR) for the QHSE team.

To report a snag: take a photo, say what is wrong, the location, the trade and the priority. The phone's position is saved as the snag's pin.

Watch out

Hold points are marked on the list. You cannot release a hold point from the phone. The releasing engineer does that in the office.

Requests

  • Material requisition: materials, quantities, needed-by date and notes. It goes straight to the buyer.
  • Raise an RFI (Site User): subject, the question, when you need the answer, urgent or not. It is saved as a draft for the document controller.
  • Incident or near miss: type, where, when and what happened. It goes to the QHSE register.

Check in

Tap the button when you arrive and again when you leave. The phone's position is checked against the site area. Check-in needs a connection, because the time comes from the server.

Working without signal

  • A grey bar saying Offline shows when there is no signal.
  • Everything you save waits in a queue on the phone. The cloud button at the top shows how many entries are waiting (red when one needs you).
  • When signal returns, the queue is sent automatically. Send now in the queue forces it.
  • An entry the office cannot accept (for example, the inspection was already closed) stays marked Needs you with the reason. Read it, then discard it.

Tip

Open the app once at the start of the day while you have signal, so it has the latest inspections and drawings.

On a computer

Today and the site diary

  • Site › Today on Site opens today's diary for your site.
  • Site › Daily Records › Site Diaries lists all diaries.

To post a diary on a computer:

  1. Open the diary and fill the tabs: Manpower, Plant, Progress, Delays, Materials, Visitors, Instructions.
  1. Check the weather is filled in.
  2. Click Post (the button name may read Post the Day). Posted diaries create the labour logs and delay events for the office.

Roll call (Site User)

When the crew have no phones, take a roll call.

  1. Open Site › Attendance › Crew Roll Call.
  1. Pick the project and the date, tick each person present, set times if needed.
  2. Confirm. Each person gets an attendance record waiting for approval.

Material requisitions

  1. Procurement & Stores, then New (or New Requisition on Home).
  1. Choose the Project, the Required Date and, on each line, the material, quantity and bill item.
  2. Click Submit. The project manager approves it, then the buyer orders it.
  3. When it arrives, the storekeeper books the delivery and issues it to the work.

Watch out

Order early. Check the delivery lead time with the buyer before you set the required date.

Inspections and snags

  • Quality & Safety › Quality › Inspections: inspections for your work. Record results here or on the phone.
  • Quality & Safety › Quality › Snags: defects to fix, with photo, location, trade and priority.

Job sheets

Site › Daily Records › Job Sheets: a daily record of the work done on a work order: bill item progress, labour, plant and materials used. Fill one per work order per day; the project manager approves it.

Approving attendance and time (supervisors)

  1. Site › Attendance › Attendance to Approve: approve or reject attendance exceptions.
  2. Labour › Time and Gangs › Time Allocation: for each person, set the bill item and cost code the hours went to. Use Split if a person worked on two jobs. Select the rows and click Approve. (See Chapter 19.)

Site photos

Site › Daily Records › Site Photos shows every photo from the app by day, bill item and location, with a map link.

Plant daily logs and fuel

  • Procurement & Stores › Plant and Fleet › Plant Daily Logs: one log per machine per day: site, operator, working, idle and breakdown hours, idle reason, meter readings, fuel and work done. Confirm sends the hours to the hire charge (hired plant) or the hour meter (own plant, for servicing).
  • Procurement & Stores › Plant and Fleet › Fuel Issues: choose the machine, the job and where the fuel came from, enter the litres and the meter reading, and click Post. The system works out the consumption and flags an issue well above the machine's norm, a meter that did not move, or more litres than the tank holds.
  • Driver Log Sheets record each vehicle's trips, and Plant Transfers move a machine between sites (Dispatch when it leaves, Receive when it arrives).

Watch out

A machine whose lifting or inspection certificate has expired may be blocked from use. The red banner on the machine says which certificate.

Daily routine

  1. Arrive, open the app, Check in.
  2. Look at Today for open inspections, hold points and snags.
  3. Take photos as work progresses.
  4. Record inspection results as soon as they are done.
  5. Raise requisitions for materials you will need next week.
  6. Before leaving: write the diary (manpower, plant, work done, delays) and Save and post the day.
  7. Check out.

Weekly routine

  1. Check Quality & Safety › Quality › Snags and close the ones that are fixed.
  2. Check requisitions you raised are approved and ordered.
  3. Supervisors: allocate and approve the week's time before Monday.
