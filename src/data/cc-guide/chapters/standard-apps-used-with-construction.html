Construction screens sit on top of standard business apps. Purchase holds the orders, Inventory the stock, Invoicing the bills and invoices, Employees the people, Maintenance the equipment requests, Project the tasks, Contacts the companies, and Dashboards the spreadsheet dashboards. This chapter describes each of their menus briefly.

The Dashboards app

Dashboards

Where: Dashboards

What it is for: Dashboards

How to use it:

  1. Open Dashboards.
  2. Pick the company, the project and the period at the top, if the screen has them.
  3. Read the tiles and charts. Click a tile, a bar or a table row to open the records behind it.
  4. Press the refresh button to read the latest figures.
