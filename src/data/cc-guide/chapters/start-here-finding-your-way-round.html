This chapter shows you every part of the screen. Once you know these, every other chapter is easy, because every screen in Construction Cloud works the same way.

Take 20 minutes, log in and follow along.

Logging in on a computer

Why it matters

Everything you do is recorded under your own name. Never share your login. If someone else uses it, their mistakes and approvals are signed with your name.

  1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
  2. Type the address your administrator gave you, for example https://construction-cloud.way4tech.com, and press Enter. The sign-in page opens.
  1. Type your Email (your login) and your Password.
  1. Click Log in. The Home screen opens.

Tip

Bookmark the address, or save it to your browser's home screen, so you never have to type it again.

Watch out: After several wrong passwords the system makes you wait before you can try again. If you have forgotten your password, click Reset Password next to the password box. You receive an email with a link to set a new one.

Your first login

Your administrator creates your user and the system sends you an invitation email. Click the link in it, choose a password and you are logged in. The link works once and expires after a few days. If it has expired, ask your administrator to send a new invitation.

If you are asked for a code

Some roles (usually Finance, Directors and Administrators) must use two-factor sign-in. After your password the system asks for a six-digit code from an app on your phone. See "Two-factor sign-in" later in this chapter.

Logging in on a phone

Site staff use the phone app at the address followed by /site, for example https://construction-cloud.way4tech.com/site. Office staff can also open the normal screens on a phone; they rearrange to fit.

  1. Open the phone's browser and go to the address followed by /site.
  2. Sign in with the same email and password you use on a computer.
  1. The site app opens on Today.
  1. To install it like an app, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen (iPhone: the Share button, then Add to Home Screen; Android Chrome: the three dots, then Install app). An icon appears on your phone's home screen.

Note

The site app keeps working without signal. What you record waits on the phone and is sent when the connection comes back. Chapter 12 explains the app in full.

My Dashboard

Every user also has a dashboard of their own: the figures, charts and lists they check every day, picked from all the dashboards, in the order and size they like. Open it from Home › My Dashboard, or click Open My Dashboard on the My Dashboard card on Home.

The first time you open it, it starts from the layout for your role (director, commercial manager, finance, project manager or site engineer).

  • Tiles show one figure. Click it to open the records behind it.
  • Charts show a trend, such as the S-curve or cash in and out.
  • Lists show up to ten rows, such as requisitions waiting. Click a row to open it.
  • The arrow in a widget's corner opens the full dashboard it comes from.

Choosing the job and the period

Use the toolbar at the top: pick All Time, This Month, This Quarter or This Year, and pick one job or All jobs. Your choice is kept for next time. Balances such as retention held are always as at today.

Changing what is on it

  1. Click Customise. The widget library opens on the right, grouped by subject. Type in the search box to find a widget.
  1. Click Add next to a widget. It lands at the end.
  2. Drag a widget by its title to move it. Use the size buttons to make it wider, narrower, taller or shorter.
  3. Click the bin to remove a widget.
  4. Click Save. Discard throws the changes away; Reset to template puts back your role's layout.

Note

The library only offers widgets your role may see. A figure your role does not see shows Hidden, never a number.

Getting around: How it works, help and tours

Home has a Getting around card with four ways to find your way.

How it works (the process maps)

Home › How it works shows the eleven main processes as chains of steps: Tender to Contract, Contract Setup, Procure to Pay, Subcontract, Measure to Cash, Variations and Claims, Site Day, Quality, Documents, Labour Cost and Closeout.

  1. Pick a process on the left. The badge next to it is the number of records waiting for you in it.
  2. Read the cards from left to right. Each card says who does the step, what happens, and what is waiting for you now (for example "3 measurements to agree").
  3. Click Open on a step to go straight to the screen, already filtered to the waiting records. A step done by another role says so instead.

Why it matters

How it works is the quickest way to see where your work fits, and what is waiting for you, without knowing any menu.

The ? help button

Every construction screen has a ? button next to the pager at the top right (on dashboards, a round ? at the bottom right). It opens a side panel with:

  • what the screen is and who uses it,
  • where it fits in the process, with the current step highlighted and what comes next,
  • three to five key steps,
  • a link to the chapter of this guide.

Empty lists also explain themselves: they say what the screen is for and how to create the first record.

What's next banners

Documents such as requisitions, valuations, certificates, variations, RFIs and site diaries show a coloured banner at the top. It says what the current stage means, what should happen next and who does it. When you are the one who acts, it has a button that does the next step (for example Submit or Issue Certificate).

Guided tours

On Home, click Tour for your role. A pointer takes you from Home to How it works, the process that matters most to your role, its first screen and the help button. It takes about two minutes. Show me how to offers task tours such as creating a project from a tender or doing a valuation. Click Stop Tour to leave at any time; you can take a tour again whenever you like.

Find anything

Press Ctrl+K and type #, or click Find anything on Home. Type two letters or more of a name or reference: a project, bill item, contract, tender, order, requisition, subcontract, drawing, RFI, variation, certificate, NCR, person or contact. Pick a result to open it.

Note

You only find records you could open from a menu.

Breadcrumbs: knowing where you are

As you open records, the trail at the top left grows: the list you came from, then the record, then anything you opened from it. This trail is called the breadcrumb.

  • Click any earlier part of the trail to go back to it.
  • The browser's back button also works.

Watch out

If you changed a record and then click away, the system saves the change for you. If the change cannot be saved (a required field is empty), it shows a red message instead. Fix the field or click the discard button (the curved arrow) to throw the change away.

Lists, and finding what you need

Most menus open a list of records.

Searching

  1. Click in the Search box at the top.
  2. Type a word, for example part of a project name.
  3. The box offers where to look, for example "Search Project for: Harlow". Use the arrow keys to pick, then press Enter.
  1. The list now shows only the matching records. The search appears as a grey tag in the box. Click the small x on the tag to remove it.

Filters

Filters are ready-made searches, such as My Records, This Month or To Approve.

  1. Click the small arrow at the right end of the search box. A panel opens with Filters, Group By and Favorites.
  1. Click a filter to switch it on. Click it again to switch it off.
  2. Two filters in the same block mean "either"; filters in different blocks mean "both".

Group by

Grouping puts the records into folders, for example one folder per project.

  1. Open the search panel.
  2. Under Group By, click Project (or any other field).
  1. Click a group's title to open or close it. Totals show at the right for number columns.

Saving a search you use often

  1. Set up the filters and grouping you want.
  2. Open the search panel and, under Favorites, click Save current search.
  3. Give it a name and click Save. Tick Default filter if you want this list to open that way every time.

Tip

Click a column title to sort by that column. Click it again to reverse the order.

Kanban

Cards can often be dragged from one column to another to change their stage. Some stages can only be changed with a button on the form, so dragging is refused there.

Form

The parts of a form:

  • Buttons at the top left (for example Submit, Approve). They move the record to its next stage.
  • Status bar at the top right, showing the stage the record is in.
  • Smart buttons in the row at the top. Each shows a count or a figure and opens the linked records. Click More to see the rest.
  • Fields, grouped in boxes and tabs.
  • The chatter on the right or at the bottom (see below).

To change a record, click in a field and type. The record is saved when you leave it, or when you click the cloud icon next to the breadcrumb.

Calendar

Switch between day, week, month and year at the top. Click an entry to see it.

Pivot

  1. Click a row or column title (with the + sign) to split it by another field, for example by month.
  2. Click Measures to choose which numbers to add up.
  3. Click a number to open the records behind it.
  4. Use the download button to save the table as an Excel file.

Graph

Choose bar, line or pie at the top. Measures picks the number, and the search panel's Group By picks the bars.

Chatter: messages, notes and history

Most records have a chatter panel. It is the record's diary: who changed what and when, plus every message about it.

  • Send message emails everyone following the record (for example the client contact). Use it only for messages the followers should receive.
  • Log note writes an internal note. Only your colleagues see it. Type @ and a name to tell a colleague; they get a notification.
  • Activity schedules a task on the record for someone (see below).
  • The paperclip shows and adds attachments.
  • Followers (the person icon) lists who receives the record's messages. Click to add or remove people.

Watch out

Send message can reach people outside the company. For internal comments, always use Log note.

Attachments

  1. Click the paperclip in the chatter.
  2. Click Attach files and choose a file, or drag a file onto the chatter.
  3. The file appears under the history. Click it to view or download.

Tip

Photos taken with the site app arrive as attachments on that day's diary, so you never need to email them.

Activities: your to-do list

An activity is a task with a due date, linked to a record. The system creates many for you, for example "Approve this requisition" or "Retention falls due". You can also create your own.

Seeing your activities

Click the clock icon at the top right. It lists your activities by type, with how many are late, due today and planned.

Click a line to open those records.

Scheduling an activity

  1. Open the record.
  2. In the chatter, click Activity.
  3. Choose the Activity Type (for example To-Do, Call, Email), a Due Date, who it is Assigned to, and a short Summary.
  1. Click Schedule. The person sees it in their activities menu.
  2. When the task is done, open the activity on the record and click Mark Done, with a short note of what happened.

Why it matters

Activities are how nothing falls between two chairs. A late activity turns red and shows on your dashboard until it is done.

Notifications and messages

The speech-bubble icon at the top right shows new messages: mentions, notes addressed to you, and direct chats with colleagues.

  • Click a message to open it.
  • Discuss (in the app grid) holds all your conversations and channels in one place.

You can choose to receive notifications by email or only inside the system. See "Your preferences" below.

Your preferences, password and language

  1. Click your picture or initial at the top right. Your user menu opens.
  1. Click Preferences. Your preferences open.
  1. Change what you need: - Language: the language of every screen and printout you open. - Timezone: must match where you work, or times on diaries and attendance will look wrong. - Notification: by email, or inside the system only. - Email Signature: added at the end of messages you send. - Your photo: click the picture to upload a new one.
  2. Click Save.

Changing your password

  1. Open Preferences and click the Security tab.
  1. Click Change Password.
  2. Type your current password, then the new one twice.
  3. Click Change Password. You stay logged in.

Tip

Use a long password that you do not use anywhere else. A short sentence works well.

Two-factor sign-in

Two-factor sign-in asks for a code from your phone as well as your password. Even if someone learns your password, they cannot log in without your phone.

You need an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy.

  1. Open Preferences, then the Security tab.
  2. Click Enable two-factor authentication.
  3. Type your password to confirm it is you.
  4. A QR code appears. Open the authenticator app, add an account and scan the code.
  5. Type the six-digit code the app shows into Verification Code and click Activate.

From now on, after your password the system asks for the current code from the app.

Watch out

If your role requires two-factor sign-in, the system sends you to a "Set up two-factor sign-in" page every time it loads until you have done it. If you lose your phone, ask your administrator to switch two-factor off for your user, then set it up again on the new phone.

Logging out

Click your picture at the top right, then Log out. Always log out on a shared computer.

Printing and exporting

Printing a record

  1. Open the record, for example a payment certificate.
  2. Click the gear icon next to the record's name at the top, then Print, or use the Print button if the form has one.
  3. Pick the document, for example Payment Certificate.
  1. A PDF downloads. Open it to print or send.

Note

Printouts use your company letterhead, set by the administrator.

Exporting a list to Excel

  1. Open the list and filter it to the records you want.
  2. Tick the box at the left of the rows you want, or the box in the title row to select all.
  3. Click Actions (the gear) and choose Export.
  1. Pick the fields you want. Drag them on the right to change the order.
  2. Click Export. An Excel file downloads.

Tip

Save your field list with Save as new template so you can export the same columns next month in one click.

Note: Some roles cannot export. The auditor role can export everything it reads but cannot change anything.

A quick practice

Try these on your own login. None of them change any data.

  1. Open Home and click your dashboard.
  2. Open Site › Daily Records › Site Diaries, search for one project, then group by Project.
  3. Switch to the calendar view and back to the list.
  4. Open one diary and read its chatter.
  5. Open your Preferences and check your time zone.
