The storekeeper checks what arrives on site, keeps the site store, issues materials to the work and moves stock between sites. Your records turn deliveries into actual cost on the right bill item. Storekeepers use the Procurement and Stores role, the same as buyers.

What you see when you log in

The same Home and Procurement Dashboard as the buyer (Chapter 15). The tiles that matter most to you are Deliveries Late and Tools Issued.

Receiving a delivery

Why it matters

The goods received note is the proof of what arrived. Finance only pays a supplier bill that matches the order and what you received. A delivery that is not booked is either paid without proof or not paid at all.

  1. Open Procurement & Stores › Buying › Goods Received.
  1. Open the note for the order (or click New and pick the purchase order).
  2. For each line, enter the quantity actually received. Note any damage or shortage.
  1. Attach the signed delivery ticket (photo or scan).
  2. Confirm it. The stock is now in the site store.

Watch out

Count before you sign the driver's ticket. Book what you counted, not what the ticket says.

The site store

Procurement & Stores › Site Stores › Site Stock shows what each site holds now, by material.

Issuing materials to the work

Issuing books the material against the bill item it is used on. This is when it becomes actual cost on that item.

  1. Open Procurement & Stores › Site Stores › Issue to Work (also under Procurement & Stores › Site Stores).
  1. Pick the project and the bill item (or work order) the material is for.
  2. Add each material and quantity.
  3. Confirm. The site store goes down and the bill item's actual cost goes up.

Moving stock between sites

Procurement & Stores › Site Stores › Site Transfers: create a transfer from one site to another, with the materials and quantities. Confirm when it leaves and when it arrives.

Checking use and waste

  • Procurement & Stores › Site Stores › Consumption Analysis: what each project used, by material and bill item.
  • Procurement & Stores › Site Stores › Material Reconciliation: for tracked materials (concrete, steel, blocks), what was delivered, what the bill says should have been used, and the waste. Dashboards & Reports › Site and Time › Waste by Material compares waste across jobs.

Tools and custody

  1. Procurement & Stores › Plant and Tools › Tools: each small tool with its tag and condition.
  2. Procurement & Stores › Plant and Tools › Custody Documents: issue tools or equipment to a person, who signs for them. Return them on the same document.
  3. Procurement & Stores › Plant and Tools › Custody Register: who holds what now.

Daily routine

  1. Book every delivery the day it arrives.
  2. Issue materials to the work as they leave the store.
  3. Check Deliveries Late and tell the site what will not arrive.

Weekly routine

  1. Count the high-value items and compare with Site Stores.
  2. Check the custody register and chase overdue tool returns.
