Construction Cloud is the system your company uses to run its construction jobs. Tenders, bills of quantities, site diaries, safety, buying, labour, valuations, certificates, invoices and the final account all live in one place. Everyone works on the same records, so the figure the director sees is the figure the site typed in.

This guide shows you how to use it. It starts from zero and goes as far as an administrator needs.

How the guide is organised

The guide has seven parts.

  1. Getting started (Chapters 0 to 2). How to log in, how every screen works, and the ten ideas the system is built on.
  2. Your role, day by day (Chapters 10 to 21). One chapter per job. Each one shows what you see when you log in, explains your dashboard tile by tile, and walks through your daily, weekly and monthly tasks.
  3. Menu by menu (Chapters 30 to 49). Every menu and screen, what it is for and who uses it.
  4. Business flows (Chapters 50 to 69). The main processes followed from start to finish, such as "from a material request to a paid supplier bill".
  5. How everything connects (Chapter 70). Which record leads to which, and where every important number comes from.
  6. Expert setup and control (Chapter 80). Setup, users and roles, approval limits, month-end close, alerts, imports and exports.
  7. Reference (Chapters 90 to 99). Glossary, questions and answers, and the menu map.

How each chapter is laid out

Most sections follow the same pattern.

  1. A short line saying what the feature is.
  2. A Why it matters box saying what it protects: money, time, safety or a claim.
  3. The menu path to open it.
  4. Numbered steps, with a screenshot at the important ones.
  5. Tips and watch-outs.

The signs used in this guide

Tip

A quicker way, a shortcut or a good habit.

Note: Extra detail that is useful but not needed to finish the task.

Watch out: A common mistake, or something that cannot be undone easily.

Why it matters: What the feature protects or saves. Read these if you wonder why a step exists.

About the screenshots

The screenshots come from a demonstration company called Kestrel Construction Ltd. It runs three jobs:

  • Harlow Court, Levels 3 to 5 Fit-Out, an office fit-out for Harlow Estates Ltd.
  • Northgate Wharf, Residential Block B, a block of flats for Northgate Living LLP.
  • Aldercar Lane Culvert and Highway Works, a civil job for Wessex County Council.

Your company, jobs, people and figures will be different. The screens and buttons will be the same.

Note

What you see depends on your role. A site engineer does not see costs or margins, and a buyer does not see valuations. If a menu in this guide is missing on your screen, your role does not include it. Ask your administrator if you think you need it.
