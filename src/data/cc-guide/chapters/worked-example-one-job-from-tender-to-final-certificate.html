Chapter 1. The bid: Fairview Medical Annex

Fairview Health Trust invited Kestrel to tender for a medical annex. The tender is in the pipeline with its own estimate bill.

Find the tender and its estimate

  1. Open Sales & Tenders › Tenders and find the card "Fairview Medical Annex".
  2. Open it. Read the client, the value and the stage. The go / no-go answers and the competitors are on its tabs.
  3. Open its estimate bill "Fairview Medical Annex: Tender Estimate" from Contracts & Setup › Bills of Quantities › Bills of Quantities.

Why it matters: The estimate stays with the tender, so the final cost can be compared with it later.

The award created the job

  1. Fairview was awarded, so a project "Fairview Medical Annex" and a draft contract CON/2026/00004 exist.
  2. Open Contracts & Setup › Contracts and open CON/2026/00004. It is still in Draft: the commercial manager now checks the terms (see the flow chapter "Setting up a contract").

Tip

Compare the four contracts in the list: Draft (Fairview), Active (Northgate and Harlow Court) and Defects (Aldercar Lane). Together they show the whole life of a contract.

Chapter 2. The start: Northgate Wharf, Residential Block B

Northgate Living LLP's residential block is in its first months on site.

Set-up done

  1. The contract bill "Northgate Wharf Block B, Contract Bill" is agreed (Contract stage).
  2. The programme PRG/2026/0002 is frozen as the baseline (Planning › Programmes).
  3. Two inspection and test plans, ITP-0001 and ITP-0002, are active (Quality & Safety › Quality › Inspection and Test Plans).

Money in early: the advance

  1. Open Commercial & Billing › Advances and Escalation › Advance Payments: ADV/2026/0001 for 385,000 is Paid.
  2. Its guarantee BG/2026/0004 is in force in Sales & Tenders › Bond Register.
  3. The first valuation VAL/NW/001 is certified and its certificate IPC/NW/001 is paid. Recovery of the advance started on it.

Quality catches a problem

  1. An inspection on the external walls failed. NCR/2026/00004 "Lintels, bearings and movement joints" was raised from it (Quality & Safety › Quality › Non-conformance Reports).
  2. Until the hold point is released, the covered bill items cannot be measured.

Chapter 3. The middle: Harlow Court, Levels 3 to 5 Fit-Out

Harlow Estates Ltd's office fit-out is the busiest job: monthly valuations, many variations, delays and a claim.

The monthly money cycle

  1. Measurement sheets MB/HC/001 to MB/HC/008 are in Commercial & Billing › Measurement › Measurement Book: older ones Certified, MB/HC/006 and 007 Measured, MB/HC/008 still Draft.
  2. Valuations VAL/HC/001 to 005 are Certified. VAL/HC/006 is at Applied, waiting for the client's certificate.
  3. Certificates IPC/HC/001 to 004 are Paid. IPC/HC/005 is Issued and waiting for payment.

Why it matters: A full measure-to-cash cycle can be followed record by record.

Change: instructions and variations

  1. Site instructions SI/2026/00001 to 00008: most Converted into variations, SI/2026/00005 Lapsed (never confirmed in writing).
  2. Variations VO-006 to VO-018 show every stage: Identified, Submitted, Under Review, Agreed (VO-014 agreed at 28,940 against an estimate of 30,800), Rejected and Cancelled.
  3. Daywork sheets DW/HC/001 to 006 run from Draft to Signed to Valued into a variation.

Delay, notices and the claim

  1. Delay events DLY/2026/00001 to 00003 are in Site › Daily Records › Delays and Stoppages.
  2. EOT/2026/0001 is Submitted (Commercial & Billing › Variations and Claims › Extension of Time Claims).
  3. Notices in Contracts & Setup › Contract Administration › Notices Register show issued, acknowledged, responded and one Lapsed (CN/2026/0005): a time bar that was missed.
  4. Claim CL/2026/0001 is Submitted (Commercial & Billing › Variations and Claims › Contract Claims).

Subcontractors and supply

  1. Eleven packages are awarded on this job, from demolition to passive fire protection. Subcontracts SUB/2026/00001 to 00011 run from Done to In Progress.
  2. Requisitions MRQ/2026/00001 onwards show Submitted, Approved and Issued. RFQ/2026/00004 has its quotes in and RFQ/2026/00006 is a draft.
  3. The award of the tiling package to Ashworth Drylining was blocked by supplier compliance (see the compliance log).

The month-end view

  1. CVR/2026/00001 is Signed. CVR/2026/00002 is the current month in Draft (Commercial & Billing › Cost Value Reconciliation › Reconciliations).
  2. Escalation statements PA/2026/0001 and 0002 are computed.
  3. Commissioning has started: FA-01 Fire alarm is Commissioned, HV-01 HVAC Pre-Commissioned, EL-01 Testing (Closeout › Commissioning › Systems).
  4. The handover package HO/001 "Levels 3 to 5 handover" is being collected.

Chapter 4. The end: Aldercar Lane Culvert and Highway Works

Wessex County Council's highway job is built and taken over. It is in its defects liability period.

Taken over

  1. Contract CON/2026/00003 is in the Defects stage.
  2. Taking-over certificate TOC/001 is Issued (Closeout › Handover › Completion Certificates). Issuing it moved the contract into the defects period and made the first retention moiety fall due.
  3. Handover package HO/002 "Aldercar Lane civils handover" is Issued to the client.
  4. EOT/2026/0002 was Awarded, so no liquidated damages apply for those days.

Defects, retention and bonds

  1. Defects reported by the client on the portal are in Closeout › Defects Period › Defects.
  2. Retention held and due is in Commercial & Billing › Retention › Retention Ledger.
  3. Bond BG/2026/0005 for Aldercar Lane is already Released.

What is left to do

  1. When the defects period ends, press Raise Final Certificate on TOC/001, work through the checklist and issue it.
  2. Release the final retention moiety, agree the final account (Closeout › Final Account › Final Accounts), and complete the closeout checks.
  3. Press Archive Project on the closeout checklist. The job is finished.

Why it matters: The last retention money is often the profit on the job. The closeout checklist makes sure it is collected.
